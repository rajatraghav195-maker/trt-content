2025 REVIEW: BANKING KA SAAL

Nifty +10.51%, Bank Nifty +17.15%, PSU Bank +30.5% — aur IT, Realty, Media laal

Published by The Retail Trader · DEC 31, 2025 · Performance Review

2025 ek accha saal tha — par sabke liye nahi. Index level par double-digit gain, aur sector level par aath mein se paanch negative. Ye us tarah ka saal hai jahan 'market ne 10% diya' aur 'mera portfolio laal hai' dono sach ho sakte hain.

INDEX LEVEL

Poora saal:
• Bank Nifty — +17.15%
• Nifty 50 — +10.51%
• Sensex — +9.06%
• Nifty 500 — +6.69%

Dhyaan do: ladder ulta hai. Nifty 50 ne Nifty 500 aur Midcap 100 dono ko peechhe chhoda. Ye 19 saal mein sirf 7 baar hua hai ki largecap midcap se aage rahe — 2025 unmein se ek hai.

SECTOR LEVEL: TEEN JEETE, PAANCH HAARE

PSU Bank 2025 ka leader tha. Hamare sector rotation study ka pattern yaad karo: 2012 se ab tak ek bhi baar pichhle saal ka leader agle saal leader nahi bana. 2026 mein PSU Bank year-to-date -2.2% par hai — pattern phir se dohraya gaya.

2024 SE TULNA

2024 mein Midcap 100 +23.86% par tha aur Nifty +8.80%. 2025 mein wo palat gaya: Midcap +5.74%, Nifty +10.51%. Ek saal mein 15 point ka swing sirf size ke basis par — bina kisi sector call ke.
