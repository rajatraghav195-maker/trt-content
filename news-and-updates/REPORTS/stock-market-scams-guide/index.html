Stock market scams India, pump and dump, operator manipulation — complete Hindi guide investors ke liye.

Common Scams in Indian Market

1. Pump and Dump

How it works:
• Operators cheap stock kharidte hain
• WhatsApp/Telegram pe tips dete hain — "Ye stock 10x hoga!"
• Retail investors kharidte hain → Price badhta hai
• Operators bechte hain → Price crash
• Retail investors ka paisa dooba

2. Insider Trading

How it works:
• Company insiders (promoters, employees) ko pata hai ki kuch hone wala hai (merger, acquisition, earnings)
• Woh pehle stock kharidte hain
• News aane pe profit book karte hain
• SEBI illegal hai — Penalty + jail possible

3. Front Running

How it works:
• Broker pata hai ki bada client order de raha hai
• Broker pehle apna order place karta hai
• Client ke order se price move hota hai
• Broker profit book karta hai

4. Circular Trading

How it works:
• Same group ke log ek stock ke shares ek dusre ko bechte hain
• Volume badhta hai dikhane ke liye
• Retail investors attract hote hain
• Operators exit karte hain

5. Fake Tips

How it works:
• WhatsApp/Telegram groups mein "SEBI registered" analyst tips deta hai
• Subscription charge ₹5,000-50,000
• Tips fail hote hain
• Paisa dooba

How to Identify Scams

Red Flags:
• "Guaranteed returns" — Market mein kuch guaranteed nahi hai
• "100% profit in 1 month" — Impossible consistently
• "Secret strategy" — Koi secret nahi hai
• "SEBI registered" but no registration number
• Pressure to buy immediately
• Penny stock tips from unknown sources

How to Verify:
• SEBI registration: sebi.gov.in pe check karo
• Tips source: Known, reputable sources se lo
• Penny stocks: Avoid tips, do your own research
• WhatsApp groups: Leave if they give buy/sell tips

How to Protect Yourself

Stop Loss Lagao

Agar scam mein fas gaye toh stop loss tumhe bachata hai.

Apna Research Karo

Tips pe blindly follow mat karo. Fundamentals check karo.

Penny Stocks Avoid Karo

Penny stocks sabse zyada manipulate hote hain.

SEBI Registered Advisor

Sirf SEBI registered advisor ki advice lo. Registration number verify karo.

Diversify

Ek stock pe sab paisa mat lagao. Diversify karo.
