JANUARY 2026 AUDIT: SAAL KI SHURUAAT KAMZOR

Nifty -3.10% — aur ye historically saal ka sabse kamzor mahina raha hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JAN 30, 2026 · Monthly Audit

January 2026 Nifty ke liye -3.10% raha. Hamare seasonality study ke hisaab se ye koi asaadharan baat nahi hai: pichhle 19 saal mein January ka average -1.27% raha hai aur sirf 26% January hare band hue hain — poore calendar ka sabse kamzor mahina.

INDEX LEVEL

20 sessions:
• Bank Nifty — +0.05%, lagbhag bilkul flat
• Nifty 50 — -3.10%
• Nifty 500 — -3.32%
• Midcap 100 — -3.39%
• Sensex — -3.46%

Bank Nifty ke alawa sab lagbhag ek jaise gire — 3.1% se 3.5% ke beech. Jab sab kuch ek hi range mein girta hai, to wo broad de-rating hota hai, kisi ek sector ki problem nahi.

Nifty ke 20 sessions mein 8 up, 12 down. Mahine ka sabse achha din pehla hi tha — 2 January (+0.70%) — aur wahi din poore 2026 ka peak ban gaya, jahan se abhi tak ki girawat naapi jati hai.

SECTOR LEVEL

Char upar, char neeche:
• Metal — +5.9%
• PSU Bank — +5.7%
• IT — +0.4%
• Bank — 0.0%
• Media — -4.1%
• Pharma — -4.4%

FMCG ka -7.7% dhyaan dene layak hai kyunki wo poore dataset ka sabse kam volatile sector hai (annualised volatility 16.5%, Nifty ke barabar). Us sector mein ek mahine mein 7.7% ka move uske apne standard se bada hai.

VOLATILITY

India VIX 9.45 ke low se 14.45 tak gaya, close 13.63. December 2025 mein wo 9.15 tak gaya tha — poore recent record ka sabse shaant level. January se wo shaanti khatam hui, aur March tak VIX 27.89 tak pahunch gaya.
