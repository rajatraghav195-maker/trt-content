What Traders Can Learn This Week

Lesson 1: Sector Rotation Is Real

IT sector under pressure while defense/PSU rally karta raha. Ye sector rotation hai — jab ek sector weak hai, dusra strong. Diversified portfolio rakhna important hai.

Lesson 2: Consolidation Breakout Patience

Market puri week sideways raha. Consolidation ke dauran trade lena = noise mein paisa khatam. Wait for breakout/breakdown with volume.

Lesson 3: Defense Sector Trend

Defense stocks (HAL, BEL, BHEL) mein strong uptrend hai. Government spending + Make in India push. Lekin chase mat karo — pullback ka wait karo.
