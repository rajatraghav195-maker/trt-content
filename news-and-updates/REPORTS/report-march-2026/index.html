MARCH 2026 AUDIT: SAAL KA SABSE BURA MAHINA

Nifty -11.31%, Bank Nifty -16.94%, India VIX 27.89 — har sector negative

March 2026 poore dataset ke sabse kharab mahinon mein se ek hai. Har index aur har sector negative band hua — aisa mahina kam aata hai, kyunki aam taur par kuch na kuch bachta hai.

INDEX LEVEL

• Sensex — -11.49%
• Nifty 500 — -11.39%
• Nifty 50 — -11.31%

Bank Nifty ka Nifty se 5.6 point zyada girna hamare Bank Nifty wale analysis se mel khata hai: uska beta 1.18 hai, aur bure dinon mein wo Nifty se zyada girta hai. March mein wo rishta poori tarah dikha.

Nifty ke 19 sessions mein 8 up aur 11 down the. Sabse bura din 19 March (-3.26%), sabse achha 24 March (+1.78%) — yaani trough ke aaspaas hi sabse bada up-day bhi aaya, wahi pattern jo humne 2008 aur 2020 mein dekha tha.

VOLATILITY: DAR KA PEAK

India VIX mahine ke andar 17.13 se 27.89 tak gaya aur wahin band hua — poore 2026 ka sabse ooncha level. Reference ke liye: December 2025 mein VIX 9.15 ke low tak gaya tha. Teen mahine mein dar teen guna ho gaya.

Hamare VIX study ke hisaab se 25-35 wale regime ke baad Nifty ka average 3-mahine ka return +1.08% raha hai, aur 60% baar positive. April mein +7.46% aaya — us average se kaafi upar, par usi direction mein.
