JANUARY 2017 AUDIT: NIFTY 4.6% UPAR

Nifty +4.59% · sabse aage Metal +15.82% · sabse peechhe IT -5.30%

Published by The Retail Trader · JAN 31, 2017 · Monthly Audit

January 2017 ka audit — is mahine ke 21 sessions mein har bade index aur har sector ne kya kiya, sab 2016-12-30 ke close se 2017-01-31 ke close tak naapa gaya.

INDEX LEVEL

21 sessions ka hisaab, sabse aage se sabse peechhe:
• Midcap 100 — +7.40%, close 15413.70
• Bank Nifty — +7.36%, close 19515.15
• Nifty 500 — +5.68%, close 7379.30
• Nifty 50 — +4.59%, close 8561.30
• Sensex — +3.87%, close 27655.96

Nifty ke 21 sessions mein 12 up aur 9 down the. Sabse achha din 2017-01-25 (+1.50%), sabse bura 2017-01-20 (-1.02%). Midcap 100 aur Nifty ke beech 2.8 point ka farq raha — bazaar ka chaudha hissa largecap se aage tha.

SECTOR LEVEL

Sabse acche aur sabse bure sector ke beech 21.1 percentage point ka farq raha:
• Metal — +15.82%
• Realty — +8.59%
• Bank — +7.36%
• PSU Bank — +6.45%

Ye spread dhyaan dene layak hai: index ne +4.59% likha, par Metal +15.82% par tha aur IT -5.30% par. Ek hi mahine mein do portfolio ke liye ye bilkul alag mahina tha.

VOLATILITY AUR DRAWDOWN

India VIX mahine mein 14.38 aur 16.83 ke beech raha aur 16.83 par band hua. Mahine ke andar Nifty ka sabse gehra peak-to-trough fall -1.0% raha — yaani mahine ka net number +4.59% hone ke bawajood beech mein itni girawat dekhi gayi.
