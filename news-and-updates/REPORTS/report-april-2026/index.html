APRIL 2026 AUDIT: REBOUND MAHINA

Nifty +7.46%, Midcap +13.55%, Realty +21.9% — March ke baad ka palatwar

Published by The Retail Trader · APR 30, 2026 · Monthly Audit

March 2026 mein Nifty 11.31% gira tha. April mein wo 7.46% wapas aaya — aur jo sectors sabse zyada gire the, wahi sabse zyada laute. Ye rebound ka classic shape hai.

INDEX LEVEL

20 sessions:
• Midcap 100 — +13.55%
• Nifty 500 — +10.50%
• Bank Nifty — +9.13%
• Nifty 50 — +7.46%
• Sensex — +6.90%

Ladder bilkul ulta hai March ke muqable: jo March mein sabse zyada gira, April mein wahi sabse zyada chadha. Nifty ke 20 sessions mein 12 up, 8 down. Sabse achha din 8 April (+3.78%) — poore 2026 ka sabse bada single-day gain.

SECTOR LEVEL: JO SABSE ZYADA GIRA, WAHI SABSE ZYADA LAUTA

April ka return, aur bracket mein March ka:
• Realty — +21.9% (March mein -16.6%)
• Media — +15.4% (March -10.9%)
• Metal — +15.2% (March -9.0%)
• FMCG — +12.2% (March -11.0%)
• Bank — +9.1% (March -16.9%)
• PSU Bank — +7.7% (March -19.8%)
• Pharma — +4.7% (March -3.1%)
• IT — +1.0% (March -5.0%)

Dhyaan do: Pharma aur IT — jo March mein sabse kam gire the — April mein sabse kam laute. Rebound ki taakat girawat ki gehrai se judi thi, kisi fundamental se nahi.

PAR POORA NUKSAAN WAPAS NAHI AAYA

Ye zaroori hai: PSU Bank March mein -19.8% gira aur April mein +7.7% laut-a. Recovery ka ganit yaad rakho — -19.8% ke baad wahin pahunchne ke liye +24.7% chahiye tha, +7.7% nahi. Rebound bada dikhta hai, par gehri girawat ko poora bharne mein bahut zyada lagta hai.

India VIX 25.52 ke high se girkar 18.46 par band hua — dar kam hua, par March se pehle wale level par nahi.
