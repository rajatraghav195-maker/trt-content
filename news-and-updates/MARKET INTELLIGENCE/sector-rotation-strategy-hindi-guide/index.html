Sector Rotation Kya Hai?

Sector rotation = jab capital ek sector se dusre sector mein move karta hai. Kabhi IT sector rally karta hai, kabhi Banking, kabhi Auto, kabhi Pharma.

Example:
• 2023 mein IT sector underperform kiya
• 2024 mein IT sector strong comeback kiya
• 2025 mein Defense/PSU sector massive rally
• 2026 mein Banking sector steady growth

Sector rotation samajhna = kab konsa sector buy karna hai, kab exit karna hai. Ye skill seekho toh consistent profit possible.

Sector Rotation Phases

Phase 1: Accumulation:
• Smart money (FII/DII) ek sector mein quietly kharidna shuru karta hai
• Volume badhta hai but price abhi nahi
• Sector media mein zyada discuss nahi hota
• Signal: Volume increase + price base formation + RSI > 50

Phase 2: Markup (Rally):
• Sector visible upmove shuru karta hai
• Media coverage starts
• Retail traders notice karte hain
• Signal: Price above 50 EMA + 200 EMA + volume surge
• Action: Best time to enter

Phase 3: Distribution:
• Smart money exit karta hai (profit booking)
• Price sideways to slightly up
• Volume high but price not moving up
• Signal: Price flat + volume high = distribution
• Action: Exit ya tighten SL

Phase 4: Decline:
• Sector neeche jaata hai
• Media abhi bhi bullish articles likh rahe hain (lagging)
• Retail traders trapped
• Signal: Price below 50 EMA + 200 EMA + RSI < 40
• Action: Avoid ya short

How to Identify Sector Rotation

Method 1: Relative Strength (RS):
• Compare sector vs Nifty 50
• If sector outperforming Nifty → RS positive → bullish
• If sector underperforming Nifty → RS negative → bearish
• Formula: Sector return / Nifty return over X period

Method 2: Money Flow (FII/DII Data):
• Check FII/DII sector-wise flow daily
• FII buying in sector = accumulation
• FII selling = distribution
• Sources: NSE website, Moneycontrol

Method 3: Sector ETF/Mutual Fund Flow:
• Sector mutual fund inflows/outflows track karo
• Increasing AUM = money flowing in
• Decreasing AUM = money flowing out

Method 4: TRT Screener:
• theretailtrader.org/stock-analysis-landing → Screener
• Filter: "Sector = [X], RSI > 60, Volume > 2x average"
• Sort by "Change %" to see hot sectors

Sector Rotation Strategy — Step by Step

Step 1: Weekly Sector Scan (Saturday):
• Check all 10 sectors' weekly performance
• Rank: top 3 performing sectors (strongest)
• Rank: bottom 3 performing sectors (weakest)
• Identify: which sectors gaining momentum (volume + price + RSI)
• Identify: which sectors losing momentum

Step 2: Select Target Sector:
• Choose top 2-3 sectors showing accumulation/markup
• Avoid sectors in decline phase
• Confirm: Is sector above 50 EMA + 200 EMA on weekly chart?

Step 3: Stock Selection Within Sector:
• Pick top 3-5 stocks within chosen sector
• Criteria: Above 200 EMA, volume > average, RSI 50-70
• Setup: Pullback to 20 EMA → bounce

Step 4: Entry:
• Stock at support + sector bullish + Nifty bullish = triple confirmation
• Entry on reversal candle
• SL below support
• Target: 1:2 R/R minimum

Step 5: Exit Strategy:
• If sector starts weakening (RS drops below Nifty) → exit
• If stock hits target → book profit
• If stock hits SL → exit (no averaging)
• Rotate capital from weak sector to strong sector

Seasonal Sector Patterns (India)

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Jan-Mar (Pre-Budget) — Strong Sectors: Infra, PSU, Defense, Reason: Budget expectations
• Apr-Jun (Q1) — Strong Sectors: IT, Reason: US budget cycle, new projects
• Jul-Sep (Monsoon) — Strong Sectors: FMCG, Auto (rural), Agri, Reason: Rural demand, monsoon impact
• Oct-Dec (Festive) — Strong Sectors: Auto, FMCG, Realty, Reason: Diwali, wedding season
• Any — Strong Sectors: Banking, Reason: Rate cycle dependent (RBI policy)

Common Mistakes

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Chasing hot sector — Jab media sector ki baat karta hai, rotation already ho chuki hoti hai
• Sector ignore karna — Sirf stock analysis karke trade lena = half picture
• Over-concentration — Poora capital ek sector mein = sector decline = total loss
• Rotation speed ignore karna — Kabhi rotation 1-2 months mein hota hai, kabhi 2-3 weeks. Flexibility rakho.
• Sector-specific news pe trade — "RBI rate cut = banking up" — already priced in ho sakta hai

FAQ

Q1: Sector rotation kitne din mein hota hai?

Weeks se months tak. Kabhi quick (2-3 weeks), kabhi slow (3-6 months). Patience rakho.

Q2: Best sector for beginners?

Banking (HDFC, ICICI) aur IT (TCS, Infosys) — high liquidity, predictable, well-covered.

Q3: Sector rotation pe options trade kar sakte hain?

Haan — sector ETF options (Nifty Bank, Nifty IT) best for sector rotation options.

Q4: TRT pe sector performance kahan dekho?

theretailtrader.org/charting → market summary panel mein sector indices live data available. Ya theretailtrader.org/index-details mein sectoral indices check karo.

Q5: Kitne sectors mein simultaneously invest karein?

3-5 sectors = good diversification. Ek sector mein max 30% portfolio.
