Market Breadth: Market Ka Real Health Check — Hinglish Guide

Market Breadth: Market Ka Real Health Check — Hinglish Guide

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Global Macro

Market breadth ek simple concept hai: Index upar ho sakta hai, lekin market ke andar kya ho raha hai? Nifty 50 upar gaya — lekin kya 50 mein se 40 stocks upar gaye ya sirf 5 heavyweights ne index ko pull kiya?

Market Breadth Kya Hai?

Market breadth ye batata hai ki market ka move "broad" hai (saare stocks participate kar rahe hain) ya "narrow" (sirf few stocks index ko drive kar rahe hain).

Key Market Breadth Indicators

1. Advance-Decline Ratio (A/D Ratio)

Formula: Advancing Stocks / Declining Stocks

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• > 2.0 — Meaning: 2 stocks up for every 1 down, Signal: Strong bull market
• 1.0 - 2.0 — Meaning: More advances than declines, Signal: Healthy market
• 0.5 - 1.0 — Meaning: More declines than advances, Signal: Weak market
• < 0.5 — Meaning: 2 stocks down for every 1 up, Signal: Strong bear market

How to check: NSE website pe daily advancing/declining stocks count hota hai. Ya TRT charting terminal pe community trends section mein.

2. Market Breadth Thrust

What: 40% se zyada stocks 2%+ upar gaye ek din mein = breadth thrust

Signal: Breadth thrust = strong rally ahead. Historically, breadth thrust ke baad market typically 5-10% upar jaata hai next 2-4 weeks mein.

3. New Highs vs New Lows

What: Kitne stocks 52-week high banaye vs 52-week low banaye

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• New highs >> new lows — Healthy bull market
• New highs ≈ new lows — Transition/uncertainty
• New lows >> new highs — Bear market warning

4. Stocks Above Moving Averages

What: Kitne stocks apne 50 DMA ya 200 DMA ke upar hain?

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• > 70% — Strong bull market
• 50-70% — Healthy market
• 30-50% — Weak/correcting market
• < 30% — Bear market

5. Cumulative A/D Line

What: Running total of (advances - declines) over time

Signal:
• A/D line rising + Nifty rising = confirmed bull market ✅
• A/D line falling + Nifty rising = divergence ⚠️ (index up but internals weak)
• A/D line rising + Nifty falling = bottom forming (internals improving)
• A/D line falling + Nifty falling = confirmed bear market ❌

Why Market Breadth Matters

Example: "Fake Rally"

Nifty 24,000 se 24,500 gaya (+2%). Lagta hai market strong hai. Lekin:
• A/D ratio: 0.6 (more stocks declining)
• New highs: 5 (very few)
• New lows: 45 (many)
• Stocks above 200 DMA: 35%

Conclusion: Nifty upar gaya sirf Reliance, HDFC, ICICI, Infosys, TCS ki wajah se (heavyweights). Baaki market weak hai. Ye "narrow rally" hai — sustainable nahi.

Example: "Healthy Correction"

Nifty 24,500 se 24,200 gir gaya (-1.2%). Lagta hai market weak hai. Lekin:
• A/D ratio: 1.8 (more stocks still advancing)
• New highs: 35
• New lows: 8
• Stocks above 200 DMA: 65%

Conclusion: Index gir gaya but internals strong. Sirf few heavyweights drag kiye. Broad market healthy hai. This is a buying opportunity.

How To Use Market Breadth In Trading

Strategy 1: Breadth Confirmation

Rule: Index breakout pe trade only if breadth confirms

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Nifty breaks resistance ✅
• A/D ratio > 1.5 ✅
• New highs > 20 ✅
• → Buy with confidence

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Nifty breaks resistance ✅
• A/D ratio < 0.8 ❌
• New highs < 5 ❌
• → Avoid breakout (likely fake)

Strategy 2: Breadth Divergence (Contrarian):
• Nifty making new highs but A/D line falling = bearish divergence
• Nifty making new lows but A/D line rising = bullish divergence
• Divergence = reversal warning within 1-2 weeks

Strategy 3: Breadth Thrust Trading:
• Watch for days where >40% stocks gain 2%+
• After thrust: buy index ETF or strong sector stocks
• Hold 2-4 weeks
• Historical win rate: 70-80%

TRT Charting Terminal Se Breadth Check Karne Ka Tarika

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Community Trends section: Shows market breadth summary
• Volume Stocks section: Shows most active stocks (proxy for breadth)
• Gainers/Losers section: Direct count of advancing vs declining
• Manual method: Count green vs red candles on Nifty 50 constituents list

Common Breadth Mistakes

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Sirf Nifty dekh kar market judge karna — Always check breadth — index can mislead
• Breadth ignore karna breakout pe — Breakout without breadth = low probability
• Single day breadth pe decision — Check 3-5 day trend, not just one day
• Mid/small cap breadth ignore — Nifty 50 breadth ≠ whole market breadth

FAQ

Q: Market breadth kahan se check karein free mein? A: NSE india website (daily summary), TRT charting terminal (community trends + gainers/losers), Moneycontrol (market breadth section).

Q: Kya breadth har din check karna chahiye? A: Haan, especially breakout/breakdown days pe. 30 seconds ka kaam hai — A/D ratio + new highs/lows check.

Q: Breadth thrust kitne din baad aata hai? A: No fixed pattern. Typically market bottom ya fresh rally start pe aata hai. 3-4 baar saal mein hota hai.
