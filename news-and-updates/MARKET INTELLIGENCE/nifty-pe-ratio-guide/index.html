Nifty PE Ratio Guide: Market Valuation Kaise Samjhe

Nifty PE ratio guide hindi — market expensive hai ya cheap, PE ratio se kaise pata lagayein. Historical PE bands aur valuation strategy.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 11, 2026 · Equities

PE Ratio Kya Hai?

PE ratio = Price to Earnings ratio. Ye batata hai ki market/company kitna "expensive" hai apne earnings ke comparison mein.

Nifty PE Formula: Nifty PE = Nifty 50 Total Market Cap / Nifty 50 Total Earnings

Simple words:
• PE 20 = investors ₹20 pay kar rahe hain har ₹1 earning ke liye
• Low PE = market "cheap" (undervalued)
• High PE = market "expensive" (overvalued)

Nifty PE Historical Bands

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• < 15 — Valuation: Very Cheap, Historical Frequency: Rare (crashes), Action: 🟢 Buy aggressively
• 15-20 — Valuation: Cheap, Historical Frequency: 20% of time, Action: 🟢 Buy/SIP continue
• 20-25 — Valuation: Fair Value, Historical Frequency: 40% of time, Action: 🟡 Normal SIP
• 25-30 — Valuation: Expensive, Historical Frequency: 25% of time, Action: 🟠 Reduce buying
• 30-35 — Valuation: Very Expensive, Historical Frequency: 10% of time, Action: 🔴 Book profits
• > 35 — Valuation: Bubble, Historical Frequency: 5% of time, Action: 🔴 Exit + cash

Current Nifty PE (Aug 2026): ~22-24 (Fair Value zone)

PE Ratio Strategy

When PE < 20 (Cheap Market):
• SIP amount badhao (2x normal)
• Lump sum invest karo (market cheap)
• Equity allocation badhao (80% equity)
• "Buy when there's blood in the streets"

When PE 20-25 (Fair Value):
• Normal SIP continue
• Equity allocation: 60-70%
• Rebalance quarterly
• "Steady as she goes"

When PE 25-30 (Expensive):
• SIP amount reduce (0.5x normal)
• Book partial profits on winners
• Equity allocation: 40-50% (shift to debt/gold)
• "Be cautious, not greedy"

When PE > 30 (Bubble):
• Stop new equity buying
• Book profits aggressively
• Move to cash/debt: 50-60%
• "Bubble pops, be ready"

PB Ratio aur Dividend Yield

PB (Price to Book) Ratio:
• Nifty PB < 3 = cheap
• Nifty PB > 5 = expensive
• Current: ~3.5-4 (fair to slightly expensive)

Dividend Yield:
• Nifty dividend yield > 2% = cheap market
• Nifty dividend yield < 1% = expensive market
• Current: ~1.2-1.5% (fair)

Combined Valuation Check:
• PE < 20 + PB < 3 + Yield > 2% = VERY CHEAP → Buy 🟢
• PE 20-25 + PB 3-4 + Yield 1-2% = FAIR → Normal 🟡
• PE > 30 + PB > 5 + Yield < 1% = EXPENSIVE → Cautious 🔴

Common PE Ratio Mistakes

Mistake 1: Single Day PE Pe Decision Lena:
• PE daily change hota hai (price + earnings both move)
• Monthly average PE dekho, not daily
• Trend matters more than point value

Mistake 2: PE Alone Deciding:
• PE + PB + Dividend Yield + Earnings Growth = full picture
• PE alone can mislead (earnings down = PE up even if price flat)

Mistake 3: "PE High = Bubble" Fixation:
• Growing economy mein PE naturally higher (earnings growing)
• India PE 22-25 is NORMAL for growth economy
• US PE 25-30 is normal (high growth tech companies)
• Compare with historical India PE, not US

Mistake 4: Market Timing Based on PE:
• PE > 30 pe exit → market can stay expensive for years
• PE < 15 pe entry → can go cheaper
• Use PE as allocation guide, not binary signal

Stock PE = individual company. Index PE = 50 companies ka average. Index PE zyada stable.
