SIRF 30 DIN: 19 SAAL KA POORA RETURN KUCH GINE-CHUNE SESSIONS SE AAYA

4,623 sessions mein se 30 nikaal do, aur ₹1 ka ₹5.29 nahi, ₹0.94 rah jata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Equities

Market timing ka sabse aam sapna ye hai: girawat se pehle nikal jao, phir wapas ghus jao. Humne apne database se naapa ki agar aap sirf kuch sabse acche din chook gaye to kya hota hai — September 2007 se July 2026, poore 4,623 trading sessions par.

PAANCH DIN CHOOKNE KI KEEMAT

Buy-and-hold ne ₹1 ko ₹5.29 banaya (+429%). Ab usmein se sabse acche din hataiye:
• Sabse acche 5 din chooke — ₹3.38 bacha. +429% girkar +238% ho gaya.
• Sabse acche 10 din chooke — ₹2.50. Aadha se zyada return sirf 10 sessions mein tha.
• Sabse acche 20 din chooke — ₹1.47. Yaani 19 saal ka kul return +47%.
• Sabse acche 30 din chooke — ₹0.94. Unnees saal market mein rehne ke baad aap ghaate mein hain.

30 din, 4,623 mein se — poore dataset ka 0.65%. Utne sessions chook jaana poore do dashak ke return ko zero se neeche le jata hai.

AUR YAHIN ASLI TWIST HAI: WO DIN KAHAN THE

Sabse acche 20 dinon mein se 13 akele 2008 ke hain aur 3 din 2020 ke. Yaani market ke sabse acche sessions wahin the jahan sabse buri girawat chal rahi thi.

Wo saare dono list ek doosre ke andar ghuse hue hain:
• Sabse acche 20 dinon mein se 15 din, sabse bure 20 dinon mein se kisi ek ke 30 din ke andar the.
• Sabse bada up-day: 18 May 2009, +17.74%.
• Uske aaspaas hi sabse bade down-days: 24 October 2008 (-12.20%) aur 21 January 2008 (-8.70%).
• 2020 ka crash bhi wahi shakal: 23 March 2020 -12.98%, aur do hafte baad 7 April 2020 +8.76%.
• Sabse bure 20 dinon mein se 11 din 2008 ke aur 5 din 2020 ke — bilkul wahi do saal jinme sabse acche din the.

ISKA MATLAB TIMING KE LIYE KYA HAI

Ek aur number: agar aap sabse bure 10 din bacha lete, to ₹1 ka ₹12.38 ban jata — buy-and-hold se dogune se bhi zyada. Ye sunne mein timing ke haq mein lagta hai.

Par dono numbers ek saath padhne padte hain. Bure din aur acche din ek hi hafton mein rehte hain. Jo system aapko 23 March 2020 se bachata, wo lagbhag zaroor 7 April 2020 ko bhi bahar rakhta — kyunki us waqt tak koi khabar badli nahi thi, sirf price badla tha.

Practically, panic mein nikalne ka matlab dono list se bahar hona hai: bure din bhi bache aur acche din bhi chook gaye. Aur chart batata hai ki us trade-off mein numbers kis taraf hain — 30 acche din chookna poore 19 saal ko mita deta hai.

EK IMANDAAR CAVEAT

Ye 'miss the best days' analysis ek known statistical trick bhi hai — ise ulta karke 'avoid the worst days' bhi utna hi dramatic dikhta hai, aur dono hi na to koi strategy hai na koi achievable outcome. Koi bhi na to top 30 din exactly chookta hai, na exactly bachata hai.

Iska sahi paath ek hi hai: is market ka return time ke saath barabar nahi phaila hua. Wo gucchon mein aata hai, aur wo guchhe sabse zyada ghabrahat wale mahinon mein padte hain. Ye timing ke khilaaf argument nahi hai — ye sirf ye batata hai ki timing galat hone par kitna mehnga padta hai.
