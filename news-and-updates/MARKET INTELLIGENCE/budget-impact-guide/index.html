Budget Impact on Stock Market — Hindi Guide

Union Budget ka stock market pe kya impact hota hai, budget day trading strategy — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 02, 2026 · Global Macro

Union Budget ka stock market pe kya impact hota hai, budget day trading strategy — complete Hindi guide.

Union Budget Kya Hai?

Union Budget Indian government ka annual financial plan hai — revenue (tax) aur expenditure (spending) ka plan. Ye February 1 ko present hota hai.

Why it matters for markets:
• Tax rates change → Corporate profits affected
• Government spending → Infrastructure, defense, etc.
• Fiscal deficit → Government borrowing
• Policy announcements → Sector-specific impact

Budget Day Market Behavior

Before Budget (1-2 weeks):
• VIX badhta hai — Uncertainty high
• Options expensive — High premium
• Market range-bound — Wait and watch
• Sector rotation — Speculation on winners/losers

Budget Day (February 1):
• Extreme volatility — 500-1000 point Nifty swings
• Gap up/down — Based on announcements
• Sector rotation — Winners surge, losers crash
• Volume spike — Very high trading activity

After Budget (1 week):
• Market digests — Impact analysis
• Winners continue — Strong sectors hold gains
• Losers recover or fall further — Depends on severity
• Volatility normalizes — VIX falls

Budget Impact by Sector

Typically Positive Sectors:
• Infrastructure — Capex increase, new projects
• Defense — Higher defense allocation
• Rural/Agri — Farm subsidies, MSP increase
• Banking — PSU bank recapitalization
• Real Estate — Housing incentives, tax benefits

Typically Negative Sectors:
• FMCG — Excise duty increase
• Tobacco — Cigarette tax increase
• Auto — Vehicle tax changes
• Metals — Import duty changes
• Oil & Gas — Fuel tax changes

Budget Day Trading Strategy

Strategy 1: Wait and Watch:
• Before budget: No fresh positions
• During budget: Observe first 30 minutes
• After budget: Trade in direction of trend

Strategy 2: Straddle/Strangle (Options):
• Before budget: Buy ATM straddle (big move expected)
• After budget: Profit from volatility
• Risk: High IV means expensive premium

Strategy 3: Sector Rotation:
• Identify winners during budget speech
• Buy winners immediately
• Short losers if confident

Strategy 4: Defensive Positioning:
• Before budget: Reduce equity exposure
• Cash/defensive stocks pe shift karo
• After budget: Re-enter based on clarity

Key Budget Announcements to Watch

Ye aath baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Income tax slabs — Consumer spending
• Corporate tax rate — Company profits
• Capital gains tax — Investment returns
• Infrastructure allocation — Infra stocks
• Defense allocation — Defense stocks
• Agriculture support — Agri/rural stocks
• Fiscal deficit target — Government borrowing
• Disinvestment plan — PSU stocks
