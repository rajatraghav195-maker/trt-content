Gold Investment India — Complete Hindi Guide

Gold mein kaise invest karein India mein, SGB, Gold ETF, Gold MF — complete Hindi guide.

Gold Mein Kyun Invest Karein?

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Inflation hedge — Paisa kam hota hai, gold badhta hai
• Safe haven — Market crash mein gold badhta hai
• Diversification — Portfolio mein gold rakhna zaroori hai
• Cultural value — India mein gold ka emotional value hai

Gold Investment Options

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Physical Gold — Returns: 8-10%, Risk: Storage risk, Liquidity: High, Tax: LTCG 20%
• SGB — Returns: Gold + 2.5%, Risk: Very Low, Liquidity: Low (8yr), Tax: Tax-free at maturity
• Gold ETF — Returns: 8-10%, Risk: Low, Liquidity: High, Tax: LTCG 20%
• Gold MF (FoF) — Returns: 8-10%, Risk: Low, Liquidity: High, Tax: LTCG 20%
• Digital Gold — Returns: 8-10%, Risk: Platform risk, Liquidity: High, Tax: LTCG 20%

Best Option: Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — Gold price + 2.5% interest
• Risk — Zero (govt backed)
• Tax — Tax-free at maturity (8 years)
• Storage — No storage needed
• Making charges — Zero

SGB sabse best hai — gold returns + interest + tax benefit.

Gold Allocation Rule

Portfolio ka 10-20% gold mein hona chahiye.

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• ₹5 lakh — Gold Allocation: ₹50,000-1,00,000, Best Option: Gold ETF
• ₹10 lakh — Gold Allocation: ₹1-2 lakh, Best Option: SGB
• ₹25 lakh — Gold Allocation: ₹2.5-5 lakh, Best Option: SGB + Gold ETF
• ₹50 lakh+ — Gold Allocation: ₹5-10 lakh, Best Option: SGB (primary)

Gold vs Equity (Historical)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 10 years — Gold Returns: 8-10%, Nifty 50 Returns: 12-14%
• 20 years — Gold Returns: 9-11%, Nifty 50 Returns: 12-14%
• 2008 crash — Gold Returns: +25%, Nifty 50 Returns: -50%
• 2020 crash — Gold Returns: +25%, Nifty 50 Returns: -30%

Gold zig-zags when equity zigs. Portfolio mein dono chahiye.
