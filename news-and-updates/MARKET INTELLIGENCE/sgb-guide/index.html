Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) — Complete Hindi Guide

SGB kya hai, kaise kharidein, benefits, returns — complete Hindi guide Indian investors ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 16, 2026 · Commodities

SGB kya hai, kaise kharidein, benefits, returns — complete Hindi guide Indian investors ke liye.

SGB Kya Hai?

Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Indian government ka gold investment scheme hai. Physical gold ki jagah government bond kharidte ho jo gold price se linked hota hai.

Simple definition: > SGB = Gold ki price pe invest karo, but physical gold mat rakho. Government bond hai, safe hai.

SGB vs Physical Gold vs Gold ETF

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — SGB: Gold price + 2.5% interest, Physical Gold: Gold price only, Gold ETF: Gold price only
• Storage — SGB: No storage, Physical Gold: Locker chahiye, Gold ETF: Demat account
• Purity — SGB: 999 (guaranteed), Physical Gold: Variable, Gold ETF: 999
• Liquidity — SGB: Low (5yr lock-in), Physical Gold: High, Gold ETF: High
• Tax — SGB: Tax-free if held till maturity, Physical Gold: LTCG applicable, Gold ETF: LTCG applicable
• Making charges — SGB: None, Physical Gold: 5-20%, Gold ETF: None
• Risk — SGB: Very low (govt backed), Physical Gold: Theft risk, Gold ETF: Market risk

SGB Benefits

1. 2.5% Annual Interest

Gold price ke upar 2.5% interest milta hai. Physical gold mein koi interest nahi.

2. Tax-Free at Maturity

8 year maturity tak hold karo → Capital gains tax ZERO.

3. No Storage Hassle

Physical gold ki tarah locker ki zaroorat nahi. Demat mein store hota hai.

4. Government Backed

Sovereign guarantee hai — default risk ZERO.

5. No Making Charges

Physical gold mein 5-20% making charge lagta hai. SGB mein nahi.

SGB Details

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Tenure — 8 years (exit option after 5 years)
• Interest — 2.5% per annum (semi-annual)
• Minimum — 1 gram
• Maximum — 4 kg (individuals)
• Price — RBI announces before each tranche
• Where to buy — Banks, Stock exchanges, Post offices

SGB Returns Example

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Investment: 100 grams gold @ ₹6,000/gram = ₹6,00,000
• After 8 years (assuming gold @ ₹9,000/gram):
• Capital gain: ₹3,00,000 (tax-free!)
• Interest: ₹1,20,000 (2.5% × 8 years)
• Total return: ₹4,20,000
• vs Physical Gold:
• Capital gain: ₹3,00,000 (taxable @ 20%)
• No interest
• Making charges lost: ₹30,000-1,20,000

How to Buy SGB

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• RBI announces new tranche (usually every 2-3 months)
• Banks/Stock exchanges pe apply karo
• Demat account mein credit hota hai
• Secondary market pe bhi kharid sakte ho (NSE/BSE)

SGB Disadvantages

Low Liquidity

5 year lock-in. Secondary market pe sell karna padega (discount pe mil sakta hai).

Price Discovery

RBI price market price se alag ho sakta hai.

Interest Taxable

2.5% interest taxable hai (income tax slab ke according).
