Lessons from 30 Years

Lesson 1: Time in Market > Timing the Market:
• 30 years CAGR = 11%
• Agar aap 30 years mein invested rehte = 24x wealth
• Agar aap sirf best 10 years mein invested = similar returns
• Agar aap sirf worst 5 years mein absent = marginal improvement
• Conclusion: Stay invested. Don't try to time.

Lesson 2: Crashes Are Temporary, Markets Are Permanent:
• 2008: -52% → Full recovery in 2 years
• 2011: -25% → Recovery in 1 year
• 2020: -38% crash → Recovery in 6 months
• 2022: -11% → Recovery in 6 months
• Every single crash was followed by new highs

Lesson 3: Negative Years Are Normal:
• 7 out of 30 years were negative (23%)
• Expect 1 negative year every 4-5 years
• Don't panic when it comes — it's normal
• Prepare mentally and financially (cash reserve)

Lesson 4: Best Years Follow Worst Years:
• 2008: -52% → 2009: +76% (best year in history)
• 2001: -12% → 2003: +74% (after consolidation)
• 2020: crash → 2021: +33% (recovery bull run)
• Don't exit after a bad year — the bounce is coming

Lesson 5: 11% CAGR = Doubling Every 7 Years:
• Rule of 72: 72/11 = 6.5 years to double
• ₹10 lakh invested today = ₹20 lakh in 7 years = ₹40 lakh in 14 years = ₹80 lakh in 21 years
• Patience + compounding = wealth

FAQ

Q1: Nifty ka average return kitna hai?

Historically ~11% CAGR over 30 years. But not linear — some years +50%, some -50%.

Q2: Kya Nifty hamesha upar jaayega?

Long-term mein yes (30-year trend is up). Short-term mein crashes aate hain. 77% years positive, 23% negative.

Q3: Worst year mein kya karein?

Don't panic sell. Continue SIP. Stagger buying. Best years follow worst years.

Q4: 2026 mein Nifty kya return degi?

Nobody knows. Historically, after 2 slow years (2025-26 at +8% and +2%), recovery years tend to be strong. But past performance ≠ future results.
