BANK NIFTY: BAZAAR KA AMPLIFIER, DONO TARAF

Beta 1.18, correlation 0.89 — aur Nifty ke sabse bure aur sabse acche 50 dinon ka record

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Global Macro

Bank Nifty India ka sabse zyada trade hone wala index hai, aur uske baare mein sabse aam baat ye kahi jaati hai ki 'wo Nifty se tez chalta hai'. Ye kitna tez, aur kis taraf — humne 4,606 common sessions par naapa, September 2007 se July 2026 tak.

DO NUMBER JO SAB KEH DETE HAIN

Poore 4,606 sessions par:
• Beta 1.18 — Nifty ke har 1% move par Bank Nifty average 1.18% hilta hai.
• Correlation 0.89 — dono lagbhag ek saath chalte hain. Ye diversification nahi hai; ye wahi bet hai, thoda tez.

0.89 ka correlation matlab Bank Nifty ki chaal ka bada hissa wahi hai jo Nifty ki chaal hai. Jo log Nifty position ke saath Bank Nifty position lete hain, wo aksar sochte hain ki do alag trades hain — numbers ke hisaab se wo lagbhag ek hi trade hai, badi size mein.

AMPLIFICATION DONO TARAF BARABAR HAI

Nifty ke sabse bure 50 din aur sabse acche 50 din alag karke dekha:
• Sabse bure 50 din — Nifty average -5.21%, Bank Nifty -5.87%. Bank lagbhag 0.66 percentage point zyada gira.
• Sabse acche 50 din — Nifty average +5.05%, Bank Nifty +5.79%. Bank lagbhag 0.74 point zyada chadha.

Ye symmetry hi asli baat hai. Bank Nifty koi 'behtar' index nahi hai — wo wahi index hai jiska volume knob thoda upar ghuma diya gaya ho. Girte bazaar mein wo zyada girta hai, badhte mein zyada badhta hai, aur dono taraf lagbhag utna hi zyada.

ISKA PRACTICAL MATLAB

Teen cheezein iske seedhe natije hain:


• Position size — Nifty ke muqable Bank Nifty mein wahi rupee-risk lene ke liye size lagbhag 15-20% kam rakhni padegi, warna aapne bina jaane exposure badha liya.
• Stop loss ki doori — 1.18 beta ka matlab hai ki Nifty par jo stop 'normal' hai, Bank Nifty par wahi stop lagbhag 18% zyada baar hit hoga. Stop asset ki apni chaal se banta hai, aadat se nahi.
• Hedging — Nifty long ke against Bank Nifty short ek hedge nahi hai. 0.89 correlation par wo do overlapping positions hain jo ek doosre ko sirf aanshik roop se kaat rahi hain, aur beech ka farq apne aap mein ek alag bet hai.

AUR EK CAVEAT

Beta aur correlation sthir nahi rehte. Ye 4,606 sessions ka poore period ka average hai; kisi ek saal mein banking sector apni alag kahani chala sakta hai — jaise 2020 mein, jab Nifty +14.9% par band hua aur Bank Nifty -2.8% par. Us saal amplifier waala rishta bilkul ulta chala.

Isliye in numbers ko background samajho, live rule nahi. Jo cheez lambe samay mein sthir rahi hai wo ye hai: Bank Nifty Nifty se alag disha mein shayad hi jata ho, aur jab jata hai to wo apne aap mein ek khabar hoti hai.
