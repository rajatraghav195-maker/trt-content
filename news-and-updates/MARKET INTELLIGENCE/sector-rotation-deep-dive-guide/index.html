Sector Rotation Strategy: Kaunsa Sector Kab Chalta Hai — Hinglish Deep Dive

Sector Rotation Strategy: Kaunsa Sector Kab Chalta Hai — Hinglish Deep Dive

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 15, 2026 · Sector Analysis

Sector rotation ek market phenomenon hai jahan paise ek sector se doosre sector mein shift hote hain, depending on economic cycle ka phase. Matlab: jab economy grow karti hai toh kuch sectors outperform karte hain, jab economy slow hoti hai toh doosre sectors lead karte hain.

Sector Rotation Kya Hai?

Ye samajhna important hai kyunki:
• Saare stocks ek saath upar nahi jaate
• Index upar ho sakta hai lekin aapka stock down ho sakta hai (wrong sector mein ho toh)
• Right sector mein hone se returns 2-3x ho sakte hain
• Wrong sector mein hone se index upar ho toh bhi aap loss mein ho sakte ho

Economic Cycle Ke 4 Phases Aur Sector Performance

Phase 1: Expansion (Economy Grow Kar Raha Hai)

Characteristics: GDP growth high, inflation low, interest rates stable/low, corporate earnings strong

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Technology — High growth, capex spending
• Consumer Discretionary — Spending power high
• Industrials — Manufacturing and infra activity
• Financials (Banks) — Credit growth, lending strong
• Real Estate — Demand high, rates low

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• FMCG (Defensive) — Boring in bull market
• Pharma (Defensive) — Less interest in safe havens
• Utilities — No excitement

Phase 2: Peak (Economy Top Pe)

Characteristics: GDP growth slowing, inflation rising, interest rates rising, earnings peaking

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Energy (Oil & Gas) — Crude prices high
• Metals & Mining — Commodity prices elevated
• Materials — Inflation benefits commodity producers
• FMCG — People still spending, stable demand

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Technology — Valuations stretched, rate hikes hurt
• Real Estate — High rates = lower demand
• Auto — Expensive loans = lower sales

Phase 3: Contraction (Economy Gir Raha Hai)

Characteristics: GDP growth negative/low, inflation falling, rates may be cut, earnings declining

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• FMCG (Defensive) — People still buy soap, food
• Pharma (Defensive) — Healthcare doesn't stop
• Utilities — Essential services
• IT Services (Export) — Weak rupee benefits exporters

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Financials — NPAs rise, credit growth slows
• Real Estate — Demand collapses
• Consumer Discretionary — Spending cuts
• Industrials — Activity slows

Phase 4: Recovery (Economy Wapas Uth Raha Hai)

Characteristics: GDP growth accelerating, inflation low, rates being cut, earnings recovering

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Financials (Banks) — Rate cuts = credit growth resumes
• Auto — Lower EMIs = higher vehicle sales
• Real Estate — Cheap loans = property demand
• Consumer Discretionary — Spending returns
• Small/Mid Caps — High beta, recovery plays

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• FMCG — Defensive money rotates out
• Pharma — Safe haven premium fades
• Utilities — Growth sectors more attractive

India-Specific Sector Rotation (2024-2026)

What Actually Happened

• FMCG stable but boring

2025 (Expansion Phase):

How To Identify Sector Rotation Early

1. Relative Strength (RS) Analysis:
• Compare sector index vs Nifty 50
• Sector outperforming Nifty for 2+ weeks = rotation likely
• Sector underperforming for 2+ weeks = money leaving

2. Volume Analysis:
• Sector stocks mein volume badh raha hai = interest coming in
• Sector stocks mein volume kam ho raha hai = interest fading

3. FII/DII Sector Flows:
• FII buying financials → bank stocks likely to rally
• FII selling IT → IT stocks likely to fall
• Track: NSE FII/DII sector-wise data (daily)

4. Earnings Momentum:
• Sector mein earnings beats zyada = rotation towards that sector
• Sector mein earnings misses = rotation away

5. News Flow:
• Government policy changes (PLI schemes, import duties)
• Global commodity moves (crude, metals)
• Regulatory changes (RBI, SEBI)

TRT Screener Settings For Sector Rotation

Setup 1: Finding Hot Sectors:
• Filter: Nifty Sector Indices
• Sort: 1-month return (descending)
• Check: 3-month return (confirm trend)
• Volume: Above 20-day average

Setup 2: Finding Stocks in Hot Sectors:
• Filter 1: Sector = [sector that's outperforming]
• Filter 2: RSI > 50 (momentum positive)
• Filter 3: Price > 50 DMA (uptrend)
• Filter 4: Volume > 20-day average (interest)
• Sort: 1-month return (descending)

Setup 3: Finding Contrarian Plays:
• Filter 1: Sector = [sector that's underperforming for 3+ months]
• Filter 2: RSI < 40 (oversold)
• Filter 3: Price near 200 DMA (long-term support)
• Filter 4: Fundamentals strong (P/E < industry average, ROE > 15%)

Sector Rotation Trading Strategy

Strategy: "Follow the Money":
• Monday morning: Check sector performance last week
• Identify: Top 2 outperforming sectors
• Select: 2-3 stocks from each hot sector (use screener)
• Entry: Pullback to support or breakout from consolidation
• Stop-loss: Below support or 3% below entry
• Exit: When sector relative strength weakens, or target hit
• Re-evaluate weekly: Is sector still hot? If not, rotate

Strategy: "Contrarian Sector Bet":
• Identify sector that's been underperforming for 3+ months
• Check fundamentals — is sector fundamentally broken or just out of favor?
• If fundamentals okay but sentiment negative = contrarian opportunity
• Wait for first sign of rotation (volume pickup, RS improvement)
• Enter on confirmation — don't catch falling knife
• Patience: contrarian plays take 2-3 months to play out

Common Sector Rotation Mistakes

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Last year ke top sector mein buy karna — Consequence: Rotation already happened, you're late, Fix: Current relative strength check karo
• Sirf news pe sector pick karna — Consequence: News late aata hai, market pehle move karta hai, Fix: Price + volume pe depend karo
• Ek sector mein over-concentrate — Consequence: Sector reversal = big loss, Fix: Max 40% in one sector
• Sector rotation ignore karna — Consequence: Index upar, aapka stock neeche, Fix: Sector check before stock selection
• Defensive sector mein bull market mein stay — Consequence: Opportunity cost, underperformance, Fix: Cycle phase identify karo

FAQ

Q: Sector rotation kitne din mein hota hai? A: Short-term rotation 2-4 weeks mein, medium-term 2-3 months mein, long-term 6-12 months mein. Day trading ke liye sector rotation irrelevant hai — ye swing/position traders ke liye hai.

Q: Kaunse sectors abhi (Aug 2026) strong hain? A: Current environment mein: Financials (strong Q1 results), Energy (crude high), FMCG (defensive money coming). IT mixed, Auto mixed, Pharma selective.

Q: Kya sector rotation Indian market mein global market se different hai? A: Haan, India ke sector dynamics alag hain. India mein FMCG aur Financials ka weight zyada hai. Global commodity moves directly Energy aur Metals pe impact karte hain. IT exports US economy pe depend karta hai.

Q: Small investor ke liye sector rotation practical hai? A: Haan, agar aap sectoral ETF ya index fund use karte ho. Individual stocks mein sector rotation track karna effort lagta hai. Nifty Bees + Bank Bees + IT Bees = simple sector rotation.
