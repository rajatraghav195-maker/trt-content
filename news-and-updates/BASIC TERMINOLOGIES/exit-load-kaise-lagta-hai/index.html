EXIT LOAD: JALDI NIKALNE KA JURMANA

Load period ka hisaab, aur wo NAV ke upar kaise lagta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 28, 2026 · Mutual Funds & AMC

Exit load ek charge hai jo fund tab lagata hai jab aap ek tay samay se pehle paisa nikaal lete ho. Uska maqsad fund ko baar baar aane jaane wale paise se bachana hai.

HISAAB KAISE HOTA HAI

Load redemption ki value par ek percentage ke roop mein lagta hai aur aapko milne wali rakam se kat jaata hai. Wo NAV mein nahi dikhta, wo alag se katta hai.

Har unit ka apna holding period hota hai. SIP mein har installment alag purchase hai, isliye purani installments load free ho sakti hain aur nayi nahi.

DHYAAN DENE WALI BAATEIN

Ye baatein aksar surprise banti hain:
• Zyadatar equity funds mein load ek saal se kam ke liye hota hai, par ye scheme par depend karta hai.
• Liquid aur overnight funds mein graded load bahut chhote period ka hota hai.
• Switch bhi redemption maana jaata hai, isliye us par bhi load lag sakta hai.
• Load aur short term capital gains tax do alag cheezein hain — dono ek saath lag sakte hain.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Redemption se pehle scheme information document mein load structure dekh lena do minute ka kaam hai. Kai baar sirf kuch din ruk jaane se poora load bach jaata hai — aur wo bachat kisi bhi timing ke anumaan se zyada pakki hai.
