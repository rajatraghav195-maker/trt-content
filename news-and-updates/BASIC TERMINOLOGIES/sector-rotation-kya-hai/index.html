SECTOR ROTATION: PAISA EK JAGAH SE DOOSRI JAGAH JAATA HAI

Rotation ka mechanism, aur usse kya samajh aata hai aur kya nahi

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 03, 2026 · Market Structure

Market mein sab kuch ek saath nahi chalta. Ek daur mein ek sector aage hota hai aur agle mein doosra. Is aane jaane ko sector rotation kehte hain.

YE HOTA KYUN HAI

Alag sectors alag cheezon par depend karte hain — interest rates, commodity prices, government spending, global demand. Jaise ye factors badalte hain, paisa un sectors mein jaata hai jahan uska tark banta hai.

Isliye rotation ek symptom hai, ek strategy nahi — wo bata rahi hai ki underlying conditions badal rahi hain.

ISE KAISE DEKHEIN

Rotation dekhne ke aam tarike:
• Sector indices ka ek hi window mein relative performance compare karna.
• Nifty ke against har sector ka ratio dekhna, absolute price nahi.
• Leadership badalne par dhyaan dena — kaun naya aage aa raha hai.
• Ye analysis hamesha ek window ke liye hota hai, permanent nahi.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Jo sector pichle saal sabse aage tha wo agle saal aksar nahi hota — aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan retail sabse zyada der se ghusta hai. Rotation ka faayda tabhi hai jab wo allocation ka framework bane, ek recent winner ki list na bane.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
