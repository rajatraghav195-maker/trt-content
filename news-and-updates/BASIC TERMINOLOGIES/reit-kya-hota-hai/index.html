REIT: PROPERTY MEIN NIVESH BINA PROPERTY KHAREEDE

Rental income ka structure, distribution, aur uske risk

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 26, 2026 · AIF, PE & Alternates

REIT ek listed vehicle hai jo commercial properties rakhta hai aur unka rent unit holders mein baant deta hai. Ye property exposure ka ek liquid roop hai, par property jaisa nahi hai.

STRUCTURE KYA HAI

REIT ke paas income dene wali properties hoti hain — aksar office parks aur malls. Unka rent aata hai, kharche nikalte hain, aur bacha hua bada hissa distribute karna hota hai.

Uski units exchange par trade hoti hain, isliye entry aur exit shares jaisi hi hoti hai — koi registry, koi stamp duty, koi buyer dhoondhna nahi.

RISK KAHAN HAI

Ye baatein REIT ke saath judi hain:
• Occupancy girne par distribution gir sakta hai — wo fixed income nahi hai.
• Interest rate badhne par listed price aksar dabaav mein aata hai.
• Portfolio kuch hi cities aur kuch hi tenants par concentrated ho sakta hai.
• Distribution ke alag hisson par tax treatment alag hota hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

REIT ko fixed deposit ka substitute samajhna sabse aam galti hai. Uska yield attractive dikhta hai, par uska price roz badalta hai aur uska payout guarantee nahi hai — wo equity jaisa behave karta hai, deposit jaisa nahi.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
