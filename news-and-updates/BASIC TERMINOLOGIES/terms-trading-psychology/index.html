TRADING PSYCHOLOGY: DIMAAG KE SHABD

FOMO, revenge trading, bias, tilt, discipline — wo galtiyan jinke naam hote hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 06, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Zyadatar traders analysis ki wajah se nahi harte — wo apne hi reaction ki wajah se harte hain. Aur mazedaar baat ye hai ki ye galtiyan itni common hain ki inke proper naam pad chuke hain. Naam pata hone ka faayda ye hai ki jab galti ho rahi ho, aap use beech mein pehchan sakte ho.

EMOTION KE NAAM

Screen ke saamne baithne par ye chaar-paanch cheezein bar-bar aati hain:
• Fear — Loss ka dar, jiski wajah se accha setup bhi chhoot jata hai ya trade jaldi band ho jati hai.
• Greed — Profit ko aur khinchne ki koshish, jab tak wo profit wapas na chala jaye.
• FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) — Move nikal chuka hai, phir bhi peechhe bhaag kar entry lena. Aksar exact top ya bottom par hoti hai ye entry.
• Hope Trading — Stop loss cross ho gaya, par ummeed par position rakhe rehna. Yahin chhota loss bada banta hai.
• Euphoria — Lagataar jeet ke baad ka overconfident phase, jahan size achanak double ho jata hai.
• Panic Selling — Girawat mein bina soche sab bech dena, aksar exact bottom ke aaspaas.
• Analysis Paralysis — Itna analysis ki trade li hi na jaye.
• Tilt — Ek bure trade ke baad ka emotional state, jahan agla har decision aur bura hota jata hai.

BEHAVIOUR KI GALTIYAN

Ye actions hain — inhe pakadna aasan hai kyunki ye journal mein saaf dikhte hain:
• Revenge Trading — Loss ke turant baad usi ko wapas kamane ke liye bada trade lena.
• Overtrading — Zaroorat se zyada trades, aksar boredom ya nuksan ki bharpai ke liye.
• Averaging Down — Losing position mein aur quantity daalna, is ummeed mein ki average sudhar jayega.
• Cutting Winners, Holding Losers — Profit jaldi book karna aur loss lambe samay tak rakhna. Ye bilkul ulta hai us cheez ke jo kaam karti hai.
• Moving the Stop Loss — Stop ko peechhe khiskana taaki trade galat sabit na ho. Ek hi galti se ek din ka biggest loss aksar yahin banta hai.
• Chasing — Har tez chalte stock ke peechhe bhaagna, bina level ya plan ke.
• Tip Trading / Herd Mentality — Kisi aur ke call par trade lena, bina ye jaane ki uska stop kahan hai.
• Position Size Creep — Dheere-dheere size badhate jana jab tak ek normal loss bhi bada na lagne lage.
• System Hopping — Har do hafte mein nayi strategy badalna, jisse kisi bhi system ko chalne ka mauka hi nahi milta.

DIMAAG KE BIASES

Ye wo mental shortcuts hain jo normal life mein kaam ke hain, par market mein mehnge padte hain:
• Confirmation Bias — Sirf wahi news aur chart dhoondhna jo aapki existing position ko sahi thehraye.
• Recency Bias — Pichhle do-teen trades ko zaroorat se zyada weight dena.
• Anchoring — Kisi ek purane price se chipke rehna, jaise wahi sahi value ho.
• Loss Aversion — Loss ka dard profit ki khushi se zyada mehsoos hona, jiski wajah se loss book karna mushkil ho jata hai.
• Overconfidence — Kuch jeet ke baad khud ko market se bada samajh lena.
• Hindsight Bias — Chart ke left side ko dekhkar lagna ki 'ye to obvious tha'. Live mein wo obvious nahi tha.
• Survivorship Bias — Sirf jeetne walon ki kahaniyan dikhna, kyunki haarne wale post nahi karte.
• Gambler's Fallacy — Ye maan lena ki chaar loss ke baad ab jeet 'due' hai. Market ko aapka record yaad nahi hota.
• Sunk Cost Fallacy — Sirf isliye position rakhe rehna kyunki usmein pehle hi bahut paisa aur time laga chuke ho.
• Lottery Bias — Sasta OTM option isliye lena kyunki uska payoff bada dikhta hai, chahe probability kitni bhi kam ho.

DISCIPLINE KE TOOLS

Emotion ko dabaya nahi jata, uske around system banaya jata hai:
• Trading Plan — Likha hua rule set: kya trade karoge, kis size mein, kahan stop, kahan exit.
• Checklist — Entry se pehle poochhe jane wale fix sawaal, taaki mood decision na le.
• Trading Journal — Har trade ka record wajah aur emotion ke saath. Yahi sabse honest feedback deta hai.
• Rules-Based System — Discretion kam, condition zyada — taaki har din ek jaisa process chale.
• Pre-Market Routine — Market khulne se pehle levels aur plan taiyar karna, khulne ke baad nahi.
• Cooling-Off Period — Do lagataar loss ke baad thoda break, taaki tilt aage na badhe.
• Screen Time Limit — Kam trade karne ka sabse aasan tareeka: kam der screen dekhna.
• Process vs Outcome — Accha process bhi kabhi loss deta hai aur bura process kabhi profit. Judge hamesha process ko karo.
• Review Routine — Hafte ke end mein saare trades dekh kar pattern nikalna.

LAMBI RACE KE SHABD

Aur akhir mein wo shabd jo career banate hain, ek trade nahi:
• Consistency — Har din ek hi process, chahe result kuch bhi ho.
• Patience — Setup ka intezaar karna, market ko force na karna.
• Detachment — Position se emotional attachment na rakhna; ye sirf ek idea hai jo sahi ya galat ho sakta hai.
• Compounding — Chhote consistent returns ka time ke saath badhna. Iske liye survival zaroori hai, sabse zaroori.
• Drawdown Recovery — Bure phase se nikalne ka plan: size kam, rules tight, confidence dheere wapas.
• Screen Experience — Wo seekh jo sirf market ke saamne baith kar aati hai, kisi course se nahi.
• Edge Trust — Apne system par bharosa, jo tabhi aata hai jab uska data aapke apne journal mein ho.
• Boredom Tolerance — Kuch na karna bhi ek position hai. Zyadatar din yahi sabse acchi position hoti hai.

PAISE KA RISHTA

Sabse zyada psychology aapke paise ki condition se banti hai, aapke chart se nahi:
• Risk Capital — Wo paisa jise poora gawa kar bhi aapki zindagi normal chalti rahe. Trading isi se honi chahiye.
• Scared Money — Wo capital jiski aapko zaroorat hai (fees, EMI, rent). Isse liya gaya har trade jaldi band ho jata hai, kyunki har red number darata hai.
• Borrowed Capital — Udhaar ya loan ka paisa. Iske saath time pressure aata hai, aur time pressure har rule tod deta hai.
• Emergency Fund — Alag rakha hua kharcha-fund, jiski wajah se aapko kabhi galat waqt par position band nahi karni padti.
• Position Size vs Neend — Practical test: agar position ki wajah se raat ko neend nahi aa rahi, to size aapke liye badi hai — chahe spreadsheet kuch bhi kahe.
• Income Expectation — Trading se har mahine fix income ki ummeed. Market monthly salary nahi deta, aur ye expectation hi overtrading ki sabse aam wajah hai.
• Opportunity Cost — Ek trade mein phase rehne ki wajah se doosri, behtar trade chhoot jana.
• Comparison Trap — Doosron ke screenshots se apne result ko naapna, bina unka risk, size ya loss dekhe.
• Lifestyle Creep — Ek acche mahine ke baad kharche badha lena, jisse agle bure mahine ka pressure double ho jata hai.

SEEKHNE KA SAFAR

Aur akhir mein wo shabd jo har trader ke seekhne wale phase describe karte hain:
• Beginner's Luck — Shuruaat mein bina system ke mila profit, jo confidence to deta hai par sabak nahi.
• Learning Curve — Wo lamba phase jahan knowledge badh rahi hai par result abhi nahi dikh raha.
• Demo to Live Gap — Paper trading mein aasan lagne wala system live paise ke saath mushkil ho jata hai, kyunki emotion ab shamil hai.
• Information Overload — Ek saath itne sources aur strategies ki koi ek bhi theek se follow na ho paye.
• Mentor vs Guru — Mentor process sikhata hai aur galtiyan dikhata hai; guru sirf calls deta hai. Doosre par depend karne se skill kabhi nahi banti.
• Paper Confidence vs Live Confidence — Backtest par bharosa aur apne live journal par bharosa do alag cheezein hain. Sirf doosri kaam aati hai.
• Plateau — Wo phase jahan improvement ruk jata hai. Yahan aksar problem knowledge ki nahi, execution ki hoti hai.
• Deliberate Practice — Har hafte ek hi cheez par kaam karna, jaise sirf entry timing ya sirf SL discipline.
• Unconscious Competence — Wo stage jahan sahi decision bina soche ho jata hai, kyunki process aadat ban chuki hai.
• Survivor's Routine — Lambe samay tak tikne walon ki common aadat: chhota size, likha hua plan, aur regular review — na ki koi secret indicator.

Analysis seekhne mein mahine lagte hain, aur khud ko handle karna seekhne mein saal. Isiliye ye journal terminologies ki series ka aakhri hissa hai — kyunki baaki sab shabd tabhi kaam karte hain jab inhe follow karne wala insaan sthir ho.

Ye journal sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi.
