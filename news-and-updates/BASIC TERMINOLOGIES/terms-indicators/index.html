INDICATORS: FORMULA WALE TOOLS

Moving average, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, VWAP, Fibonacci — kaun kya batata hai aur kab jhooth bolta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 04, 2026 · Indicators

Indicator koi jaadu nahi hai — wo sirf price aur volume ka ek formula hai jo chart ko doosre angle se dikhata hai. Iska sabse bada faayda ye hai ki wo ek number de deta hai; sabse bada khatra bhi yahi hai, kyunki number confident lagta hai chahe wo purana data hi kyun na dikha raha ho.

INDICATOR KI GRAMMAR

Pehle wo shabd jo har indicator par lagoo hote hain:
• Indicator — Price, volume ya time par lagaya gaya calculation, jo ek line ya bar ki shakal mein dikhta hai.
• Overlay — Wo indicator jo price chart ke upar hi banta hai, jaise moving average ya Bollinger Bands.
• Oscillator — Wo indicator jo neeche alag panel mein ek range ke andar ghoomta hai, jaise RSI ya stochastic.
• Leading vs Lagging — Leading move se pehle hint dene ki koshish karta hai (aur zyada galat hota hai); lagging move ke baad confirm karta hai (aur late hota hai).
• Period / Length — Kitni candles ka data use hoga. RSI 14 matlab pichhli 14 candles.
• Smoothing — Data ka average lekar shor kam karna; isi se lag paida hota hai.
• Signal — Indicator ki wo condition jise aap action ka trigger maante ho, jaise crossover ya level cross.
• Divergence — Price kuch aur kar raha hai aur indicator kuch aur. Ye momentum kamzor hone ka classic hint hai.
• Confluence — Do-teen alag cheezein ek hi jagah ek hi baat keh rahi hon. Yahi indicators ka sahi use hai.
• Repainting — Kuch indicators candle band hone ke baad apna signal badal dete hain. Backtest mein ye jhoothi performance dikhata hai.
• Whipsaw Signals — Sideways market mein indicator ka baar-baar ulta signal dena.

TREND WALE INDICATORS

Ye batate hain direction kya hai, timing nahi:
• SMA (Simple Moving Average) — Pichhli N candles ke close ka simple average. 50 SMA aur 200 SMA sabse common hain.
• EMA (Exponential Moving Average) — Wahi average, par naye data ko zyada weight. Isliye ye SMA se tez react karta hai.
• Crossover — Do moving averages ka ek doosre ko cross karna, jise trend change ka signal maana jata hai.
• Golden Cross — Chhota MA (jaise 50) bade MA (jaise 200) ke upar aa jaye — long-term bullish ka popular signal.
• Death Cross — Uska ulta: 50 ka 200 ke neeche jaana.
• Dynamic Support / Resistance — Uptrend mein price aksar ek moving average par aakar ruk jata hai, jaise wo ek chalta hua support ho.
• Supertrend — ATR par bana ek trend-following tool jo price ke upar ya neeche ek line dikhata hai aur flip hota rehta hai.
• ADX / DMI — Trend ki strength naapta hai (direction nahi). Low ADX matlab range, high ADX matlab strong trend.
• Parabolic SAR — Dots jo price ke upar-neeche shift hote hain; trailing stop ke idea ke liye use hota hai.
• Ichimoku Cloud — Ek hi system mein trend, support-resistance aur momentum dikhane wala Japanese tool.

MOMENTUM WALE INDICATORS

Ye batate hain move mein dum kitna bacha hai:
• RSI (Relative Strength Index) — 0 se 100 ke beech ka oscillator, default period 14. Ye recent gains aur losses ka ratio dikhata hai.
• Overbought — RSI 70 ke upar. Iska matlab 'girega' nahi hota — strong trend mein RSI hafton tak 70 ke upar reh sakta hai.
• Oversold — RSI 30 ke neeche. Wahi baat ulta: sasta dikhna aur girna band hona do alag cheezein hain.
• Bullish Divergence — Price naya low bana raha hai par RSI naya low nahi bana raha. Selling ka dum kam ho raha hai.
• Bearish Divergence — Price naya high bana raha hai par RSI peechhe reh gaya.
• MACD — Do EMAs (aam taur par 12 aur 26) ka farq, uske upar ek 9-period signal line.
• MACD Histogram — MACD line aur signal line ka gap bars mein. Bars chhote hone lage matlab momentum thanda ho raha hai.
• Stochastic — Close ko pichhle N candles ke high-low range ke andar kahan hai, ye percentage mein batata hai.
• CCI (Commodity Channel Index) — Price ka apne average se deviation naapta hai; naam ke bawajood har asset par use hota hai.
• Rate of Change (ROC) — N candles pehle ke price se aaj ka percentage change.

VOLATILITY AUR VOLUME WALE

Ye batate hain move kitna bada hai aur uske peechhe participation kitni:
• Bollinger Bands — Ek 20-period moving average ke upar-neeche 2 standard deviation ke bands. Bands price ke saath phailte-sikudte hain.
• Squeeze — Bands ka bahut paas aa jaana, matlab volatility bahut kam — aksar ek badi move se pehle.
• ATR (Average True Range) — Ek candle ka average movement, points mein. Stop loss ki width decide karne ka sabse practical tool.
• Standard Deviation — Price apne average se kitna bhatakta hai, statistically.
• Volume — Ek period mein kitni quantity trade hui. Move ke saath volume badhna us move ki credibility badhata hai.
• Volume Spike — Average se kai guna zyada volume, aksar news, breakout ya institutional activity par.
• OBV (On Balance Volume) — Up candles ka volume jodta aur down candles ka ghatata hai, taaki accumulation ya distribution dikhe.
• VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — Din ka volume ke hisaab se average price. Institutions apna execution isi ke against judge karte hain.
• MFI (Money Flow Index) — RSI jaisa hi, par usmein volume bhi shamil hota hai.

LEVEL BANANE WALE TOOLS

Ye indicator kam, aur level-generator zyada hain:
• Fibonacci Retracement — Ek swing low se high tak line kheench kar 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% aur 78.6% ke levels nikalna, jahan pullback rukne ka chance dekha jata hai.
• Fibonacci Extension — Wahi ratios aage badha kar target zone banana, jaise 127% ya 161.8%.
• Pivot Points — Pichhle din ke high, low aur close se banaya gaya central pivot aur uske upar-neeche R1-R3, S1-S3 levels.
• CPR (Central Pivot Range) — Pivot ke saath do aur lines (TC aur BC). Patli CPR aksar trending din se jodi jati hai, chaudi CPR range wale din se.
• Anchored VWAP — VWAP jo kisi khaas event se shuru kiya jaye, jaise result day ya swing low.
• Volume Profile — Price ke against volume ka distribution, jo dikhata hai kis price par sabse zyada business hua.

AUR TOOLS JO ROZ DIKHTE HAIN

Ye utne famous nahi hain jitne RSI aur MACD, par charts par inka naam aksar sunai deta hai:
• Keltner Channel — Moving average ke around ATR-based bands. Bollinger jaisa dikhta hai, par volatility ka measure alag hai.
• Donchian Channel — Pichhli N candles ka highest high aur lowest low. Breakout systems ki buniyaad.
• Williams %R — Stochastic jaisa oscillator, jo 0 se -100 ke beech chalta hai.
• CMF (Chaikin Money Flow) — Volume ke saath paisa andar aa raha hai ya bahar ja raha hai, ye naapta hai.
• Accumulation / Distribution Line — OBV ka cousin, jo candle ke andar close ki position ko bhi weight deta hai.
• Awesome Oscillator — Do simple moving averages ka farq, histogram ke roop mein.
• TRIX — Triple-smoothed moving average ka rate of change; noise bahut kam, lag zyada.
• Aroon — Naya high ya naya low kitne time pehle bana, ye naap kar trend ki freshness batata hai.
• Vortex — Do lines jinke crossover ko trend change ka signal maana jata hai.
• Elder Ray (Bull / Bear Power) — Buyers aur sellers ka apne average par kitna control hai.
• ZigZag — Chhote moves hata kar sirf bade swings dikhane wala drawing tool. Ye repaint karta hai, isliye signal ke liye nahi, structure padhne ke liye.
• Linear Regression Channel — Price ke through ek best-fit line aur uske around standard deviation bands.
• Supertrend Multiplier — Supertrend ka wo setting jo line ko price se kitni door rakhe, ye decide karta hai.

SETTINGS AUR TRAPS

Indicator galat nahi hota, uska istemal galat hota hai. Ye rahe wo shabd jo us galti ko describe karte hain:
• Default Settings — Wo values jo tool ke saath aati hain (RSI 14, MACD 12-26-9). Inka faayda ye hai ki duniya inhi ko dekhti hai.
• Optimisation — Past data par best-performing settings dhoondhna.
• Curve Fitting / Over-Optimisation — Itna optimise kar dena ki system sirf purane data par kaam kare, aage kabhi nahi. Ye backtest ki sabse badi bimari hai.
• Look-Ahead Bias — Test mein galti se aisi information use ho jana jo us waqt available hi nahi thi.
• Source — Indicator kis price par calculate ho: close, open, hl2 (high-low ka average), ohlc4.
• Offset — Indicator ko aage ya peechhe shift karna. Shift kiya hua signal live trading mein waisa nahi milta.
• Multi-Timeframe Indicator — Bade timeframe ka indicator chhote chart par dikhana.
• Indicator Alert — Ek condition par notification set karna, taaki screen par baithe rehne ki zaroorat na pade.
• Signal Lag — Signal aane tak move ka kitna hissa nikal chuka hai.
• Indicator Stacking — Ek hi cheez naapne wale kai indicators lagana (jaise RSI + Stochastic + CCI) aur unki agreement ko confirmation samajh lena. Ye confluence nahi, ek hi opinion teen baar hai.
• Clean Chart — Sirf wo do-teen tools rakhna jinka rule aapko zubani yaad ho. Baaki sab dekhne mein professional lagta hai, decide kuch nahi karta.

Indicator ka sahi istemal ye hai ki wo aapke structure-based idea ko confirm ya reject kare. Agar chart par pehle se koi level nahi hai, to das indicator lagane se bhi trade nahi milega — sirf das opinions milenge.

Ye journal sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi. Har term ko apne chart par khud dekho, tabhi wo yaad rehta hai.
