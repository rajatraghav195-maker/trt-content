TICKER, ISIN AUR NAAM: EK STOCK KI TEEN PEHCHAAN

Symbol badal sakta hai, ISIN nahi — aur ye farq kab kaam aata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Market Basics

Ek stock ke teen naam hote hain — company ka naam, exchange ka ticker symbol, aur uska ISIN. Teenon alag kaam karte hain, aur teesra sabse sthir hai.

TEENON KA KAAM

Company ka naam badal sakta hai. Ticker symbol bhi badal sakta hai, aur ek hi company ka NSE aur BSE par alag symbol ho sakta hai.

ISIN ek unique identifier hai jo security ko identify karta hai. Demat holdings ISIN par hi track hoti hain, symbol par nahi.

YE FARQ KAB KAAM AATA HAI

In situations mein sirf ISIN hi reliable hota hai:
• Company ka naam ya symbol badalne ke baad purane records match karna.
• Do exchanges ke data ko ek saath jodna.
• Corporate action ke baad nayi security identify karna.
• Historical price data mein sahi series dhoondhna.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Purani watchlist aur purane records mein symbol change sabse aam silent problem hai — ek naam wahi rehta hai par wo ab kisi aur cheez ko point kar raha hota hai. Apne records mein ISIN rakhna is poore confusion ko khatam kar deta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
