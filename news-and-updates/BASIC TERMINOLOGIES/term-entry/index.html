Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 31, 2026 · Risk Management

Entry — Position lene ka price aur uske peechhe ka reason.

YE KAHAN FIT HOTA HAI

Ye term Risk Management ke andar aati hai, "TRADE-LEVEL RISK" wale hisse mein. Ek single trade ke andar ke shabd:

ISKE SAATH PADHNE WALE TERMS

Isi group ke baaki shabd — inhe saath samajhna zyada kaam aata hai:
• Stop Loss
• Hard SL vs Mental SL
• Target
• Risk-Reward Ratio (RR)
• R-Multiple
• Exit
• Trailing Stop Loss
• Breakeven Stop
• Scaling Out / Partial Booking
• Invalidation Level

Poora group aur uske saare examples ek jagah padhne ke liye "RISK MANAGEMENT: BACHNE KI DICTIONARY" wala journal dekho — ye term wahin se li gayi hai.

Ye entry sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi. Charges, margins, lot size aur expiry rules exchange time-time par badalte hain, isliye koi bhi number apne broker ya exchange par verify karo.
