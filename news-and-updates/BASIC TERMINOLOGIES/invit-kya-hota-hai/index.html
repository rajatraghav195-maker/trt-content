INVIT: INFRASTRUCTURE ASSETS SE INCOME

Roads, transmission aur pipelines jaise assets ka listed roop

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 28, 2026 · AIF, PE & Alternates

InvIT REIT jaisa hi structure hai, par uske paas property nahi, infrastructure assets hote hain — jaise toll roads, power transmission lines ya gas pipelines.

CASH FLOW KAHAN SE AATA HAI

In assets ka revenue aksar lambe contracts se aata hai — toll collection, transmission charges ya capacity payments. Isliye cash flow relatively predictable hota hai.

Wahi cash flow kharche aur debt servicing ke baad unit holders ko distribute hota hai.

KYA DEKHNA CHAHIYE

Ye chaar cheezein iska character tay karti hain:
• Asset ki concession ya contract ki bachi hui duration.
• Counterparty kaun hai — sarkari entity ya private.
• Trust ke level par kitna debt hai.
• Distribution ka kitna hissa capital return hai aur kitna income.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Kai infrastructure assets ki life limited hoti hai — concession khatam hone par asset wapas chala jaata hai. Isliye yahan yield ke saath ye poochhna zaroori hai ki wo yield kitne saal aur milegi.
