SUPERTREND: EK LINE JO SIRF DO STATE BATATI HAI

ATR par bana trailing level, aur uska ranging market mein anjaam

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 30, 2026 · Indicators

Supertrend ek line hai jo bhaav ke upar ya neeche hoti hai aur sirf do state batati hai. Uski simplicity hi uska sabse bada faayda hai aur sabse badi trap bhi.

BANTA KAISE HAI

Wo ATR par bana hota hai — ek volatility based distance bhaav se rakh kar line khinchi jaati hai, aur bhaav us line ko cross kare to state palat jaati hai.

Do settings hoti hain: ATR ka period aur uska multiplier. Multiplier chhota karne par signals zyada aur galat zyada; bada karne par kam aur der se.

KAHAN KAAM KARTA HAI

Iska behaviour market ke type par poori tarah depend karta hai:
• Saaf trend mein wo lambi chaal ko pakad kar rakhta hai — yahi uska design hai.
• Range mein wo lagataar galat signal deta hai, dono taraf.
• Wo hamesha trend ke andar hi rehta hai, isliye top aur bottom kabhi nahi deta.
• Har signal par position palatna charges mein hi bada nuksaan bana deta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Kisi bhi trend following tool ki sabse imaandaar tasveer uske bure daur mein dikhti hai, achhe daur mein nahi. Backtest dekhte waqt sabse pehle ye dekho ki range wale mahinon mein usne kitna diya — kyunki saal ka bada hissa wahi hota hai.
