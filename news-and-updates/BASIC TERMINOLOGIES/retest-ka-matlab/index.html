RETEST: TOOTA HUA LEVEL WAPAS AAKAR KYA BATATA HAI

Support ka resistance ban jaana, aur uska practical upyog

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 30, 2026 · Price Action

Ek level toot jaane ke baad bhaav aksar us level par wapas aata hai. Us wapsi ko retest kehte hain, aur wo ek breakout ke baare mein aksar breakout se zyada bata deti hai.

ROLE BADALNA

Jo level pehle support tha, toot jaane par wo resistance ban jaata hai. Ulta bhi utna hi sach hai. Iska tark simple hai — us level par jo log phanse the, unka behaviour badal jaata hai.

Retest mein wo level hold kar jaaye to breakout ko confirmation milta hai. Wapas andar chala jaaye to wo breakout fail tha.

ISKA UPYOG

Retest ek behtar entry deta hai, par uski shartein hain:
• Retest ka intezaar karne ka matlab hai ki kuch moves miss honge — wo iski laagat hai.
• Har breakout retest nahi deta, khaas kar tez trending market mein.
• Stop loss retest wale level ke door hi rakhna padta hai, warna wo bahut tight ho jaata hai.
• Volume retest par kam hona aksar accha maana jaata hai — matlab wapas bechne wale kam hain.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Retest ka intezaar ek discipline hai, ek prediction nahi. Agar wo nahi aata to trade nahi hota — aur wo bilkul acceptable outcome hai, kyunki us waqt aapka koi paisa risk par nahi tha.
