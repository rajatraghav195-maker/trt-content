INSIDE BAR AUR OUTSIDE BAR: EK CANDLE JO PICHLI KE ANDAR YA BAHAR HAI

Compression aur expansion ka sabse simple visual signal

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 22, 2026 · Price Action

Do candles ka aapas mein rishta akeli candle se zyada batata hai. Inside aur outside bar wahi rishta hain — ek batata hai ki range sikud rahi hai, doosra ki wo phat gayi.

DONO KA MATLAB

Inside bar wo candle hai jiska poora high low pichli candle ke high low ke andar hai. Iska matlab hai ki us session mein pichle session se kam disagreement tha — range sikud gayi.

Outside bar ulta hai — uska high pichli se upar aur low pichli se neeche. Yahan dono taraf ke log active the aur range phail gayi.

INHE KAISE PADHEIN

Ye chaar baatein practical hain:
• Inside bar apne aap mein direction nahi batata — wo sirf compression batata hai.
• Compression ke baad expansion aana aam hai, par uski disha inside bar se nahi milti.
• Lagataar do teen inside bars compression ko aur mazboot karte hain.
• Outside bar aksar volatility badhne ka pehla sanket hota hai, trend ka nahi.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Ye patterns higher timeframe par zyada maayne rakhte hain. Ek minute chart par har doosri candle inside bar hoti hai — wahan wo information nahi, sirf noise hai.
