PEHLE SURAKSHA, PHIR NIVESH: KRAM KYUN MAAYNE RAKHTA HAI

Emergency fund aur insurance ke bina investing kyun kamzor hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 06, 2026 · Retirement & Insurance

Nivesh shuru karne se pehle do cheezein honi chahiye — kuch mahine ka emergency fund aur zaroori insurance. Ye boring lagta hai, par yahi wo cheez hai jo baaki plan ko tootne se bachaati hai.

KRAM KA TARK

Investment ka faayda samay se aata hai. Agar beech mein ek emergency aapko galat waqt par bechne par majboor kar de, to poora compounding toot jaata hai.

Insurance ka kaam bhi wahi hai — ek bada, achanak kharcha aapke long term corpus tak na pahunche.

DONO KA SIZE

Ek practical dhaancha ye hai:
• Emergency fund kuch mahinon ke kharche ke barabar, aur wo bhi liquid form mein.
• Health cover parivaar ke size aur city ke hospital costs ke hisaab se.
• Term cover tab tak jab tak koi aap par financially depend karta hai.
• Ye teenon ek baar set karke saal mein ek baar review karna kaafi hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Emergency fund ka return kam hona uski kamzori nahi, uska design hai. Uska kaam badhna nahi hai — uska kaam us din maujood hona hai jab baaki sab kuch bechna sabse bura faisla hoga.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
