RISK BUDGET: PAISA NAHI, RISK BAANTNA

Allocation ko capital ke hisaab se nahi, risk ke hisaab se sochna

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 31, 2026 · Risk Management

Zyadatar log allocation ko paise mein sochte hain — itna equity mein, itna debt mein. Ek zyada useful tarika ye hai ki risk ko baanta jaaye, capital ko nahi.

FARQ KYA HAI

Agar portfolio ka aadha hissa equity mein hai aur aadha debt mein, to capital barabar baanta gaya. Par portfolio ka lagbhag saara movement equity se aayega.

Risk ke hisaab se dekhein to wo portfolio balanced nahi hai — wo ek equity portfolio hai jiske saath thoda cushion hai.

RISK BUDGET KAISE BANAYEIN

Ek simple version bhi kaafi kaam karta hai:
• Har holding ka contribution nikaalo — uska weight gunaa uski volatility.
• Dekho ki total movement ka kitna percent kis holding se aa raha hai.
• Jo ek holding budget se zyada le rahi ho, uska weight ghatao.
• Ye review saal mein ek do baar kaafi hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Ye exercise pehli baar karne par lagbhag sabko ek hi cheez dikhti hai — portfolio ka jitna risk unhone socha tha, asli risk usse kaafi zyada hai. Wo ek baar dekh lena hi is exercise ka poora faayda hai.
