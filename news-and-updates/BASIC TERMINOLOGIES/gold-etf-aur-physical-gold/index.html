GOLD ETF, DIGITAL GOLD AUR PHYSICAL GOLD: TEENON KA FARQ

Custody, charges aur liquidity ke hisaab se teenon ka hisaab

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 06, 2026 · AIF, PE & Alternates

Gold lene ke aaj kai raaste hain aur unka return lagbhag ek hi cheez se juda hai. Farq return mein nahi, custody, charges aur exit mein hai.

TEENON KA DHAANCHA

Physical gold aapke paas hota hai. Uske saath making charges, purity ka sawaal aur storage ka kharcha aata hai, aur bechte waqt buyer ka apna deduction.

Gold ETF ek listed fund hai jo gold rakhta hai. Uski units demat mein aati hain aur exchange par bikti hain, ek expense ratio ke saath.

Digital gold ek platform ka product hai jahan aapke naam par gold rakha jaata hai. Yahan sabse zaroori sawaal custody aur regulation ka hai.

CHUNAAV KIS AADHAR PAR

Ye chaar sawaal faisla saaf kar dete hain:
• Aapko gold pehenna hai ya uska exposure chahiye.
• Kitne saal ke liye rakhna hai — charges ka asar duration par badhta hai.
• Exit kab aur kitni jaldi chahiye ho sakta hai.
• Custody kiske paas hai aur wo kis niyam ke andar hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Jewellery ko investment maan kar rakhna aam hai, par uska making charge hi entry par ek bada nuksaan hai jo return mein kabhi dikhta nahi. Investment aur upyog ko alag rakhna hi is confusion ka sabse seedha hal hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
