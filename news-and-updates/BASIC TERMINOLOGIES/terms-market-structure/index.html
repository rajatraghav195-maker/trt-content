MARKET STRUCTURE: LEVELS, ZONES AUR SWINGS

Support, resistance, higher high, BOS, supply-demand — chart ka skeleton

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 03, 2026 · Market Structure

Ek hi candle pattern ek jagah accha kaam karta hai aur doosri jagah bilkul fail ho jata hai — farq location ka hai. Market structure wahi location ki bhasha hai: kaun se level important hain, trend abhi kis shape mein hai, aur kis zone mein bade orders chhupe hote hain.

LEVEL KI BUNIYAAD

Sabse pehle wo levels jo har chart par milte hain:
• Support — Wo price area jahan buyers itne active ho jate hain ki girawat ruk jati hai.
• Resistance — Wo price area jahan sellers hawi ho jate hain aur upar ka move ruk jata hai.
• Role Reversal / Flip — Toota hua resistance aage support ban jata hai, aur toota hua support resistance. Isi ko polarity flip kehte hain.
• Trendline — Ek jhuki hui line jo swing lows (ya highs) ko jodti hai. Ye dynamic support/resistance hai.
• Channel — Do parallel trendlines ke beech chalta price.
• Round Number / Psychological Level — 100, 500, 25,000 jaise numbers, jahan orders naturally jama hote hain.
• Previous Day High / Low — Kal ke high aur low. Intraday traders ke liye ye sabse zyada dekhe jaane wale levels mein se hain.
• All-Time High (ATH) / All-Time Low (ATL) — Ab tak ka sabse ooncha aur sabse neecha price.

SWING AUR TREND KA DHAANCHA

Trend ki definition feel se nahi, structure se banti hai. Ye rahe uske parts:
• Swing High — Ek peak jiske dono taraf ki candles usse neeche hain.
• Swing Low — Ek bottom jiske dono taraf ki candles usse upar hain.
• Higher High (HH) / Higher Low (HL) — Har naya peak pichhle se ooncha aur har dip pichhle se ooncha. Yehi uptrend ki technical definition hai.
• Lower High (LH) / Lower Low (LL) — Ulta pattern: har rally chhoti, har fall gehri. Yehi downtrend hai.
• Market Structure — Inhi swings ka sequence, jo batata hai market kis mood mein hai.
• BOS (Break of Structure) — Trend ki direction mein ek naya swing point toot-na, matlab trend abhi zinda hai.
• CHoCH (Change of Character) — Pehli baar structure ulta tootna, jo possible trend change ka pehla hint hai.
• Leg — Ek swing se doosre tak ka move. Trend inhi legs se banta hai.
• Higher Timeframe Bias — Bade timeframe ka trend, jise chhote timeframe par trade karte waqt background ki tarah use kiya jata hai.

ZONE-BASED SOCH

Naye traders line kheenchte hain, experienced log zone banate hain — kyunki orders ek exact price par nahi, ek area mein hote hain:
• Supply Zone — Wo area jahan se pehle tez selling aayi thi; price wapas aaye to sellers phir mil sakte hain.
• Demand Zone — Wo area jahan se pehle tez buying aayi thi.
• Order Block — Wo aakhri candle cluster jahan se ek bada move shuru hua. Iska idea ye hai ki wahan institutional orders bache hue ho sakte hain.
• Fair Value Gap / Imbalance — Price ka itni tezi se bhaagna ki beech mein ek area bina proper trading ke chhoot jaye. Market aksar wapas aakar use bharta hai.
• Liquidity — Wo jagah jahan bahut saare orders (khaas kar stop loss) jama hain, jaise obvious high, low ya round number ke thoda upar-neeche.
• Liquidity Grab / Stop Hunt — Price ka un stops ko chhoo kar turant palat jaana. Isi wajah se obvious level par lagaya SL aksar hit hota hai.
• Equal Highs / Equal Lows — Do ya zyada barabar levels, jinke peechhe stops ka cluster hone ka chance zyada hota hai.
• Mitigation — Price ka wapas usi zone par aana jahan se move shuru hua tha.
• Consolidation Zone — Wo box jahan price kaafi der ruka. Isse nikalna hi aksar next move ka trigger hota hai.

TIMEFRAME AUR TRADER KE TYPE

Same chart, alag timeframe, bilkul alag kahani:
• Timeframe — Ek candle kitne time ki hai. Chhota timeframe zyada noise, bada timeframe zyada context.
• Multi-Timeframe Analysis — Ek hi stock ko do-teen timeframes par dekhna: bada trend ke liye, chhota entry ke liye.
• HTF / LTF — Higher timeframe aur lower timeframe ka short form.
• Top-Down Analysis — Pehle index, phir sector, phir stock, phir entry timeframe — upar se neeche aana.
• Scalping — Minute-minute ke chhote moves, din mein kai trades.
• Intraday Trading — Position usi din khuli aur usi din band.
• Swing Trading — Kuch din se kuch hafte tak position rakhna.
• Positional / Investing — Mahine ya saal ka horizon, jahan structure fundamentals se milta hai.

MARKET KE MOOD

Aur akhir mein, wo teen states jinke beech market ghoomta rehta hai — strategy inhi ke hisaab se badalni padti hai:
• Trending Market — Saaf HH-HL ya LH-LL. Yahan trend ke saath chalna kaam karta hai.
• Range-Bound Market — Do levels ke beech aage-peeche. Yahan support par kharidna aur resistance par bechna kaam karta hai, breakout trades nahi.
• Choppy Market — Na trend na saaf range; chhote jhatke jo stop loss khate rehte hain.
• Expansion vs Contraction — Volatility ka phailna aur sikudna. Chhoti range ke baad badi range aati hai, aur ulta bhi.
• Opening Range — Din ke pehle 15-30 minute ka high-low, jise kai intraday systems reference banate hain.
• Intraday High / Low — Aaj ka abhi tak ka sabse ooncha aur neecha price.
• Volume Confirmation — Structure toota to sahi, par volume ke bina — aksar wahi fakeout ban jata hai.

TREND KE PHASES

Bade moves achanak nahi aate — unse pehle ek lambi taiyari hoti hai. Ye us taiyari ki classic vocabulary hai:
• Accumulation — Lambi range jismein bade players chupchap kharid rahe hote hain. Chart boring lagta hai, isliye zyadatar log yahin bore ho kar nikal jate hain.
• Markup — Accumulation ke baad ka upar ka trend, jahan move sabko dikhne lagta hai.
• Distribution — Top par lambi range, jahan wahi bade players dheere-dheere bech rahe hote hain.
• Markdown — Distribution ke baad ki girawat.
• Spring — Range ke neeche ek jhootha breakdown, jo stops le kar wapas range ke andar aa jata hai.
• Upthrust — Uska ulta: range ke upar ek jhootha breakout.
• Re-accumulation — Trend ke beech ki wo range jo continuation mein tootti hai, reversal mein nahi.
• Effort vs Result — Volume (effort) bahut aur price move (result) kam. Ye mismatch aksar batata hai ki koi doosri taraf absorb kar raha hai.
• Absorption — Ek level par lagataar selling aane ke bawajood price ka na girna.
• Climax — Bahut bada volume aur bahut badi candle, aksar move ke ant mein.

LEVEL KI QUALITY KAISE JAANCHE

Har line jo aap kheenchte ho, ek level nahi hoti. Ye wo shabd hain jinse ek level ki quality judge hoti hai:
• Touch Count — Level kitni baar test hua. Do-teen clean touches ek line ko level banate hain, ek touch nahi.
• Reaction Strength — Level se palat kar price kitna door gaya. Jitni badi reaction, utna important level.
• Freshness — Ek untested zone aksar us zone se strong hota hai jo pehle hi kai baar use ho chuka hai.
• Break and Close vs Wick Break — Level ke paar close hona aur sirf wick ka nikal jaana bilkul alag baatein hain. Zyadatar fakeouts wick breaks hote hain.
• Confluence Zone — Do-teen alag tareeke (level, moving average, Fibonacci) ek hi price par mil jayen.
• Level Cluster — Kai levels ka ek chhoti si range mein jama ho jana, jo ek chaudi barrier banata hai.
• Magnet Level — Wo price jiski taraf market baar-baar kheencha chala aata hai, jaise round number ya heavy-volume level.
• Price Memory — Wo purana area jahan pehle bada action hua tha; market use aksar dobara respect karta hai.
• Invalidation — Wo point jiske paar jaane par level ka matlab hi khatam ho jata hai.
• Measured Move — Pattern ki apni height ko breakout point se aage naap kar ek rough target nikalna.
• Round Trip — Price ka breakout karke wapas poori range mein lautt aana — trap ka sabse saaf shape.

Structure aapko batata hai kahan dekhna hai; price action batata hai kab. Dono milkar ek trade banate hain — indicator uske baad aata hai, uski jagah nahi.

Ye journal sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi. Har term ko apne chart par khud dekho, tabhi wo yaad rehta hai.
