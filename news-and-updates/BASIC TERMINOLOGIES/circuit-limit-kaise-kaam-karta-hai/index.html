CIRCUIT LIMIT: JAB BHAAV EK DIN MEIN AUR HIL HI NAHI SAKTA

Upper aur lower circuit ka mechanism, aur circuit mein phanse hue trade ka sach

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 25, 2026 · Market Basics

Circuit limit ek price band hai jiske bahar us din bhaav ja hi nahi sakta. Wo bhaav ko rukne par majboor nahi karta — wo sirf us din ke liye range fix kar deta hai, taaki ek panic ya ek manipulation din bhar mein sab kuch na bigaad de.

BAND KAISE TAY HOTA HAI

Har stock ka band pichle din ke closing price par lagta hai. Kuch stocks par 20 percent, kuch par 10 ya 5 percent, aur jin par derivatives available hain unme aksar dynamic price band chalta hai jo din ke andar widen ho sakta hai.

Index par bhi market wide circuit hote hain. Wahan zyada girawat par poora market kuch der ke liye rok diya jaata hai, aur bahut badi girawat par din bhar ke liye band bhi ho sakta hai.

CIRCUIT MEIN PHANSNA KYA HOTA HAI

Upper circuit par sirf buyers hote hain aur lower par sirf sellers. Iska seedha matlab:
• Lower circuit mein aapka sell order queue mein khada rahega, execute hoga ya nahi ye guarantee nahi.
• Upper circuit mein kharidna utna hi mushkil hai — order lagta hai, bharta nahi.
• Lagataar circuit lagne wale stock mein exit kai din tak nahi milti.
• Isliye illiquid smallcap mein position size hi wo cheez hai jo aapke haath mein hai, exit nahi.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Circuit ko opportunity samajh kar us mein ghusna sabse mehnga sabak hota hai. Upar wala circuit aapko andar to le jaayega, par jab wahi stock ulta ghoomega to niche wala circuit aapko bahar nahi jaane dega.
