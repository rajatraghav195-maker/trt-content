CORPORATE FD: ZYADA INTEREST KA MATLAB ZYADA RISK

Bank FD se antar, credit rating ka role, aur default ka scenario

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 01, 2026 · AIF, PE & Alternates

Company FD bank FD se zyada interest deti hai, aur wo extra interest kisi generosity se nahi aata. Wo extra risk ka daam hai, aur ye baat structure mein hi likhi hui hai.

BUNIYADI FARQ

Bank deposit ek regulated bank ke paas hota hai aur uspar deposit insurance ka ek layer hota hai, ek limit tak.

Company FD ek company ko diya gaya unsecured loan hai. Company theek chali to interest milta hai; nahi chali to aap ek unsecured creditor ho.

KYA DEKHNA CHAHIYE

Ye cheezein faisle se pehle dekhna chahiye:
• Credit rating aur uska recent trend — downgrade sabse bada signal hota hai.
• Company ka cash flow aur maujooda debt.
• Rate ka apne peer group se antar — bahut zyada rate ek warning hai.
• Premature withdrawal ke rules.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Rating ek snapshot hai, guarantee nahi — wo tenure ke beech badal sakti hai. Isliye ek hi company mein bada hissa daalna, chahe rating aaj achhi ho, wahi galti hai jo har credit event mein dohrayi jaati hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
