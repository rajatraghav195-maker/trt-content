FUND MANAGER BADAL GAYA: KYA KARNA CHAHIYE

Manager change ka asar, aur turant redeem karne se pehle kya dekhein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Mutual Funds & AMC

Jis manager ke track record ki wajah se aapne fund chuna tha, wo chala jaaye to sawaal uthta hai ki ab kya. Iska ek hi seedha jawaab nahi hai, par sochne ka ek dhaancha hai.

KITNA FARQ PADTA HAI

Kuch funds bahut process driven hote hain — team, framework aur mandate manager se bade hote hain. Wahan ek change ka asar kam hota hai.

Doosri taraf kuch funds ek high conviction manager ke style par hi chalte hain. Wahan change ka matlab asli mein ek naya fund hai jiska naam purana hai.

FAISLA LENE SE PEHLE

Ye chaar cheezein dekhna kaafi hota hai:
• Naye manager ka apna track record aur style kya raha hai.
• Agle kuch mahinon mein portfolio kitna badla — turnover se pata chalta hai.
• Mandate wahi hai ya scheme ka character hi badal gaya.
• Redeem karne par exit load aur tax kitna lagega.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Turant redeem karna aksar mehnga hota hai aur zaroori kam. Do teen quarter portfolio disclosures dekh lena ek sasta intezaar hai, aur wo aapko anumaan ki jagah evidence deta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
