BONUS, SPLIT AUR RIGHTS: TEENON ALAG CHEEZEIN HAIN

Corporate actions jo share count badal dete hain, aur inme se kaun wealth banata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 27, 2026 · Market Basics

Teenon corporate actions ke baad aapke paas share zyada ho jaate hain aur bhaav gir jaata hai, isliye log teenon ko ek hi cheez samajh lete hain. Ganit teenon ka alag hai, aur ek mein to aapko paisa dena padta hai.

TEENON KA MECHANISM

Bonus mein company apne reserves se aapko free extra shares deti hai. 1:1 bonus par share double, bhaav aadha, aapki total value wahi. Naya paisa kahin se nahi aata.

Split mein face value tod di jaati hai. Ek 10 rupee face value ka share paanch 2 rupee walon mein badal jaata hai. Yahan bhi value wahi rehti hai, sirf ticket size chhota ho jaata hai.

Rights issue in dono se alag hai — wahan company aapko discount par naye share kharidne ka haq deti hai, aur uske liye aapko paisa dena hota hai. Wo fundraising hai, gift nahi.

DHYAAN KIS BAAT KA

Ye chaar galatfehmiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain:
• Bonus se wealth nahi banti — sirf same wealth zyada tukdon mein baant jaati hai.
• Bonus ke baad bhaav "sasta" nahi hua, wo adjust hua hai.
• Rights issue mein hissa na lena aapki holding ko dilute karta hai.
• Purani price chart bonus aur split ke liye adjust honi chahiye, warna gap ek crash jaisa dikhta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Chart par jab bhi ek stock bina kisi news ke exactly aadha ya ek tihaai ho jaaye, sabse pehle corporate action check karo. Unadjusted raw price par nikaala gaya long term return sirf galat hota hai, thoda nahi — bilkul galat.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
