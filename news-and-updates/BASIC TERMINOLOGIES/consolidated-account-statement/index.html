CAS: SAARE NIVESH EK HI STATEMENT MEIN

Consolidated Account Statement kya dikhata hai aur wo kyun kaam ka hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 30, 2026 · Market Basics

Alag alag jagah nivesh hone par poori tasveer kahin ek jagah nahi dikhti. CAS wahi ek jagah hai — ek statement jo aapke PAN se jude holdings ko ek saath dikhata hai.

ISME KYA AATA HAI

Demat mein pade shares, bonds aur ETFs, aur mutual fund folios — sab ek hi document mein, transactions ke saath.

Wo periodically apne aap aata hai, aur jab bhi transactions hon tab bhi. Uske liye alag se request karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

ISKA UPYOG

CAS ka sabse practical upyog reconciliation hai:
• Bhoole hue folios aur purane accounts saamne aa jaate hain.
• Har jagah nomination status ek saath dikh jaata hai.
• Total portfolio value ek jagah — allocation review ke liye yahi base hai.
• Parivaar ke liye ye ek ready made list hai ki nivesh kahan kahan hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Saal mein ek baar CAS poora padhna un chhoti cheezon ko pakad leta hai jo warna saalon tak chhoti rehti hain — ek purana folio, ek galat bank detail, ek missing nominee. Ye poore portfolio ka annual health check hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
