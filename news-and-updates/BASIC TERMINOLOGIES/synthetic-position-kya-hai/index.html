SYNTHETIC POSITION: OPTIONS SE FUTURES JAISI POSITION

Put call parity ka practical roop aur wo kyun kaam mein aata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Derivatives (F&O)

Options ko jodkar aisi position banayi ja sakti hai jo lagbhag futures jaisa behave kare. Isi ko synthetic position kehte hain, aur uske peeche ek simple relationship hai.

BUNIYADI RISHTA

Ek call kharidna aur ussi strike ka put bechna milkar lagbhag long futures jaisa payoff deta hai. Ulta karne par short futures jaisa.

Isi rishte ko put call parity kehte hain. Wo bataata hai ki call, put aur underlying ke bhaav aapas mein bandhe hue hain — teenon azaad nahi hain.

YE KAAM KAHAN AATA HAI

Iska istemal aksar mechanical wajahon se hota hai:
• Kabhi options mein liquidity futures se behtar hoti hai.
• Margin ka hisaab dono raaston mein alag baith sakta hai.
• Ek maujooda position ko bina band kiye direction badalne mein kaam aata hai.
• Parity se bahut door bhaav dikhna aksar liquidity ka sanket hai, free money ka nahi.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Synthetic position "chhupi hui arbitrage" ki tarah dikhti hai par wo sirf ek alag raasta hai wahi risk lene ka. Uska payoff futures jaisa hai, iska matlab uska nuksaan bhi futures jaisa hi hai.
