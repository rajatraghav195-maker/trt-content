CONTRACT NOTE: HAR TRADE KA ASLI BILL

Brokerage, STT, stamp duty aur baaki charges kahan dikhte hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Compliance & Regulation

Har trading din ke baad ek contract note aata hai jise zyadatar log kabhi kholte nahi. Wo us din ka legal record hai, aur wahi ek jagah hai jahan aapka asli kharcha likha hota hai.

ISME KYA HOTA HAI

Har trade ka time, quantity aur price, aur uske upar sab charges alag alag line mein. Brokerage sirf ek line hai — baaki lines statutory charges ki hoti hain.

Yahi document dispute mein evidence banta hai, aur yahi tax filing ke liye base hota hai.

CHARGES KI LINES

Ye alag alag cheezein hain aur alag alag logon ko jaati hain:
• Brokerage broker ki fees hai — sirf yahi negotiable hoti hai.
• STT ek tax hai jo trade par lagta hai, aur segment ke hisaab se alag hota hai.
• Exchange transaction charges exchange ke hote hain.
• Stamp duty, SEBI turnover fees aur GST bhi alag lines hain.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Mahine mein ek baar total charges ko apne total profit se compare karo. Bahut se active accounts mein wo ratio hi poora jawaab de deta hai ki strategy kaam kar rahi hai ya sirf activity ho rahi hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
