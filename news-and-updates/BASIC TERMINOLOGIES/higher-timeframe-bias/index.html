HIGHER TIMEFRAME BIAS: BADA CHART CHHOTE PAR RAAJ KARTA HAI

Ek hi stock ke do alag trend, aur unme priority kaise tay karein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 01, 2026 · Market Structure

Daily chart upar dikhta hai aur pandrah minute ka neeche. Dono sahi hote hain. Confusion tab hoti hai jab dono ko ek hi sawaal ka jawaab maan liya jaaye.

DONO KA ALAG KAAM

Bada timeframe disha batata hai — us stock mein paisa kis taraf ja raha hai. Chhota timeframe timing batata hai — entry kahan aur stop kahan.

Isliye faisla bade se aata hai aur execution chhote se. Ulta karne par aap har chhoti chaal par apna view badalte raho ge.

EK SIMPLE DHAANCHA

Teen timeframe ka combination aksar kaafi hota hai:
• Sabse bada — sirf disha, koi trade nahi.
• Beech wala — setup dhoondhna.
• Sabse chhota — entry aur stop.
• Teenon ka ratio aam taur par chaar se paanch guna rakha jaata hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Timeframe badalna hi sabse aam discipline failure hai — trade pandrah minute par liya jaata hai aur loss hone par use daily wala "investment" bana diya jaata hai. Entry se pehle ye tay karna ki ye trade kis chart ka hai, us poore problem ko khatam kar deta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
