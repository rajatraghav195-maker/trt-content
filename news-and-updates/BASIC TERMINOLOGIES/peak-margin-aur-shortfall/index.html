MARGIN SHORTFALL AUR PENALTY: KAM PAISE PAR POSITION KA ANJAAM

Intraday margin reporting kaise hoti hai aur penalty kis par lagti hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 27, 2026 · Derivatives (F&O)

Margin ka hisaab din ke ant mein nahi hota — wo din ke andar random samay par snapshot le kar hota hai. Isliye ek position jo shaam tak theek dikh rahi ho, wo bhi shortfall bana sakti hai.

SNAPSHOT KA MATLAB

Clearing corporation din mein kai baar har client ka margin aur available funds ka snapshot leta hai. Us waqt agar required margin available se zyada thi, to wo shortfall record ho jaata hai.

Shortfall par penalty broker par lagti hai, aur broker use client se wasoolta hai. Iska record aapke ledger mein aata hai.

SHORTFALL KAB BANTA HAI

Ye sabse aam wajahein hain:
• Position pehle le li aur funds baad mein transfer kiye.
• Volatility badhne se margin din ke beech mein badh gayi.
• Hedge leg pehle band kar diya, jisse naked position ki margin turant badh gayi.
• Ek segment ka loss doosre segment ki available margin kha gaya.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Hedged position band karte waqt hamesha naked leg pehle band karo, hedge baad mein. Ulta karne par kuch minute ke liye aapki margin requirement kai guna ho jaati hai — aur snapshot usi minute mein aa sakta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
