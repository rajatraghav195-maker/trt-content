IMPACT COST: BADE ORDER KI CHHUPI HUI FEES

Depth kaise padhein, aur ek stock kitna liquid hai ye kaise naapein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 06, 2026 · Order Flow

Brokerage dikh jaati hai, taxes dikh jaate hain, par impact cost bill mein kahin nahi aati. Wo aapke fill price ke andar chhup jaati hai — aur bade order mein wo baaki sab charges se badi ho sakti hai.

DEPTH KYA BATATI HAI

Order book mein har price level par kitni quantity padi hai, ye depth dikhati hai. Agar best bid par sirf sau share hain aur aap paanch hazaar bech rahe ho, to baaki neeche ke levels par bikega.

Isi wajah se do stocks ka spread ek jaisa dikh sakta hai par unka asli liquidity bilkul alag ho sakta hai.

LIQUIDITY KAISE JAANCHEIN

In cheezon ko dekhna kaafi hota hai:
• Average daily traded value, sirf volume nahi — bhaav ke saath multiply karke.
• Best bid aur ask ke beech ka spread, percentage mein.
• Top paanch levels par padi total quantity.
• Din bhar mein kitne minute aisi rahe jab quote hi nahi tha.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Apne position size ko us stock ki average daily value ke saath compare karo. Agar aapki position us value ka bada hissa hai, to aap us stock ke liye ab retail nahi ho — aur exit us din nahi milegi jis din sabko chahiye hogi.
