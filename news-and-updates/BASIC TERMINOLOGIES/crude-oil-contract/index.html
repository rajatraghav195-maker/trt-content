CRUDE OIL: SABSE ZYADA GLOBAL CONTRACT

Kaunse factors bhaav hilaate hain, aur uski volatility ka character

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 27, 2026 · Derivatives & Commodities

Crude sabse zyada trade hone wali commodities mein se ek hai, aur uska bhaav lagbhag poori tarah global factors par chalta hai. Local demand ka usme bahut chhota role hai.

BHAAV KYA HILATA HAI

Supply side par producer countries ke production decisions, geopolitical events aur inventory data. Demand side par global growth, travel aur industrial activity.

Inventory data periodically release hota hai aur us waqt bhaav mein tez chaal aana normal hai. Us release ka timing pehle se public hota hai.

VOLATILITY KA CHARACTER

Crude ka behaviour equity se kaafi alag hai:
• Ek hi headline par bahut badi chaal aa sakti hai, dono taraf.
• Trading hours lambe hain, isliye position raat mein bhi chalti rehti hai.
• Currency ka asar bhi judta hai, kyunki international price dollar mein hota hai.
• Margin volatility ke saath tezi se badhti hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Crude mein position size wo sabse badi galti hai jo equity ki aadat se aati hai. Ek lot ki contract value aur uski ek din ki normal chaal — dono pehle nikaalo, aur tabhi tay karo ki ek lot bhi aapke account ke liye theek hai ya nahi.
