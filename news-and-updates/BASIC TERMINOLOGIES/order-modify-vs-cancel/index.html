ORDER MODIFY KAREIN YA CANCEL KARKE NAYA LAGAYEIN

Queue priority ka rishta, aur kaunsa kab behtar hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 05, 2026 · Order Flow

Ek pending order ka bhaav badalna ho to do raaste hain — use modify karo ya cancel karke naya lagao. Dono ka anjaam ek jaisa nahi hota.

QUEUE PRIORITY

Order book mein ek hi bhaav par kai orders hote hain, aur wo time ke hisaab se line mein lagte hain. Pehle aaya order pehle bharta hai.

Price badalne par wo priority chali jaati hai — order line ke aakhir mein chala jaata hai. Quantity kam karne par aksar priority bachi rehti hai, badhane par nahi.

PRACTICAL FARQ

Ye baatein faisle mein aati hain:
• Liquid stock mein queue priority se khaas farq nahi padta.
• Illiquid stock mein wo hi tay karti hai ki aap bharoge ya nahi.
• Modify aur cancel dono par charges aam taur par nahi lagte, par order rate limits hote hain.
• Baar baar modify karna algo ki tarah behave karta hai aur limits mein aa sakta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Order ko peeche bhaagte hue baar baar modify karna sabse aam intraday habit hai, aur wo aksar us fill par khatam hoti hai jo aapko chahiye hi nahi tha. Ek bhaav tay karo aur usi par rehne do — ya trade chhod do.
