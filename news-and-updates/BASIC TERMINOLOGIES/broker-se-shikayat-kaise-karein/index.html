BROKER SE SHIKAYAT: OFFICIAL RAASTA KYA HAI

Grievance ka sahi kram — broker, exchange, SEBI, aur uske baad

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 22, 2026 · Compliance & Regulation

Broker ke saath dikkat hone par zyadatar log ya to support par chillate rehte hain ya haar maan lete hain. Ek official escalation ladder maujood hai, aur uska har step record par hota hai.

KRAM KYA HAI

Pehla step broker ke apne grievance channel par likhit complaint hai. Har registered broker ko ek designated compliance officer aur ek investor grievance email rakhna hota hai.

Wahan se jawaab na mile ya jawaab galat lage to exchange ke investor grievance mechanism mein jaya ja sakta hai, aur uske upar SEBI ka online complaint system hai.

COMPLAINT KO MAZBOOT KAISE BANAYEIN

Ek acchi complaint mein ye cheezein hoti hain:
• Client code, tareekh aur order number — kahani nahi, record.
• Contract note, ledger aur order log ka screenshot ya PDF.
• Aapne kya maanga aur kya hua, do line mein.
• Pehle kis channel par likha tha aur kab — escalation ka apna proof.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Phone par hui baat ka koi wajood nahi hota. Har baar likh kar bhejna, aur apna copy rakhna, poore process mein sabse zyada kaam aane wali aadat hai — chahe complaint kabhi karni hi na pade.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
