COMMODITY MARKET KE LAMBE HOURS: FAAYDA AUR KHATRA

Raat tak chalne wale session ka asar position aur behaviour dono par

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 03, 2026 · Derivatives & Commodities

Commodity market equity se kaafi der tak khula rehta hai, kyunki uske underlying global markets raat mein active hote hain. Ye ek suvidha bhi hai aur ek risk bhi.

LAMBE HOURS KYUN

Crude, gold aur base metals ka asli price discovery international exchanges par hota hai. Agar local market unke saath khula na rahe to har subah ek bada gap aata.

Isliye evening session global data releases ke saath overlap karta hai, aur wahin sabse zyada volatility hoti hai.

ISKA DOOSRA PEHLU

Lambe hours ke apne nuksaan hain:
• Screen ke saamne zyada der rehne se decision quality girti hai.
• Raat ke session mein liquidity din se kam ho sakti hai.
• Global data release ke aas paas spread chaura ho jaata hai.
• Thakawat mein liye gaye faisle poore din ka result badal dete hain.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Apna trading window khud tay karo, market ka poora window mat lo. Ek fixed time par band karna aur uske baad sirf existing positions manage karna — ye ek rule hai jo lambe hours wale market mein sabse zyada kaam aata hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
