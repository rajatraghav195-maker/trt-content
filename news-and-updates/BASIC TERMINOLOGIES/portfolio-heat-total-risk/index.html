PORTFOLIO HEAT: SAARE OPEN TRADES KA JODA HUA RISK

Ek trade ka risk chhota hai, par das trades ka jod kitna hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 01, 2026 · Risk Management

Har trade par ek percent risk lena samajhdaari lagti hai. Par agar ek saath pandrah trades khule hain to aapka total risk pandrah percent hai — aur wo baat kahin dikhti nahi.

HEAT KA MATLAB

Portfolio heat aapke saare open positions ka total risk hai — har position ka entry se stop tak ka nuksaan, sab jod kar.

Ye number roz badalta hai. Naya trade lene par badhta hai, stop upar khiskane par ghatta hai, position band hone par nikal jaata hai.

ISKA UPYOG

Ek maximum heat tay karna ek practical rule hai:
• Total heat ek limit se upar ho to naya trade nahi, chahe setup kitna hi acha lage.
• Correlated positions ko ek hi trade ki tarah count karna chahiye.
• Profit mein stop upar khiskane se heat kam hoti hai aur nayi jagah banti hai.
• Heat ka number roz likhna hi is rule ko asli banata hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Sabse bure hafte aksar ek galat trade se nahi aate — wo das theek trades se aate hain jo ek hi din ek saath galat ho gaye. Heat ki limit hi wo ek cheez hai jo us scenario ko rokti hai.
