MARKET ORDER AUR LIMIT ORDER: KAUNSA KAB

Certainty of fill banaam certainty of price — dono ek saath nahi milte

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 22, 2026 · Order Flow

Har order in do mein se ek cheez chunta hai: ya to bhaav pakka, ya fill pakka. Dono ek saath kabhi nahi milte, aur zyadatar bura fill isi baat ko na samajhne se aata hai.

DONO KA VYAVHAAR

Market order kehta hai — jo bhi best available bhaav hai, us par bhar do. Fill lagbhag pakka hota hai, par bhaav wo mil sakta hai jo aapne socha bhi nahi tha.

Limit order kehta hai — is bhaav par ya isse behtar par hi bharna. Bhaav pakka hota hai, par order bhare bina pada reh sakta hai ya poora bhi nahi bharta.

KAUNSA KAHAN THEEK HAI

Ye chunaav liquidity par tikta hai, aadat par nahi:
• Bahut liquid stock aur normal market mein market order ka nuksaan mamooli hota hai.
• Illiquid stock mein market order sabse mehnga faisla ho sakta hai — spread hi bada hota hai.
• Opening ke pehle minute aur bade news ke turant baad market order sabse zyada slip karta hai.
• Exit emergency mein certainty of fill zyada zaroori ho jaati hai — wahan limit zid nahi karni chahiye.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Order lagane se pehle depth window ek baar dekh lo. Agar best bid aur best ask ke beech ka farq hi aapke expected profit ka bada hissa hai, to us trade ka ganit order type se pehle hi kharab hai.
