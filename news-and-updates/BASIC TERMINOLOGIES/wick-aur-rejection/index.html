LAMBI WICK: US BHAAV PAR KYA HUA THA

Shadow ka matlab, aur rejection ko kab serious lena chahiye

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 24, 2026 · Price Action

Candle ka body batata hai ki session kahan khula aur band hua. Wick batata hai ki bhaav kahan tak gaya aur wahan tik nahi paaya. Wahi na tik paana hi wick ka poora message hai.

WICK BANTA KAISE HAI

Upar ki lambi wick ka matlab hai ki bhaav wahan tak gaya, par sellers ne use wapas neeche khinch liya. Neeche ki lambi wick ulta batati hai.

Isliye wick ek jagah par asafal koshish ka record hai — ek level jise test kiya gaya aur jo hold ho gaya.

KAB SERIOUS LENA CHAHIYE

Har lambi wick maayne nahi rakhti. Ye conditions use weight deti hain:
• Wo kisi pehle se maujood important level par bani ho.
• Us session ka volume normal se zyada ho.
• Wo higher timeframe candle ki wick ho, ek minute ki nahi.
• Uske baad ki candle us rejection ko confirm kare, contradict na kare.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Illiquid stock mein lambi wick aksar rejection nahi, sirf ek patla order book hoti hai — ek chhota order bhaav ko door le gaya aur wo wapas aa gaya. Wahan wick ek story nahi, ek liquidity problem hai.
