EK TRADE PAR ASLI KHARCHA KITNA HAI

Brokerage se aage — poore round trip ka hisaab kaise nikaalein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Order Flow

Log brokerage compare karte hain aur baaki charges ko ignore kar dete hain. Kai segments mein brokerage total kharche ka chhota hissa hoti hai, aur baaki sab har broker par lagbhag ek jaisa hota hai.

KHARCHE KE HISSE

Brokerage broker ka hai. Uske alawa exchange transaction charges, securities transaction tax, stamp duty, SEBI fees aur in sab par GST lagta hai.

Inme se zyadatar statutory hain, yaani wo har broker par same rahenge. Isliye "zero brokerage" ka matlab zero cost kabhi nahi hota.

ROUND TRIP KA HISAAB

Sahi tarika ye hai:
• Entry aur exit dono ka kharcha jodo — ek trade ka matlab do transactions hai.
• Us total ko position size se divide karke percentage nikaalo.
• Wahi percentage aapka breakeven hai — usse kam move par aap loss mein ho.
• Chhote target wali strategies mein ye percentage hi poora edge kha sakta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Har broker ka charges calculator hota hai — ek baar apni typical trade size daal kar breakeven percentage nikaal lo. Wo ek number aapko bata dega ki aapki strategy ka target realistic hai ya wo charges ke neeche hi dab jaayega.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
