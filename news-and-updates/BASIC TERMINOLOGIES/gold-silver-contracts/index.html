GOLD AUR SILVER CONTRACTS: EK JAISE LAGTE HAIN, HAIN NAHI

Dono ka alag character, aur contract sizes ka farq

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 28, 2026 · Derivatives & Commodities

Sona aur chandi dono precious metals hain aur aksar ek saath chalte hain. Par unka behaviour alag hai, aur trading ke liye wo farq bahut maayne rakhta hai.

DONO KA CHARACTER

Gold ka bada hissa investment aur central bank demand se chalta hai. Isliye wo aksar uncertainty ke daur mein majboot hota hai.

Silver mein industrial demand ka hissa bada hai. Isliye wo ek taraf precious metal ki tarah aur doosri taraf industrial metal ki tarah behave karta hai — aur uski volatility gold se saaf zyada rehti hai.

CONTRACT KE ANTAR

Exchange par dono ke kai variants hote hain:
• Alag alag size ke contracts hote hain, taaki chhote participants bhi aa sakein.
• Chhote contracts ki liquidity aksar bade se kam hoti hai.
• Purity aur delivery specification har contract mein alag likhi hoti hai.
• International price dollar mein hota hai, isliye rupee ki chaal bhi return mein aati hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Silver ka ek percent gold ke ek percent jaisa nahi hota — uski normal daily chaal kaafi badi hoti hai. Dono mein ek jaisa lot count rakhna do bilkul alag risk lena hai, chahe rupee value paas paas dikhe.
