NEWS AUR PRICE: KHABAR AANE TAK AKSAR DER HO CHUKI HOTI HAI

Discounting kaise hoti hai, aur good news par gir jaana kyun normal hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Advisory & Research

Achhi khabar par stock gir jaata hai aur log confuse ho jaate hain. Iska jawaab market ke ek simple vyavhaar mein hai — bhaav khabar ka nahi, ummeed aur khabar ke antar ka reaction deta hai.

DISCOUNTING KA MATLAB

Jab tak koi khabar aati hai, uska bada hissa pehle hi bhaav mein aa chuka hota hai — kyunki log uski ummeed par pehle hi position le lete hain.

Isliye asli reaction is baat par hota hai ki actual result ummeed se behtar tha ya kharaab. Achha result bhi girawat de sakta hai agar ummeed usse zyada thi.

ISKA PRACTICAL ROOP

Ye baatein isi vyavhaar se nikalti hain:
• Event se pehle position lena ek alag risk hai — wahan aap ummeed ke against khel rahe ho.
• Result ke din volatility badhti hai, isliye option premium bhi pehle se mehnga hota hai.
• Headline padh kar li gayi position aksar sabse bure bhaav par lagti hai.
• Ek hi khabar par do stocks ka reaction ulta ho sakta hai, positioning ki wajah se.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Agar koi baat aapko news app par dikh rahi hai to wo har trading desk par pehle se hai. Us information par edge nahi hota — edge us par hota hai ki aap us information ko kaise interpret karte ho, aur kitna intezaar kar sakte ho.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
