COMMODITY DELIVERY: EXPIRY PAR ASLI MAAL

Delivery contracts, warehouse receipts, aur tender period

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 01, 2026 · Derivatives & Commodities

Kuch commodity contracts expiry par asli maal ki delivery maangte hain. Ye retail participant ke liye lagbhag kabhi maqsad nahi hota, par uske rules jaanna zaroori hai.

PROCESS KAISA HAI

Expiry se pehle ek tender period hota hai jisme delivery intention di jaati hai. Uske baad delivery exchange approved warehouses ke through hoti hai.

Maal ka proof ek warehouse receipt hota hai, jo demat form mein rakha ja sakta hai. Uske saath quality assaying aur storage charges bhi jude hote hain.

RETAIL KE LIYE MATLAB

Ye baatein practical hain:
• Delivery nahi chahiye to position tender period se pehle band karni hoti hai.
• Expiry ke paas margin badh jaati hai, jaise stock derivatives mein hoti hai.
• Kuch contracts cash settled hote hain — ye contract specification mein likha hota hai.
• Delivery lene par storage aur insurance ka kharcha aapka hota hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Har commodity contract lene se pehle ek line check karna kaafi hai — ye cash settled hai ya compulsory delivery. Ye ek line poore trade ke exit plan ko tay kar deti hai, aur wo galat samajhna sabse mehnga surprise banta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
