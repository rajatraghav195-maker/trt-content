AGRI COMMODITIES: SEASON KA SEEDHA ASAR

Sowing, harvest aur monsoon ka bhaav se rishta

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Derivatives & Commodities

Agricultural commodities ka bhaav ek calendar par chalta hai jo baaki markets mein nahi hota. Sowing, growing aur harvest — teen daur, aur teenon mein bhaav ka behaviour alag.

SEASON KA CYCLE

Harvest ke aas paas supply sabse zyada hoti hai, isliye bhaav aksar dabaav mein rehta hai. Uske baad stock kam hone lagta hai aur bhaav dheere upar aata hai.

Monsoon aur weather is poore cycle ka sabse bada variable hain, aur wo forecast par nahi, actual par asar daalte hain.

ISKI APNI PROBLEMS

Agri contracts ke apne practical issues hain:
• Liquidity metals aur energy se kaafi kam hoti hai.
• Government policy — export ban, import duty, MSP — bhaav ko raaton raat badal sakti hai.
• Quality specification aur delivery centre bahut specific hote hain.
• Kuch contracts par regulatory restrictions samay samay par lagti hain.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Seasonality ek tendency hai, ek rule nahi — har saal wo dohrayi nahi jaati. Aur patli liquidity mein ek galat position se nikalna sabse mushkil hota hai, isliye yahan size ka faisla view se pehle aana chahiye.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
