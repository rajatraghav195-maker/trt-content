INSIDER TRADING: RETAIL KE LIYE ISKA MATLAB KYA HAI

Unpublished price sensitive information ka daayra, aur wo aapko kaise chhoo sakta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 02, 2026 · Compliance & Regulation

Insider trading ko log sirf badi companies ke top logon ki samasya samajhte hain. Uski definition usse kaafi chaudi hai, aur ek aam employee ya uska rishtedaar bhi uske daayre mein aa sakta hai.

DAAYRA KITNA CHAUDA HAI

Kanoon unpublished price sensitive information ki baat karta hai — aisi jaankari jo public nahi hai aur jo public hone par bhaav hila sakti hai. Results, badi deal, management change, ye sab isme aate hain.

Aur "insider" sirf director nahi hota. Jise bhi wo jaankari mili hai, wo us jaankari ke saath us stock mein trade nahi kar sakta.

PRACTICAL SITUATIONS

Ye scenarios asli mein aate hain:
• Apni hi company ke results se pehle uske share mein trade karna.
• Ghar ya dost se mili andar ki baat par trade karna.
• Vendor ya consultant ke roop mein mili jaankari par trade karna.
• Wo jaankari kisi aur ko de dena taaki wo trade kare.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Listed company mein kaam karne walon ke liye company ka apna trading window aur pre-clearance process hota hai — usko follow karna sirf formality nahi hai. Aur ek simple test hamesha kaam karta hai: agar ye baat kal akhbaar mein nahi hai aur aap uspar trade kar rahe ho, to ruk jao.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
