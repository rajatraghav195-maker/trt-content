COMPLIANCE: SEBI RULEBOOK KE SHABD

Depository, clearing, AML, surveillance, social stock exchange — market ki plumbing ki bhasha

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Compliance & Regulation

Har trade ke peechhe ek rulebook hai jo aapko kabhi dikhta nahi — jab tak kuch galat na ho jaye. Ye shabd usi rulebook ke hain: depository operations, clearing aur settlement, intermediary compliance, anti-money-laundering aur social stock exchange. Source hain NISM ke Series III, VI, VII aur XXIV workbooks.
