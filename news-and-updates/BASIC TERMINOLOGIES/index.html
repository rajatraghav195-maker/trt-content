Basic Terminologies

Every Basic Terminologies article on The Retail Trader — Indian market analysis, reports and trading education.

  • MARKET BASICS: BAZAAR KI ABC — Share, index, demat, IPO, circuit — har naye trader ke pehle 50 shabd, Hinglish mein
  • PRICE ACTION: CANDLE KI ZUBAAN — Candle, wick, gap, breakout, fakeout — chart bina kisi indicator ke kya bolta hai
  • MARKET STRUCTURE: LEVELS, ZONES AUR SWINGS — Support, resistance, higher high, BOS, supply-demand — chart ka skeleton
  • INDICATORS: FORMULA WALE TOOLS — Moving average, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, VWAP, Fibonacci — kaun kya batata hai aur kab jhooth bolta hai
  • ORDER FLOW: ORDER SE EXECUTION TAK — Bid-ask, limit vs market, SL-M, slippage, open interest — button dabane se pehle ye padho
  • DERIVATIVES: FUTURES & OPTIONS KI DICTIONARY — Lot, expiry, strike, premium, Greeks, IV — F&O ka har zaroori shabd, seedhi bhasha mein
  • RISK MANAGEMENT: BACHNE KI DICTIONARY — Stop loss, R-multiple, position size, drawdown, expectancy — capital bachane wale shabd
  • TRADING PSYCHOLOGY: DIMAAG KE SHABD — FOMO, revenge trading, bias, tilt, discipline — wo galtiyan jinke naam hote hain
  • MUTUAL FUNDS: SCHEME KI ZUBAAN — NAV, TER, exit load, folio, SIF — NISM ke mutual fund workbooks ki poori dictionary
  • DERIVATIVES DESK: CONTRACT KI DICTIONARY — Futures, options, currency, interest rate aur commodity — NISM ke derivatives workbooks ke terms
  • ADVISORY & RESEARCH: SALAH KI VOCABULARY — Risk profiling, asset allocation, valuation, ratios — investment adviser aur research analyst ke terms
  • COMPLIANCE: SEBI RULEBOOK KE SHABD — Depository, clearing, AML, surveillance, social stock exchange — market ki plumbing ki bhasha
  • ALTERNATES: AIF, PE AUR PMS KA LEXICON — Commitment, drawdown, carry, hurdle rate, clawback — private capital ki poori vocabulary
  • RETIREMENT & INSURANCE: BAAD KI PLANNING — Pension, annuity, EPF, NPS, life cover, estate planning — lambi race ke shabd
  • INVESTOR PROTECTION FUND: BROKER DEFAULT PAR KYA HOTA HAI — IPF ka daayra, aur wo kis nuksaan ko cover nahi karta
  • LOT SIZE AUR CONTRACT VALUE: F&O KA ASLI SIZE — Premium chhota dikhne aur exposure bada hone ka farq
  • NFO: NAYA FUND OFFER — 10 RUPEE WALI GALATFEHMI — Naye fund ki NAV sasti nahi hoti, aur usme kya dekhna chahiye
  • AUM: FUND KA SIZE KAB SAMASYA BAN JAATA HAI — Bada corpus kis category mein faayda hai aur kis mein bojh
  • CONTRACT NOTE: HAR TRADE KA ASLI BILL — Brokerage, STT, stamp duty aur baaki charges kahan dikhte hain
  • DELISTING: JAB STOCK EXCHANGE SE HI HAT JAATA HAI — Voluntary aur compulsory delisting, reverse book building, aur shareholder ke options
  • GREY MARKET PREMIUM: WO NUMBER JISKA KOI EXCHANGE NAHI HAI — GMP kahan se aata hai, kya nahi batata, aur uspar bharosa karne ka kharcha
  • REVIEW KA SCHEDULE: KAB DEKHNA HAI, YE PEHLE SE TAY KARO — Har din portfolio dekhne ka nuksaan, aur ek fixed cycle ka faayda
  • PRE-OPEN SESSION: 9:00 SE 9:15 KE BEECH KYA HOTA HAI — Opening price kaise banta hai, aur us window mein kya kar sakte ho
  • ROLLOVER COST: AGLE MAHINE JAANE KA ASLI KHARCHA — Spread, cost of carry aur roll karte waqt kya dekhna chahiye
  • STEP-UP SIP: HAR SAAL THODA BADHTA HUA NIVESH — Income badhne ke saath contribution badhana, aur uska lamba asar
  • TICK SIZE AUR PRICE BAND: BHAAV KIS SEEDHI PAR CHALTA HAI — Minimum price movement, freeze quantity aur order rules ki buniyad
  • ALGO TRADING: RETAIL KE LIYE NIYAM KYA KEHTE HAIN — API, automation aur approval — kya allowed hai aur kya nahi
  • EK TRADE PAR ASLI KHARCHA KITNA HAI — Brokerage se aage — poore round trip ka hisaab kaise nikaalein
  • COMMODITIES PORTFOLIO MEIN: DIVERSIFIER YA DISTRACTION — Equity se alag behaviour, aur allocation ka imaandaar sawaal
  • CURRENCY RISK: VIDESHI NIVESH MEIN EK EXTRA VARIABLE — Rupee ki chaal se foreign investment ka return kaise badalta hai
  • DELIVERY PERCENTAGE: KITNA VOLUME ASLI MEIN KHAREEDA GAYA — Traded volume aur delivery volume ka farq, aur uski seemayein
  • INDICATOR KA LAG AUR SETTINGS KA JAAL — Har indicator price se hi banta hai — iska matlab kya hai
  • LOCK-IN: PAISA BAANDHNE KA DAAM KYA HONA CHAHIYE — Illiquidity premium ka concept, aur wo kab jaayaz hai
  • NEWS AUR PRICE: KHABAR AANE TAK AKSAR DER HO CHUKI HOTI HAI — Discounting kaise hoti hai, aur good news par gir jaana kyun normal hai
  • NOMINATION AUR WILL: PARIVAAR KE LIYE SABSE ZAROORI PAPERWORK — Transmission ka process, aur ek simple list jo sab kuch aasan bana deti hai
  • ROUND NUMBERS: BHAAV WAHIN KYUN RUKTA HAI — Psychological levels ka mechanism, aur unka istemal
  • TICKER, ISIN AUR NAAM: EK STOCK KI TEEN PEHCHAAN — Symbol badal sakta hai, ISIN nahi — aur ye farq kab kaam aata hai
  • AGRI COMMODITIES: SEASON KA SEEDHA ASAR — Sowing, harvest aur monsoon ka bhaav se rishta
  • ANCHOR INVESTOR: IPO SE EK DIN PEHLE KYA HOTA HAI — Anchor book, lock-in period aur uska listing ke baad par asar
  • EK SE ZYADA STRATEGY: PAISA UNME KAISE BAANTEIN — Alag strategies ka aapas mein rishta, aur allocation ka aadhar
  • FUND MANAGER BADAL GAYA: KYA KARNA CHAHIYE — Manager change ka asar, aur turant redeem karne se pehle kya dekhein
  • INTERNATIONAL FUNDS: VIDESHI MARKET MEIN NIVESH — Feeder structure, currency ka asar, aur regulatory limits
  • MUHURAT TRADING: EK GHANTE KA SPECIAL SESSION — Ye session kya hai, aur uska practical matlab kya hai
  • ORDER REJECT KYUN HOTA HAI: AAM WAJAHON KI LIST — Rejection messages ka matlab, aur unme se kaunsi aapki galti hai
  • ORDER BOOK, TRADE BOOK AUR POSITION BOOK: TEEN ALAG SCREEN — Teenon kya dikhate hain, aur kaunsa sach hai
  • SYNTHETIC POSITION: OPTIONS SE FUTURES JAISI POSITION — Put call parity ka practical roop aur wo kyun kaam mein aata hai
  • CLOSING PRICE: DIN KA SABSE ZYADA MAAYNE RAKHNE WALA NUMBER — Intraday spike aur close mein farq, aur settlement ka rishta
  • DIVERGENCE: BHAAV AUR INDICATOR KA ALAG ALAG KEHNA — Regular aur hidden divergence, aur unki timing ki problem
  • PEHLE SURAKSHA, PHIR NIVESH: KRAM KYUN MAAYNE RAKHTA HAI — Emergency fund aur insurance ke bina investing kyun kamzor hai
  • GOLD ETF, DIGITAL GOLD AUR PHYSICAL GOLD: TEENON KA FARQ — Custody, charges aur liquidity ke hisaab se teenon ka hisaab
  • GUARANTEED RETURN: WO EK SHABD JO POORI BAAT KHATAM KAR DETA HAI — Market linked product mein guarantee kyun sambhav nahi, aur baaki red flags
  • HEDGE AUR INSURANCE: DONO KA DAAM ALAG HOTA HAI — Risk hataana, risk transfer karna, aur uske liye kitna dena theek hai
  • IMPACT COST: BADE ORDER KI CHHUPI HUI FEES — Depth kaise padhein, aur ek stock kitna liquid hai ye kaise naapein
  • INDEX REBALANCING: JAB STOCK INDEX MEIN AATA YA JAATA HAI — Inclusion aur exclusion ka mechanism, aur us din ka volume
  • RATIO SPREAD: JAHAN EK EXTRA LEG POORA RISK BADAL DETA HAI — Unbalanced position ka ganit aur wo hissa jahan loss capped nahi rehta
  • RISKOMETER: WO METER JO SIRF EK BAAT BATATA HAI — Risk labels ka matlab, aur wo kya nahi batate
  • SCREENER: FILTER SE IDEA TAK KA RAASTA — Screener kya de sakta hai, aur uske output ko research samajhna kyun galat hai
  • BASE METALS: INDUSTRIAL DEMAND KA THERMOMETER — Copper, zinc aur aluminium kya batate hain, aur unka cycle
  • BOOK BUILDING AUR FIXED PRICE ISSUE MEIN FARQ — Price band, cut-off price aur discovery ka process
  • FUND OF FUNDS: FUND KE ANDAR FUND — Do layer ke kharche, aur wo kab bhi jaayaz hote hain
  • INDEX KAISE BANTA HAI: FREE FLOAT WEIGHTAGE KA MATLAB — Nifty aur Sensex mein har company ka weight kaise tay hota hai
  • OPENING RANGE: DIN KE PEHLE KUCH MINUTE — Shuruaati range kya batati hai, aur uska galat upyog kya hai
  • OPPORTUNITY COST: JO PAISA YAHAN HAI WO WAHAN NAHI HAI — Ek phansi hui position ka asli kharcha sirf uska loss nahi hai
  • ORDER MODIFY KAREIN YA CANCEL KARKE NAYA LAGAYEIN — Queue priority ka rishta, aur kaunsa kab behtar hai
  • RIGHTS ENTITLEMENT: WO CHEEZ JO KHUD BIK SAKTI HAI — RE kya hai, wo demat mein kaise aati hai, aur uski expiry
  • STOCHASTIC: CLOSE APNI RANGE MEIN KAHAN BAND HUA — Oscillator ka logic, aur overbought ka wahi purana jhooth
  • ASBA AUR UPI MANDATE: IPO KA PAISA BLOCK KAISE HOTA HAI — Application ke paise ka pura raasta, aur mandate fail hone ki aam wajahein
  • CALENDAR SPREAD: DO ALAG EXPIRY KE BEECH KA TRADE — Near aur far month ka farq, aur ye position kis cheez par paisa banati hai
  • ADVISORY FEES KE MODEL: KAUN KIS BAAT KA PAISA LE RAHA HAI — Fee only, commission aur performance based — teenon ke incentives alag hain
  • INFLATION AUR LAMBI HORIZON: EK DHEEMA LEKIN PAKKA RISK — Fixed income par tikne wala plan tees saal mein kya ho jaata hai
  • SEBI REGISTERED HAI YA NAHI: KHUD KAISE VERIFY KAREIN — Registration number ka matlab, aur wo kya guarantee karta hai aur kya nahi
  • SEQUENCE RISK: KAB NUKSAAN HUA, YE KITNA MAAYNE RAKHTA HAI — Ek jaisa average return, do bilkul alag anjaam
  • SLIPPAGE: SOCHA HUA BHAAV AUR MILA HUA BHAAV — Wo chhota farq jo backtest ko live results se alag kar deta hai
  • STRUCTURED PRODUCT: CAPITAL PROTECTION KA ASLI GANIT — Debt aur derivative ka combination, aur uski chhupi hui laagat
  • TRACKING ERROR: INDEX FUND INDEX SE PEECHE KYUN REHTA HAI — Tracking error aur tracking difference ka farq, aur unki wajahein
  • BASKET ORDER: KAI ORDERS EK SAATH — Multi leg strategies aur portfolio entry ke liye ek practical tool
  • CASH RAKHNA BHI EK FAISLA HAI — Fully invested rehne ka dabaav, aur cash ka option value
  • COMMODITY MARKET KE LAMBE HOURS: FAAYDA AUR KHATRA — Raat tak chalne wale session ka asar position aur behaviour dono par
  • ELSS: TAX BENEFIT KE SAATH EQUITY, AUR EK LOCK-IN — Har installment ka apna lock-in, aur tax season ki jaldbaazi
  • MOMENTUM AUR EXHAUSTION: TEZ CHAAL KE DO ALAG ANT — Acceleration kab strength hai aur kab thakawat
  • OBV: VOLUME KO EK CHALTI HUI LINE BANANA — On Balance Volume ka logic, aur divergence ka uska version
  • RETIREMENT CORPUS: KITNA CHAHIYE, YE NIKALTE KAISE HAIN — Aaj ka kharcha, inflation, aur withdrawal rate ka simple dhaancha
  • SECTOR ROTATION: PAISA EK JAGAH SE DOOSRI JAGAH JAATA HAI — Rotation ka mechanism, aur usse kya samajh aata hai aur kya nahi
  • TRADING SUSPENSION: JAB STOCK MEIN TRADE HI NAHI HO RAHA — Suspension ki wajahein, aur shareholder ke paas kya option hota hai
  • ARBITRAGE FUND: EQUITY MEIN HOKAR BHI DIRECTION SE AAZAD — Cash aur futures ke farq se return, aur wo kab kam ho jaata hai
  • DISCLOSURE PADHNA: REPORT KA SABSE CHHOTA AUR SABSE ZAROORI HISSA — Interest, holding aur compensation ke disclosures kya batate hain
  • EXERCISE AUR ASSIGNMENT: OPTION KA ANT KAISE HOTA HAI — Buyer ka haq, seller ki zimmedari, aur automatic exercise ka niyam
  • FUND CATEGORIES: LARGE, MID, SMALL AUR FLEXI KA MATLAB — SEBI ki categorisation ke baad naam ka matlab kya fix ho gaya
  • INSIDER TRADING: RETAIL KE LIYE ISKA MATLAB KYA HAI — Unpublished price sensitive information ka daayra, aur wo aapko kaise chhoo sakta hai
  • IPO ALLOTMENT: LOT KIS AADHAR PAR MILTA HAI — Oversubscription, lottery aur retail category ka mechanism
  • SHORT DELIVERY AUR AUCTION: JAB SHARE DELIVER HI NAHI HOTA — Auction settlement ka mechanism aur wo penalty jo kharidne wale ko nahi, bechne wale ko lagti hai
  • TAIL RISK: WO EVENT JO "KABHI NAHI HOTA" AUR HO JAATA HAI — Normal distribution ka jhooth, aur bade moves ki asli frequency
  • UNLISTED SHARES: LISTING SE PEHLE KHAREEDNE KA GANIT — Price discovery, liquidity aur lock-in — teenon yahan alag hote hain
  • ADX: TREND HAI YA NAHI, YE BATANE WALA INDICATOR — Direction nahi, strength — aur yahi uska sabse bada upyog hai
  • COMMODITY DELIVERY: EXPIRY PAR ASLI MAAL — Delivery contracts, warehouse receipts, aur tender period
  • CORPORATE FD: ZYADA INTEREST KA MATLAB ZYADA RISK — Bank FD se antar, credit rating ka role, aur default ka scenario
  • HIGHER TIMEFRAME BIAS: BADA CHART CHHOTE PAR RAAJ KARTA HAI — Ek hi stock ke do alag trend, aur unme priority kaise tay karein
  • IOC AUR DAY ORDER: ORDER KITNI DER ZINDA REHTA HAI — Validity ka matlab, aur immediate or cancel kab kaam aata hai
  • LIQUIDITY RISK: ENTRY AASAN, EXIT MUSHKIL — Chhote stocks mein bechne ka risk, aur wo bure din mein kyun badhta hai
  • MERGER AUR DEMERGER: AAPKI HOLDING KA KYA HOTA HAI — Swap ratio, record date, aur naye shares ka credit
  • PORTFOLIO HEAT: SAARE OPEN TRADES KA JODA HUA RISK — Ek trade ka risk chhota hai, par das trades ka jod kitna hai
  • PPF: LAMBI HORIZON WALA SARKARI OPTION — Tenure, interest calculation, aur partial withdrawal ke niyam
  • PULLBACK AUR REVERSAL: WOHI GIRAWAT, DO ALAG MATLAB — Trend ke andar ka rest aur trend ka khatm hona — farq kaise dekhein
  • BALANCED ADVANTAGE: EQUITY AUR DEBT KE BEECH AUTOMATIC SWITCH — Dynamic asset allocation ka model, aur uski limitations
  • BUYBACK: COMPANY APNE HI SHARE KYUN KHAREEDTI HAI — Tender route aur open market route ka farq, aur acceptance ratio ka asli ganit
  • ICEBERG ORDER: BADA ORDER CHHOTE TUKDON MEIN — Disclosed quantity ka mechanism aur wo kis samasya ko hal karta hai
  • MODEL PORTFOLIO: EK LIST JO SABKE LIYE EK JAISI HAI — Model portfolio ka faayda, aur wo aapka portfolio kyun nahi hai
  • NOMINEE: WO EK FIELD JISE LOG KHAALI CHHOD DETE HAIN — Nomination ka kaam, aur nominee owner nahi hota — ye farq kyun bada hai
  • EXPIRY KE PAAS PREMIUM KA VYAVHAAR — Time value ka aakhri hafta, aur out of the money option ka anjaam
  • PARTIAL FILL: ORDER AADHA BHARA — Average price kaise banta hai, aur adhoori position ka kya karein
  • RISK BUDGET: PAISA NAHI, RISK BAANTNA — Allocation ko capital ke hisaab se nahi, risk ke hisaab se sochna
  • SWP AUR STP: PAISA NIKALNE AUR SHIFT KARNE KE TARIKE — Systematic withdrawal aur transfer ka mechanism, aur kaunsa kis kaam ka
  • 52-WEEK HIGH / LOW — Pichhle ek saal ka sabse ooncha aur sabse neecha price; trend aur extremes samajhne ka sabse quick reference.
  • ABSOLUTE RETURN — Total percentage gain ya loss, bina time ke hisaab ke.
  • ABSORPTION — Ek level par lagataar selling aane ke bawajood price ka na girna.
  • ACCUMULATION — Lambi range jismein bade players chupchap kharid rahe hote hain.
  • ACCUMULATION / DISTRIBUTION LINE — OBV ka cousin, jo candle ke andar close ki position ko bhi weight deta hai.
  • ADDITIONAL / AD-HOC MARGIN — Exchange kisi khaas scrip par extra margin laga sakta hai, aksar volatility ya surveillance ki wajah se.
  • ADX / DMI — Trend ki strength naapta hai (direction nahi).
  • ALL-TIME HIGH (ATH) / ALL-TIME LOW (ATL) — Ab tak ka sabse ooncha aur sabse neecha price.
  • ALPHA — Benchmark se upar ka extra return.
  • AMO (AFTER MARKET ORDER) — Market band hone ke baad lagaya order, jo agle din open par queue mein jata hai.
  • ANALYSIS PARALYSIS — Itna analysis ki trade li hi na jaye.
  • ANCHORED VWAP — VWAP jo kisi khaas event se shuru kiya jaye, jaise result day ya swing low.
  • ANCHORING — Kisi ek purane price se chipke rehna, jaise wahi sahi value ho.
  • AROON — Naya high ya naya low kitne time pehle bana, ye naap kar trend ki freshness batata hai.
  • ASK / OFFER — Wo lowest price jispe koi abhi bechne ko taiyaar hai.
  • ASM / GSM — Surveillance frameworks jinke andar aaye stocks par extra margin aur trading restrictions lag jate hain.
  • ATM (AT THE MONEY) — Strike lagbhag spot ke barabar.
  • ATR (AVERAGE TRUE RANGE) — Ek candle ka average movement, points mein.
  • ATR-BASED SL — Stock ki apni average movement ka multiple.
  • AUCTION SESSION — Wahi settlement auction, jahan short delivery poori ki jati hai.
  • AUTO FIT — Visible candles ke hisaab se scale apne aap adjust karna.
  • AUTO SQUARE-OFF — Intraday position agar aapne band nahi ki, to broker cut-off time par khud band kar deta hai, aur uska charge bhi lagta hai.
  • AVERAGE HOLDING PERIOD — Ek trade kitne der rakha jata hai.
  • AVERAGE WIN / AVERAGE LOSS — Aam jeet aur aam haar ka size.
  • AVERAGING — Position mein aur quantity jodna.
  • AVERAGING DOWN — Losing position mein aur quantity daalna, is ummeed mein ki average sudhar jayega.
  • AWESOME OSCILLATOR — Do simple moving averages ka farq, histogram ke roop mein.
  • BACKTESTING — Purane data par rules test karna.
  • BANK NIFTY — Sirf banking stocks ka index.
  • BAR CHART (OHLC) — Har period ek vertical bar, jiske left par open aur right par close ka tick hota hai.
  • BAR REPLAY — History ko candle-by-candle aage chala kar practice karna, aage ka result dekhe bina.
  • BASIS — Futures price aur spot price ka farq.
  • BASKET ORDER — Kai orders ek saath ready karke ek click mein bhejna, jaise multi-leg option strategy.
  • BEAR MARKET — Lambi girawat, aam taur par top se 20% se zyada.
  • BEAR PUT SPREAD — Uska ulta, girawat ke liye.
  • BEARISH CANDLE — Close, open se neeche.
  • BEARISH DIVERGENCE — Price naya high bana raha hai par RSI peechhe reh gaya.
  • BEARISH ENGULFING — Ek badi red candle jo pichhli green candle ko poora dhak leti hai.
  • BEGINNER'S LUCK — Shuruaat mein bina system ke mila profit, jo confidence to deta hai par sabak nahi.
  • BENCHMARK COMPARISON — Apna return Nifty jaise index se compare karna.
  • BETA — Ek stock index ke against kitna hilta hai.
  • BID — Wo highest price jispe koi abhi kharidne ko taiyaar hai.
  • BID-ASK IMBALANCE — Ek taraf quantity ka bahut zyada hona, jise short-term pressure ka hint maana jata hai.
  • BID-ASK SPREAD — Dono ka farq.
  • BLACK SWAN — Bahut rare, bahut bada aur unexpected event.
  • BLUE CHIP — Badi, purani, financially strong company jiska accha track record poore business cycle mein raha ho.
  • BODY — Open aur close ke beech ka mota hissa.
  • BOLLINGER BANDS — Ek 20-period moving average ke upar-neeche 2 standard deviation ke bands.
  • BONUS ISSUE — Free extra shares.
  • BOOK VALUE — Company ki assets minus liabilities, per share.
  • BOREDOM TOLERANCE — Kuch na karna bhi ek position hai.
  • BORROWED CAPITAL — Udhaar ya loan ka paisa.
  • BOS (BREAK OF STRUCTURE) — Trend ki direction mein ek naya swing point toot-na, matlab trend abhi zinda hai.
  • BRACKET ORDER — Ek hi jagah entry, target aur stop loss — teeno saath.
  • BREAK AND CLOSE VS WICK BREAK — Level ke paar close hona aur sirf wick ka nikal jaana bilkul alag baatein hain.
  • BREAKDOWN — Wahi cheez neeche ki taraf: support tootna.
  • BREAKEVEN — Wo underlying price jahan option buyer ka na profit na loss: call mein strike + premium, put mein strike - premium.
  • BREAKEVEN STOP — Stop ko entry price par le aana, jisse trade risk-free ho jata hai.
  • BREAKOUT — Price ek important level ke upar nikal jaana, aam taur par volume ke saath.
  • BROKER — SEBI-registered member jiske through aapka order exchange tak jata hai; badle mein wo brokerage charge karta hai.
  • BROKERAGE — Broker ka charge, jo delivery aur intraday mein alag hota hai.
  • BSE — Bombay Stock Exchange, jiska flagship index Sensex hai; ye Asia ka sabse purana exchange hai.
  • BULK DEAL / BLOCK DEAL — Exchange par report hone wale bade sauda, jinse pata chalta hai ki koi bada player enter ya exit kar raha hai.
  • BULL CALL SPREAD — Ek call kharidna aur upar wala call bechna.
  • BULL MARKET — Lambe samay tak upar chalta market, jahan har girawat khareedi jati hai.
  • BULLISH CANDLE — Close, open se upar.
  • BULLISH DIVERGENCE — Price naya low bana raha hai par RSI naya low nahi bana raha.
  • BULLISH ENGULFING — Ek badi green candle jo pichhli red candle ko poora dhak leti hai.
  • BUYBACK — Company apne hi shares wapas kharidti hai, aam taur par market price se upar.
  • CAGR — Saal ke hisaab se compounded return, jo alag-alag time period ki tulna possible banata hai.
  • CALENDAR SPREAD — Alag expiry wale do options ka combination, jo time decay ke farq par chalta hai.
  • CALL AUCTION — Illiquid stocks aur pre-open ke liye price discovery ka special mechanism.
  • CALL OPTION (CE) — Upar jaane par faayda dene wala option.
  • CANDLESTICK — Ek time period ka price summary: open, high, low, close.
  • CANDLESTICK CHART — Wahi data, par body aur wick ke saath.
  • CAPITAL ALLOCATION — Total capital ka kitna hissa kis strategy ya segment mein lagega.
  • CARRY FORWARD — Position ko agle din tak rakhna, jiske liye poora overnight margin chahiye.
  • CAS (CONSOLIDATED ACCOUNT STATEMENT) — NSDL/CDSL ka monthly statement jo saari holdings ek jagah dikhata hai.
  • CASH SEGMENT VS DERIVATIVE SEGMENT — Cash mein actual share kharide jate hain, derivative mein contract.
  • CASH SETTLEMENT VS PHYSICAL SETTLEMENT — Index F&O cash mein settle hota hai; stock F&O expiry tak rakhne par actual delivery ban sakti hai, isliye expiry par stock positions ko dhyan se handle karn…
  • CCI (COMMODITY CHANNEL INDEX) — Price ka apne average se deviation naapta hai; naam ke bawajood har asset par use hota hai.
  • CHANGE IN OI — Aaj kitne naye contracts bane ya khatam hue.
  • CHANNEL — Do parallel trendlines ke beech chalta price.
  • CHASING — Har tez chalte stock ke peechhe bhaagna, bina level ya plan ke.
  • CHECKLIST — Entry se pehle poochhe jane wale fix sawaal, taaki mood decision na le.
  • CHOCH (CHANGE OF CHARACTER) — Pehli baar structure ulta tootna, jo possible trend change ka pehla hint hai.
  • CHOPPY MARKET — Na trend na saaf range; chhote jhatke jo stop loss khate rehte hain.
  • CIRCUIT FREEZE — Upper ya lower circuit par pahunche stock mein ek taraf ka order book poora khaali ho jata hai — theoretically order lag sakta hai, practically execute nahi …
  • CIRCUIT LIMIT — Ek din mein price kitna upar-neeche ja sakta hai uski limit (2%, 5%, 10% ya 20%).
  • CLEAN CHART — Sirf wo do-teen tools rakhna jinka rule aapko zubani yaad ho.
  • CLIMAX — Bahut bada volume aur bahut badi candle, aksar move ke ant mein.
  • CLOSE-ONLY RESTRICTION — Aisi position jismein sirf exit allowed hai, nayi entry nahi.
  • CLOSING PRICE — Din ka official close, jo aam taur par aakhri 30 minute ka weighted average hota hai, sirf last trade nahi.
  • CMF (CHAIKIN MONEY FLOW) — Volume ke saath paisa andar aa raha hai ya bahar ja raha hai, ye naapta hai.
  • CNC (DELIVERY) — Poora paisa lagta hai, share demat mein aate hain, jab tak chaho rakho.
  • COMPARISON / OVERLAY — Do symbols ko ek hi chart par superimpose karna, relative performance dekhne ke liye.
  • COMPARISON TRAP — Doosron ke screenshots se apne result ko naapna, bina unka risk, size ya loss dekhe.
  • COMPOUNDING — Chhote consistent returns ka time ke saath badhna.
