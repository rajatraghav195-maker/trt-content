PRICE ACTION: CANDLE KI ZUBAAN

Candle, wick, gap, breakout, fakeout — chart bina kisi indicator ke kya bolta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 02, 2026 · Price Action

Price action ka matlab hai chart ko waise padhna jaise wo hai — bina indicator ke, sirf candle, level aur move. Har candle ek chhoti si kahani hai: kis price par din khula, kahan tak buyers le gaye, kahan sellers ne pakad liya, aur kis price par ladai khatam hui. Ye rahe uss kahani ke shabd.

CANDLE KA ANATOMY

Ek candle ke andar chaar number hote hain, aur bas:
• Candlestick — Ek time period ka price summary: open, high, low, close. Ek 5-minute candle matlab 5 minute ki poori kahani.
• OHLC — Open (shuruaat), High (sabse ooncha), Low (sabse neecha), Close (ant). Har candle yahi chaar number dikhati hai.
• Body — Open aur close ke beech ka mota hissa. Badi body matlab ek taraf ka control strong tha.
• Wick / Shadow — Body ke upar-neeche ki patli line, jo high aur low tak jati hai. Lambi wick matlab price wahan gaya lekin ruk nahi paya.
• Bullish Candle — Close, open se upar. Screen par aam taur par green.
• Bearish Candle — Close, open se neeche. Aam taur par red.
• Timeframe — Ek candle kitne time ki hai: 1 min, 5 min, 15 min, 1 hour, daily, weekly.
• Range — Candle ka high minus low. Badi range wale din volatility zyada hoti hai.
• Tick — Price ka sabse chhota possible change; ek candle inhi chhote moves se banti hai.

SINGLE AUR DOUBLE CANDLE PATTERNS

Kuch shapes baar-baar dikhte hain, isliye unke naam pad gaye. Yaad rakho: pattern sirf ek hint hai, guarantee nahi.
• Doji — Open aur close lagbhag barabar. Matlab buyers aur sellers dono thak gaye — indecision.
• Hammer — Chhoti body upar, lambi wick neeche. Sellers ne neeche le jaane ki koshish ki, buyers wapas le aaye.
• Shooting Star — Ulta hammer: chhoti body neeche, lambi wick upar. Upar jaakar reject hua.
• Marubozu — Lagbhag poori body, wick na ke barabar. Ek taraf ka clean control.
• Spinning Top — Chhoti body, dono taraf wick. Range aur confusion ka signal.
• Bullish Engulfing — Ek badi green candle jo pichhli red candle ko poora dhak leti hai.
• Bearish Engulfing — Ek badi red candle jo pichhli green candle ko poora dhak leti hai.
• Harami — Chhoti candle jo pichhli badi candle ke andar hi rehti hai. Momentum slow ho raha hai.
• Tweezer Top / Bottom — Do candles jinke high (ya low) lagbhag same. Ek level baar-baar reject ho raha hai.

BADE CHART PATTERNS

Kai candles milkar shapes banate hain jinke apne naam hain:
• Double Top — Do baar same level tak jaakar wapas girna; M jaisa dikhta hai.
• Double Bottom — Do baar same level tak girkar wapas uthna; W jaisa dikhta hai.
• Head and Shoulders — Teen peaks jahan beech wala sabse ooncha ho, aur neeche ek neckline banti hai.
• Triangle — Range dheere-dheere sikudti hai (ascending, descending ya symmetrical). Ek badi move ki taiyari.
• Flag / Pennant — Tez move ke baad ek chhota sa sideways rest, phir wahi direction continue.
• Wedge — Dono trendlines ek hi taraf jhuki hui, range chhoti hoti hui.
• Cup and Handle — Ek round bottom jiske baad ek chhota pullback — lambi consolidation ke baad breakout ka shape.
• Rectangle / Range — Price do saaf levels ke beech aage-peeche ghoomta rehta hai.

MOVE KE SHABD

Ab wo shabd jo har trading conversation mein aate hain — inhe galat samajhna sabse mehnga padta hai:
• Trend — Price ki overall direction. Upar jaa raha hai to uptrend, neeche to downtrend.
• Sideways / Range — Na upar na neeche; price ek band ke andar ghoom raha hai.
• Pullback — Trend ke against ek chhota temporary move. Uptrend mein thoda girna normal hai.
• Retracement — Wahi pullback, aam taur par percentage mein naapa gaya (jaise move ka 38% wapas).
• Reversal — Trend ka actually palat jaana, sirf ruk jaana nahi.
• Breakout — Price ek important level ke upar nikal jaana, aam taur par volume ke saath.
• Breakdown — Wahi cheez neeche ki taraf: support tootna.
• Fakeout / False Breakout — Level toota, sabne entry li, aur price turant wapas andar aa gaya. Retail ka sabse common trap.
• Retest — Breakout ke baad price wapas usi level par aakar check karta hai ki wo ab support ban gaya hai ya nahi.
• Gap Up / Gap Down — Aaj ka open kal ke close se kaafi upar ya neeche. Aksar news ya global cues ki wajah se.
• Gap Fill — Price wapas aakar us khaali gap wale area ko cover kar leta hai.
• Consolidation — Move ke baad ka rest period, jahan price energy jama karta hai.
• Momentum — Move ki speed. Tez badhta price momentum strong, sust move momentum weak.
• Whipsaw — Price ka dono taraf jhatke dena, jisme stop loss dono taraf hit ho jate hain.
• Impulse vs Correction — Impulse trend ki direction ka tez move; correction uske against ka slow move.
• Confirmation — Wo condition jo aap trade lene se pehle maangte ho, jaise candle ka level ke upar close hona.

CANDLE PATTERNS: PART 2

Kuch patterns do se teen candles milkar banate hain. Inhe alag se yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi — inka logic ek hi hai: ek taraf ka control chhoot raha hai.
• Morning Star — Girawat, phir ek chhoti indecision candle, phir ek strong green candle. Teen candle ka bottom formation.
• Evening Star — Uska ulta, top par banne wala teen-candle formation.
• Three White Soldiers — Lagataar teen strong green candles, har ek pichhli se upar band.
• Three Black Crows — Lagataar teen strong red candles. Trend change ka aggressive version.
• Piercing Pattern — Red candle ke baad ek green candle jo uske aadhe se upar band ho.
• Dark Cloud Cover — Uska ulta: green candle ke baad red candle jo uske aadhe se neeche band ho.
• Hanging Man — Hammer jaisi shape, par uptrend ke top par. Same shape, alag location, alag matlab.
• Dragonfly Doji — Lambi neeche ki wick, open aur close upar. Neeche se strong rejection.
• Gravestone Doji — Lambi upar ki wick, open aur close neeche.
• Inside Bar — Poori candle pichhli candle ki range ke andar. Market ruk kar soch raha hai.
• Outside Bar — Poori candle pichhli candle ki range ke bahar dono taraf. Volatility phaili.
• Pin Bar — Ek lambi wick wali candle jo kisi level ko chhoo kar reject ho gayi.
• Rising / Falling Three Methods — Bade move ke beech teen chhoti opposite candles, aur phir wahi trend continue.

CHART KE TYPE AUR SCALE

Ek hi data ko dikhane ke kai tareeke hain, aur har tareeka alag cheez highlight karta hai:
• Line Chart — Sirf closing prices ko jodne wali line. Noise sabse kam, detail bhi sabse kam.
• Bar Chart (OHLC) — Har period ek vertical bar, jiske left par open aur right par close ka tick hota hai.
• Candlestick Chart — Wahi data, par body aur wick ke saath. India mein sabse zyada use hota hai.
• Heikin Ashi — Averaged candles, jo trend ko smooth dikhati hain. Yaad rakho: ispar dikhne wale prices actual traded prices nahi hote.
• Renko — Sirf ek fix price move par nayi brick banti hai, time ka koi role nahi.
• Point and Figure — X aur O ka chart, jo sirf meaningful price moves record karta hai.
• Linear vs Log Scale — Linear mein barabar rupee ka move barabar dikhta hai; log mein barabar percentage ka. Lambi history dekhte waqt log scale hi sahi picture deta hai.
• Price Scale / Time Scale — Chart ke right aur bottom wale axes, jinhe drag karke aap zoom badal sakte ho.
• Auto Fit — Visible candles ke hisaab se scale apne aap adjust karna.
• Bar Replay — History ko candle-by-candle aage chala kar practice karna, aage ka result dekhe bina.
• Comparison / Overlay — Do symbols ko ek hi chart par superimpose karna, relative performance dekhne ke liye.

Price action ka poora khel yahi hai: candle batati hai kya hua, aur uska location batata hai wo important kyun hai. Agla journal usi location ke baare mein hai — market structure.

Ye journal sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi. Har term ko apne chart par khud dekho, tabhi wo yaad rehta hai.
