COMPANY KI KHABAR: PEHLE EXCHANGE PAR AATI HAI

Exchange filings hi primary source kyun hain, aur unhe kaise padhein

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 27, 2026 · Market Basics

Har material jaankari company ko pehle exchange ko deni hoti hai. Yaani jo baat news channel par aa rahi hai, wo uske pehle exchange ki website par likhit roop mein maujood thi.

YAHI PRIMARY SOURCE KYUN HAI

Exchange filing company ka apna, signed, timestamped statement hota hai. News uski interpretation hoti hai, aur interpretation mein context chhoot sakta hai.

Filing mein wo detail hoti hai jo headline mein nahi aati — conditions, timelines aur wo baatein jo deal ko badal sakti hain.

KAUNSI FILINGS DEKHNI CHAHIYE

In categories mein sabse zyada material information hoti hai:
• Financial results aur unke saath ka press release.
• Board meeting outcomes.
• Investor presentations aur earnings call transcripts.
• Material events — orders, acquisitions, management changes, regulatory notices.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Ek stock mein position hai to uske filings ka alert lagana ek chhota kaam hai jo bahut kaam aata hai. Zyadatar bade surprises pehle ek filing mein aate hain — sirf unhe padhne wale kam hote hain.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
