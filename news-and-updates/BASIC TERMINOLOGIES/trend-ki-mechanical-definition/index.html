TREND: EK DEFINITION JISME OPINION KI JAGAH NAHI

Higher high aur higher low ka sequence, aur wo kab toot-ta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 24, 2026 · Market Structure

Trend shabd sabse zyada istemal hota hai aur sabse kam define kiya jaata hai. Uski ek mechanical definition maujood hai, aur wo aapke faislon ko opinion se bahar nikaal deti hai.

DEFINITION KYA HAI

Uptrend wo hai jisme har naya swing high pichle se upar ho aur har naya swing low bhi pichle se upar. Downtrend uska ulta.

Jab tak ye sequence chal raha hai, trend zinda hai — chahe wo aapko slow lage ya thaka hua. Aur jab sequence toota, trend toota, chahe wo aapko strong lage.

ISKE FAAYDE

Ek mechanical definition ye deti hai:
• Har din ek hi jawaab, chahe aapka mood kuch bhi ho.
• Ek clear invalidation point — trend kab galat hua, ye pehle se pata hota hai.
• Alag alag timeframes par alag alag trend ho sakte hain, aur wo contradiction nahi hai.
• Backtest karna sambhav ho jaata hai, kyunki rule likha ja sakta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Har analysis se pehle apna timeframe likh lo. "Trend upar hai" bina timeframe ke ek adhura vaakya hai — daily par uptrend aur hourly par downtrend ek hi samay mein bilkul normal hai.
