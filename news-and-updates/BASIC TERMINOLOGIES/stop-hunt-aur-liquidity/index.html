STOP HUNT: BHAAV WAHIN KYUN JAATA HAI JAHAN AAPKA STOP THA

Liquidity pools ka concept, bina kisi conspiracy ke

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 30, 2026 · Market Structure

Stop lagne ke turant baad bhaav wapas aa jaana itna aam hai ki log ise sazish samajhne lagte hain. Uski wajah bahut simple hai, aur wo aapke apne behaviour se shuru hoti hai.

LIQUIDITY KAHAN HOTI HAI

Bade orders ko bharne ke liye doosri taraf orders chahiye. Sabse zyada orders wahin jama hote hain jahan sabse zyada log stop lagate hain — obvious levels ke thoda paar.

Isliye bhaav ka un jagahon tak jaana ek natural mechanism hai, kisi ek player ka faisla nahi. Wahan liquidity hai, isliye wahan trade hota hai.

ISKE SAATH KAISE JEEYEIN

Isse poora bachaav sambhav nahi, par ye madad karta hai:
• Stop ko obvious level par nahi, uske thoda door rakhna.
• Stop ki jagah structure se tay karna, apne loss ki limit se nahi.
• Agar structure ke hisaab se stop bahut door hai, to position size chhota karna.
• Bahut tight stop ka matlab hai zyada baar bahar nikalna, chahe view sahi ho.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Stop ka kaam aapko galat hone par bahar nikaalna hai, aapko sahi saabit karna nahi. Agar wo baar baar lag raha hai to sawaal stop ka nahi — sawaal ye hai ki entry sahi jagah par ho rahi hai ya nahi.
