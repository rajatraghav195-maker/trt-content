OPPORTUNITY COST: JO PAISA YAHAN HAI WO WAHAN NAHI HAI

Ek phansi hui position ka asli kharcha sirf uska loss nahi hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 05, 2026 · Risk Management

Ek position jo saalon se wahin padi hai, uska nuksaan sirf uska loss nahi hai. Us paise ne is beech kuch aur bhi nahi kiya — aur wo doosra hissa aksar bada hota hai.

HISAAB DO HISSON KA HAI

Pehla hissa dikhta hai — position kitni neeche hai. Doosra hissa nahi dikhta — us paise ne agar kahin aur hota to kya kiya hota.

Isi wajah se ek position ko "bhool jaao, kabhi to wapas aayegi" wali soch ka daam sirf intezaar nahi hai.

ISE FAISLE MEIN KAISE LAYEIN

Ek simple review sawaal kaafi hai:
• Agar ye paisa aaj cash hota, to main ise kahan lagata.
• Kya main wahi jagah chunta jahan wo abhi laga hua hai.
• Is position ko wapas aane mein kitne percent chahiye, aur kitna samay.
• Us samay mein ek simple alternative kya de deta.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Loss book karna galti maanne jaisa lagta hai, isliye tala jaata hai. Par tax ke hisaab se booked loss ka ek upyog bhi hota hai, aur us paise ka dobara kaam par lagna uska asli faayda hai — dono milkar wo faisla utna mehnga nahi rehta jitna lagta hai.
