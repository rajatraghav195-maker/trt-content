GREY MARKET PREMIUM: WO NUMBER JISKA KOI EXCHANGE NAHI HAI

GMP kahan se aata hai, kya nahi batata, aur uspar bharosa karne ka kharcha

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Market Basics

Har IPO ke saath ek number ghoomta hai jise GMP kehte hain, aur bahut log usi ke aadhar par apply ya avoid karte hain. Us number ke peeche koi exchange, koi regulator aur koi verifiable data nahi hota.

YE AATA KAHAN SE HAI

GMP ek unofficial, unregulated over the counter market ka indication hai jahan listing se pehle sauda hota hai. Uska koi central record nahi hota, aur quote dene wale kuch hi log hote hain.

Isi wajah se ek hi din ke do alag source do alag GMP dikha sakte hain, aur kisi ke paas dono ko verify karne ka koi tarika nahi hota.

KYA DHYAAN RAKHNA HAI

GMP ke saath ye baatein tathya hain, opinion nahi:
• Ye enforceable nahi hai — koi contract nahi hai, koi settlement guarantee nahi hai.
• Ye bahut patli demand se banta hai, isliye ise move karna sasta hai.
• Listing ke din ka actual price GMP se bahut door ja sakta hai, dono taraf.
• GMP ke aadhar par leverage lena ek anumaan par udhaar lena hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Agar ek issue tabhi acha lagta hai jab GMP zyada ho, to faisla issue ke baare mein nahi ho raha — wo ek afwaah ke baare mein ho raha hai. Prospectus, financials aur valuation teenon listing ke baad bhi wahi rahenge.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
