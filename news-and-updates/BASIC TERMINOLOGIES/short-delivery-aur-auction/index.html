SHORT DELIVERY AUR AUCTION: JAB SHARE DELIVER HI NAHI HOTA

Auction settlement ka mechanism aur wo penalty jo kharidne wale ko nahi, bechne wale ko lagti hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 02, 2026 · Order Flow

Delivery mein bechne ke baad agar seller ke paas share nahi nikle to exchange us kami ko khud poora karta hai. Us process ko auction kehte hain, aur uska kharcha default karne wale se wasoola jaata hai.

PROCESS KYA HAI

Settlement ke din agar shares short hain to clearing corporation auction market mein wo shares kharidta hai aur kharidne wale ko de deta hai. Kharidne wale ka nuksaan nahi hota — use share mil jaata hai.

Bechne wale se auction price ke hisaab se rakam wasooli jaati hai, aur wo price aksar us din ke market price se kaafi bura hota hai.

YE KAB HOTA HAI

Retail ke liye short delivery in situations mein banti hai:
• BTST mein bech diya aur delivery aayi hi nahi.
• Holding pledge ya kisi aur process mein block thi.
• Galat account se sell order laga diya gaya.
• Corporate action ki wajah se quantity waqt par credit nahi hui.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Auction ka nuksaan predictable nahi hota — wo us din ki auction bidding par tikta hai, isliye wo aapke soche hue loss se bahut bada ho sakta hai. Isi ek wajah se BTST ko ek casual trade ki tarah nahi lena chahiye.
