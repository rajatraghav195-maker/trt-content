GUARANTEED RETURN: WO EK SHABD JO POORI BAAT KHATAM KAR DETA HAI

Market linked product mein guarantee kyun sambhav nahi, aur baaki red flags

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 06, 2026 · Compliance & Regulation

Market se juda koi bhi product fixed return guarantee nahi de sakta. Ye niyam ki baat bhi hai aur ganit ki bhi — jahan return volatile hai, wahan certainty bech pana sirf jhooth ho sakta hai.

DAAWA KAISE PACKAGE HOTA HAI

Aksar wo seedha "guarantee" nahi kehte. Wo "assured", "fixed monthly payout", "risk free" ya "capital protected with high returns" jaise shabd use karte hain, jinka koi legal weight nahi hota.

Kai baar shuruaat mein payout aata bhi hai — wo naye logon ke paise se hota hai, kisi return se nahi.

RED FLAGS KI LIST

In mein se ek bhi dikhe to ruk jao:
• Fixed percentage monthly return ka vaada, chahe market kuch bhi kare.
• Paisa kisi vyakti ke personal account ya kisi third party wallet mein maangna.
• Jaldi karo, offer aaj tak hai wala pressure.
• Naye log laane par extra commission — ye product nahi, chain hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Ek simple filter hamesha chalta hai: agar wo aapko bina registration, bina agreement, bina risk disclosure ke paisa bhejne ko keh rahe hain, to product ka naam kuch bhi ho, faisla wahin ho jaana chahiye.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
