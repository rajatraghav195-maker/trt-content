BREAKOUT AUR FAKEOUT: EK HI CANDLE, DO ALAG ANJAAM

Failed breakout kyun hota hai, aur usse kaise bachein

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 28, 2026 · Price Action

Bhaav ek level tod deta hai, sab log andar aate hain, aur wo wapas andar chala jaata hai. Ye itna aam hai ki isse ek exception samajhna hi sabse badi galti hai.

FAKEOUT HOTA KYUN HAI

Har chhote level ke aas paas stop loss orders jama hote hain. Un stops ka trigger hona apne aap kuch der ke liye bhaav ko us disha mein le jaata hai.

Agar us move ke peeche asli demand nahi hai to wo utna hi tez wapas aata hai, aur ab andar aaye hue log galat taraf phanse hote hain.

FARQ KAISE PEHCHAANEIN

Ek asli breakout mein aksar ye cheezein hoti hain:
• Volume normal se saaf zyada, sirf ek spike nahi.
• Level ke bahar candle ka close hona, sirf touch nahi.
• Us level par wapas aakar use support ki tarah hold karna.
• Higher timeframe par bhi wahi level toota hua dikhna.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Har breakout pakadne ki koshish sabse mehngi aadat hai. Kuch chhod dena strategy ka hissa hai — aur ek confirmed breakout par thoda bura entry price ek failed breakout ke poore nuksaan se sasta padta hai.
