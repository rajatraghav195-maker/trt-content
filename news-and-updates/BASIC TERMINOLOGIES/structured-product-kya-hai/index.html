STRUCTURED PRODUCT: CAPITAL PROTECTION KA ASLI GANIT

Debt aur derivative ka combination, aur uski chhupi hui laagat

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 04, 2026 · AIF, PE & Alternates

Structured products ek package hote hain jo "capital protection ke saath market upside" jaisa waada karte hain. Andar se wo do simple cheezon ka jod hote hain, aur wo jod khud aap samajh sakte ho.

ANDAR KYA HOTA HAI

Aam dhaancha ye hai — paisa ka bada hissa ek debt instrument mein jaata hai taaki maturity par mool raashi wapas ban jaaye, aur chhota hissa options mein jaata hai taaki upside mile.

Isliye "protection" kisi jaadu se nahi, us debt portion ke interest se aati hai. Wo interest hi aapka option budget ban jaata hai.

KAHAN DHYAAN CHAHIYE

Ye baatein aksar pitch mein saaf nahi hoti:
• Protection issuer ke credit par tikti hai — issuer default kare to protection bhi nahi.
• Participation rate aksar sau percent se kam hota hai.
• Upside par cap ho sakta hai, jise headline mein nahi likha jaata.
• Early exit par value model se nikalti hai, aur wo aksar aapke against hoti hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Agar aap wahi structure khud bana sakte ho — kuch hissa safe instrument mein aur thoda hissa index exposure mein — to poochho ki extra fees kis cheez ki hai. Kai baar jawaab sirf packaging hota hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
