TERM INSURANCE AUR INVESTMENT WALE PLAN: DONO KO ALAG RAKHO

Pure protection ka ganit, aur mixed products mein kya chhup jaata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 28, 2026 · Retirement & Insurance

Insurance ka kaam parivaar ko aapki income ke na hone se bachana hai. Investment ka kaam paisa badhana hai. Jab dono ek hi product mein mila diye jaate hain, to aksar dono theek se nahi hote.

TERM PLAN KA GANIT

Term insurance mein aap ek premium dete ho aur agar policy period mein kuch ho jaaye to nominee ko sum assured milta hai. Kuch na ho to premium wapas nahi aata — aur yahi uska poora design hai.

Isi wajah se uska premium bahut kam hota hai aur cover bahut bada. Wo protection ka sabse sasta roop hai.

MIXED PRODUCTS MEIN KYA DEKHEIN

Investment cum insurance plans mein ye poochhna chahiye:
• Cover kitna hai — aksar wo aapki zaroorat se kaafi kam hota hai.
• Charges kya kya hain aur wo pehle kuch saalon mein kitne hain.
• Lock-in aur surrender value kya hai.
• Us paise ka expected return ek simple alternative se kitna alag hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

"Paisa wapas mil jaata hai" wali baat aakarshak lagti hai par wo free nahi hai — uska daam kam cover aur zyada premium hai. Protection aur investment ko alag rakhne se dono ka hisaab saaf dikhta hai, aur dono ka chunaav independent ho jaata hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
