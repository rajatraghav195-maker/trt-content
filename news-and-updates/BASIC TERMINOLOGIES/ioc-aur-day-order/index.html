IOC AUR DAY ORDER: ORDER KITNI DER ZINDA REHTA HAI

Validity ka matlab, aur immediate or cancel kab kaam aata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 01, 2026 · Order Flow

Har order ke saath ek validity hoti hai jo batati hai wo kitni der market mein rahega. Zyadatar log default chhod dete hain, par wo choice kuch situations mein maayne rakhti hai.

DO AAM VALIDITY

Day order poore din ke liye zinda rehta hai. Market band hone tak na bhare to wo apne aap cancel ho jaata hai.

IOC yaani immediate or cancel turant bharne ki koshish karta hai. Jitna us waqt bhar sakta hai bhar leta hai, baaki turant cancel ho jaata hai.

IOC KAB KAAM AATA HAI

Ye validity kuch specific situations ke liye hai:
• Jab aap sirf abhi ke available bhaav par hi lena chahte ho, baad mein nahi.
• Jab aap nahi chahte ki aapka order book mein dikhta rahe.
• Multi leg strategies mein, jahan ek leg ka pending rehna risk banata hai.
• Algo systems mein, jahan har order ka lifecycle chhota rakha jaata hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Day order chhod dena zyadatar retail trades ke liye theek hai. Par ek baat yaad rakhni chahiye — din bhar pada hua limit order us news ke baad bhi zinda rehta hai jo aapke view ko poori tarah badal chuki hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
