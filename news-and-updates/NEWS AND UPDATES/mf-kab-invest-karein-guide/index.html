Mutual Fund Mein Kab Invest Karein?

Answer: Abhi. Market high ho ya low, SIP start karo. Timing ki zaroorat nahi.

Market High vs Low

Market at All-Time High:
• SIP start karo — Waiting for crash = Years waste
• STP use karo — Debt fund se equity mein transfer
• Don't wait — Market aur upar ja sakta hai

Market Crashed:
• Lump sum karo — Cheap prices mil rahe hain
• SIP continue karo — Zyada units milenge
• Don't panic sell — Recovery hogi

SIP Day Doesn't Matter

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1st — "Salary ke baad best"
• 15th — "Mid-month average"
• 28th — "Month-end cheap"

Truth: Day se koi fark nahi padta. Consistency matters.

Best Time of Year

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• January — Usually positive
• March — Year-end adjustments
• October — Often good entry
• December — Santa rally

Ye patterns hain, guaranteed nahi. SIP in any month works.

Cheat Sheet

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 MF MEIN KAB INVEST KAREIN
• 📊 Answer: Abhi. Timing ki zaroorat nahi.
• 📊 Market high → STP use karo
• 📊 Market crash → Continue SIP (zyada units)
• 📊 Any day works — Consistency matters
• 📊 Don't wait for crash — years waste hote hain
