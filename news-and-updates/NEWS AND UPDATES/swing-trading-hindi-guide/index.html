Swing Trading Kya Hai?

Swing trading ek positional trading style hai jisme aap stocks 2-10 din (ya kabhi 2-3 weeks) hold karte ho. Goal: price ke "swings" (ups and downs) se profit banana.

Swing trading vs others:

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Intraday — Hold Time: Same day, Time Required: Full day monitoring, Best For: Full-time traders
• Swing — Hold Time: 2-10 days, Time Required: 30 min/day, Best For: Working professionals
• Positional — Hold Time: Weeks-Months, Time Required: 15 min/week, Best For: Investors
• Long-term — Hold Time: Years, Time Required: 5 min/month, Best For: Wealth building

Swing trading best for:
• Working professionals (job ke saath kar sakte hain)
• Jo intraday ka stress nahi le sakte
• Jo patience rakhte hain
• Jo technical analysis basics jante hain

Swing Trading Setup

Chart Setup:
• Main chart: Daily (1D) — trend identify karne ke liye
• Entry chart: 4-hour (4H) — precise entry ke liye
• Indicators: 20 EMA, 50 EMA, 200 SMA, RSI (14), Volume

Trend Filter (Sabse Pehle):
• Price above 200 SMA = Uptrend (sirf LONG trades)
• Price below 200 SMA = Downtrend (sirf SHORT trades)
• Price crossing 200 SMA repeatedly = Sideways (NO TRADE)

Golden rule: Trend ke against trade mat lo. Uptrend mein sirf buy, downtrend mein sirf sell.

Swing Trading Strategy — Pullback Entry

Strategy: 20 EMA Pullback in Uptrend

Setup:
• Stock 200 SMA ke upar hai (uptrend confirmed)
• Stock ne strong upmove kiya
• Stock 20 EMA ya 50 EMA ke taraf pullback kar raha hai
• Pullback ke dauran volume kam hai (healthy sign)
• 20 EMA ko touch karke bounce back

Entry Rules:
• Price 20 EMA ko touch kare ya thoda neeche jaaye
• Bullish reversal candle banaye (hammer, bullish engulfing, morning star)
• Volume on reversal candle > previous 3 candles average
• RSI 40-60 zone mein ho (not overbought)

Entry: Reversal candle close ke next day, opening pe ya candle close pe

Stop Loss: Reversal candle ke low ke thoda neeche (ya 20 EMA se 1 ATR neeche)

Target:
• Target 1: Previous swing high (1:2 R/R minimum)
• Target 2: 1.5x previous swing high distance
• Trailing SL: Supertrend ya 20 EMA pe trail karo

Example: RELIANCE pe swing trade:
• RELIANCE daily chart:
• Above 200 SMA ✅ (uptrend)
• Made high of ₹2,900
• Pulled back to 20 EMA at ₹2,840
• Bullish hammer at ₹2,840 with volume
• Entry: ₹2,845 (next candle open)
• SL: ₹2,820 (hammer low - ₹5)
• Target 1: ₹2,890 (previous high) = ₹45 reward vs ₹25 risk = 1.8 R/R
• Target 2: ₹2,935 = ₹90 reward vs ₹25 risk = 3.6 R/R

Swing Trading Strategy — Breakout Entry

Strategy: Resistance Breakout with Volume

Setup:
• Stock ek resistance level pe 2-3 baar reject ho chuka hai
• Consolidation near resistance (tight range)
• Suddenly stock resistance ko break karta hai
• Breakout candle: Large body, close above resistance, volume 2x+ average

Entry Rules:
• Breakout candle close above resistance
• Volume at least 2x of 20-day average
• RSI > 55 (momentum present)
• Market (Nifty) bhi bullish ya neutral

Entry: Breakout candle close pe ya next day opening

Stop Loss: Breakout candle ke low ya resistance level (ab support ban chuka hai) ke thoda neeche

Target:
• Target 1: Consolidation range jitna (measured move)
• Target 2: 2x consolidation range

Example: TCS pe breakout:
• TCS daily chart:
• Resistance at ₹4,200 (tested 3 times in 2 months)
• Consolidation at ₹4,150-4,200 for 8 days
• Breakout candle: ₹4,220 close, volume 3x average
• Entry: ₹4,220
• SL: ₹4,180 (consolidation low)
• Target 1: ₹4,270 (₹50 range × 1)
• Target 2: ₹4,320 (₹50 range × 2)

Swing Trading Risk Management

Position Sizing:
• Risk per trade: 1-2% of capital
• ₹1,00,000 capital → max ₹2,000 risk per trade
• SL distance se position size calculate karo:
• Entry: ₹500, SL: ₹480, Risk/share: ₹20
• Position: ₹2,000 / ₹20 = 100 shares
• Investment: 100 × ₹500 = ₹50,000

Max Open Positions:
• Beginners: 2-3 max open swing trades
• Intermediate: 4-5
• Each trade independent hone chahiye (different sectors)

Risk-Reward Ratio:
• MINIMUM 1:2
• Entry: ₹100, SL: ₹95 (₹5 risk) → Target minimum: ₹110 (₹10 reward)
• 1:2 se kam R/R = trade skip karo
• Better: 1:3 ya 1:4 R/R

Weekly Loss Limit:
• 2 consecutive losing trades → stop for the week
• Review what went wrong
• Come back Monday fresh

Swing Trading Routine (Working Professionals Ke Liye)

Morning (15 min) — 8:45 AM:
• Check global markets (US close, Asian open)
• Check Nifty pre-market
• Scan watchlist for any overnight gaps
• Set/adjust alerts on TRT

Evening (20 min) — 7:00 PM:
• Update chart — daily candle close
• Check: any setup forming? (pullback to 20 EMA? breakout?)
• If setup: set entry order for next day
• If holding: check SL and target, update if trailing
• Update trading journal

Weekend (1 hour) — Saturday:
• Full chart review of watchlist (30+ stocks)
• Identify setups for coming week
• Read market news/events for next week
• Review past week's trades
• Update watchlist (add/remove stocks)

Total time: ~35 min/day, 1 hour/weekend = 4 hours/week. Manageable with job.

Swing Trading Mistakes

Mistake 1: Patience Na Rakhna:
• Setup form hone mein 3-5 din lag sakte hain
• Bina setup ke trade = gambling
• "Kuch toh trade karte hain" → capital loss

Mistake 2: SL Bahut Tight Rakhna:
• Daily noise mein SL hit hoga
• SL at least 1.5 ATR distance pe rakho
• 2-3% SL for swing trades (stock volatility pe depend)

Mistake 3: Profit Early Book Karna:
• 1:2 R/R target set kiya → let it hit
• "₹10 profit ho gaya, book karte hain" → R/R 1:1 ho gaya
• Trailing SL use karo, profit ko bada hone do

Mistake 4: News/Tips Pe Trade Lena:
• "Reliance results acche aayenge" → speculation, not trading
• Chart pe setup hona chahiye. News = context, not trigger.

Mistake 5: Sector Diversification Na Karna:
• 3 trades IT sector mein = actually 1 trade (sector falls = all 3 fall)
• Different sectors: 1 IT, 1 Banking, 1 Auto = real diversification

FAQ

Q1: Swing trading ke liye kitna capital chahiye?

Minimum ₹50,000. ₹1,00,000+ ideal. Kam capital mein position sizing aur diversification mushkil.

Q2: Swing trading safe hai?

Intraday se zyada safe (no leverage, no time pressure). Lekin risk hai. SL + position sizing + discipline = risk controlled.

Q4: Kitne trades per month?

3-5 good setups per month milenge. Quality > quantity. 2-3 profitable trades/month = 10-15% monthly return possible.

Q5: Swing trading aur intraday mein difference?

Intraday: same day close, high stress, leverage, full time needed. Swing: 2-10 days, low stress, no leverage needed, 30 min/day enough. Beginners ke liye swing BEST.
