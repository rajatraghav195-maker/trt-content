GOAL BASED INVESTING: HAR RUPAYE KA EK KAAM

Goal, timeline aur risk ko jodna — aur ek portfolio ko kai hisson mein baantna

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Zyadatar log ek hi bade portfolio ki tarah sochte hain aur uska ek hi return target rakhte hain. Isse do dikkatein aati hain: risk poore paise par ek jaisa lag jaata hai, aur zaroorat par paisa nikalna hamesha galat waqt par padta hai.

GOAL PEHLE, PRODUCT BAAD MEIN

Har paise ka ek kaam hona chahiye aur ek tareekh. 'Teen saal baad gaadi', 'aath saal baad bachche ki padhai', 'pachees saal baad retirement' — teenon alag cheezein hain aur teenon ka risk alag hona chahiye.

Product ka chunaav uske baad aata hai. Ulta karne par — pehle product chunna aur phir uske liye goal dhundhna — lagbhag hamesha mismatch hota hai.

TIMELINE HI RISK TAY KARTI HAI

Ek mota dhaancha:
• Do saal se kam — market ka risk lena mushkil hai. Yahan capital ki suraksha pehli shart hai.
• Teen se paanch saal — thoda equity chal sakta hai, par volatility ka poora bojh nahi.
• Saat saal se zyada — equity ka case mazboot hota jaata hai, kyunki bure daur ko bharne ka waqt milta hai.
• Aur jaise-jaise goal paas aata hai, us goal ka paisa dheere-dheere kam risk mein shift karna hi asli kaam hai.

GOAL KE PAAS AAKAR SABSE BADI GALTI

Paisa poori tarah equity mein rakh kar goal ki tareekh tak pahunch jaana. Agar us saal market gir gaya, to poora plan us ek saal ki kismat par aa jaata hai.

Isliye goal se do-teen saal pehle se equity ka hissa ghatana ek standard practice hai. Ye return kam karta hai — aur wahi uska maqsad hai. Us stage par certainty, return se zyada keemti hai.

BUCKET SOCH

Ek tarika ye hai ki portfolio ko alag-alag buckets mein baant do — har goal ka apna bucket, apna allocation, apna review.

Iska sabse bada faayda emotional hai: ek bucket ka bura saal poore portfolio ka bura saal nahi lagta, aur aap ek goal ke liye liye gaye risk ko doosre goal ke paise se cover karne ki koshish nahi karte.

INFLATION KO MAT BHOOLO

Aaj ka ₹10 lakh das saal baad ka ₹10 lakh nahi hai. Goal ka target amount aaj ke bhaav par nahi, us waqt ke anumaanit bhaav par hona chahiye.

Yahi wo ek adjustment hai jo zyadatar plans mein chhoot jaata hai — aur uske bina lagta hai ki goal poora ho gaya, jabki asal mein kam pad gaya.
