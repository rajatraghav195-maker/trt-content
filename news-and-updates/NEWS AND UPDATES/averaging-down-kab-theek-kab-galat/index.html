AVERAGING DOWN: 'SASTA HO GAYA' AUR 'GIR RAHA HAI' MEIN FARQ

Averaging ka logic, wo kab kaam karta hai, aur wo trading mein kyun ulta padta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Position girti hai, aur pehla vichaar aata hai: 'aur le lo, average neeche aa jaayega'. Kabhi ye samajhdaari hoti hai aur kabhi ye account khatam karne ka sabse aam raasta. Farq ek hi sawaal se tay hota hai.

WO EK SAWAAL

Kya keemat isliye giri hai ki market ka mood kharab hai, ya isliye ki business ke baare mein kuch naya aur bura pata chala hai?

Pehle case mein aapko ek behtar bhaav mil raha hai. Doosre case mein aap ek girti hui cheez mein aur paisa daal rahe ho. Chart dono mein ek jaisa dikhta hai — farq sirf wajah mein hai.

INVESTING MEIN ISKI JAGAH HAI

Ek lambi soch wale investor ke liye planned averaging ek vaajib tarika hai — SIP khud ek tarah ka averaging hi hai. Wahan wo pehle se plan kiya hua hota hai, aur uski capital pehle se allot hoti hai.

Zaroori shart yahi hai: plan pehle bana ho. Ek pehle se likha hua plan aur ek girawat dekh kar liya gaya faisla, do bilkul alag cheezein hain, chahe order ek jaisa dikhe.

TRADING MEIN YE LAGBHAG HAMESHA GALAT HAI

Trade ka matlab hi ye hai ki aapne ek level tay kiya tha jahan aap galat sabit ho jaate. Averaging down us level ko maan-ne se inkaar hai.

Aur ganit bhi khilaaf hai:
• Position badi ho gayi hai theek us waqt jab wo aapke khilaaf ja rahi hai.
• Aapka original stop ab poore position par lagega, yaani nuksaan pehle se bada ho gaya.
• Break-even ab door hai, aur aapko sahi hone ke liye aur bada move chahiye.
• Capital block ho gayi hai, isliye agar kahin aur achha mauka mila to aap le hi nahi paoge.

SABSE KHATARNAK ROOP

Averaging down jab ek trade se shuru hokar 'ab to ye investment hai' mein badal jaata hai — yahin sabse bada nuksaan hota hai. Ye position ka nahi, kahani ka badalna hai, aur kahani hamesha nuksaan ko justify karne ke liye badalti hai.

Ek simple niyam bahut bachata hai: trade ko investment mein promote karne ki ijazat mat do. Jo cheez trade ke rules se li thi, wo trade ke rules se hi khatam hogi.

ULTA TARIKA

Bahut tajurbekaar log ulta karte hain — jeet ti hui position mein add karte hain, harti hui mein nahi. Isse capital wahan jaati hai jahan market unhe sahi bata raha hai, na ki wahan jahan wo khud ko sahi sabit karna chahte hain.
