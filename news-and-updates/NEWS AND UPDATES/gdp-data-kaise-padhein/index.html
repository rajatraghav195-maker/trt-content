GDP: EK NUMBER JO PEECHE KA HAAL BATATA HAI

Real vs nominal, quarterly data ki seema, aur market GDP par kam react kyun karta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

GDP economy ka sabse bada headline number hai, aur ironically market usse sabse kam react karta hai. Iski wajah samajhna khud ek acchi macro lesson hai.

GDP HAI KYA

GDP ek avadhi mein desh ke andar bane saare final goods aur services ki value hai. Ye ek total hai — ek score, ek forecast nahi.

Ye quarterly aur annual dono roop mein aata hai, aur uske saath GVA jaisa ek aur paimana bhi hota hai jo taxes aur subsidies ka hisaab alag rakhta hai.

REAL AUR NOMINAL

Nominal GDP aaj ke bhaav par hai — usme mehngai bhi shaamil hai. Real GDP se mehngai hata di jaati hai, isliye wo asli growth dikhata hai.

Jab koi 'growth' bole to poochna banta hai — real ya nominal. Zyada mehngai wale daur mein nominal number bahut acha dikh sakta hai jabki asli utpadan lagbhag flat ho.

MARKET KAM REACT KYUN KARTA HAI

GDP data kaafi der se aata hai — jo quarter khatam ho chuka hai uska hisaab hafte-mahine baad milta hai. Tab tak market kai taaza indicators dekh chuka hota hai.

Market GDP se pehle ye dekhta hai:
• Monthly high-frequency data — GST collection, auto sales, PMI, electricity demand, credit growth.
• Company results — actual demand ka sabse seedha subooot yahi hota hai.
• Government spending aur capex ka data.
• Global cues — kyunki ek bade hisse ki demand bahar se aati hai.

REVISIONS — JO KOI NAHI PADHTA

GDP ke numbers baad mein revise hote hain, kabhi-kabhi kaafi. Isliye ek quarter ke headline par poora nazariya banana kamzor padta hai.

Behtar tarika ye hai ki teen-chaar quarters ka trend dekha jaaye. Direction akela number se kahin zyada bharosemand hota hai.

PHIR ISKA FAAYDA KYA HAI

GDP se aap trade nahi karte. Usse aap context banate ho — economy ka phase kaunsa hai, aur uska matlab kis tarah ke business ke liye kya hai.

Cyclical sectors economy ke phase se bandhe hote hain aur defensive kam. Yahi sabse practical istemal hai: ek naqsha, na ki ek signal.
