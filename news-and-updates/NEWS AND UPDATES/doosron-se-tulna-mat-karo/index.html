TULNA: DOOSRON KE RETURNS DEKH KAR APNA PLAN TODNA

Screenshot culture, survivorship, aur apne hi benchmark par tike rehna

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Aapka saal theek chal raha tha. Phir kisi ne ek screenshot bhej diya — ek hi trade mein bada faayda. Agle din se aapka plan alag lagne lagta hai. Ye tulna hi wo cheez hai jo achhe systems ko sabse zyada todti hai.

AAP JO DEKH RAHE HO WO POORI TASVEER NAHI HAI

Log jeet ka screenshot bhejte hain, haar ka nahi. Ek hi trade ka screenshot ye nahi batata ki uske peeche kitni haar hain, kitni capital lagi thi, aur kitna risk liya gaya tha.

Aur bahut saare screenshots asli bhi nahi hote. Demo account, edited image, ya ek cherry-picked din — teenon aam hain. Jo cheez verify nahi ho sakti, wo data nahi hai.

SURVIVORSHIP — JO DIKHTE HAIN WO BACHE HUE HAIN

Aap sirf unhe dekhte ho jo abhi tak khel mein hain aur bol rahe hain. Jo log wahi tarika apna kar bahar ho gaye, wo koi post nahi karte.

Isliye kisi tarike ki safalta ka andaza uske mashhoor logon se lagana ulta hai. Wo sample already filter hokar aaya hai.

APNA BENCHMARK KYA HONA CHAHIYE

Doosron ke return ki jagah ye teen cheezein naapo:
• Kya maine apne rules follow kiye — percentage mein.
• Mera drawdown apne tay limit ke andar raha ya nahi.
• Meri expectancy pichle 50 trades par kya hai.
• Aur ek bahar ka benchmark — index. Agar aapka lamba nateeja index se kam hai, to wo ek asli sawaal hai. Kisi anjaan aadmi ka screenshot nahi.

RISK KE BINA RETURN KA KOI MATLAB NAHI

'Maine is mahine 40% banaya' apne aap mein koi jaankari nahi hai. Kitni capital par, kitna risk lekar, aur kitni baar aisa hua — teenon ke bina wo number bekaar hai.

Ek aadmi jo har mahine poori capital ek trade par lagata hai, wo kabhi na kabhi 40% banayega hi. Uska ek aur nateeja bhi hai, aur wo screenshot nahi banta.

EK CHHOTI SI PRACTICAL BAAT

Jin groups aur feeds se aapko lagataar 'main peeche reh gaya' mehsoos hota hai, unse door hona apne aap mein ek risk management step hai. Ye kamzori nahi hai — ye us shor ko hatana hai jo aapke faislon ko badal raha tha.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
