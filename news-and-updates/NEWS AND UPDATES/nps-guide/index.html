NPS Guide India — National Pension System (Hindi)

NPS kya hai, kaise invest karein, tax benefits, returns — complete Hindi guide India ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 11, 2026 · Features

NPS Kya Hai?

NPS = National Pension System. Government ka retirement savings scheme hai. Long-term invest karo, retirement pe regular pension milta hai.

Key features:
• Government-backed retirement fund
• Tax benefit under 80C + 80CCD(1B)
• Market-linked returns (10-12%)
• Lock-in till 60 (partial withdrawal allowed)

NPS Account Types

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Tier I — Anyone (mandatory for govt employees)
• Tier II — Only if Tier I exists (voluntary, no lock-in)

NPS Fund Options

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Aggressive (LC-75) — Equity: 75%, Debt: 25%, Government: 0%, Risk: High
• Moderate (LC-50) — Equity: 50%, Debt: 50%, Government: 0%, Risk: Medium
• Conservative (LC-25) — Equity: 25%, Debt: 75%, Government: 0%, Risk: Low
• Auto Choice — Equity: Age-based, Debt: Age-based, Government: Age-based, Risk: Auto-adjusted

Default: Auto Choice (age-based allocation — young = more equity, old = more debt)

NPS Tax Benefits

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 80C — Limit: ₹1.5 lakh, Benefit: Standard deduction
• 80CCD(1B) — Limit: ₹50,000, Benefit: Extra deduction (NPS exclusive)
• 80CCD(2) — Limit: 10% of salary, Benefit: Employer contribution (if applicable)

Total possible deduction: ₹2 lakh+ (₹1.5L 80C + ₹50K 80CCD(1B))

NPS Returns

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 5 years — 10-12%
• 10 years — 10-11%
• Since inception — ~10%

NPS vs PPF vs EPF

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — NPS: 10-12%, PPF: 7.1%, EPF: 8.15%
• Risk — NPS: Medium, PPF: Very Low, EPF: Very Low
• Lock-in — NPS: Till 60, PPF: 15 years, EPF: Till retirement
• Extra tax benefit — NPS: ₹50K (80CCD(1B)), PPF: None, EPF: None
• Equity exposure — NPS: Yes (up to 75%), PPF: No, EPF: No

How to Open NPS

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• eNPS portal: enps.nsdl.com
• Aadhaar-based instant opening
• Minimum ₹1,000 per year
• PAN + Bank account required
• PRAN card milta hai (Permanent Retirement Account Number)

NPS Withdrawal Rules

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• At 60 — 60% lump sum (tax-free), 40% annuity (mandatory)
• Before 60 — 20% lump sum, 80% annuity
• Partial — 3 times in tenure (max 25% of own contribution)
