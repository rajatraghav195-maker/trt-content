REVENGE TRADING: NUKSAAN KO USI DIN WAPAS PAANE KI JIDD

Ek haar ke baad ka wo ghanta jismein zyadatar accounts kharab hote hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Ek stop hit hua. Nuksaan chhota tha, planned tha, aur bilkul theek tha. Par agle 40 minute mein aap teen aur trades lete ho, bina soche, badi size par — aur din ka nuksaan das guna ho jaata hai. Isko revenge trading kehte hain.

YE GUSSA NAHI, HISAAB CHUKANA HAI

Revenge trading market ke khilaaf nahi hoti. Wo apne aap ke khilaaf hoti hai — aap ye sabit karna chahte ho ki aap galat nahi the.

Isliye ye trades market ke setup se nahi aati. Wo ek aankde se aati hain jo screen par red mein likha hai, aur jise aap usi din zero karna chahte ho.

PATTERN LAGBHAG EK JAISA HAI

Kram aksar aisa hota hai:
• Ek planned nuksaan hota hai.
• Turant agli trade li jaati hai, bina setup ke, 'wapas paane' ke liye.
• Wo bhi ulti jaati hai. Ab nuksaan do guna hai aur jaldi teen guna badh gayi.
• Size badhti hai kyunki ab chhoti trade se wapas nahi aayega.
• Din ke ant mein nuksaan wo hai jo poore hafte ki kamai kha gaya.

SABSE ZAROORI BAAT: YE FAISLA PEHLE HOTA HAI

Us pal mein aap khud ko nahi rok paoge. Ye maan lena hi sabse imandaar shuruaat hai. Isliye rok pehle se lagti hai.

Ek daily loss limit tay karo, aur uske hit hote hi terminal band. Koi soch-vichar nahi, koi 'ek aur' nahi. Rule jitna mechanical hoga, utna hi wo us waqt kaam karega jab dimaag kaam nahi kar raha.

TIME-OUT KA NIYAM

Ek aur simple rule: kisi bhi stop-out ke baad 30 minute tak koi nayi position nahi. Isse aapka system nahi rukta — aap sirf us window se bahar nikal jaate ho jismein sabse buri trades hoti hain.

Bahut traders ne apna sabse bada sudhaar sirf isi ek rule se paaya hai. Wo koi strategy nahi hai; wo ek darwaza band karna hai.

HAAR KA MATLAB

Ek planned stop-out haar nahi hai. Wo system ka kharcha hai, jaise dukaan ka kiraya. Jo trader har stop ko personal le leta hai, wo ek aisi cheez se ladh raha hai jo har roz hone wali hai.

Jis din stop-out ke baad kuch mehsoos hi na ho — us din aap system chala rahe ho, apne aap ko nahi.
