Stock Market Mein Paisa Kab Lagein — Timing Guide (Hindi)

Stock market mein invest karne ka best time kya hai, market timing vs time in market — Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Features

Stock market mein invest karne ka best time kya hai, market timing vs time in market — Hindi guide.

Best Time to Invest

Short answer: Abhi. Right now.

Long answer: "Time in the market" beats "timing the market" — hamesha.

Market Timing vs Time in Market

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Perfect timing (best day every year) — 14.2% CAGR
• Always invested (day 1 se) — 12.5% CAGR
• Worst timing (worst day every year) — 8.6% CAGR
• Missed 10 best days — 7.8% CAGR
• Missed 20 best days — 5.2% CAGR

Key insight: Missing just 10 best days in 20 years = Returns HALF ho jaate hain!

SIP = Best Timing Strategy

SIP se tumhe timing ki zaroorat nahi:
• High market pe: Kam units milte hain
• Low market pe: Zyada units milte hain
• Average out ho jaata hai
• Rupee cost averaging kaam karta hai

When NOT to Invest

All Money at Once

Sab paisa ek baar mein mat lagao. SIP ya STP use karo.

Borrowed Money

Loan leke invest karna = Suicide. Market gira toh loan + loss dono.

Emergency Fund Se

Emergency fund invest mat karo. Liquid fund mein rakho.

FOMO Mein

"Market bahut upar hai, sab kama rahe hain!" — FOMO se loss hota hai.

Panic Sell

"Market gir raha hai, sab bech do!" — Panic sell se loss hota hai.

Lump Sum vs SIP Timing

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Market at all-time high — SIP (6-12 months)
• Market crashed 20%+ — Lump sum (50%) + SIP (50%)
• Regular salary — Monthly SIP
• Bonus/windfall — STP (3-6 months)
• Uncertain market — SIP
