How to Earn Passive Income from Stocks — Hindi Guide

Stocks se passive income kaise kamayein — dividends, covered calls, MTF income — Hindi guide.

Stocks Se Passive Income

1. Dividend Income

High dividend yield stocks kharidne → Quarterly/annual dividend milta hai.

Example Portfolio (₹10 lakh):
• Coal India — Allocation: ₹2L, Yield: 6%, Annual Dividend: ₹12,000
• ONGC — Allocation: ₹1.5L, Yield: 5%, Annual Dividend: ₹7,500
• ITC — Allocation: ₹2L, Yield: 4%, Annual Dividend: ₹8,000
• Power Grid — Allocation: ₹1.5L, Yield: 4.5%, Annual Dividend: ₹6,750
• Others — Allocation: ₹3L, Yield: 4%, Annual Dividend: ₹12,000
• Total — Allocation: ₹10L, Yield: ~4.6%, Annual Dividend: ₹46,250/year

2. Covered Call Income

Stocks rakho + OTM Call becho → Premium income monthly.

Example:
• 250 Reliance shares (₹6.25L)
• Sell 1 OTM call monthly: ₹5,000-10,000 premium
• Annual income: ₹60,000-1,20,000

3. Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)

Mutual fund se monthly withdrawal.

Example:
• ₹25 lakh in equity MF
• SWP: ₹15,000/month
• Remaining corpus grows

4. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)

Commercial real estate ka rental income.

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Embassy REIT — 6-7%
• Mindspace REIT — 6-7%
• Brookfield REIT — 6-7%
