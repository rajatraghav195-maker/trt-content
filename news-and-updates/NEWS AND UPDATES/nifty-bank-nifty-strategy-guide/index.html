Nifty aur Bank Nifty Kya Hai?

Nifty 50: India ke top 50 companies ka index (NSE). Diversified — IT, Banking, Auto, Pharma, FMCG, etc. Bank Nifty: Top 12 banking stocks ka index (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, etc.).

Key differences:

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stocks — Nifty 50: 50, Bank Nifty: 12
• Volatility — Nifty 50: Moderate, Bank Nifty: High
• Movement — Nifty 50: Steady, Bank Nifty: Fast
• Best for — Nifty 50: Swing, positional, Bank Nifty: Intraday, options
• Expiry — Nifty 50: Thursday (weekly + monthly), Bank Nifty: Thursday (weekly + monthly)
• Lot size — Nifty 50: 50, Bank Nifty: 15
• Sector dependency — Nifty 50: Diversified, Bank Nifty: Banking only (RBI, NPAs, credit growth)

Nifty Intraday Strategy

Strategy: Opening Range Breakout (ORB)

Setup:
• Mark first 15 min high and low (9:15-9:30)
• After 9:30, wait for breakout
• If price breaks 15-min high → Buy (bullish)
• If price breaks 15-min low → Sell (bearish)
• Volume on breakout candle > average

Entry: Breakout candle close SL: Opposite side of range (15-min low for buy, high for sell) Target: Range width × 1.5

Example:
• 9:15-9:30 Nifty range: 24,580 - 24,650
• 9:32: Price breaks 24,650 with volume → Buy
• Entry: 24,650
• SL: 24,580 (70 points risk)
• Target: 24,750 (range 70 × 1.5 = 105 points)

Strategy: VWAP Bounce (Intraday):
• Price above VWAP = bullish
• Price pulls back to VWAP
• Bounces off VWAP with bullish candle
• Entry: bounce candle
• SL: below VWAP
• Target: day high

Bank Nifty Intraday Strategy

Bank Nifty zyada volatile hai — isliye opportunities bhi zyada, risk bhi.

Strategy: Morning Momentum:
• Check overnight global cues
• 9:15-9:20: Mark opening 5-min candle
• If 5-min candle strong bullish (large body, close near high) → Buy
• If strong bearish → Sell
• SL: 5-min candle ke low/high
• Target: 1:2 R/R

Strategy: Bank Nifty Expiry Day Strategy:
• Expiry day = high theta decay
• Option premiums fall fast
• Strategy: Sell options (advanced only) OR Buy ATM options early morning
• Best setup: Buy ATM CE/PE at 9:30, exit by 11:30
• Don't hold options past 1 PM on expiry (theta kills premium)

Nifty Swing Trading Strategy

Strategy: 20 EMA Pullback (Daily Chart):
• Nifty above 200 SMA (uptrend)
• Strong upmove
• Pullback to 20 EMA
• Bullish candle at 20 EMA (hammer, engulfing)
• RSI 40-60 (not overbought)
• Entry: Reversal candle close
• SL: Below 20 EMA or candle low
• Target: Previous swing high

Strategy: Support/Resistance Breakout:
• Mark key levels on daily chart:
• 24,000, 24,200, 24,400, 24,600, 24,800, 25,000
• When price breaks resistance with volume → Buy
• SL: Broken level (now support) ke thoda neeche
• Target: Next round number

Bank Nifty Swing Strategy

Strategy: RSI Divergence + Support:
• Bank Nifty daily chart
• Price at major support (previous swing low, 200 EMA, Fibonacci 61.8%)
• RSI bullish divergence (price lower low, RSI higher low)
• Bullish reversal candle
• Entry: Candle close
• SL: Support - 100 points
• Target: 200-400 points (Bank Nifty moves fast)

Nifty Options Strategy (Beginner Friendly)

Strategy: Weekly ATM Call/Put:
• Monday/Tuesday: Buy ATM Nifty CE ya PE
• Reason: Monday/Tuesday pe theta decay slow hai
• Hold 2-3 days
• Target: 50-100% premium gain
• SL: 30-40% premium loss

Example:
• Monday: Nifty at 24,600
• Buy 24,600 CE at ₹120 (Monday, 5 days to expiry)
• Wednesday: Nifty at 24,700 → CE at ₹180
• Sell: ₹180 → Profit = ₹60 × 50 = ₹3,000
• Investment: ₹120 × 50 = ₹6,000
• ROI: 50% in 2 days ✅

Rules:
• Buy Monday/Tuesday only (avoid Wednesday/Thursday — theta fast)
• ATM strike (not OTM)
• SL at 40% premium loss
• Don't hold till expiry day

Common Nifty/Bank Nifty Mistakes

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Both ek saath trade karna — Nifty aur Bank Nifty highly correlated. Don't take same direction trade on both = double risk.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Expiry pe options kharidna — Thursday pe options buy = theta death. Sell options (advanced) or avoid.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Bina market trend ke trade — Nifty/Bank Nifty trend check karo pehle. Trend ke against trade = loss.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Over-leveraging — Index options leverage attractive but dangerous. 1-2 lots max for beginners.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Global cues ignore karna — US markets, Asian markets, crude oil, dollar — sab impact karta hai.

FAQ

Q1: Nifty ya Bank Nifty — kya trade kare?

Beginners: Nifty (less volatile, more predictable). Experienced: Bank Nifty (more movement, more opportunity).

Q2: Kitna capital chahiye?

Nifty 1 lot (50 shares) ATM option: ~₹5,000-10,000 premium. Bank Nifty 1 lot (15 shares): ~₹3,000-8,000. Keep ₹50,000+ capital.

Q3: Best time to trade Nifty/Bank Nifty?

9:30-11:00 (morning momentum) and 1:30-3:00 (afternoon + European open). Avoid 11:00-1:30 (choppy).
