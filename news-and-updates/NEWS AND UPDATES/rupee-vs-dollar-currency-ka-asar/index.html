RUPEE VS DOLLAR: EK NUMBER JO POORE BAZAAR KO HILATA HAI

FII flows, IT aur FMCG par ulta asar, crude ka connection, aur RBI se lekar aapke portfolio tak ki poori chain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Business channel par ek line roz aati hai: 'rupee ne naya low banaya'. Zyadatar log ise ek desh-bhakti wala headline samajh kar aage badh jaate hain. Par ye ek number hai jo IT stocks, oil companies, FMCG margins, RBI ki policy aur foreign investors ke paise — sab ko ek saath chhoota hai.

PEHLE, 'RUPEE GIRA' KA MATLAB KYA HAI

USDINR ka quote batata hai ki ek dollar kharidne ke liye kitne rupaye chahiye. Ye number badhna — maan lo 84 se 86 — matlab rupee kamzor hua, dollar mehnga hua. Number ghatna matlab rupee mazboot hua.

Isliye 'rupee ne low banaya' aur 'USDINR ne high banaya' ek hi baat hai. Chart dekhte waqt ye ulta lagta hai aur yahin sabse pehli galatfehmi hoti hai.

FII FLOWS — SABSE SEEDHI KADI

Foreign investors India mein dollar laate hain, use rupee mein badalte hain aur stocks kharidte hain. Nikalte waqt ulta karte hain.

Do taraf ka rishta hai. Jab FIIs bade paimane par bechte hain, to rupee par bikwali ka dabav aata hai — kyunki nikalta paisa dollar mein badla jaata hai. Aur jab rupee kamzor ho raha hota hai, to foreign investor ka dollar return ghat jaata hai chahe stock rupee mein flat hi kyun na ho — jisse bechne ka aur karan ban jaata hai. Yahi loop girawat ke daur mein market ko tez neeche le jaata hai.

Isliye badi girawat ke dinon mein sirf Nifty nahi, FII ka daily buy/sell figure aur USDINR — teenon ek saath dekhne se tasveer poori banti hai.

SAME NEWS, ULTA ASAR: IT VS FMCG

Ek hi rupee ka girna alag companies ke liye alag matlab rakhta hai:
• Exporters (IT, pharma, textiles) — inki kamai dollar mein aati hai aur kharch rupee mein hota hai. Rupee kamzor hone par har dollar zyada rupaye mein badalta hai, to bina ek naya client jode margin badh jaata hai.
• Importers aur import-heavy manufacturers — inhein raw material, components ya crude bahar se kharidna padta hai. Rupee kamzor hone par wahi cheez mehngi padti hai aur margin dabta hai.
• FMCG aur consumer companies — kaafi packaging aur raw material crude-linked hota hai, jiski keemat dollar mein tay hoti hai. Rupee kamzor + crude mehnga = double dabav.
• Foreign debt wali companies — jinhone dollar mein udhaar liya hai, unki repayment rupee mein badi ho jaati hai, chahe business waisa hi chal raha ho.

Yahi wajah hai ki rupee ki ek badi chaal ke din sector performance ek jaisi nahi hoti — IT green aur consumer red, ek hi khabar par.

CRUDE OIL — WO TEESRA KHILADI JISE ALAG NAHI KAR SAKTE

India apni crude oil ki zaroorat ka bahut bada hissa import karta hai, aur crude ki keemat dollar mein hoti hai. Isliye India ka oil bill do cheezon par depend karta hai: crude ka bhaav, aur rupee ka level.

Sabse kharab combination wahi hai jab dono ek saath ulte chalein — crude mehnga ho aur rupee kamzor ho. Tab import bill dono taraf se badhta hai, trade deficit chauda hota hai, aur chauda deficit khud rupee par aur dabav daalta hai. Ye ek self-reinforcing loop hai, aur macro headlines ka zyadatar dar isi loop se aata hai.

RBI KYA KARTA HAI, AUR WO AAPKI EMI TAK KAISE PAHUNCHTA HAI

Chain kuch aise chalti hai:
• Rupee tezi se girta hai — RBI apne forex reserves se dollar bechkar girawat ko dheema karta hai. Iska maqsad rupee ko kisi level par 'rokna' nahi hota, sirf volatility kam karna hota hai.
• Kamzor rupee imported inflation laata hai — oil, electronics, edible oil, sab mehnga. Ye retail inflation number mein dikhta hai.
• Inflation upar jaaye to RBI ke paas rate kam karne ki gunjaish ghat jaati hai, aur zarurat padne par badhani padti hai.
• Rate badhte hain to loans mehnge hote hain, companies ka interest cost badhta hai, aur equity valuations par dabav aata hai — khaaskar un sectors par jo udhaar par chalte hain.

Isliye currency ka move sirf currency traders ka mamla nahi hai. Wo do-teen kadam mein aakar aapke equity portfolio par baith jaata hai.

EK RETAIL TRADER KO ISSE KYA KARNA CHAHIYE

Currency ko predict karne ki koshish mat karo — ye duniya ka sabse bada aur sabse zyada institution-driven market hai, aur wahan aapka edge sabse kam hai.

Iske bajaye ise context ki tarah use karo. Agar rupee lagataar kamzor ho raha hai aur crude bhi chadh raha hai, to ye jaan lena kaam ka hai ki import-heavy sectors headwind mein hain aur FII flows par dabav rahega.
