Dividend Yield Investing Kya Hai?

Dividend yield investing = Aise stocks kharidna jo regular aur high dividend dete hain. Passive income ke liye.

Formula:
• Dividend Yield = (Annual Dividend / Stock Price) × 100
• Example: ₹20 dividend, ₹500 stock price → 4% yield

Dividend Yield vs Growth

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Income — Dividend Stocks: Regular dividends, Growth Stocks: No/minimal dividend
• Returns — Dividend Stocks: Price + Dividend, Growth Stocks: Price only
• Risk — Dividend Stocks: Lower, Growth Stocks: Higher
• Best for — Dividend Stocks: Income seekers, Growth Stocks: Wealth builders
• Tax — Dividend Stocks: Dividend taxable, Growth Stocks: LTCG 12.5%

Dividend Portfolio Strategy

Step 1: Select 8-10 Stocks:
• High dividend yield (> 3%)
• Consistent payer (5+ years)
• Strong fundamentals
• Different sectors (diversify)

Step 2: Reinvest Dividends

Dividends wapas invest karo → Compounding ka fayda.

Step 3: Hold Long-Term

Dividend investing long-term strategy hai. 10-20 years hold karo.

Dividend Tax in India

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• All dividends — Taxable at income tax slab rate
• TDS — 10% if dividend > ₹5,000/year
