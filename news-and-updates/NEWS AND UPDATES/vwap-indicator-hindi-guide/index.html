VWAP Kya Hai?

VWAP ka full form hai Volume Weighted Average Price. Ye indicator batata hai ki din bhar mein average price kitna raha jab volume ko bhi consider karein.

Simple words mein: VWAP = price × volume ka average. Isliye ye sirf intraday ke liye hai (din ke shuru se end tak).

Institutional traders (FII, DII, mutual funds) VWAP use karte hain apne large orders ke execution price decide karne ke liye. Agar institutional buyer VWAP ke around buy karta hai, toh retail trader ko bhi VWAP important lagna chahiye.

Key uses:
• Intraday trend direction
• Support/resistance (dynamic)
• Entry/exit levels
• Institutional activity detection

VWAP Calculation aur Display

Formula

VWAP = Σ(Typical Price × Volume) / Σ(Volume)

Where:
• Typical Price = (High + Low + Close) / 3
• Volume = that candle's volume

Display:
• Ek line chart ke upar draw hoti hai
• Din ke 9:15 AM se start hoti hai
• Din ke 3:30 PM tak rehti hai
• Next din reset ho jaati hai (fresh VWAP)

VWAP Bands

Kuch platforms VWAP ke saath bands bhi dete hain (±1 std dev, ±2 std dev). Ye Bollinger Bands ki tarah kaam karta hai but VWAP ke around.

VWAP Trading Strategies

Strategy 1: VWAP Bounce (Trend Following)

Buy setup:
• Price VWAP ke upar hai = bullish intraday trend
• Price VWAP ki taraf pullback karta hai
• VWAP line ko touch karta hai
• Bounce back upar (bullish candle)
• Entry: Bounce candle close ke baad
• Stop loss: VWAP ke thoda neeche
• Target: Day's high ya previous resistance

Sell setup: Reverse — price below VWAP, pullback to VWAP, rejection.

Strategy 2: VWAP Breakout

Buy setup:
• Price VWAP ke neeche consolidated hai (morning)
• Suddenly price VWAP ko upar break karta hai + volume
• Entry: Breakout candle close ke baad
• Stop loss: VWAP ke thoda neeche
• Target: 1:2 risk-reward minimum

Strategy 3: VWAP + Moving Average Combo:
• VWAP = intraday anchor
• 9 EMA = short-term momentum
• Buy: Price above VWAP + 9 EMA above VWAP = strong bullish
• Sell: Price below VWAP + 9 EMA below VWAP = strong bearish
• Avoid: Price crossing VWAP repeatedly (choppy, no clear trend)

VWAP for Different Times of Day

Morning (9:15 - 10:30 AM):
• VWAP abhi fresh hai, zyada movement
• First 15 min: avoid (high volatility, VWAP unstable)
• 9:30 - 10:30: First meaningful VWAP signals

Midday (11:00 AM - 1:30 PM):
• VWAP stable ho chuki hai
• Best time for VWAP bounce trades
• Lower volatility = more reliable signals

Afternoon (2:00 - 3:30 PM):
• VWAP pura din ka average hai
• Price VWAP se door hai toh mean reversion possible
• Closing auction (3:00-3:30) mein VWAP important for institutional execution

VWAP Limitations

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Sirf intraday ke liye — VWAP din ke shuru se reset hota hai. Daily/weekly charts pe meaningless.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Lagging indicator — VWAP past data ka average hai. Future predict nahi karta.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Choppy din mein useless — Jab price VWAP ke around bar-bar cross karta hai, trend clear nahi.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Opening volatility — Pehle 15 min VWAP unstable hai. Wait till 9:30.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Not a standalone indicator — VWAP + volume + price action combo mein best kaam karta hai.

VWAP aur Institutional Activity

Smart Money Detection:
• Agar price VWAP ke upar hai aur volume high = institutions buying
• Agar price VWAP ke neeche hai aur volume high = institutions selling
• Agar price VWAP ke around hai aur volume low = sideways, institutions inactive

VWAP Reject = Institutional Rejection

Jab price VWAP ko touch karke wapas jaata hai, toh iska matlab institutions ne uss level pe buy/sell kiya hai. Ye ek strong signal hai.

Common Mistakes

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• VWAP ko support/resistance manna har time — VWAP dynamic hai, din bhar move karta hai. Static S/R ki tarah treat mat karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• First 15 min mein trade lena — VWAP unstable hai opening mein. 9:30 ke baad enter karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• VWAP pe daily chart pe use karna — VWAP sirf intraday hai. Daily chart pe 20 SMA use karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Bina volume ke VWAP trade lena — VWAP + volume = meaningful. Bina volume ke VWAP breakout = fake.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Target set na karna — VWAP se entry, but target kya? Day's high/low ya 1:2 R/R set karo.

FAQ

Q1: VWAP kaunse timeframe pe best hai?

1 min, 5 min, aur 15 min charts pe best. Intraday ke liye 5 min + VWAP = classic combo.

Q2: Kya VWAP swing trading mein use ho sakta hai?

Technically haan but not useful. VWAP din ke shuru se reset hota hai. Swing trading ke liye 20/50/200 SMA better hai.

Q3: VWAP aur Moving Average mein difference?

Moving Average = sirf price ka average. VWAP = price + volume ka average. VWAP zyada accurate hai because volume weight karta hai.
