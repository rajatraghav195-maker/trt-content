Options Trading Advanced Strategies — Pata Hona Chahiye

What is Options Trading? Quick Recap

Options ek derivative instrument hai jisme tumhare paas right hota hai (obligation nahi) ek underlying asset ko specific price pe kharidne ya bechne ka, ek specific date tak.

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Call Option: Kharidne ka right (bullish view)
• Put Option: Bechne ka right (bearish view)
• Premium: Option ka price jo tum pay karte ho
• Strike Price: Jis price pe deal hona hai
• Expiry: Jab option expire hota hai

Advanced Strategy 1: Covered Call

Kya hai: Tum already shares hold karte ho aur uspe call option sell karte ho.

Kab use karein:
• Jab tumhare shares pe neutral-to-mildly-bullish view ho
• Jab extra income generate karna ho (premium collect)

Example:
• Tumhare paas 100 shares hain ₹150 ke
• Tum ₹160 strike ka call sell karte ho ₹5 premium pe
• Agar expiry tak share ₹160 se neeche rehta → premium ₹5 profit (₹500 total)
• Agar share ₹165 jaye → profit = premium + (160-150) = ₹15/share = ₹1,500 total
• Agar share ₹160 ke upar jaye → shares exit ho jayenge but profit banao

Fayda: Regular income, downside protection (premium se) Nuksan: Upside capped hai — share agar ₹200 jaye tab bhi ₹160 pe sell karna padega

Advanced Strategy 2: Cash-Secured Put

Kya hai: Tum ek put option sell karte ho aur underlying shares ke liye full cash reserve rakhte ho.

Kab use karein:
• Jab tum ek share kharidna chahte ho but kam price pe
• Jab extra income chahiye while waiting

Example:
• Reliance current price ₹2,500
• Tum ₹2,300 strike ka put sell karte ho ₹30 premium pe
• Agar Reliance ₹2,300 se upar rehta → ₹30 profit (keep premium)
• Agar Reliance ₹2,300 se neeche jaye → shares ₹2,300 pe assign honge (jo tum kharidna chahte the)

Fayda: Shares kam price pe milte hain + premium income Nuksan: Agar share crash kare toh loss hota hai (but original intent tha kharidna)

Advanced Strategy 3: Iron Condor

Kya hai: 4 options combine karke ek range-bound strategy banate hain.

Kab use karein:
• Jab market ko range-bound expect karte ho (low volatility)
• Event ke baad jab big move expected nahi

Structure:
• OTM Call Sell (upper limit)
• Higher OTM Call Buy (protection)
• OTM Put Sell (lower limit)
• Lower OTM Put Buy (protection)

Example:
• Nifty at 22,000
• Sell 22,200 Call, Buy 22,300 Call (bear call spread)
• Sell 21,800 Put, Buy 21,700 Put (bull put spread)
• Profit zone: 21,800-22,200
• Max profit: premium received
• Max loss: (strike difference) - premium received

Fayda: Defined risk, defined reward, range mein profit Nuksan: Profit limited hai, adjustments tricky hain

Advanced Strategy 4: Butterfly Spread

Kya hai: 3 strike prices pe options combine karke ek low-cost, high-reward strategy.

Kab use karein:
• Jab specific price level pe expiry expect karte ho
• Low risk, high reward seekers

Structure (Long Butterfly):
• 1 ATM Call Buy
• 2 OTM Calls Sell
• 1 Further OTM Call Buy

Example:
• Nifty at 22,000
• Buy 22,000 Call, Sell 2x 22,100 Calls, Buy 22,200 Call
• Max profit: at 22,100 on expiry = ₹100 - net premium paid
• Max loss: net premium paid only

Fayda: Bahut low cost, high reward potential Nuksan: Profit sirf ek specific point pe, timing bahut important

Advanced Strategy 5: Calendar Spread

Kya hai: Same strike, same type (call/put), but different expiry pe options buy+sell karna.

Kab use karein:
• Jab volatility increase expect karte ho (near expiry vs far expiry)
• Low cost directional play

Example:
• Sell this week 22,000 Call @ ₹50
• Buy next week 22,000 Call @ ₹80
• Net cost: ₹30
• Agar volatility badhe → far week call zyada appreciate karega → profit

Fayda: Low cost, volatility play Nuksan: Time decay dono sides pe kaam karta hai — timing critical

Option Greeks — Advanced Samjho

Greeks bina options advanced trading possible nahi:

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Delta — Kya Batata Hai: Price change sensitivity, Important: ATM call = 0.5, ATM put = -0.5
• Gamma — Kya Batata Hai: Delta change rate, Important: ATM pe highest, expiry pe highest
• Theta — Kya Batata Hai: Time decay per day, Important: Long options = theta loss, short = theta profit
• Vega — Kya Batata Hai: Volatility sensitivity, Important: High IV = expensive options, low IV = cheap
• Rho — Kya Batata Hai: Interest rate sensitivity, Important: Generally minor impact

Practical use:
• Delta hedging → direction-neutral position banana
• Theta selling → time decay se income generate karna
• Vega play → volatility increase/decrease pe bet lagana

Common Mistakes (Advanced Level):
• Position size bada karna after win — ek winning trade pe confidence badhake next trade ka size double karna. Discipline rakh.
• Adjustment na karna — jab position against jaye, adjust karna seekho, hold aur hope mat karo.
• IV ignore karna — high IV pe options buy karna = time decay + IV crush = double loss.
• Event risk underestimate karna — RBI policy, budget, results, global events pe naked positions mat le.
• Stop loss na lagana — options mein stop loss zaroori, aur trailing stop loss aur bhi zaroori.
• Over-trading — har din trade nahi karna. Best setups pe hi trade karo.

FAQ

Q: Advanced options trading mein kitna capital chahiye? A: Minimum ₹50,000-₹1,00,000 recommended. Defined risk strategies (iron condor, butterfly) se start karo.

Q: Weekly options ya monthly options? A: Beginners ke liye monthly. Weekly options time decay fast hai, experience chahiye.

Q: Hedging kya hai aur kyun zaroori hai? A: Hedging = opposite position leke risk kam karna. Naked options se bhi bada loss ho sakta, hedging se defined rahe.

Q: Options mein consistent profit kaise banaye? A: 3 cheezein — discipline (stop loss follow), patience (sirf best setups), aur risk management (1-2% rule per trade).
