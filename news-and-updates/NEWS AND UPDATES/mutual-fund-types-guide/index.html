Mutual Fund Types — Complete Hindi Guide

Mutual fund ke types — equity, debt, hybrid, index, ELSS, sectoral — complete Hindi guide Indian investors ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 10, 2026 · Features

Mutual Fund Types Overview

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Equity Fund — Invests In: Stocks, Risk: High, Returns: 12-18%, Best For: Growth
• Debt Fund — Invests In: Bonds, Risk: Low, Returns: 6-9%, Best For: Stability
• Hybrid Fund — Invests In: Both, Risk: Medium, Returns: 8-12%, Best For: Balance
• Index Fund — Invests In: Index stocks, Risk: Medium, Returns: 12-14%, Best For: Passive
• ELSS — Invests In: Stocks (tax saving), Risk: Medium, Returns: 12-15%, Best For: Tax saving
• Sectoral Fund — Invests In: Specific sector, Risk: Very High, Returns: Variable, Best For: Sector bet

Equity Funds

Large Cap Fund:
• Invests in: Top 100 companies
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 12-15%
• Best for: Conservative equity investors

Mid Cap Fund:
• Invests in: Companies ranked 101-250
• Risk: High
• Returns: 15-18%
• Best for: Growth seekers

Small Cap Fund:
• Invests in: Companies ranked 251+
• Risk: Very High
• Returns: 18-22%
• Best for: Aggressive investors

Flexi Cap Fund:
• Invests in: Any size company
• Risk: Medium-High
• Returns: 14-16%
• Best for: All investors (most flexible)

Multi Cap Fund:
• Invests in: Min 25% each in large, mid, small
• Risk: Medium-High
• Returns: 14-17%
• Best for: Diversified equity exposure

ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme):
• Invests in: Stocks
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 12-15%
• Lock-in: 3 years
• Tax benefit: Section 80C (₹1.5L deduction)

Sectoral/Thematic Fund:
• Invests in: Specific sector (Banking, IT, Pharma)
• Risk: Very High
• Returns: Variable
• Best for: Sector conviction bets

Debt Funds

Liquid Fund:
• Invests in: Very short-term debt (up to 91 days)
• Risk: Very Low
• Returns: 4-6%
• Best for: Emergency fund, parking money

Short Duration Fund:
• Invests in: Bonds with 1-3 year maturity
• Risk: Low
• Returns: 6-8%
• Best for: 1-3 year goals

Corporate Bond Fund:
• Invests in: High-quality corporate bonds
• Risk: Low-Medium
• Returns: 7-9%
• Best for: Moderate risk-takers

Gilt Fund:
• Invests in: Government securities
• Risk: Low
• Returns: 6-8%
• Best for: Safety-first investors

Dynamic Bond Fund:
• Invests in: Mix of short and long-term bonds
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 7-10%
• Best for: Interest rate plays

Hybrid Funds

Aggressive Hybrid Fund:
• Allocation: 65-80% equity, 20-35% debt
• Risk: Medium-High
• Returns: 10-14%
• Best for: First-time equity investors

Conservative Hybrid Fund:
• Allocation: 10-25% equity, 75-90% debt
• Risk: Low-Medium
• Returns: 7-10%
• Best for: Low-risk investors wanting some equity

Balanced Advantage Fund:
• Allocation: Dynamic (equity-debt mix changes)
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 9-12%
• Best for: Hands-off investors

Index Funds

Nifty 50 Index Fund:
• Tracks: Nifty 50
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 12-14% (market returns)
• Expense ratio: 0.1-0.2%
• Best for: Passive investors, beginners

Nifty Next 50 Index Fund:
• Tracks: Nifty Next 50
• Risk: Medium-High
• Returns: 14-16%
• Best for: Growth + passive

Sensex Index Fund:
• Tracks: BSE Sensex
• Risk: Medium
• Returns: 12-14%
• Best for: BSE believers

Fund Selection Criteria

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Expense ratio — < 1% (equity), < 0.5% (index)
• Track record — 5+ years
• Fund size — > ₹1,000 crore
• Consistency — Beat benchmark in 4/5 years
• Fund manager — Experienced, stable
• Exit load — < 1% (if any)

Recommended Portfolio by Risk
