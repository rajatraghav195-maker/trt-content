SPREADS: EK LEG KHARIDO, EK BECHO — AUR RISK KO DEEWAR DO

Vertical spreads ka logic — kam premium, defined loss, aur uski keemat

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Naked option kharidna ya bechna do extremes hain: ek mein probability aapke khilaaf hai, doosre mein tail risk. Spread beech ka raasta hai — do legs, ek saath, taaki dono taraf ek deewar khadi ho jaaye.

VERTICAL SPREAD KYA HAI

Ek hi expiry mein, ek strike ka option kharidna aur doosre strike ka bechna. Kharida hua leg aapka view hai; becha hua leg uski keemat kam karta hai aur ek taraf ka risk band kar deta hai.

Isse do cheezein hoti hain: net premium kam ho jaata hai, aur maximum profit bhi capped ho jaata hai. Ye trade-off hi poori strategy hai.

CHAAR BUNIYADI SHAKLEIN

Naam alag hain, tarkeeb ek hi hai:
• Bull call spread — neeche ka call kharido, upar ka becho. Halka bullish view, kam laagat, capped profit.
• Bear put spread — upar ka put kharido, neeche ka becho. Halka bearish view, wahi structure ulta.
• Bull put spread — neeche ka put becho, usse aur neeche ka kharid kar hedge karo. Credit aata hai, risk defined rehta hai.
• Bear call spread — upar ka call becho, usse aur upar ka kharid lo. Wahi cheez doosri taraf.

SPREAD KYA THEEK KARTA HAI

Naked selling ki sabse badi dikkat gap risk hai — raat ki khabar par nuksaan ki koi seema nahi. Spread mein doosra leg wahi seema banata hai. Aap pehle se jaante ho ki sabse bura din kitna bura ho sakta hai.

Naked buying ki sabse badi dikkat theta hai. Spread mein aapne jo leg becha hai, uska theta aapke haq mein kaam karta hai, isliye rozana ka kharch kam ho jaata hai.

AUR ISKI KEEMAT KYA HAI

Do legs matlab do baar charges, do baar spread cross karna, aur exit par bhi wahi. Chhote size par ye laagat profit ke muqable bhaari pad jaati hai.

Doosri baat: capped profit sirf kaagaz par acha lagta hai. Jis din aapka view bilkul sahi nikalta hai aur move bada hota hai, spread aapko wahin rok deta hai jahan aapne khud deewar banayi thi. Ye insurance ka premium hai — aur insurance tabhi samajh aata hai jab wo kaam aata hai.

SHURUAAT KAISE HO

Spread ko strategy ki tarah nahi, risk ki paribhasha ki tarah socho. Position lene se pehle ye likh lo: maximum loss kitna, maximum profit kitna, aur break-even kahan. Agar ye teen numbers pehle se nahi likh sakte, to wo spread nahi hai — wo do alag positions hain jo saath baith gayi hain.
