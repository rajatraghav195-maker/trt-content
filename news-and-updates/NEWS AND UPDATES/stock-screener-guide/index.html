Stock Screener Kya Hai?

Stock screener ek tool hai jo tumhare criteria ke basis pe stocks filter karta hai. Jaise job portal pe filters lagate ho (salary, location, experience) — waise stock screener mein filters lagate ho (P/E, market cap, sector).

Simple definition: > Stock screener = "Mujhe aise stocks dikhao jinka PE < 20, market cap > ₹20,000 crore, aur ROE > 15% hai." Screen. Results. Done.

Manual Research vs Screener — Kya Farak Hai?

NSE/BSE pe 5,000+ listed companies hain. Manually har company ka financials check karna practically impossible hai. Screener is problem ko solve karta hai — pehle criteria set karo, phir sirf un stocks ko deeply research karo jo criteria pass karte hain. Ye time bachata hai aur emotional/random stock-picking se bhi bachata hai.

Screening Criteria

Fundamental Filters:
• P/E Ratio — Good Value: < 20, Use Case: Value investing
• P/B Ratio — Good Value: < 3, Use Case: Asset-based valuation
• ROE — Good Value: > 15%, Use Case: Profitability
• ROCE — Good Value: > 15%, Use Case: Capital efficiency
• Debt/Equity — Good Value: < 1, Use Case: Low leverage
• Revenue Growth — Good Value: > 15%, Use Case: Growth stock
• Profit Growth — Good Value: > 15%, Use Case: Profitable growth
• Dividend Yield — Good Value: > 2%, Use Case: Income investing
• Market Cap — Good Value: > ₹20,000 Cr, Use Case: Large-cap filter
• Promoter Holding — Good Value: > 50%, Use Case: Strong ownership

Technical Filters:
• Above 200 DMA — Good Value: Yes, Use Case: Uptrend
• RSI — Good Value: 30-50, Use Case: Pullback in uptrend
• MACD — Good Value: Bullish crossover, Use Case: Trend change
• Volume — Good Value: > 2x average, Use Case: Breakout confirmation
• 52-Week High — Good Value: Near high, Use Case: Momentum
• Support — Good Value: At support level, Use Case: Entry zone

Screening Strategies

Strategy 1: Value Investing Screen:
• P/E < 20
• P/B < 3
• ROE > 15%
• Debt/Equity < 1
• Market Cap > ₹5,000 Cr
• Revenue Growth > 10%

→ Undervalued quality stocks

Strategy 2: Growth Stock Screen:
• Revenue Growth > 20%
• Profit Growth > 25%
• ROE > 18%
• Debt/Equity < 0.5
• Market Cap > ₹10,000 Cr

→ High-growth companies

Strategy 3: Momentum Screen:
• Above 200 DMA: Yes
• RSI: 50-70
• 52-Week High: Within 10%
• Volume: > 1.5x average
• Market Cap > ₹5,000 Cr

→ Stocks in strong uptrend

Strategy 4: Dividend Income Screen:
• Dividend Yield > 3%
• Payout Ratio < 60%
• ROE > 12%
• Market Cap > ₹10,000 Cr
• 5+ years consistent dividend

→ Reliable dividend payers

Strategy 5: Pullback Entry Screen:
• Above 200 DMA: Yes
• RSI: 30-45
• Near 50 DMA support
• MACD: Turning positive
• Volume: Increasing

→ Quality stocks at pullback

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:

• Fundamental filters (P/E, ROE, Market Cap, Debt/Equity) aur technical filters (RSI, DMA, Volume) dono ek jagah set kar sakte ho
• Results ko watchlist mein directly add kar sakte ho further tracking ke liye

Advantage TRT ka: Screening ke baad seedha /charting SUPERCHART pe result stock open kar sakte ho — alag tool switch karne ki zarurat nahi.

Common Screening Mistakes

Too Many Filters

10 filters lagaoge toh 2-3 stocks milenge. 4-5 focused filters use karo.

Only Fundamental OR Technical

Dono combine karo — fundamental se quality dhundho, technical se entry time karo.

Screening Once and Forgetting

Weekly screen karo — new stocks aate hain, old stocks drop out.

Buy Without Research

Screening = starting point. Deep research karo before buying.
