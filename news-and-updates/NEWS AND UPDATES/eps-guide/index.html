EPS kya hai, kaise calculate hota hai, kaise use karein stock analysis mein — complete Hindi guide.

EPS Kya Hai?

EPS ka full form hai Earnings Per Share. Ye batata hai ki company ne per share kitna profit kamaya hai.

EPS = Net Profit / Total Shares Outstanding

Simple definition: > EPS = Company ka total profit ÷ Kitne shares hain. Matlab har ek share pe kitna profit aaya.

Example:
• TCS ka net profit: ₹45,000 crore (annual)
• TCS ke total shares: 365 crore
• EPS: ₹45,000 / 365 = ₹123 per share

Matlab: Har TCS ke share ne ₹123 ka profit generate kiya.

EPS Interpretation

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• EPS badh raha hai — Meaning: Company ka profit grow ho raha hai, Action: Bullish signal ✅
• EPS ghir raha hai — Meaning: Company ka profit kam ho raha hai, Action: Bearish signal ❌
• EPS stable hai — Meaning: Company consistent hai, Action: Neutral
• EPS negative hai — Meaning: Company loss mein hai, Action: Caution ⚠️

EPS Growth Rate

EPS ka YEAR-OVER-YEAR growth zyada important hai than absolute EPS:

EPS Growth Rate = (Current EPS - Previous EPS) / Previous EPS × 100

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• > 20% — High growth 🔥
• 10-20% — Good growth
• 5-10% — Moderate growth
• 0-5% — Slow growth
• Negative — Declining ❌

Types of EPS

1. Trailing EPS (TTM):
• Based on: Last 4 quarters
• Use: Most common, backward-looking
• Formula: Sum of last 4 quarters' EPS

2. Forward EPS:
• Based on: Analyst estimates for next 12 months
• Use: Forward-looking, growth expectations
• Formula: Estimated future EPS

3. Reported EPS:
• Based on: GAAP accounting standards
• Use: Official numbers (can include one-time items)

4. Adjusted EPS:
• Based on: Excluding one-time items
• Use: Core business performance (more useful)

EPS and PE Ratio Connection

PE Ratio = Stock Price / EPS

Example:
• TCS price: ₹3,800
• TCS EPS: ₹123
• PE Ratio: ₹3,800 / ₹123 = 30.9x

Matlab:
• EPS badhega → PE girega (assuming price same) → Stock sasta lagega
• EPS girega → PE badhega → Stock mehenga lagega

Ye connection bahut important hai — EPS growth directly PE ratio ko affect karta hai.

EPS-Based Valuation

Fair Value Calculation:
• Fair Price = EPS × Industry Average PE
• Example:
• Company EPS: ₹50
• Industry PE: 20
• Fair Price: ₹50 × 20 = ₹1,000
• Agar stock ₹800 pe hai → Undervalued (20% discount)
• Agar stock ₹1,200 pe hai → Overvalued (20% premium)

PEG Ratio (EPS + PE Combined):
• PEG = PE Ratio / EPS Growth Rate
• Example:
• PE = 25, EPS Growth = 20%
• PEG = 25 / 20 = 1.25
• PEG < 1 = Undervalued
• PEG 1-2 = Fair value
• PEG > 2 = Overvalued

EPS Red Flags

EPS Growth Without Revenue Growth

Agar EPS badh raha hai lekin revenue flat hai → Cost cutting se profit badha hai. Ye sustainable nahi hota long-term.

One-Time Gains Boosting EPS

Company ne property bech di → EPS ek baar badha. Next year wapas girega. Adjusted EPS dekho.

Share Buybacks Artificially Boosting EPS

Company apne shares buy back karti hai → Shares kam → EPS automatically badhta hai even if profit same hai.

Negative EPS (Loss-Making)

Company loss mein hai toh EPS negative hoga. Ye sab stocks avoid karo unless turnaround story hai.
