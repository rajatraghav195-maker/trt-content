Mutual Fund Returns (Average)

• Large Cap — 1 Year: 15-20%, 3 Years: 12-15%, 5 Years: 12-14%, 10 Years: 11-13%
• Mid Cap — 1 Year: 20-30%, 3 Years: 15-20%, 5 Years: 14-18%, 10 Years: 13-16%
• Small Cap — 1 Year: 25-40%, 3 Years: 18-25%, 5 Years: 16-22%, 10 Years: 14-18%
• Flexi Cap — 1 Year: 18-25%, 3 Years: 14-18%, 5 Years: 13-16%, 10 Years: 12-15%
• Index Fund — 1 Year: 15-20%, 3 Years: 12-15%, 5 Years: 12-14%, 10 Years: 12-14%
• ELSS — 1 Year: 18-25%, 3 Years: 14-18%, 5 Years: 13-16%, 10 Years: 12-15%
• Debt Fund — 1 Year: 6-8%, 3 Years: 6-7%, 5 Years: 6-7%, 10 Years: 6-8%

Returns Reality Check

What to Expect:
• Large Cap/Index: 12-14% long-term
• Mid Cap: 14-18% long-term
• Small Cap: 16-22% long-term (but very volatile)
• Debt: 6-8% (stable, low risk)

What NOT to Expect:
• "50% returns every year" — Unrealistic
• "Guaranteed returns" — Market mein kuch guaranteed nahi
• "Past returns = Future returns" — Not true

Returns vs Inflation

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Savings Account — Returns: 3-4%, Inflation: 6%, Real Returns: -2%
• FD — Returns: 6-7%, Inflation: 6%, Real Returns: 0-1%
• Gold — Returns: 8-10%, Inflation: 6%, Real Returns: 2-4%
• Real Estate — Returns: 8-12%, Inflation: 6%, Real Returns: 2-6%
• Equity MF — Returns: 12-15%, Inflation: 6%, Real Returns: 6-9%

Equity MF beats inflation by 6-9% — best wealth creator.
