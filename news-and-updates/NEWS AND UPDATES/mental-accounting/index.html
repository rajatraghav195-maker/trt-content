MENTAL ACCOUNTING: HAR PAISA EK JAISA HAI, PAR LAGTA NAHI

Profit ka paisa aur apna paisa — dimaag unhe alag rakhta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 31, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Market se jeeta hua paisa alag lagta hai. Log usse zyada risk le lete hain, kyunki wo "house money" jaisa lagta hai. Wo paisa bilkul waisa hi hai jaisa baaki paisa.

YE BUCKETS BANTE KAISE HAIN

Dimaag paise ko uske source ke hisaab se alag alag khaton mein rakhta hai — salary, bonus, profit, gift. Financially wo sab ek hi cheez hai.

Trading mein iska roop ye hota hai ki profit wale paise par discipline dheela ho jaata hai, aur wahi paisa sabse pehle wapas jaata hai.

DOOSRE ROOP

Ye bias aur bhi jagah dikhta hai:
• Ek position mein loss hai aur doosri mein profit — dono ka net dekhne ke bajaye alag alag sochna.
• Ek "long term" bucket banana jahan har phansa hua trade daal diya jaata hai.
• Loan chalte hue nivesh karna, dono ko alag khaton mein rakh kar.
• Tax refund ya bonus ko "extra" maan kar alag rules par kharch karna.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Ek hi net worth number dekhna is bias ka sabse simple ilaaj hai. Jab sab kuch ek hi jagah dikhta hai, to ye sawaal apne aap saaf ho jaata hai ki ye risk poore portfolio ke hisaab se theek hai ya nahi.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
