Stock Market Crash Kya Hai?

Stock market crash tab hota hai jab market bahut tez aur bahut zyada girta hai — usually 10%+ in a few days or weeks. Ye panic selling ki wajah se hota hai.

Definition: > Crash = Market 10%+ gira hai in short time. Panic, fear, sab bech rahe hain.

Famous Indian Market Crashes:
• 2008 Global Crisis — Nifty Drop: -60%, Duration: 12 months, Recovery Time: 5 years
• 2020 COVID — Nifty Drop: -40%, Duration: 2 months, Recovery Time: 6 months
• 2016 Demonetization — Nifty Drop: -6%, Duration: 1 week, Recovery Time: 2 weeks
• 2022 Rate Hikes — Nifty Drop: -18%, Duration: 8 months, Recovery Time: 12 months
• 2015 China Scare — Nifty Drop: -23%, Duration: 11 months, Recovery Time: 14 months

Pattern: Market crash hota hai, but ALWAYS recover hota hai. Timeframe varies — 6 months to 5 years.

Crash Mein Kya Karein? (Action Plan)

Step 1: PANIC MAT KARO

"Paisa doob gaya!" — Sirf tab tak jab tak bechoge nahi. Unrealized loss ≠ realized loss. Market recover hota hai.

Step 2: Portfolio Review Karo:
• Kaunsi stocks hain portfolio mein?
• Quality stocks hain ya junk?
• Debt-free companies hain ya leveraged?
• Long-term fundamentals change hue?

Step 3: Quality Stocks Hold Karo:
• Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank, Infosys — ye sab recover honge
• Inhe BECHNE ka nahi — HOLD karne ka
• Infact, BUY karo agar saste mil rahe hain

Step 4: Weak Stocks Exit Karo:
• Penny stocks, debt-laden companies, management issues — EXIT
• Ye recover nahi honge ya bahut slow honge
• Loss book karo aur quality mein shift karo

Step 5: SIP Continue Karo

"SIP band karta hoon, market girega" — BILKUL GALAT. Market girne pe SIP se SASTE units milte hain. Ye SIP ka sabse bada fayda hai.

Step 6: Cash Reserve Maintain Karo:
• 6 months ka emergency fund hona chahiye
• Crash mein buying opportunity milti hai — cash chahiye
• Borrow karke invest mat karo

Step 7: Buy the Dip (Quality Mein):
• Nifty PE < 18 ho → Buying opportunity
• Quality stocks 30-40% gire hain → Accumulate
• Thoda thoda kharido (don't go all-in)

Crash Mein Kya NAHI Karna

Panic Selling

"Sab bech raha hoon, bachaao!" — Bottom pe sell karna = permanent loss. Market recover hota hai, tumhare sold stocks nahi.

Checking Portfolio Daily

Har din portfolio dekhoge toh anxiety badhegi. Weekly check karo, daily nahi.

Averaging Down (Quality Stocks Ke Alawa)

"Gir raha hai, aur kharid leta hoon" — Sirf QUALITY stocks mein averaging karo. Junk stocks mein averaging = loss double.

Borrow Karke Invest Karna

"Loan leke invest karta hoon, sasta mil raha hai" — Market aur neeche ja sakta hai. Loan pe interest + portfolio loss = double disaster.

Following Tips

"Ye stock recover hoga, kharid lo" — Bina research ke kuch mat karo. Apna analysis karo.

Historical Recovery Data

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 2008 — Bottom: Nifty 2,252, Recovery Level: 6,000, Time to Recover: 5 years
• 2020 — Bottom: Nifty 7,511, Recovery Level: 15,000, Time to Recover: 6 months
• 2022 — Bottom: Nifty 15,183, Recovery Level: 20,000, Time to Recover: 12 months

Lesson: Long-term investors ke liye crash = buying opportunity. Short-term traders ke liye crash = capital preservation.

Crash Se Kaise Prepare Karein?

Pre-Crash Preparation:
• Emergency fund — 6 months expenses in liquid fund
• Diversification — 10-15 stocks across sectors
• No leverage — Bina margin ke invest karo
• Quality stocks — Debt-free, strong fundamentals
• SIP running — Automatic buying during crash

During Crash:
• Hold quality — Mat becho
• Exit junk — Weak stocks se exit
• SIP continue — Cheap units milegi
• Buy quality — Thoda thoda accumulate
• Stay calm — Emotions ko control karo

Post-Crash:
• Review portfolio — Kya sahi kiya, kya galat
• Rebalance — Sector allocation adjust karo
• Learn — Crash se seekho, improve karo
• Prepare for next — Crash phir aayega (always does)
