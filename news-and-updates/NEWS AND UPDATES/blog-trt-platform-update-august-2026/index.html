Introduction

Dosto, TRT (The Retail Trader) ke saath ek naya month shuru ho chuka hai. Pichhle months mein humne bahut kaam kiya tha, aur ab August 2026 mein woh updates finally live ho gaye hain. Aaj hum baat karenge ki kya-naya launch hua, kaise use karna hai, aur aage kya plan hai.

1. Charting Terminal: SUPERCHART Ab Live Hai

Sabse badi khushi ka mauka — hamara charting terminal ab officially live hai SUPERCHART ke naam se.

SUPERCHART Mein Kya Milega?:
• Real-time charts: Nifty, Bank Nifty, aur individual stocks ke real-time data ke saath advanced charting tools
• Multiple timeframes: 1-minute se lekar monthly tak — aapke strategy ke hisaab se choose karein
• Drawing tools: Trendlines, Fibonacci retracement, channels — sab kuch built-in hai
• Indicators: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Moving Averages aur bahut saare technical indicators

Superchart ka main goal tha ki har ek retail trader ko professional-level charting tools mil sakein bina kisi mehngi subscription ke. Ab ye mumkin ho gaya hai.

Kaise Use Karein? Platform par jaayein, Charting section mein jayein, aur apna preferred symbol select karein. Agar aapko technical analysis nahi aata, toh hamari terminology guides padhein — woh bhi ab available hain.

2. 15 Terminology Categories — Market Vocabulary Complete

Traders ke liye sabse bada challenge hota hai market ki language samajhna. Support, resistance, volume, RSI, drawdown — ye terms sunte hi naye traders dar jaate hain.

Isliye humne 15 terminology categories publish ki hain jo pure market vocabulary ko cover karti hain:

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Technical Analysis Basics — Chart patterns, candlestick formations
• Indicators Explained — RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands meanings
• Options Terminology — Call/Put, Greeks, premium, strike price
• Futures Fundamentals — Lot size, margin, expiry concepts
• Market Psychology — Fear, greed, FOMO explained simply
• Risk Management — Position sizing, stop-loss, risk-reward ratio
• Fundamental Terms — P/E ratio, EPS, book value, debt-equity
• Sector Analysis — How sectors rotate and what drives them
• IPO Vocabulary — Greenshoe, anchor investor, book building
• Derivatives Basics — Hedging, speculation, spread strategies
• Volume & Price Action — Volume analysis, price discovery mechanisms
• Candlestick Patterns — Doji, hammer, engulfing patterns explained
• Order Types — Market order, limit order, stop-loss order types
• Market Microstructure — Bid-ask spread, liquidity, slippage concepts
• Macro Terms — GDP, inflation, interest rates impact on markets

Ye saari categories free access ke liye available hain platform par. Koi bhi term samajhna ho, search karein — detailed explanation mil jayegi.

3. Screener: Technical + Fundamental Filters Live

Screener ek aisa tool hai jo millions of stocks mein se unhe filter karta hai jo aapki criteria match karte hain. TRT ka screener ab live hai aur yeh do major categories mein kaam karta hai:

Technical Screener:
• Price above/below moving averages
• RSI oversold/overbought levels
• Volume spike detection
• Pattern screening (head & shoulders, double bottom, etc.)

Fundamental Screener:
• P/E ratio filters
• Debt-to-equity ratio screening
• Revenue growth checks
• Profit margin analysis

Dono ko combine karke aap ek advanced screen bana sakte hain — jaise "stock jo RSI 30 ke neeche ho aur P/E 20 se kam ho." Ye filter strategy traders aur investors dono ke liye useful hai.

4. Market Calendar: IPO, Earnings, Events Sab Ek Jagah

Har trader ko pata hona chahiye ki upcoming mein kya hone wala hai. TRT ka Market Calendar ab sab kuch ek jagah laata hai:

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• IPO Schedule: Naye IPOs ke dates aur details
• Earnings Calendar: Company results ki dates — kab kaunsa quarterly result de raha hai
• Economic Events: RBI decisions, GDP data, inflation numbers, employment reports
• Options Expiry: Weekly aur monthly expiry dates

Calendar ko subscribe karke aap notifications bhi set kar sakte hain. Taaki koi important event miss na ho.
