Asset Allocation Kya Hai?

Asset allocation ka matlab hai apne investments ko alag-alag asset classes mein distribute karna — stocks, bonds, gold, cash — based on risk tolerance, goals, aur time horizon.

Simple definition: > Asset allocation = "Kitna paisa kahan lagana hai." Stocks mein kitna, bonds mein kitna, gold mein kitna — ye decide karna.

Main Asset Classes

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Equity (Stocks) — Risk: High, Return: 12-15%, Best For: Growth, long-term
• Debt (Bonds/FD) — Risk: Low, Return: 6-8%, Best For: Stability, income
• Gold — Risk: Medium, Return: 8-10%, Best For: Hedge, inflation protection
• Real Estate — Risk: Medium, Return: 8-12%, Best For: Long-term wealth
• Cash — Risk: Very Low, Return: 3-4%, Best For: Emergency, liquidity

Asset Allocation by Age

Rule of Thumb: 100 - Age = Equity %:
• Age 25: 75% equity, 25% debt/gold
• Age 35: 65% equity, 35% debt/gold
• Age 45: 55% equity, 45% debt/gold
• Age 55: 45% equity, 55% debt/gold
• Age 65: 35% equity, 65% debt/gold

Detailed Age-Based Allocation:
• 20-30 — Equity: 70-80%, Debt: 10-15%, Gold: 5-10%, Cash: 5%
• 30-40 — Equity: 60-70%, Debt: 15-25%, Gold: 5-10%, Cash: 5%
• 40-50 — Equity: 50-60%, Debt: 25-35%, Gold: 5-10%, Cash: 5-10%
• 50-60 — Equity: 35-45%, Debt: 35-45%, Gold: 10%, Cash: 10-15%
• 60+ — Equity: 20-30%, Debt: 45-55%, Gold: 10%, Cash: 15-20%

Asset Allocation by Risk Profile

Conservative (Low Risk):
• Large Cap / Index Fund — 30%
• Debt / FD — 40%
• Gold — 15%
• Cash — 15%

Balanced (Medium Risk):
• Large + Mid Cap — 50%
• Debt — 25%
• Gold — 15%
• Cash — 10%

Aggressive (High Risk):
• Large + Mid + Small Cap — 70%
• International — 10%
• Gold — 10%
• Debt — 5%
• Cash — 5%

Asset Allocation by Goal

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Emergency fund — Time Horizon: Immediate, Allocation: 100% cash/liquid fund
• Vacation — Time Horizon: 6-12 months, Allocation: 80% debt, 20% equity
• Car — Time Horizon: 2-3 years, Allocation: 50% equity, 50% debt
• House down payment — Time Horizon: 3-5 years, Allocation: 40% equity, 50% debt, 10% gold
• Child education — Time Horizon: 10-15 years, Allocation: 70% equity, 20% debt, 10% gold
• Retirement — Time Horizon: 20+ years, Allocation: 80% equity, 10% debt, 10% gold

How to Implement

Step 1: Decide Risk Tolerance:
• Kitna loss sah sakte ho?
• Time horizon kitna hai?
• Income stable hai?

Step 2: Choose Allocation:
• Age-based rule follow karo
• Risk profile ke according adjust karo

Step 3: Select Instruments:
• Equity — Index Fund, Flexi Cap, ETF
• Debt — Liquid Fund, Short Duration Fund
• Gold — Gold ETF, SGB
• Cash — Savings Account, Liquid Fund

Step 4: Invest & Rebalance:
• SIP se start karo
• Yearly rebalance karo
• Age badhne ke saath adjust karo

Asset Allocation Mistakes

100% Equity

"Bahut aggressive hoon!" — Ek crash mein 50% loss. Debt/gold zaroori hai for stability.

100% Debt/FD

"Risk nahi lena" — Inflation se purchasing power girti hai. Equity zaroori hai for growth.

No Gold

Gold hedge ka kaam karta hai. Jab stocks girein, gold usually badhta hai.

Never Adjust

25 saal ki umar mein allocation aur 55 mein same nahi hona chahiye. Age ke saath adjust karo.

Emotional Allocation

"Bull market hai, 100% equity rakhta hoon" — Plan ke according rakhao, emotions se nahi.
