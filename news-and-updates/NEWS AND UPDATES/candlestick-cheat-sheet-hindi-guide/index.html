Candlestick Patterns Cheat Sheet — Hinglish Complete Guide

Candlestick Kya Hai? Quick Recap

Ek candle = 4 prices:
• Open: Candle ka start price
• High: Period ka highest price
• Low: Period ka lowest price
• Close: Candle ka end price

Green/White candle = Close > Open (bullish) Red/Black candle = Close < Open (bearish)

Upper shadow (wick): High - max(Open, Close) Lower shadow (tail): min(Open, Close) - Low

SINGLE CANDLE PATTERNS

1. Doji (दोजी):
• Dikhta kaise: Open aur Close almost same — body bahut chhoti ya line jaisi
• Matlab: Indecision — buyers aur sellers mein balance
• Action: Trend context mein dekho. Uptrend ke baad doji = reversal possible. Downtrend ke baad doji = bottom possible.
• Types:
• Standard Doji: Body almost invisible
• Long-legged Doji (Rickshaw man): Long shadows dono sides — high volatility, indecision
• Dragonfly Doji: Long lower shadow, no upper shadow — bullish reversal signal (downtrend mein)
• Gravestone Doji: Long upper shadow, no lower shadow — bearish reversal signal (uptrend mein)

2. Hammer (हैमर):
• Dikhta kaise: Chhoti body (top pe), long lower shadow (2x body se zyada), no/very small upper shadow
• Matlab: Sellers ne price neeche push kiya lekin buyers ne wapas khareecha — bullish rejection
• Action: Downtrend ke end mein appear ho to bullish reversal signal. Confirmation ke liye next candle green ho.
• Color: Green ya red dono valid hain, lekin green zyada strong

3. Hanging Man (लटकता आदमी):
• Dikhta kaise: Hammer jaisa hi — chhoti body, long lower shadow, no upper shadow
• Matlab: Same as hammer BUT uptrend ke top pe appear ho to bearish reversal
• Action: Uptrend ke baad yeh appear ho to caution. Next candle red ho to sell.
• Color: Red zyada bearish

4. Shooting Star (टिनका तारा):
• Dikhta kaise: Chhoti body (bottom pe), long upper shadow (2x body se zyada), no/very small lower shadow
• Matlab: Buyers ne price upar push kiya lekin sellers ne wapas becha — bearish rejection
• Action: Uptrend ke top pe appear ho to bearish reversal signal. Next candle red ho to confirm.
• Color: Red zyada strong

5. Inverted Hammer (उल्टा हैमर):
• Dikhta kaise: Shooting Star jaisa — chhoti body (bottom pe), long upper shadow
• Matlab: Downtrend ke bottom pe appear ho to bullish reversal signal
• Action: Confirmation ke liye next candle green ho. Gap up green = strong buy signal.

6. Spinning Top (घूमता लट्टू):
• Dikhta kaise: Chhoti body, long shadows dono sides
• Matlab: Indecision — volatility high lekin direction clear nahi
• Action: Trend ke context mein dekho. Breakout/breakdown ke baad spinning top = pause, trend continue ho sakta ya reverse.

7. Marubozu (मारुबोज़ू):
• Dikhta kaise: No shadows (ya bahut chhote) — sirf body
• Green Marubozu: Open = Low, Close = High — sabse bullish candle
• Red Marubozu: Open = High, Close = Low — sabse bearish candle
• Matlab: Full control — green mein buyers dominant, red mein sellers dominant
• Action: Trend direction mein strong move. Green marubozu support pe = buy. Red marubozu resistance pe = sell.

TWO CANDLE PATTERNS

8. Bullish Engulfing (बुलिश एंगल्फिंग):
• Dikhta kaise: Pehli red candle, doosri green candle — green body pehle red body ko completely cover karti hai
• Matlab: Buyers ne sellers ko overwhelm kar diya — strong bullish reversal
• Action: Downtrend ke bottom pe appear ho to strong buy signal. Next candle green ho to confirm.
• Volume: High volume pe engulfing = zyada strong

9. Bearish Engulfing (बेयरिश एंगल्फिंग):
• Dikhta kaise: Pehli green candle, doosri red candle — red body pehle green body ko completely cover karti hai
• Matlab: Sellers ne buyers ko overwhelm — strong bearish reversal
• Action: Uptrend ke top pe appear ho to strong sell signal. Next candle red ho to confirm.

10. Tweezer Top (ट्वीज़र टॉप):
• Dikhta kaise: Do candles ka high same level pe, pehli green doosri red
• Matlab: Resistance pe rejection — buyers dono baar fail
• Action: Uptrend ke top pe = bearish reversal signal. Stop loss = tweezer high ke upar.

11. Tweezer Bottom (ट्वीज़र बॉटम):
• Dikhta kaise: Do candles ka low same level pe, pehli red doosri green
• Matlab: Support pe rejection — sellers dono baar fail
• Action: Downtrend ke bottom pe = bullish reversal signal. Stop loss = tweezer low ke neeche.

12. Harami (हरमी):
• Bullish Harami: Pehli red (big), doosri green (small, pehli ke body ke andar)
• Matlab: Sellers weak ho rahe, buyers enter — reversal signal
• Action: Downtrend mein = bullish reversal. Confirmation next green candle.
• Bearish Harami: Pehli green (big), doosri red (small, pehli ke body ke andar)
• Matlab: Buyers weak, sellers enter — reversal signal
• Action: Uptrend mein = bearish reversal. Confirmation next red candle.

13. Piercing Line (भेदी लाइन):
• Dikhta kaise: Pehli red candle, doosri green candle — green ka Open pehle red ke low se neeche, Close red ki body ke aadhe se upar
• Matlab: Sellers ne gap down kiya but buyers ne wapas push — bullish reversal
• Action: Downtrend ke bottom pe = strong buy signal

14. Dark Cloud Cover (काला बादल):
• Dikhta kaise: Pehli green candle, doosri red candle — red ka Open pehle green ke high se upar, Close green ki body ke aadhe se neeche
• Matlab: Buyers ne gap up kiya but sellers ne push down — bearish reversal
• Action: Uptrend ke top pe = strong sell signal

THREE CANDLE PATTERNS

15. Morning Star (मॉर्निंग स्टार):
• Dikhta kaise: 3 candles — (1) Red big, (2) Small body (green ya red) gap down, (3) Green big candle jo pehli red ke body ke aadhe se upar close
• Matlab: Sellers exhausted, indecision, buyers take control — bullish reversal
• Action: Downtrend ke bottom pe = very strong buy signal. Volume increasing = stronger.

16. Evening Star (ईवनिंग स्टार):
• Dikhta kaise: 3 candles — (1) Green big, (2) Small body gap up, (3) Red big candle jo pehli green ke body ke aadhe se neeche close
• Matlab: Buyers exhausted, indecision, sellers take control — bearish reversal
• Action: Uptrend ke top pe = very strong sell signal. Volume increasing = stronger.

17. Three White Soldiers (तीन सफेद सैनिक):
• Dikhta kaise: 3 consecutive green candles, each higher close, each higher high, small lower shadows
• Matlab: Strong sustained buying — bullish reversal/continuation
• Action: Downtrend ke baad = strong reversal. Support pe = strong buy. All 3 candles increasing volume = strongest.

18. Three Black Crows (तीन काले कौवे):
• Dikhta kaise: 3 consecutive red candles, each lower close, each lower low, small upper shadows
• Matlab: Strong sustained selling — bearish reversal/continuation
• Action: Uptrend ke baad = strong reversal. Resistance pe = strong sell. All 3 candles increasing volume = strongest.

19. Three Inside Up:
• Dikhta kaise: (1) Red big, (2) Green small (harami pattern), (3) Green bigger jo pehli red ki high se upar close
• Matlab: Harami confirmation + breakout — bullish reversal confirmed
• Action: Strong buy signal. Stop loss = pehli red ka low.

20. Three Inside Down:
• Dikhta kaise: (1) Green big, (2) Red small (harami pattern), (3) Red bigger jo pehli green ki low se neeche close
• Matlab: Harami confirmation + breakdown — bearish reversal confirmed
• Action: Strong sell signal. Stop loss = pehli green ka high.

IMPORTANT RULES:
• Context is king: Candlestick patterns kabhi akele trade mat karo. Trend, support/resistance, volume — sab dekho.
• Confirmation: 1-2 candles ka wait karo confirm ke liye. Breakout pe enter karo, na ki pattern pe.
• Volume matters: High volume pe pattern = strong. Low volume = weak signal.
• Timeframe: Higher timeframe (daily/weekly) patterns = zyada reliable. 1-min/5-min = noise.
• Stop loss hamesha: Pattern ke low/high ke neeche/upar stop loss lagao.
• Don't force patterns: Har candle pattern nahi hai. Sirf clear, obvious patterns trade karo.

FAQ

Q: Candlestick patterns kitne reliable hain? A: Akele 60-70%. Indicator + support/resistance ke saath 80%+. Context se reliability badhti hai.

Q: Best timeframe candlestick trading ke liye? A: Daily aur weekly sabse reliable. Intraday ke liye 15-min. 1-min avoid karo (noise).

Q: Har candle pattern trade karna chahiye? A: Nahi. Sirf clear, obvious, context-supported patterns. Ambiguous patterns skip karo.

Q: Candlestick + RSI combo kaise use kare? A: Bullish candlestick + RSI oversold = strong buy. Bearish candlestick + RSI overbought = strong sell. Confluence = confirmation.
