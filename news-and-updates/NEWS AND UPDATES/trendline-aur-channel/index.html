TRENDLINE: EK LINE JO AAP KHUD KHEENCHTE HO

Sahi trendline kaise banti hai, channel kya batata hai, aur objectivity kaise bachayein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Trendline chart ka sabse subjective tool hai. Do log ek hi chart par do alag lines kheench lenge, aur dono apne aap ko sahi bata payenge. Isliye trendline ka asli sabak uske rules mein nahi, uske discipline mein hai.

BANTI KAISE HAI

Uptrend mein badhte hue lows ko jodo. Downtrend mein girte hue highs ko. Kam se kam do points chahiye line banane ke liye, aur teesra point hi use valid karta hai.

Do point se line hamesha ban jaati hai — kisi bhi do points se ek line ban sakti hai. Isliye do-point wali trendline koi jaankari nahi hai, wo sirf ek geometry hai.

WICK SE JODEIN YA BODY SE

Iska koi ek sahi jawab nahi hai. Bodies se banayi line shor kam dikhati hai; wicks se banayi line asli extremes ko izzat deti hai.

Zaroori sirf ye hai ki aap ek tarika chuno aur usi par tike raho. Jo trader har baar wahi tarika chunta hai jisse line 'kaam karti dikhe', wo chart nahi padh raha — apni umeed padh raha hai.

CHANNEL — DONO TARAF KI LINE

Agar trendline ke samanantar doosri line bhi price ko chhoo rahi ho, to ek channel ban jaata hai. Channel batata hai ki chaal kis daayre mein chal rahi hai.

Channel ke andar price ka upper line tak na pahunch paana aksar pehla nishan hota hai ki trend thak raha hai — breakdown se pehle.

TOOTNE KA MATLAB

Trendline tootne par teen sawaal:
• Kis timeframe par tooti — 15-minute ki line tootna aur weekly line tootna ek baat nahi hai.
• Kaise tooti — ek chhoti candle se sarak kar, ya ek badi candle se dhakka dekar? Dono ka matlab alag hai.
• Volume kya keh raha tha — bina participation ke tootna aksar wapas ho jaata hai.

SABSE ZAROORI DISCIPLINE

Trendline peeche se kheenchi jaati hai par aage ke liye use hoti hai. Isliye ek line kheench kar uska screenshot le lena aur baad mein khud check karna ki wo kaam aayi ya nahi — ye ek chhoti si aadat hai jo trendline ko bhrm se hataakar tool bana deti hai.

Aur agar aapko ek chart par teen se zyada lines kheenchni pad rahi hain tab jaakar structure dikh raha hai, to structure hai hi nahi.
