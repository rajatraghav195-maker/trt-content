Stock Market Se Paisa Kaise Nikalein — Hindi Guide

Stocks se profit kaise book karein, withdrawal process — complete Hindi guide beginners ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 12, 2026 · Features

Stocks se profit kaise book karein, withdrawal process — complete Hindi guide beginners ke liye.

Stocks Se Paisa Kaise Nikalein?

Step 1: Shares Becho:
• Broker app kholo (Zerodha/Groww)
• Portfolio mein jao
• Stock select karo
• "Sell" button dabao
• Quantity aur order type select karo
• Confirm karo

Step 2: Settlement (T+1)

Shares bechne ke baad:
• T+1 day mein paisa trading account mein aata hai
• Trading account se bank account mein transfer karo

Step 3: Bank Transfer:
• Broker app mein "Funds" section
• "Withdraw" pe click karo
• Amount daalo
• Bank account mein transfer (usually same day or next day)

Profit Booking Strategy

Systematic Profit Booking:
• +20% — 25% shares becho
• +40% — 25% aur becho
• +60% — 25% aur becho
• +100% — Remaining becho

Target-Based Booking:
• Target price set karo pehle se
• Target hit → Sell
• SL set karo downside protection ke liye

Withdrawal Timings

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Shares sell — Instant (market hours)
• Settlement — T+1 day
• Trading → Bank — Same day (if before cut-off)
• Bank credit — 1-2 hours (NEFT/IMPS)

Tax on Withdrawal

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• < 1 year (STCG) — 20%
• > 1 year (LTCG) — 12.5% above ₹1.25L
