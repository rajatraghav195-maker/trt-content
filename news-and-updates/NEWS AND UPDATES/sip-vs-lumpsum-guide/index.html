SIP vs Lump Sum — Kya Better Hai? (Hindi)

SIP aur lump sum mein kya fark hai, kaunsa better hai, kab kaunsa use karein — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 16, 2026 · Features

SIP Kya Hai?

SIP ka full form hai Systematic Investment Plan. Har month fixed amount automatically invest hota hai mutual fund mein.

Simple definition: > SIP = Har month ₹X invest karna, automatically. Timing ki zaroorat nahi.

SIP Example:
• Monthly SIP: ₹10,000
• Fund: Nifty 50 Index Fund
• Duration: 10 years
• Expected return: 12%

Result:
• Total invested: ₹12,00,000 (₹10K × 120 months)
• Estimated value: ₹23,00,000+
• Profit: ₹11,00,000+

Lump Sum Kya Hai?

Lump sum ka matlab hai ek baar mein poora paisa invest karna.

Simple definition: > Lump sum = Ek baar mein ₹5,00,000 invest karna. SIP mein ₹10,000/month × 50 months = same amount.

Lump Sum Example:
• One-time investment: ₹12,00,000
• Fund: Nifty 50 Index Fund
• Duration: 10 years
• Expected return: 12%

Result:
• Total invested: ₹12,00,000
• Estimated value: ₹37,00,000+
• Profit: ₹25,00,000+

SIP vs Lump Sum — Comparison

Ye das baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Investment — SIP: Monthly installments, Lump Sum: One-time
• Market timing — SIP: Not needed (auto-averaged), Lump Sum: Timing matters
• Risk — SIP: Lower (rupee cost averaging), Lump Sum: Higher (all at once)
• Best market — SIP: Any market, Lump Sum: Best at market bottom
• Worst market — SIP: N/A, Lump Sum: Worst at market top
• Returns — SIP: Slightly lower (delayed investment), Lump Sum: Higher (if timed right)
• Discipline — SIP: Automatic, Lump Sum: Manual
• Psychology — SIP: Easy (auto-debit), Lump Sum: Hard (fear of investing all)
• Best for — SIP: Salaried people, Lump Sum: People with large capital
• Minimum — SIP: ₹500/month, Lump Sum: ₹5,000+

Returns Comparison — Math

Scenario 1: Market Goes Up (Bull Market)

SIP: ₹10,000/month for 12 months, market up 20%

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Total invested: ₹1,20,000
• Value: ~₹1,32,000
• Return: ~10% (averaged out)

Lump Sum: ₹1,20,000 on Day 1, market up 20%

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Total invested: ₹1,20,000
• Value: ₹1,44,000
• Return: 20%

Winner: Lump sum (agar market upar jaaye)

Scenario 2: Market Goes Down (Bear Market)

SIP: ₹10,000/month for 12 months, market down 20%

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Total invested: ₹1,20,000
• Value: ~₹1,08,000
• Loss: ~10% (averaged out, cheap units mili)

Lump Sum: ₹1,20,000 on Day 1, market down 20%

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Total invested: ₹1,20,000
• Value: ₹96,000
• Loss: 20%

Winner: SIP (rupee cost averaging se kam loss)

Scenario 3: Market Volatile (Up-Down)

SIP: Averaged out, smooth returns Lump Sum: Wild ride, unpredictable

Winner: SIP (stability)

Rupee Cost Averaging — SIP Ka Secret

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?:
• Jan — NAV: ₹100, Units Bought (₹10K): 100.0
• Feb — NAV: ₹80, Units Bought (₹10K): 125.0
• Mar — NAV: ₹90, Units Bought (₹10K): 111.1
• Apr — NAV: ₹110, Units Bought (₹10K): 90.9
• May — NAV: ₹100, Units Bought (₹10K): 100.0
• Total — Units Bought (₹10K): 527.0 units

Average NAV: ₹10,000 × 5 / 527 = ₹94.87 (market ka average ₹96 tha)

SIP ne tumhe average price se SASTA diya — kyunki girne pe zyada units mili.

Kab SIP Karo, Kab Lump Sum?

SIP Best Hai Jab:
• ✅ Salaried ho (monthly income)
• ✅ Market direction unclear hai
• ✅ Large capital nahi hai
• ✅ Timing nahi karna chahte
• ✅ Discipline chahiye
• ✅ Beginner ho

Lump Sum Best Hai Jab:
• ✅ Large capital available hai
• ✅ Market bahut gira hai (bottom ke paas)
• ✅ Long-term horizon (5+ years)
• ✅ Market timing seekha hai
• ✅ Experienced investor ho
• ✅ Bonus/inheritance mila hai

Hybrid Approach (Best of Both):

STP (Systematic Transfer Plan):
• Lump sum debt fund mein daalo
• Monthly equity fund mein transfer karo
• Best of both: lump sum invest + SIP averaging

Common Myths

Myth 1: SIP Hamesha Better Hai

Nahi! Agar market bottom pe ho toh lump sum zyada profit dega. SIP har situation mein best nahi hai.

Myth 2: Lump Sum Risky Hai

Depends on timing. Market bottom pe lump sum = best decision ever. Market top pe lump sum = worst decision.

Myth 3: SIP Band Karo Jab Market Gire

BILKUL GALAT. Market girne pe SIP CONTINUE karo — cheap units mil rahi hain. Ye SIP ka sabse bada fayda hai.

Myth 4: ₹500 SIP Se Kuch Nahi Hoga

₹500/month × 20 years × 12% = ₹4,99,574. Compound interest ka kamaal. Start chhota, scale up later.
