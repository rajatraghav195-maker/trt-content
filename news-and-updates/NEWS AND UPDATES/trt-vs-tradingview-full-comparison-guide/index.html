Where TRT Wins Decisively

1. Hinglish Education (Unbeatable Moat)

TradingView will NEVER make 200+ Hinglish articles. Their business model is global — they can't justify India-specific Hinglish content. TRT IS the Hinglish market education platform.

2. Price Accessibility

₹199/month vs ₹1,500+/month. For a Tier-2/3 Indian trader earning ₹15,000-30,000/month, TradingView is unaffordable. TRT Pro costs less than one Zomato order per week.

3. India-First Design

NSE/BSE native, Indian market calendar, IPO calendar, Indian index details with full fundamental data — TRT is built for India first. TradingView is global-first, India is an afterthought.

4. Beginner Onboarding

TRT's 15 terminology categories in Hinglish take a complete beginner from "share market kya hai" to understanding order flow, F&O, risk management. TradingView assumes you already know trading.

5. Educational Positioning

TRT is education-first, tools-second. TradingView is tools-first, education-later. Different audiences, different funnels.

Where TradingView Wins Decisively

1. Charting Power

Pine Script, custom indicators, 100K+ community indicators, exotic chart types, second-based intervals, multi-timeframe analysis — TradingView is a professional-grade platform.

2. Ecosystem

Broker integrations, paper trading, backtesting, API access, desktop app, mobile app — complete trading operating system.

3. Community

50M+ users, millions of shared ideas, creator monetization — network effect moat.

4. AI

AI Copilot beta live. TRT's AI Intel is still "Coming Soon."

5. Global Coverage

Stocks, crypto, forex, commodities, bonds — all global markets in one platform.

Final Verdict

TradingView is the better platform. TRT is the better choice for most Indian retail traders.
