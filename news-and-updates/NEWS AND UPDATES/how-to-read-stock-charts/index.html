Stock Charts Kya Hain?

Stock charts ek visual representation hai ki price ne time ke saath kya kiya — upar gaya, neeche gaya, ya same raha. Charts se traders patterns identify karte hain aur trading decisions lete hain.

Types of Charts

1. Line Chart:
• Kya hai: Sirf closing prices ko line se connect karta hai
• Best for: Quick overview, trend identification
• Pros: Simple, clean
• Cons: No open/high/low info

2. Candlestick Chart:
• Kya hai: Har candle open, high, low, close dikhata hai
• Best for: Detailed analysis, pattern recognition
• Pros: Maximum information
• Cons: Thoda complex beginners ke liye

3. Bar Chart:
• Kya hai: OHLC bars (similar to candlestick)
• Best for: Western technical analysis
• Pros: Clear OHLC
• Cons: Less visual than candlesticks

Candlestick Chart Reading

Anatomy of a Candle:
• | ← Upper Wick (High)
• │ │ ← Body (Open to Close)
• | ← Lower Wick (Low)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Green/White candle: Close > Open (price badha) → Bullish
• Red/Black candle: Close < Open (price gira) → Bearish
• Body: Open aur Close ka difference
• Wicks: High aur Low tak ka movement

Chart Timeframes

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1-min — Candle Represents: 1 minute ka OHLC, Best For: Scalping
• 5-min — Candle Represents: 5 minutes ka OHLC, Best For: Intraday
• 15-min — Candle Represents: 15 minutes ka OHLC, Best For: Intraday
• 1-hour — Candle Represents: 1 hour ka OHLC, Best For: Day trading
• Daily — Candle Represents: 1 din ka OHLC, Best For: Swing trading
• Weekly — Candle Represents: 1 week ka OHLC, Best For: Positional
• Monthly — Candle Represents: 1 month ka OHLC, Best For: Long-term

Rule: Higher timeframe pe trend identify karo, lower timeframe pe entry lo.

Key Chart Patterns

Reversal Patterns:
• Head & Shoulders — Bearish reversal
• Inverse Head & Shoulders — Bullish reversal
• Double Top — Bearish reversal
• Double Bottom — Bullish reversal
• Rising Wedge — Bearish
• Falling Wedge — Bullish

Continuation Patterns:
• Flag — Trend continuation
• Pennant — Trend continuation
• Triangle — Breakout expected
• Rectangle — Range, then breakout

Chart Reading Steps

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Higher timeframe dekho — Weekly/daily pe trend identify karo
• Support/Resistance mark karo — Key levels draw karo
• Patterns dhundho — Reversal ya continuation
• Indicators lagao — RSI, MACD, Moving Averages
• Volume check karo — Confirmation ke liye
• Entry/exit plan banao — Based on analysis
