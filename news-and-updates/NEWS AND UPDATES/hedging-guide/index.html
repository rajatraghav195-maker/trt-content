Hedging Ki Buniyadi Samajh — Portfolio Protection Guide (Hindi)

Hedging kya hai aur apne portfolio ko kaise protect karein — complete Hindi guide. Put options, protective calls, portfolio hedging strategies sab samjhein.

Hedging Kya Hai?

Hedging ka matlab hai apne existing position ko loss se protect karna. Jaise insurance — ghar ka insurance karate ho toh ghar jalne pe paisa milta hai. Hedging bhi wahi hai — portfolio gire toh hedge profit dega.

Simple definition: > Hedging = Apne investment ka insurance. Portfolio gire toh hedge se profit aaye — loss kam ho.

Hedging Ka Analogy

Socho tumne ₹5,00,000 ka RELIANCE ka stock kharida. Tum bullish ho, lekin darr hai ki results ke baad girega.

Without hedge: RELIANCE 10% gira → ₹50,000 loss With hedge: RELIANCE 10% gira → ₹50,000 loss on stock + ₹35,000 profit on hedge = Net loss ₹15,000

Hedge ne loss ₹50,000 se ₹15,000 kar diya. Insurance jaisa — premium diya, protection mila.

Hedging Ke 3 Main Tarike

Tarika 1: Protective Put (Sabse Simple)

Setup:
• Tumhare paas RELIANCE ka stock hai (long position)
• RELIANCE ka PUT option kharido (insurance)
• Agar stock gira → Put option profit dega → loss offset

Example:
• RELIANCE at ₹2,500
• Tumhare paas 100 shares (₹2,50,000 investment)
• Kharido: 2,400 PE @ ₹30 (insurance premium)
• Cost: ₹30 × 100 = ₹3,000

Scenario 1: RELIANCE gira ₹2,300

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock loss: 100 × ₹200 = ₹20,000
• Put profit: 100 × ₹70 = ₹7,000
• Net loss: ₹13,000 (instead of ₹20,000)

Scenario 2: RELIANCE badha ₹2,700

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock profit: 100 × ₹200 = ₹20,000
• Put loss: ₹3,000 (premium paid — expires worthless)
• Net profit: ₹17,000

Hedge ka cost = Premium paid = ₹3,000. Ye tumhara insurance premium hai.

Tarika 2: Covered Call (Income + Protection)

Setup:
• Tumhare paas stock hai (long position)
• CALL option sell karo (premium milega)
• Premium = Income (thoda protection bhi)

Example:
• RELIANCE at ₹2,500
• Tumhare paas 100 shares
• Sell: 2,600 CE @ ₹40
• Income: ₹40 × 100 = ₹4,000

Scenario 1: RELIANCE same rahe ya thoda gira

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock: No change ya small loss
• Call premium: ₹4,000 profit (retained)
• Net: ₹4,000 income ✅

Scenario 2: RELIANCE badha ₹2,700

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock profit: ₹20,000
• Call loss: ₹6,000 (2,600 se upar jaane pe)
• Net profit: ₹14,000 (limited upside, but profit)

Covered call = Income strategy + thoda downside protection. Upside limited hai, but regular income milti hai.

Tarika 3: Collar (Both Sides Protected)

Setup:
• Tumhare paas stock hai
• PUT option kharido (downside protection)
• CALL option sell karo (premium se PUT ka cost offset)

Example:
• RELIANCE at ₹2,500
• Buy: 2,400 PE @ ₹30 (protection)
• Sell: 2,600 CE @ ₹40 (income)
• Net cost: ₹30 - ₹40 = +₹10 (FREE hedge!)

Collar ka fayda: Downside protected, upside limited, but cost bahut kam ya FREE.

Portfolio Hedging (Index Level)

Nifty Put Hedge

Setup:
• Tumhare paas diversified stock portfolio hai
• Nifty PUT option kharido
• Agar market gira → Nifty Put profit → portfolio loss offset

Example:
• Portfolio value: ₹10,00,000
• Nifty at 20,000
• Buy: 19,500 PE (monthly expiry) @ ₹80
• Lot size: 25
• Cost: ₹80 × 25 = ₹2,000 per lot

Hedge ratio: Portfolio value / (Nifty level × lot size) = ₹10,00,000 / (20,000 × 25) = 2 lots

Total hedge cost: ₹2,000 × 2 = ₹4,000

Agar Nifty 5% gira (19,000):
• Portfolio loss: ~₹50,000 (5%)
• Put profit: ~₹25,000 per lot × 2 = ₹50,000
• Net loss: ₹0 (fully hedged!)

Insurance cost: ₹4,000 per month (0.4% of portfolio).

Hedging Ke Rules

Rule 1: Hedge Cost Kam Rakho

Hedge ka cost 0.5-1% of portfolio se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Zyada expensive hedge = returns kha jaayega.

Rule 2: Hedge Hamesha Rakho (Events Ke Time):
• Quarterly results se pehle
• Budget day se pehle
• Election results se pehle
• RBI policy se pehle
• Global events (US Fed, wars) se pehle

Rule 3: Hedge Close Karo Jab Danger Khatam

Event khatam → hedge close karo. Premium waste mat hone do.

Rule 4: Perfect Hedge Impossible Hai

100% protection possible nahi hai. Hedge 70-80% protection deta hai. Accept karo.

Rule 5: Hedging ≠ Profit Strategy

Hedging LOSS prevention hai, profit strategy nahi. Hedge ka cost tumhara insurance premium hai. Profit stock se aana chahiye, hedge se nahi.

Hedging Cost Comparison

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Protective Put — Cost: 0.5-2% per month, Protection: Downside fully protected, Best For: Stock holders
• Covered Call — Cost: FREE (income), Protection: Limited upside, thoda downside, Best For: Income seekers
• Collar — Cost: FREE or minimal, Protection: Both sides protected, Best For: Conservative
• Index Put — Cost: 0.3-0.5% per month, Protection: Portfolio-level protection, Best For: Diversified portfolios

Common Hedging Mistakes

Hedge Nahi Karna ("Kuch Nahi Hega")

"Market toh hamesha upar jaata hai" — 2008, 2020, 2022 yaad karo. Bina hedge ke portfolio 30-50% gir sakta hai.

Bahut Mehenga Hedge

ATM options bahut expensive hain. OTM options (2-3% away) use karo — saste hain aur sufficient protection dete hain.

Hedge Kabhi Close Nahi Karna

Event khatam ho gaya, hedge abhi bhi open hai → premium decay ho raha hai. Event ke baad hedge close karo.

Hedge Pe Profit Expect Karna

Hedge insurance hai. Insurance pe profit expect karte ho? Nahi na. Hedge ka kaam hai loss prevent karna — profit nahi banana.
