Dividend Investing Kya Hai?

Dividend investing mein tum aise stocks kharidte ho jo regular dividend dete hain — har quarter ya saal mein cash payment milta hai.

Simple definition: > Dividend investing = Aise companies mein invest karna jo profit ka hissa tumhe cash mein dete hain. Tumhara paisa tumhare liye kaam karta hai — bina stock bechhe income aati hai.

Dividend Investing vs Growth Investing

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Income — Dividend Investing: Regular cash flow, Growth Investing: No income (reinvested)
• Tax — Dividend Investing: Slab rate, Growth Investing: LTCG 10%
• Volatility — Dividend Investing: Lower, Growth Investing: Higher
• Returns — Dividend Investing: 6-10% (yield + appreciation), Growth Investing: 12-20%
• Best for — Dividend Investing: Retirees, income seekers, Growth Investing: Long-term wealth
• Risk — Dividend Investing: Lower, Growth Investing: Moderate-High

Dividend Portfolio Building

Step 1: Select 10-15 Dividend Stocks:
• Har sector se 1-2 stocks
• Minimum 5% dividend yield target
• 10+ years dividend payment history

Step 2: Analyze Sustainability:
• Payout ratio < 60% — Company profit ka 60% se kam de rahi hai
• Debt-to-equity < 1 — Company zyada leveraged nahi hai
• Revenue growing — Profit grow ho raha hai
• Consistent payments — 10+ years track record

Dividend Yield Calculation

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Dividend Yield = (Annual Dividend / Stock Price) × 100
• Example:
• Coal India price: ₹450
• Annual dividend: ₹25
• Yield: (₹25 / ₹450) × 100 = 5.56%

Dividend Taxation (India)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Dividend income = Taxable at slab rate
• TDS: 10% if dividend > ₹5,000 per company
• PAN not provided → 20% TDS

Tax planning:
• Growth stocks may be more tax-efficient (LTCG 10%)
• But dividend provides regular income (psychological benefit)
