DIVIDEND: KAMAI KA WO HISSA JO WAPAS AATA HAI

Dividend yield, payout ratio, ex-date ka mechanism, aur high yield ka jaal

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Dividend ek seedhi cheez lagti hai — company profit ka kuch hissa shareholders ko de deti hai. Par uske aas-paas kuch mechanics hain jinhe na jaanne se log ulta nuksaan kar lete hain.

EX-DATE KA JAADU JO JAADU NAHI HAI

Dividend paane ke liye aapke paas record date tak shares hone chahiye. Uske liye ek cut-off hota hai jise ex-date kehte hain — us din se stock 'without dividend' trade karta hai.

Aur ex-date par stock aam taur par lagbhag utna hi neeche khulta hai jitna dividend hai. Ye normal hai. Isliye 'dividend ke liye ek din pehle kharido' wali tarkeeb muft paisa nahi deti — aap ek jeb se doosri jeb mein paisa daal rahe ho, aur beech mein charges aur tax lag jaate hain.

DIVIDEND YIELD

Yield matlab saal bhar ka dividend, aaj ke price ke hisaab se kitna percent baithta hai. Ye ek useful number hai par ismein ek chhupa hua jaal hai.

Yield tab bhi badhta hai jab price girta hai. Isliye sabse zyada yield wali list mein aksar wo companies aati hain jinka stock bahut gira hua hai. Wahan yield keemat ka nateeja hai, quality ka nahi.

PAYOUT RATIO — ZYADA KAAM KA NUMBER

Payout ratio batata hai ki kamai ka kitna hissa dividend mein ja raha hai. Bahut zyada payout ka matlab hai ki company ke paas apne business mein lagane ke liye kam bach raha hai.

Isse jo sawaal nikalte hain:
• Kya company ke paas growth ke liye jagah hai — agar haan, to bahut zyada payout aksar mauka ganwana hai.
• Kya dividend cash flow se aa raha hai — profit se dividend declare karna aur cash se dena, do alag cheezein hain.
• Kya dividend lagataar raha hai — ek saal ka bada special dividend recurring income nahi hai.
• Kya udhaar lekar dividend diya ja raha hai — ye dikhta hai financing cash flow mein, aur ye ek red flag hai.

TAX KA HISSA

India mein dividend ab receiver ke haath mein taxable hai, uske apne slab ke hisaab se, aur uspar TDS bhi lag sakta hai. Iska matlab ye hai ki high-slab wale investor ke liye dividend ka net faayda utna nahi hota jitna gross yield dikhata hai.

Exact rates aur limits time-time par badalte hain, isliye ye apne tax advisor ya official source par verify karna hi sahi tarika hai.

EK SANTULIT NAZARIYA

Dividend achhi cheez hai jab wo ek mazboot business ke bache hue cash se aa raha ho. Wo ek warning hai jab wo company ki growth ki jagah le raha ho, aur ek jaal hai jab uska yield sirf girti keemat se bana ho.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
