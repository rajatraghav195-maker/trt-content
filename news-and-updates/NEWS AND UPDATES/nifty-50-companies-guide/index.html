Nifty 50 Kya Hai?

Nifty 50 India ka sabse important stock index hai. Ye NSE (National Stock Exchange) ke top 50 stocks ka basket hai jo Indian economy ko represent karte hain.

Basic Facts:
• Full name: Nifty 50 (ya NIFTY 50)
• Base year: November 3, 1995
• Base value: 1,000
• Managed by: NSE Indices Limited (formerly IISL)
• Review: Half-yearly (January aur July)
• Current level: ~20,000+ (August 2026)

Selection Criteria

Koi bhi stock Nifty 50 mein tab aata hai jab:
• Listing: NSE pe listed ho (6+ months)
• Trading frequency: 100% trading sessions mein trade hua ho
• Free-float market cap: Top 50 mein ho
• Liquidity: Average daily turnover sufficient ho
• Domicile: India mein incorporated ho

Nifty 50 — Key Observations

Top 10 Stocks = ~50% of Nifty

HDFC Bank (13%) + Reliance (10%) + ICICI Bank (8%) + TCS (4%) + Infosys (4%) + ITC (4%) + Bharti Airtel (3%) + HUL (3%) + L&T (2%) + ICICI Bank ki insurance (2%) = ~53%

Matlab: In 10 stocks ka performance Nifty ka aadha movement decide karta hai. Agar HDFC Bank aur Reliance dono gir jaayein → Nifty girega regardless of other 40 stocks.

Sector Concentration Risk

Banking sector (~35%) ka weight zyada hai. RBI policy, NPA cycle, ya banking crisis → Nifty pe disproportionate impact.

Nifty vs Sensex:
• Nifty 50 = 50 stocks (NSE)
• Sensex = 30 stocks (BSE)
• Dono similar stocks hain, but Nifty zyada diversified hai
• Institutional investors Nifty ko prefer karte hain

FAQ

Nifty 50 mein kitni companies hain?

Exactly 50 companies. Half-yearly review mein companies add/remove hoti hain.

Nifty 50 ka weightage kaise decide hota hai?

Free-float market capitalization weighted. Jiska free-float market cap zyada, uska weight zyada.

Reliance ka Nifty mein kitna weight hai?

Approximately 10%. HDFC Bank ke baad second highest.

Nifty 50 mein kaunsi company sabse badi hai?

Market cap wise: Reliance Industries. Weight wise: HDFC Bank.

Nifty 50 kab change hota hai?

January aur July mein half-yearly review. NSE Indices Limited decide karta hai.
