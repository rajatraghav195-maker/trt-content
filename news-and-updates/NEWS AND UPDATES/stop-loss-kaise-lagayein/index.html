STOP LOSS: JAGAH APKI SOCH SE NAHI, CHART SE AATI HAI

Percentage stop, structure stop, volatility stop — aur stop hatana kyun sabse mehnga hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Stop loss ke baare mein do galat baatein sabse zyada chalti hain: ek, ki stop lagane se hi stop hit hota hai; do, ki 'mentally' stop rakhna kaafi hai. Dono un logon ki baatein hain jinka stop kabhi lagta nahi kyunki wo use maante hi nahi.

STOP KA KAAM KYA HAI

Stop ka kaam nuksaan se bachana nahi hai. Uska kaam ye tay karna hai ki nuksaan kitna hoga. Ye chhota sa farq hi poori soch badal deta hai.

Stop ye bhi kehta hai ki aapki analysis kahan galat sabit ho jaayegi. Agar wo jagah nahi bata sakte, to trade ka thesis hi saaf nahi hai.

TEEN TARIKE

Har ek ka apna kaam hai:
• Percentage stop — entry se ek fixed % neeche. Simple hai, par chart ki koi baat nahi sunta. Ye aksar wahin lag jaata hai jahan normal shor hota hai.
• Structure stop — kisi swing low, support zone ya pattern ke us paar. Ye behtar hai kyunki iska matlab hai: yahan tootne par mera setup khatam.
• Volatility stop — stock ki apni average range ke hisaab se. Shaant stock par tight, volatile stock par chaudha. ATR jaisa paimana isi kaam aata hai.

Ek acchi aadat: pehle structure se stop ki jagah tay karo, phir usse position size nikalo. Ulta karne par log stop ko size ke hisaab se kheenchte hain — aur wahi sabse aam galti hai.

STOP HATANA — SABSE MEHENGI AADAT

Position ulti gayi, stop paas aa raha hai, aur trader use thoda peeche khiska deta hai. Ek baar. Phir dobara. Har baar wo apne aap ko bataata hai ki wo 'zyada room' de raha hai.

Yahi wo ek aadat hai jo chhote nuksaan ko account-ending nuksaan mein badalti hai. Ek stop jo hataya ja sakta ho, wo stop nahi hai — wo ek suggestion hai.

STOP HIT HONA FAILURE NAHI HAI

Ek achha system 40-50% baar stop hit karta hai. Ye system ki khaami nahi, uska kharcha hai.

Asli failure ye hai: stop hit hua aur aapko pata hi nahi ki kyun. Har stop-out ke baad ek line likh lo — setup galat tha, timing galat thi, ya stop jagah galat thi. Teenon ka ilaaj alag hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Order system mein daal do, sar mein nahi. Mental stop us waqt kaam karta hai jab aap shaant hote ho — aur jis din uski zaroorat hoti hai, us din aap shaant nahi hote.
