Candlestick Pattern Scanner — Automatic Pattern Detection Guide (Hindi)

Candlestick patterns automatically kaise dhundhein — scanner tools, Chartink screeners, filters — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 03, 2026 · Features

Schema: FAQPage, Article

Pattern Scanner Kya Hai?

Candlestick pattern scanner ek automated tool hai jo saare stocks ko ek saath scan karke candlestick patterns (Doji, Hammer, Engulfing, etc.) identify karta hai — bina tumhe manually har stock ka chart dekhne ki zaroorat ke.

Manual method: 500+ stocks ke charts individually kholna, pattern dhundhna — ghanto lag sakte hain.

Scanner method: Ek query run karo, scanner seconds mein sabhi matching stocks dikha deta hai.

Ye especially useful hai jab tum poore market mein specific setup dhoond rahe ho — jaise "aaj kis kis stock mein bullish engulfing bana hai."

Scanner Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Scanner har stock ki OHLC (Open, High, Low, Close) data check karta hai
• Predefined mathematical rules apply karta hai (e.g., Doji ke liye: open aur close bahut close hone chahiye, body chhoti honi chahiye)
• Jo stocks criteria match karte hain, unhe result list mein dikhata hai
• Real-time ya end-of-day dono modes mein available hota hai

Common patterns jo scanners detect karte hain:
• Doji, Hammer, Inverted Hammer
• Bullish/Bearish Engulfing
• Morning Star, Evening Star
• Shooting Star, Hanging Man
• Three White Soldiers, Three Black Crows

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:

Recommendation: Beginners TRT SUPERCHART se start karein (free, integrated), advanced screening ke liye Chartink formula scans powerful hain.

How to Use a Scanner — Step-by-Step (Chartink Example)

Ye das baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• chartink.com pe jao → "Screener" section open karo
• Candlestick pattern filter select karo dropdown se (e.g., "Bullish Engulfing")
• Additional filters add karo:
• Market cap > ₹5,000 Cr (liquid, established stocks)
• Volume > 1 lakh shares (avoid illiquid stocks)
• Price > ₹50 (avoid penny stocks agar chaho)
• Run scanner click karo → matching stocks ki list dikhegi
• Manually verify — har stock ka chart khol kar pattern visually confirm karo
• Support/Resistance + RSI confirm karo — pattern akela kaafi nahi hota
• Confirmed setups ko watchlist mein add karo aur trade plan banao

Scanner Results Ko Trust Kyun Nahi Karna Chahiye — Blind Trading Se Bachein

Mistake 1: Scanner Signal = Automatic Buy

Scanner sirf candidates deta hai, trading signals nahi. Pattern bana hai matlab ye nahi ki trade lena hi hai.

Mistake 2: Volume Confirmation Skip Karna

Bina volume ke candlestick pattern weak hota hai. Hamesha check karo ki pattern ke din volume average se zyada tha ya nahi.

Mistake 3: Trend Context Ignore Karna

Downtrend mein bullish pattern ka matlab alag hota hai uptrend ke comparison mein. Overall trend dekhna zaroori hai pattern ke saath.

Mistake 4: Support/Resistance Ke Bina Trade Karna

Sabse reliable patterns wahi hote hain jo key support/resistance levels pe bante hain. Random jagah pe bana pattern kam reliable hota hai.

Scanner + Confirmation Checklist

Pattern detect hone ke baad, trade lene se pehle ye 4 cheezein confirm karo:

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Volume — pattern ke din volume average se zyada tha?
• Trend Context — overall trend ke saath align ho raha hai ya against?
• Support/Resistance — pattern key level ke paas bana hai?
• RSI/Other Indicators — momentum confirmation mil raha hai?

Agar 3-4 factors match karte hain, tabhi high-probability setup consider karo.

FAQ — Pattern Scanner Ke Baare Mein Common Sawaal

Kya candlestick scanner accurate hote hain?

Pattern detection mathematically accurate hota hai (rules-based), lekin pattern ka "reliability" context pe depend karta hai — volume, trend, aur support/resistance ke saath combine karke dekhna zaroori hai.

Free scanner use karu ya paid?

Beginners ke liye free tools (Chartink free tier, TRT SUPERCHART) kaafi hain. Advanced custom filters aur real-time alerts chahiye toh paid tier consider karo.

Kya scanner intraday trading ke liye use kar sakte hain?

Haan, real-time scanners intraday ke liye bhi available hain — lekin lower timeframe pe patterns zyada noisy hote hain, isliye extra confirmation zaroori hai.

Ek din mein kitne patterns scan kar sakte hain?

Zyadatar scanners unlimited scans allow karte hain — Chartink jaise tools poore NSE/BSE universe (2,000+ stocks) ko seconds mein scan kar dete hain.

Kaunsa pattern sabse reliable hai scanning ke liye?

Bullish/Bearish Engulfing aur Morning/Evening Star patterns generally zyada reliable mane jaate hain single-candle patterns (jaise Doji) ke comparison mein, especially jab volume aur trend confirm karein.
