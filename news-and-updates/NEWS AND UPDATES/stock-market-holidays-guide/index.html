Special Trading Sessions

Muhurat Trading (Diwali):
• Date: 4 November 2026 (Wednesday)
• Timing: 1 hour special session (usually 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM)
• Significance: Auspicious trading session on Diwali
• What happens: Market opens for 1 hour, symbolic trading takes place
• Historical: Usually green (positive close)

Pre-Open Session:
• Normal days: 9:00 AM - 9:15 AM
• Holidays: Market closed

Market Timings:
• Pre-open — 9:00 - 9:15 AM
• Normal trading — 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM
• Closing session — 3:30 - 3:40 PM
• Post-close — 3:40 - 4:00 PM

Weekend Trading?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Saturday: Market CLOSED (except Muhurat Trading)
• Sunday: Market CLOSED
• Only weekdays (Monday-Friday) trading hoti hai

Holiday Impact on Trading

Before Holiday:
• Volume: Usually low (traders vacation pe)
• Volatility: Can be higher (low liquidity)
• Positions: Many traders close positions before long weekends

After Holiday:
• Gap ups/downs: Possible (global events during holiday)
• Volume: Higher than normal (pent-up activity)
• F&O expiry: May shift if holiday falls on expiry day

F&O Expiry Adjustments

Agar Nifty expiry (Thursday) holiday pe hai → Previous trading day pe expiry hoti hai.
