Wealth Creation Ka Formula

Wealth = Income - Expenses + Investment Returns × Time

Simple version: Kam kharcha karo, zyada invest karo, time do.

5 Wealth Creation Rules

Rule 1: Pay Yourself First

Salary aate hi 20% invest karo, phir kharcha karo. Kharcha ke baad invest nahi hota.

Rule 2: Increase SIP Every Year

Har saal 10-15% SIP increase karo (salary badhne ke saath). ₹5K se start, 10 saal baad ₹20K+.

Rule 3: Never Stop SIP

Bear market, crash, pandemic — SIP continue karo. Low prices = More units = More wealth.

Rule 4: Avoid Lifestyle Inflation

Salary ₹30K → ₹1L → Expenses ₹25K → ₹90K mat karo. Expenses kam, invest zyada.

Rule 5: Compound Interest ka Respect

₹10K/month @ 12% for 30 years = ₹3.52 CRORE. Time do, magic hoga.

Wealth Destroyers

Lifestyle Inflation

"Salary badhi, car upgrade, house upgrade" — Expenses badhne pe wealth nahi banti.

Debt Trap

Credit card debt, personal loans — Interest tumhara wealth kha jaata hai.

No Emergency Fund

Emergency mein investments bechna padta hai — Loss book hota hai.

FOMO Trading

"Bitcoin/crypto/penny stock mein sab kama rahe hain" — FOMO se loss hota hai.

Timing the Market

"Market girega tab invest karunga" — Market aur upar jaata hai while you wait.
