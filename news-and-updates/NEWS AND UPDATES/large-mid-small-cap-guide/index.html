Small Cap vs Mid Cap vs Large Cap — Hindi Guide

Large cap, mid cap, small cap mein kya difference hai — complete Hindi guide Indian investors ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Features

Large cap, mid cap, small cap mein kya difference hai — complete Hindi guide Indian investors ke liye.

Market Cap Categories

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Large Cap — Market Cap: Top 100 companies, Examples: Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank
• Mid Cap — Market Cap: 101-250 companies, Examples: Persistent, Trent, Tata Elxsi
• Small Cap — Market Cap: 251+ companies, Examples: < ₹20,000 Cr market cap

Comparison

Ye aath baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Risk — Large Cap: Low, Mid Cap: Medium, Small Cap: Very High
• Returns — Large Cap: 10-14%, Mid Cap: 14-18%, Small Cap: 18-25%+
• Volatility — Large Cap: Low, Mid Cap: Medium, Small Cap: Very High
• Liquidity — Large Cap: Very High, Mid Cap: High, Small Cap: Low
• Research — Large Cap: Abundant, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: Limited
• Crash impact — Large Cap: Falls less, Mid Cap: Falls more, Small Cap: Falls most
• Recovery — Large Cap: Fast, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: Slow
• Best for — Large Cap: Conservative, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: Aggressive

Returns Comparison (Historical)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1 year — Nifty 50 (Large): 15-20%, Nifty Midcap 150: 20-30%, Nifty Smallcap 250: 25-40%
• 3 years — Nifty 50 (Large): 12-15%, Nifty Midcap 150: 15-20%, Nifty Smallcap 250: 18-25%
• 5 years — Nifty 50 (Large): 12-14%, Nifty Midcap 150: 14-18%, Nifty Smallcap 250: 16-22%
• 10 years — Nifty 50 (Large): 12%, Nifty Midcap 150: 14%, Nifty Smallcap 250: 15%

Note: Small caps zyada returns dete hain but risk bhi zyada hai. 2008 mein small caps 70%+ gire the.

Portfolio Allocation by Risk

Conservative:
• Large Cap — 70%
• Mid Cap — 20%
• Small Cap — 10%

Balanced:
• Large Cap — 50%
• Mid Cap — 30%
• Small Cap — 20%

Aggressive:
• Large Cap — 30%
• Mid Cap — 35%
• Small Cap — 35%

When to Buy

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Bull market (early) — Small caps outperform
• Bull market (late) — Large caps safer
• Bear market — Large caps fall less
• Recovery — Small caps recover fast

How to Invest

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Index Fund — Large Cap: Nifty 50, Mid Cap: Nifty Midcap 150, Small Cap: Nifty Smallcap 250
• Active MF — Large Cap: Large Cap Fund, Mid Cap: Mid Cap Fund, Small Cap: Small Cap Fund
• ETF — Large Cap: Nifty BeES, Mid Cap: —, Small Cap: —
• Direct Stocks — Large Cap: Blue chips, Mid Cap: Select mid caps, Small Cap: Avoid (too risky)
