Trading Psychology: 10 Galtiyan Jo Har Trader Karta Hai — Hinglish Guide

Trading Psychology: 10 Galtiyan Jo Har Trader Karta Hai — Hinglish Guide

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 19, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Sab log indicators, patterns, aur strategies seekhte hain. Lekin 90% traders loss karte hain. Kyun? Kyunki trading mein psychology > technique. Aapke dimaag ka kaisa kaam karta hai, ye aapke P&L se zyada determine karta hai.

Trading Psychology — Sabse Zaroori Skill Jo Sabse Kam Padha Jata Hai

Ye article mein 10 sabse common psychological mistakes cover karenge — jo practically har trader karta hai, especially beginners.

1. FOMO — "Bhaagna Padega, Miss Nahi Karna Chahiye!" 😱

Kya hota hai: Koi stock 15% upar gaya. Aap dekh rahe ho. "Yaar, agar main 2 ghante pehle buy karta toh 15% profit hota." Aap chase karte ho, entry lete ho at top.

Kya hota hai next: Stock reverses. Aap top pe buy kiye, ab 5% neeche. Stop-loss hit. Loss.

Fix:
• Missed trade = not a loss. Bad entry = loss.
• Stock already 15% upar hai toh risk/reward unfavorable hai
• "Agar entry miss ho gayi toh next setup wait karo" — ye discipline banani hai
• Rule: 3% se zyada upar gaya stock toh chase mat karo

2. Revenge Trading — "Ek Loss Cover Karunga Abhi" 😤

Kya hota hai: Aapko ek trade mein ₹2,000 loss hua. Frustration. "Abhi ek aur trade karke ye ₹2,000 wapas laata hoon." Aap immediately next trade lete ho bina proper setup ke.

Kya hota hai next: Wo trade bhi loss. Ab total loss ₹4,000. Aur zyada frustration. Aur ek aur trade. Aur loss. Spiral.

Fix:
• Loss ke baad 15 minute break — screen se door
• "Market kal bhi open hai" — ye yaad rakho
• Max 3 trades/day rule (agar teen loss huye toh trading band)
• Daily loss limit set karo: "₹3,000 loss = no more trades today"

3. Overtrading — "Aur Ek Trade, Bas Aur Ek" 📊

Kya hota hai: 5 trades done. 2 profit, 3 loss. Net negative. "Aur ek kar leta hoon, recover ho jayega." 10 trades done. Ab commission bhi zyada. Net worse.

Kya hota hai next: Overtrading se aap fatigued ho jaate ho. Decision-making kharab hota hai. Har trade worse than previous.

Fix:
• Quality > Quantity: 2-3 good setups > 10 random trades
• Pre-define: "Aaj sirf 3 trades karunga"
• Har trade ke liye checklist: Setup, Entry, Stop-loss, Target — sab pre-defined
• Agar setup nahi mil raha toh no trade is a good trade

4. No Stop-Loss — "Market Wapas Aayega" 🙏

Kya hota hai: Aapne stock buy kiya. Stop-loss lagaya tha. Stock stop-loss ke paas pahuncha. Aapne stop-loss hata diya — "thoda aur wait karta hoon, wapas aayega."

Kya hota hai next: Stock aur gir gaya. Ab 8% loss. Aur wait. 15% loss. Ab "abbe bech dun toh loss book karna padega." 25% loss. Trap.

Fix:
• Stop-loss mandatory hai. BEFORE entry.
• Mental stop-loss = no stop-loss. Order place karo.
• Stop-loss hataana = trading rule violation. No exceptions.
• "Stop-loss hit hua = wrong trade. Accept. Move on."

5. Averaging Down — "Aur Buy Kar Leta Hoon, Average Kam Hoga" 📉

Kya hota hai: Stock buy kiya ₹100 pe. Gir gaya ₹90 pe. "Aur buy kar leta hoon, average ₹95 ho jayega." Aur buy. ₹85 pe. Aur buy. ₹80 pe. Average ₹88. Ab total position double. Risk double.

Kya hota hai next: Stock ₹70 pe. Ab 22% loss on double position. Averaging down ne loss ko 2x kar diya.

Fix:
• Averaging down = good investing, bad trading
• Trading mein: wrong direction mein position add mat karo
• Agar bullish thesis broken hai toh exit, average mat karo
• Exception: If you're a long-term investor and fundamentals still strong, averaging down is fine. But trader? Never.

6. Confirmation Bias — "Main Sahi Hoon, Market Galat Hai" 🧠

Kya hota hai: Aapne buy kiya. Stock gir gaya. Aap reasoning dhoondhte ho — "ye support hai, ye bullish divergence hai, ye RSI oversold hai." Sirf apne view ko support karne wale reasons dhoondhte ho. Counter-arguments ignore.

Kya hota hai next: Stock aur girta hai. Aap still convinced "main sahi hoon, temporary dip hai." 20% loss. 30% loss.

Fix:
• Har trade ke liye thesis likho — "kyun buy kiya, kyun sell karunga"
• Thesis break hone pe exit — "agar stock X level neeche gaya toh thesis wrong hai"
• Actively counter-arguments dhoondho — "kya bearish case hai?"
• "Market is never wrong. My analysis can be wrong."

7. Anchoring — "₹150 Tha, Ab ₹100 Hai — Sasta Hai!" ⚓

Kya hota hai: Stock ₹150 pe tha. Ab ₹100 pe hai. "50% sasta ho gaya! Buy karna chahiye."

Kya hota hai next: Stock ₹70 pe. ₹50 pe. "Sasta" tha ₹100 pe, ab aur "sasta" hai ₹50 pe. Lekin kyun gira? Maybe fundamentals kharab hain. Maybe industry disruption hai.

Fix:
• Price history ≠ valuation. Stock ka fair value depend karta hai fundamentals pe, not past price
• "₹150 tha" is irrelevant. Current value kya hai? Growth kya hai? Earnings kya hai?
• Sirf price drop dekh ke buy mat karo — reason dhoondho kyun gira
• "Falling knife" catch karna dangerous hai

8. Hindsight Bias — "Pata Tha Ye Hoga" 🔮

Kya hota hai: Market gir gaya. Aap dekhte ho: "Yaar, ye toh obvious tha. RSI overbought tha, resistance pe rejection hua tha. Pata tha ye girne wala hai."

Kya hota hai next: Next time aap overconfident ho. Bada trade lete ho. Kyunki "pata tha last time bhi." Lekin market always different moves karta hai.

Fix:
• "Pata tha" = galat. Aap wahan trade nahi kiye, isliye ab bol rahe ho
• Hindsight mein sab kuch obvious lagta hai. Real-time mein nahi.
• Apne trades journal mein likho BEFORE result, not after
• "I didn't know" accept karna = maturity

9. Sunk Cost Fallacy — "Itna Loss Book Kiya Toh Sab Bekar Ho Jayega" 💸

Kya hota hai: Stock mein 30% loss hai. "Agar bech dunga toh ₹30,000 loss book karna padega. Hold karta hoon, recover hoga."

Kya hota hai next: 40% loss. 50% loss. "Ab toh aur nahi bech sakta." 60% loss. Capital stuck. Opportunity cost — ye paisa kahin aur invest hota toh profit bana sakta tha.

Fix:
• Sunk cost = gone. Aapka decision aage ka hona chahiye, not past ka
• Question: "Agar aaj cash mein milta, toh ye stock buy karta?" Agar nahi, toh hold kyun?
• Loss book karna = mistake accept karna = learning
• "Recover hoga" = hope, not strategy

10. Ego — "Main Profitable Trader Hoon, Loss Nahi Hoga" 💪

Kya hota hai: 3 profitable trades. Ego boost. "Trading easy hai. Bada trade kar leta hoon." Position size double. Risk double.

Kya hota hai next: 4th trade loss. Bada trade tha, toh bada loss. Ego hurt. Revenge trading (see #2). Spiral.

Fix:
• 3 trades se trader nahi bante. 300 trades se pata chalta hai.
• Position size HAMESHA 1-2% risk per trade. Profitable streak pe bhi.
• "Main profitable hoon" ego ko side mein rakho
• Daily target: "X% profit" nahi. "Rules follow kiya ya nahi" — ye target ho.
• Process > Outcome. Good process + bad outcome = still good. Bad process + good outcome = still bad.

Bonus: Trading Psychology Rules (Print Karke Screen Pe Lagao)

Ye das baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• FOMO mat karo — missed trade is not a loss
• Loss ke baad break lo — 15 minute minimum
• Max 3-4 trades/day — quality over quantity
• Stop-loss BEFORE entry — non-negotiable
• Averaging down = trading suicide — don't add to losers
• Thesis likho, thesis break pe exit — no hoping
• Past price ≠ fair value — fundamentals matter
• Hindsight mein sab genius — real-time mein nahi
• Sunk cost ko chhodo — aage socho
• Ego ko side mein rakho — process > outcome

Trading Journal: Psychology Track Karne Ka Tarika

Har trade mein ye fields add karo:

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Entry emotion — "Excited, FOMO pe entry liya"
• Exit emotion — "Panic sell, stop-loss hit"
• Rule followed? — "No — stop-loss hata diya"
• Mistake — "Averaged down instead of exit"
• Lesson — "Thesis break pe exit, no exceptions"

Monthly review: kaunsa mistake sabse zyada repeat ho raha hai? Uspe focus karo.

FAQ

Q: Kya ye psychology mistakes sab beginners karte hain? A: Nahi. Experienced traders bhi karte hain, especially profitable streak ke baad. Ego aur overconfidence experienced traders ka sabse bada enemy hai.

Q: In mistakes se kaise bacha jaaye? A: Trading journal rakho. Rules pre-define karo. Auto-stop-loss lagao. Daily loss limit set karo. Aur sabse important: losses ko personally mat lo. Ye business expense hai.

Q: Kya trading psychology improve hoti hai time ke saath? A: Haan, lekin sirf active effort se. Journaling without action = waste. Mistake identify + rule create + rule follow = improvement.

Q: FOMO se kaise bacha jaaye? A: Pre-defined watchlist rakho. Entry levels pe limit orders lagao. Stock already upar gaya toh chase mat karo. "Aaj nahi toh kal" — market hamesha opportunity deta hai.

Q: Ek din mein kitna loss acceptable hai? A: Apna daily loss limit set karo — capital ka 3-5%. Agar wo limit hit ho gaya toh trading band. Force yourself to stop.
