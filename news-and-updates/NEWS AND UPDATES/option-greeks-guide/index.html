Option Greeks Kya Hain?

Option Greeks wo 5 factors hain jo batate hain ki option ki price kaise change hogi jab market conditions badlein:

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Delta (Δ) — Price ka effect
• Gamma (Γ) — Delta ka change
• Theta (Θ) — Time ka effect (decay)
• Vega (ν) — Volatility ka effect
• Rho (ρ) — Interest rate ka effect

Simple language mein: Greeks batate hain ki agar Nifty 100 points upar jaaye, ya 1 din beet jaaye, ya volatility badh jaaye — toh tumhare option ki price kitni badhegi ya giregi.

Delta (Δ) — Price Sensitivity

Kya batata hai:

Agar underlying (Nifty/stock) ₹1 badhe → option kitna badhega/girega.

Values:
• Deep ITM Call — Delta Range: 0.8-1.0, Meaning: Stock jaisa move karega
• ATM Call — Delta Range: 0.5, Meaning: Half move karega
• Deep OTM Call — Delta Range: 0.0-0.2, Meaning: Barely move karega
• ITM Put — Delta Range: -0.8 to -1.0, Meaning: Ulti direction mein full move
• ATM Put — Delta Range: -0.5, Meaning: Ulti direction mein half move

Practical Use:
• Nifty at 20,000, 20,200 CE ka Delta = 0.6
• Nifty 100 points upar jaaye → Option ka price ~₹60 badhega
• Nifty 100 points neeche jaaye → Option ka price ~₹60 girega

Delta as Probability:

Delta approximately probability hai ki option ITM expire hoga:
• Delta 0.5 = 50% chance ITM expire hoga
• Delta 0.3 = 30% chance
• Delta 0.8 = 80% chance

Ye bahut useful hai — delta dekhte hi samajh aa jaata hai ki option kitna "in the money" hai.

Gamma (Γ) — Delta Ka Change

Kya batata hai:

Delta kitna change hoga jab underlying ₹1 badhe.

Simple Example:
• Nifty 20,000 CE, Delta = 0.5, Gamma = 0.02
• Nifty 100 points upar → Delta badh ke 0.52 ho jayega
• Nifty 100 points neeche → Delta ghir ke 0.48 ho jayega

Why Gamma Matters:

ATM options ka Gamma sabse zyada hota hai. Matlab ATM options ka delta sabse fast change hota hai.

Gamma risk: ATM options near expiry pe bahut volatile hain — chhote move mein delta dramatic change hota hai. Ye sellers ke liye risk hai.

Gamma Scalping:

Advanced traders gamma ka use karke profit banate hain:
• ATM option buy karo (long gamma)
• Jab price upar jaaye → delta badh raha hai → thoda underlying bech do (hedge)
• Jab price neeche jaaye → delta ghat raha hai → thoda underlying kharido
• Ye repeated hedging se profit

Theta (Θ) — Time Decay

Kya batata hai:

Har din beetne pe option ki price kitni giregi (sab factors same rahein toh).

The Harsh Truth:

Options ka time value har din girta hai. Ye option BUYERS ke liye enemy hai aur option SELLERS ke liye friend.

Example:
• Nifty 20,200 CE, Price = ₹100, Theta = -5
• Kal tak agar Nifty same rahe → Option ₹95 ho jayega
• 5 din baad → Option ₹75 ho jayega (sirf time decay se)

Theta Near Expiry:

Last 7 days mein theta sabse fast girta hai. ATM options ka theta curve:

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 30 days — ~₹1-2/day
• 15 days — ~₹3-4/day
• 7 days — ~₹5-7/day
• 3 days — ~₹10-15/day
• Expiry day — ~₹20-30+/day

Matlab: Option buyers ke liye last week bahut dangerous hai. Option sellers last week mein sabse zyada profit banate hain.

Theta Strategy:
• Buy options early (20-30 days before expiry) — theta slow hai
• Sell options near expiry (last 7 days) — theta fast hai
• Avoid holding ATM options overnight near expiry — theta bahut khaata hai

Vega (ν) — Volatility Sensitivity

Kya batata hai:

Agar implied volatility (IV) 1% badhe → option ki price kitni badhegi.

Example:
• Nifty 20,200 CE, Price = ₹100, Vega = 8
• IV 2% badh jaaye → Option ₹116 ho jayega (₹100 + 2×8)
• IV 2% gire → Option ₹84 ho jayega

IV Crush:

Earnings/results se pehle IV bahut badh jaata hai. Results ke baad IV suddenly girta hai — chahe stock upar jaaye ya neeche.

Matlab: Results se pehle option buy karna EXPENSIVE hai (high IV). Results ke baad IV crush se option ki price girti hai even if stock moves in your direction.

Best strategy: Results se pehle SELL options (high IV premium), results ke baad BUY back (IV crush se profit).

India VIX Connection:

India VIX Indian market ka implied volatility index hai:
• VIX high (>20): Options expensive hain (high premium)
• VIX low (<12): Options cheap hain (low premium)
• VIX rising: Buying options better
• VIX falling: Selling options better

Rho (ρ) — Interest Rate Effect

Kya batata hai:

Interest rate 1% badhe → option ki price kitni change hogi.

Practical Impact:

Rho ka impact bahut chhota hai compared to delta, theta, aur vega. Most traders Rho ignore karte hain.

Exception: Long-term options (LEAPS) ya high interest rate environment mein Rho matter karta hai.

RBI Connection:

RBI jab rate badhta hai → Call options slightly expensive, Put options slightly cheap. But effect minimal hai (1-2% of option price).

Greeks Cheat Sheet (Option Buyer)

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 GREEKS FOR OPTION BUYERS
• DELTA (Δ):
• 📊 ITM option lo → Delta 0.7-0.8 (zyada move)
• 📊 ATM option lo → Delta 0.5 (balanced)
• 📊 OTM option lo → Delta 0.2-0.3 (cheap, less likely)
• THETA (Θ):
• ⚠️ Time tumhara DUSHMAN hai
• ⚠️ Options 20-30 din pehle kharido (theta slow)
• ⚠️ Last 7 din mein mat hold karo (theta fast)
• ⚠️ ATM options ka theta sabse zyada
• VEGA (ν):
• ✅ IV low ho tab kharido (cheap premium)
• ✅ IV high ho tab becho (expensive premium)
• ✅ VIX < 12 = Good time to BUY
• ✅ VIX > 20 = Good time to SELL

Greeks Cheat Sheet (Option Seller)

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 GREEKS FOR OPTION SELLERS
• DELTA (Δ):
• 📊 ATM sell karo → Delta 0.5 (balanced risk)
• 📊 OTM sell karo → Delta 0.2-0.3 (high probability)
• 📊 Delta-neutral strategies use karo
• THETA (Θ):
• ✅ Time tumhara DOST hai
• ✅ Last 7 din mein sell karo (theta fast decay)
• ✅ ATM options pe sabse zyada theta milta hai
• ✅ Calendar spreads banao (sell near-term, buy far-term)
• VEGA (ν):
• ✅ IV high ho tab sell karo (mehenga premium)
• ✅ IV crush se profit (results/events ke baad)
• ✅ Iron condors, credit spreads use karo
• GAMMA (Γ):
• ⚠️ Near expiry ATM pe gamma bahut high hai
• ⚠️ Chhote moves mein delta dramatic change hoga
• ⚠️ Gamma risk manage karo with position sizing

Greeks Combined — Real Example

Setup: Nifty at 20,000. You buy 20,200 CE (14 days to expiry).

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Delta — Value: 0.45, Meaning: Nifty 100 upar → Option ~₹45 badhega
• Gamma — Value: 0.03, Meaning: Delta 0.45 se 0.48 ho jayega
• Theta — Value: -6, Meaning: Har din ₹6 girega (time decay)
• Vega — Value: 10, Meaning: IV 1% badhe → Option ₹10 badhega

Scenario 1: Nifty 100 points upar jaaye, 1 din baad:
• Delta profit: +₹45
• Theta loss: -₹6
• Net: +₹39

Scenario 2: Nifty same rahe, 1 din baad:
• Delta profit: ₹0
• Theta loss: -₹6
• Net: -₹6

Scenario 3: Nifty 100 points neeche jaaye, 1 din baad:
• Delta loss: -₹45
• Theta loss: -₹6
• Net: -₹51

Lesson: Option buying mein SIRF tab profit hota hai jab move theta se zyada ho. Isliye buyers ko BIG moves chahiye — chhote moves mein theta kha jaata hai profit.

Common Greeks Mistakes

Sirf Delta Dekhna

"Delta 0.5 hai, Nifty 100 upar jaayega toh ₹50 profit" — Nahi! Theta ₹6 daily khaata hai. 5 din hold kiya toh ₹30 theta ne kha liya.

Theta Ignore Karna (Buyers)

Option buyers ko time decay bahut hurt karta hai. Near expiry ATM options hold karna = slow bleeding.

Gamma Risk Ignore Karna (Sellers)

Near expiry ATM sell kiya, Nifty 200 points upar gaya — delta 0.3 se 0.7 ho gaya. Position size 2x+ ho gaya. Margin call aa sakta hai.

IV Crush Se Pehle Buy Karna

Results se pehle option kharidna = expensive premium. Results ke baad IV crush se option price girti hai even if stock moves in your direction.

Greeks Static Samajhna

Greeks har second change hote hain. Delta badlega jab price badlega (gamma). Theta badlega jab time badlega. Greeks ek snapshot hai — continuously update karo.
