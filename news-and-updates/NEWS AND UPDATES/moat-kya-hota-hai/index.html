MOAT: WO KHAAI JO COMPETITION KO DOOR RAKHTI HAI

Business ka wo hissa jo profit ko bachaata hai — aur kaise pehchana jaaye

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Business ki duniya ka ek kadwa niyam hai: jahan zyada profit hai, wahan competition aata hai, aur competition profit kha jaata hai. Moat wo cheez hai jo is niyam ko dheema kar deti hai.

MOAT KA MATLAB

Moat koi ratio nahi hai. Wo business ka ek structural faayda hai jiski wajah se doosre log aasani se ussi jagah aakar wahi cheez waise nahi kar sakte.

Isi wajah se moat wali company saalon tak apna margin bacha kar rakh paati hai, jabki bina moat wali company ka margin har naye competitor ke saath ghisata jaata hai.

MOAT KE AAM ROOP

Aksar in shakal mein milta hai:
• Brand — log ek naam ke liye zyada dete hain, chahe product lagbhag same ho.
• Switching cost — customer ko badalna mehnga ya jhanjhat bhara ho. Enterprise software aur banking mein ye aam hai.
• Network effect — jitne zyada log istemal karte hain, utna hi service har naye user ke liye behtar hoti hai.
• Cost advantage — scale, location ya process ki wajah se company sabse sasta bana paati hai.
• Regulation ya licence — kanooni roop se hi entry mushkil ho.

MOAT KAISE DIKHTA HAI NUMBERS MEIN

Sabse saaf nishan: lambe samay tak tika hua margin aur tika hua return on capital, chahe industry mein competition badh raha ho.

Agar kisi company ka margin dus saal se lagbhag ek jagah tika hua hai aur uske competitors ka ghisa hai, to wahan kuch hai jo numbers ke peeche kaam kar raha hai. Wahi dhundhne layak cheez hai.

MOAT MITTA BHI HAI

Koi moat hamesha ke liye nahi hota. Technology puraane switching costs khatam kar deti hai, regulation badal jaata hai, aur brand ek generation mein bemani ho sakta hai.

Isliye moat ko ek constant ki tarah mat maano. Har saal ye poochho: kya wo cheez abhi bhi kaam kar rahi hai jiski wajah se ye company alag thi?

AUR EK CHETAVNI

Moat ki kahani sabse zyada bikne wali kahani hai, aur isi wajah se sabse zyada mehngi bhi. Ek acchi company aur ek achha investment do alag cheezein hain — beech mein keemat khadi hoti hai. Moat sirf ye batata hai ki business tikau hai; wo ye nahi batata ki aaj ka bhaav vaajib hai.
