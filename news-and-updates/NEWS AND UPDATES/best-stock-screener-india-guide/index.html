Stock Screener Kya Hai?

Stock screener ek tool hai jo aapko hazaaron stocks mein se filter karne deta hai — aapke criteria ke hisab se. Manual 1,600+ NSE stocks check karna impossible hai. Screener seconds mein karta hai.

Use cases:
• "Mujhe saare stocks chahiye jinka P/E < 20 aur ROE > 15%"
• "Mujhe stocks chahiye jinka RSI < 40 aur price above 200 MA"
• "Mujhe high volume stocks chahiye jo aaj 5%+ upar gaye"
• "Mujhe dividend yield > 4% stocks chahiye"
