MULTIPLE TIMEFRAME: EK HI CHART, TEEN ALAG SACH

Bade timeframe se disha, chhote se entry — aur timeframe badal kar khud ko dhoka dena

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Ek hi stock daily chart par bullish, hourly par bearish, aur 5-minute par bilkul flat dikh sakta hai. Teenon galat nahi hain. Wo teen alag sawaalon ke jawab hain — aur galti tab hoti hai jab hum ek sawaal ka jawab doosre sawaal par laga dete hain.

TEEN LEVEL KA DHAANCHA

Ek aam structure:
• Higher timeframe — disha tay karne ke liye. Yahan se ye pata chalta hai ki hum kis taraf trade dhundhenge.
• Trading timeframe — setup dhundhne ke liye. Yahin structure, level aur pattern dekhe jaate hain.
• Lower timeframe — sirf entry aur stop ko theek karne ke liye. Yahan se view nahi banaya jaata.

Aam anupaat lagbhag 1:4 se 1:6 ka rakha jaata hai — jaise daily, hourly aur 15-minute. Exact numbers utne zaroori nahi jitna ye ki teenon ka kaam alag ho.

SABSE BADI GALTI: TIMEFRAME SHOPPING

Position loss mein hai. Trader chhote timeframe se bade timeframe par chala jaata hai, jahan abhi bhi sab theek dikh raha hai, aur khud ko bata deta hai ki view abhi zinda hai.

Ye analysis nahi hai, ye rahat dhundhna hai. Position lene ka timeframe pehle tay hota hai, aur usi par uska faisla hona chahiye — chahe doosra timeframe kuch bhi kah raha ho.

STOP AUR TARGET BHI TIMEFRAME SE AATE HAIN

Agar setup hourly chart ka hai to stop bhi hourly structure se aana chahiye, 5-minute ke shor se nahi. Chhote timeframe ka stop bade timeframe ke setup par lagane se aap lagbhag hamesha jaldi bahar hote ho — aur phir wahi trade aapke bina chalta hai.

CONFLICT KA MATLAB

Jab bade aur chhote timeframe ulte ho jaayein, to iska matlab ye nahi ki ek galat hai. Iska matlab hai ki abhi trend ke andar ek pullback chal raha hai.

Ye khud ek jaankari hai: pullback bade trend ke haq mein entry ka mauka bhi ho sakta hai, aur trend ke tootne ki shuruaat bhi. Us farq ko chhote timeframe par structure hi batata hai — indicator nahi.

EK SIMPLE ANUSHASAN

Chart kholne ka ek hi kram rakho: pehle bada, phir madhyam, phir chhota. Har baar wahi. Jo trader roz alag kram se dekhta hai, wo roz alag conclusion nikalta hai — aur use lagta hai ki market badal gaya hai.
