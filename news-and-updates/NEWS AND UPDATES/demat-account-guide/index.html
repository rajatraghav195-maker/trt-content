Demat Account Kya Hai?

Demat account tumhare shares ka digital locker hai. Jaise bank account mein paisa rehta hai, demat account mein shares rehte hain.

Simple definition: > Demat = Dematerialized account. Physical shares ki jagah digital form mein shares store karte hain.

Demat vs Trading Account:
• Kya hai — Demat Account: Shares ka locker, Trading Account: Buy/sell ka interface
• Kya store karta hai — Demat Account: Shares, bonds, MF units, Trading Account: Kuch nahi (execution only)
• Analogy — Demat Account: Bank vault, Trading Account: ATM card
• Zaroori? — Demat Account: Haan, Trading Account: Haan

Dono chahiye share market mein trade karne ke liye. Aajkal brokers dono ek saath dete hain.

Demat Account Kholene Ke Liye Kya Chahiye?

Documents:
• PAN Card — Required?: ✅ Mandatory, Notes: Without PAN demat nahi khulega
• Aadhaar Card — Required?: ✅ Mandatory, Notes: Address proof ke liye
• Bank Account — Required?: ✅ Mandatory, Notes: Linked bank account chahiye
• Passport Photo — Required?: ✅ Required, Notes: Digital upload
• Signature — Required?: ✅ Required, Notes: White paper pe sign, scan/upload
• Income Proof — Required?: Optional, Notes: F&O trading ke liye (salary slip/ITR)
• Cancelled Cheque — Required?: Optional, Notes: Bank verification ke liye

Eligibility:
• Age: 18+ years
• Nationality: Indian resident, NRI bhi kar sakte hain
• PAN: Mandatory

Demat Account Charges

Ye aath baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Account opening — ₹0-200
• Annual maintenance (AMC) — ₹0-300/year
• Brokerage (delivery) — ₹0-20/order
• Brokerage (intraday) — ₹20/order
• Brokerage (F&O) — ₹20/order
• STT (government tax) — 0.1% (delivery)
• GST — 18% on brokerage
• Stamp duty — State-wise vary

Total cost per trade: Brokerage + STT + GST + Stamp duty ≈ ₹25-50 per ₹1 lakh trade

Common Demat Account Mistakes

Brokerage Pe Sirf Focus Karna

Low brokerage achha hai, but platform stability, customer support, app quality bhi dekho.

Multiple Accounts Rakhna

Ek broker kaafi hai. 3-4 demat accounts = unnecessary charges (AMC).

Trading Aur Demat Mix Karna

Demat = storage, Trading = execution. Dono separate concepts hain, but brokers dono ek saath dete hain.

Login Credentials Share Karna

Kabhi bhi apna broker login/password kisi ko mat do. OTP bhi share mat karo.

Inactive Account

Agar demat account use nahi kar rahe toh close kar do. AMC lagta hai even if no trading.

Demat Account Band Kaise Karein?

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Broker ko email karo (account closure request)
• Form fill karo (online ya offline)
• All holdings clear karo (sab shares bech do ya transfer karo)
• Outstanding dues pay karo
• 7-15 din mein account close
