CANDLESTICK: EK MOMBATTI MEIN POORA DIN

Body, wick aur unka matlab — aur wo galti jo har naya trader pattern ke naam par karta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Ek candle chaar numbers ko ek shakal mein badal deti hai: open, high, low, close. Bas itna. Baaki jo bhi naam aur pattern hain, wo inhi chaar numbers ke arrangement hain — koi jaadu nahi.

BODY AUR WICK ALAG BAAT KEHTE HAIN

Body open aur close ke beech ka hissa hai — yaani us period mein aakhir mein faisla kya hua. Wick us range ko dikhata hai jahan tak price gaya par tik nahi paaya.

Lambi wick ka matlab hai ki wahan koshish hui aur usse ulti taraf se dhakel diya gaya. Choti body ka matlab hai ki poore period mein ladai barabar rahi. Yahi do observations 80% candlestick reading hain.

KUCH SHAKLEIN JO ROZ DIKHTI HAIN

Naam yaad rakhne se zyada zaroori matlab yaad rakhna hai:
• Lambi body, chhoti wick — ek taraf ka saaf control. Buyers ya sellers ne poora period apne paas rakha.
• Chhoti body, dono taraf lambi wick — confusion. Price dono taraf gaya aur wapas aa gaya. Ise doji-type candle kehte hain.
• Neeche lambi wick, upar chhoti body — girawat par kharidaar aaye. Ye rejection ka nishan hai, reversal ki guarantee nahi.
• Upar lambi wick — tezi par bechne wale aaye. Wahi baat ulti taraf.

PATTERN AKELA KUCH NAHI KEHTA

Ek hammer support zone par banna aur beech maidan mein banna — dono ek jaisi candle hain, par matlab ek nahi hai. Context ke bina pattern sirf ek shakal hai.

Isliye pehle ye poocho: ye candle kahan bani? Kisi purane level par, ek lambi chaal ke baad, ya bina kisi wajah ke beech mein? Jawab hi pattern ko matlab deta hai.

TIMEFRAME BADALTE HI SHAKAL BADAL JAATI HAI

Ek 5-minute chart ki khatarnak dikhne wali candle daily chart par ek chhoti si wick ban jaati hai. Dono sach hain, par dono ek hi sawaal ka jawab nahi de rahe.

Isliye timeframe pehle chuno, phir pattern dhundo. Ulta karne par aap wahi dekhte ho jo aap dekhna chahte the — har timeframe mein kuch na kuch mil hi jaata hai.

AUR EK IMANDAAR BAAT

Candlestick patterns bahut purane hain aur bahut logon ko pata hain. Jo cheez sabko pata ho, uska edge apne aap ghisa hua hota hai. Candle padhna seekho kyunki wo price ki bhasha hai — is umeed mein nahi ki koi ek shakal aapko signal de degi.
