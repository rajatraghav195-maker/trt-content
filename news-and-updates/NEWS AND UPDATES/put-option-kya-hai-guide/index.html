Put Option Kya Hai: Bearish Trading Ka Pehla Step

Put Option Kya Hai?

Put option ek contract hai jo aapko kisi stock/index ko ek pre-decided price (strike price) pe BECHNE ka right deta hai — obligation nahi. Is right ke liye aap premium pay karte ho.

Simple words:
• Aapko lagta hai Reliance ka price neeche aayega
• Aap ₹2,800 strike ka Put kharidte ho ₹40 premium pe
• Agar Reliance ₹2,750 ho gaya → aapka profit = ₹10/share (₹50 - ₹40)
• Agar Reliance ₹2,850 pe raha → aapka loss = ₹40 (sirf premium)

Put option = Bearish view. Aap expect karte ho price neeche aayega.

Call vs Put:
• Call = price UPAR jaayega (bullish)
• Put = price NEECHE aayega (bearish)

Put Option Profit/Loss Calculation

Buy 1 Lot Nifty 24,500 PE at ₹60

Cost: ₹60 × 50 shares = ₹3,000

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 24,700 — Value: 0, Profit/Loss: -₹3,000 (max loss)
• 24,600 — Value: 0, Profit/Loss: -₹3,000
• 24,560 — Value: 0, Profit/Loss: -₹3,000 (breakeven)
• 24,500 — Value: 0, Profit/Loss: -₹3,000
• 24,400 — Value: ₹5,000, Profit/Loss: +₹2,000
• 24,300 — Value: ₹10,000, Profit/Loss: +₹7,000
• 24,200 — Value: ₹15,000, Profit/Loss: +₹12,000

Breakeven: Strike - Premium = 24,500 - 60 = 24,440

Max loss: Premium = ₹3,000 (limited) Max profit: Strike - 0 - Premium = ₹2,22,000 (if Nifty goes to 0, theoretical)

ITM, ATM, OTM for Puts

Nifty at 24,600:

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• ITM — Strike: Above spot, Example: 24,700 PE, Logic: Strike > spot = put has intrinsic value
• ATM — Strike: At spot, Example: 24,600 PE, Logic: Strike = spot
• OTM — Strike: Below spot, Example: 24,500 PE, Logic: Strike < spot = sirf time value

Put buyers: ATM ya slightly ITM kharido for best risk-reward.

Put Option Kab Kharide?

Best Conditions:
• Bearish view — market down trend, weak stock
• High VIX expected to go higher (volatility = put premium up)
• Resistance rejection — stock strong resistance pe reject ho
• Bad news expected — poor results, sector downgrade
• 5-7+ days to expiry — time value preserved

Put as Hedging (Insurance):
• Portfolio hai worth ₹10L (long stocks)
• Market crash ki tension hai
• Buy Nifty Put = portfolio insurance
• Agar market crash → put profit offsets portfolio loss
• Agar market up → put premium loss = insurance cost (acceptable)

Put Option Strategies

Strategy 1: Long Put (Simplest):
• Buy ATM/slightly ITM put
• Hold 2-5 days
• Target: 50-100% premium gain
• SL: 30-40% premium loss

Strategy 2: Bear Put Spread:
• Buy 1 ITM Put + Sell 1 OTM Put
• Lower cost than naked put
• Max profit = spread width - net premium
• Max loss = net premium paid

Strategy 3: Protective Put (Hedging):
• Hold shares + Buy Put
• = downside protection
• Agar stock crash → put profit covers loss
• Cost = put premium (like insurance premium)

Common Put Option Mistakes

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• OTM put kharidna — 70% OTM expire zero
• Expiry pe hold — theta kills premium
• Bina SL ke — premium zero ho sakta hai
• Hedging ke bina naked put — theta + time = loss even if direction right
• Har baar put kharidna — markets 70% time upar jaate hain, puts lose more often

FAQ

Q1: Put option se max profit kitna?

Limited (strike - premium). If Nifty 24,500 PE at ₹60, max profit = 24,500 - 0 - 60 = 24,440 × 50 = ₹12,22,000 (if Nifty goes to 0 — impossible practically).

Q2: Put option kaun kharidta hai?

Bearish traders, hedgers (portfolio protection), arbitrageurs.

Q3: Call aur Put ek saath kharid sakte hain?

Haan — straddle/strangle strategy. Advanced. Beginners avoid.
