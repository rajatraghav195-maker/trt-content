F&O Expiry Kya Hai?

F&O ka matlab hai Futures and Options. Expiry ka matlab hai ki contract ka last din — us din ke baad contract exist nahi karta.

Simple definition: > F&O expiry = Contract ka last din. Is din ke baad ya toh contract settle hota hai (cash) ya exercise hota hai.

Indian Market Expiry Schedule:
• Nifty Weekly — Expiry Day: Every Thursday, Type: Cash settled
• Nifty Monthly — Expiry Day: Last Thursday of month, Type: Cash settled
• Bank Nifty Weekly — Expiry Day: Every Wednesday, Type: Cash settled
• Bank Nifty Monthly — Expiry Day: Last Wednesday of month, Type: Cash settled
• Stock F&O — Expiry Day: Last Thursday of month, Type: Physical delivery
• Sensex — Expiry Day: Every Friday (weekly), Type: Cash settled

Weekly vs Monthly Expiry

Weekly Expiry (Nifty — Every Thursday)

Characteristics:
• Har hafte naya contract
• Bahut fast time decay (theta)
• Low premium (cheap options)
• High gamma risk (ATM options volatile)
• Intraday traders ka favorite

Best for: Experienced traders, intraday, scalping

Avoid if: Beginner, swing trader, positional

Monthly Expiry (Last Thursday)

Characteristics:
• Mahine ka ek contract
• Slower time decay
• Higher premium
• Lower gamma risk
• Position traders use karte hain

Best for: Beginners, swing traders, options sellers

Avoid if: Intraday trader (kam volatility)

Expiry Day Trading — Kya Hota Hai?

Expiry Day Characteristics:
• Premium bahut fast girta hai (theta decay maximum)
• ATM options bahut volatile hain (gamma high)
• Max Pain ki taraf market move karta hai
• Volume bahut high hota hai
• Spread bahut wide hota hai (liquidity kam)

Expiry Day Numbers (Typical):
• Avg range — Nifty Expiry: 150-300 points, Bank Nifty Expiry: 400-800 points
• ATM option move — Nifty Expiry: 50-150% intraday, Bank Nifty Expiry: 100-300% intraday
• Volume spike — Nifty Expiry: 3-5x normal, Bank Nifty Expiry: 5-10x normal
• Max Pain accuracy — Nifty Expiry: 70-80%, Bank Nifty Expiry: 60-70%

Expiry Day Strategies

Strategy 1: Sell ATM Straddle (Most Popular)

Setup:
• Expiry day morning mein ATM Call + ATM Put sell karo
• Max Pain level identify karo
• Market Max Pain ki taraf jaayega → dono options ka premium girega
• Buy back at lower price → Profit

Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Sell 20,000 CE @ ₹80
• Sell 20,000 PE @ ₹70
• Total premium: ₹150

Agar Nifty 20,000 pe close kare:
• Both expire worthless
• Profit: ₹150 × 25 = ₹3,750 per lot

Risk: Nifty bahut zyada move kare toh unlimited loss. Stop loss zaroori.

Strategy 2: Iron Condor (Limited Risk)

Setup:
• OTM Call sell + Further OTM Call buy
• OTM Put sell + Further OTM Put buy
• Profit zone: Range between sold strikes

Better than straddle: Risk limited hai. Agar market range se bahar jaaye toh maximum loss defined hai.

Strategy 3: Directional Play (Expiry Day)

Setup:
• Market trend identify karo (bullish ya bearish)
• OTM option buy karo direction ke hisaab se
• Expiry day pe fast move expected
• Quick profit book karo (30-60 minutes)

Risk: Bahut volatile hai. Stop loss tight rakhao. Position size chhota rakhao.

Strategy 4: Expiry Day Scalping

Setup:
• ATM options lo (Call ya Put — direction ke hisaab se)
• 10-20 points ka target
• 5-10 points ka stop loss
• Multiple trades in a day

Ye strategy sirf experienced traders ke liye hai. Bahut fast execution chahiye.

Expiry Day Risk Management

Rules (Non-Negotiable):
• Max 1% risk per trade (expiry day pe zyada risky hai)
• Position size half of normal day
• Stop loss tight rakhao (30-50% of premium)
• No new entries after 2:30 PM (settlement time)
• All positions close by 3:15 PM (auto square-off)

Expiry Day Don'ts

❌ OTM options hold till expiry (worthless ho jaayega) ❌ ATM straddle without stop loss ❌ Expiry day pe new strategy try karna ❌ News ke basis pe expiry trade ❌ Expiry day pe averaging down

Max Pain — Expiry Day Ka Target

Kya Hai Max Pain?

Max Pain wo strike price hai jahan maximum option buyers ko loss hoga expiry pe. Market Max Pain ki taraf move karta hai expiry day pe.

Kaise Calculate Karo?:
• Option chain dekho
• Har strike pe total OI calculate karo (calls + puts)
• Jahan sabse kam total loss hoga option buyers ko = Max Pain

Max Pain Trading:
• Expiry day morning mein Max Pain identify karo
• Market Max Pain se bahut upar hai → SHORT (expect neeche aayega)
• Market Max Pain se bahut neeche hai → LONG (expect upar jaayega)
• Target: Max Pain level

Accuracy: ~70-80% on Nifty expiry days
