Smallcase Kya Hai?

Smallcase ek thematic portfolio platform hai jahan curated stock baskets milte hain. Jaise mutual fund mein fund manager stocks choose karta hai, waise smallcase mein experts ya algorithms stocks ka basket banate hain.

Simple definition: > Smallcase = Ready-made stock portfolio. Theme ya strategy based. Direct tumhare demat mein.

Smallcase vs Mutual Fund

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Ownership — Smallcase: Tumhare demat mein, Mutual Fund: Fund house ke paas
• Transparency — Smallcase: Full (har stock dikhta hai), Mutual Fund: Limited
• Customization — Smallcase: Stocks add/remove kar sakte ho, Mutual Fund: Nahi kar sakte
• Min investment — Smallcase: ~₹3,000-5,000, Mutual Fund: ₹500 SIP
• Expense — Smallcase: ₹0-100/quarter + brokerage, Mutual Fund: 0.5-2% expense ratio
• Tax — Smallcase: Har transaction taxable, Mutual Fund: Fund level taxation
• Rebalancing — Smallcase: Tumhe manually karna padta hai, Mutual Fund: Automatic

Types of Smallcases

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Thematic — EV, AI, Pharma, Banking
• Strategic — Quality, Momentum, Value
• Model Portfolios — Long-term, Balanced, Aggressive
• Smart Beta — Equal weight, Factor-based

Popular Smallcases

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• All Weather Investing — Theme: Balanced, Min Investment: ~₹5,000
• Top 100 Stocks — Theme: Large cap, Min Investment: ~₹10,000
• Momentum — Theme: High momentum stocks, Min Investment: ~₹8,000
• Dividend — Theme: High dividend yield, Min Investment: ~₹7,000
• Electric Vehicle — Theme: EV theme, Min Investment: ~₹5,000

Smallcase Advantages

Transparency

Har stock dikhta hai. Exactly pata hai kahan paisa laga hai.

Direct Ownership

Stocks tumhare demat mein hote hain. Fund ke paas nahi.

Customization

Agar koi stock pasand nahi aaya toh hata sakte ho.

No Lock-in

Kabhi bhi bech sakte ho. No exit load (mostly).

Smallcase Disadvantages

Brokerage Lagta Hai

Har buy/sell pe brokerage lagta hai. Mutual fund mein nahi lagta.

Rebalancing Manual

Smallcase rebalance kare toh tumhe manually execute karna padta hai.

Short Track Record

Zyadatar smallcases ka 2-3 saal ka track record hai. Long-term performance unknown.

Tax Inefficient

Har rebalance mein capital gains tax lagta hai. Mutual fund mein fund level taxation hota hai.
