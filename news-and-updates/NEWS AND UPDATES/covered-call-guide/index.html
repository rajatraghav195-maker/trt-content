Covered Call Kya Hai?

Covered call ek income strategy hai jahan tumhare paas pehle se stocks hain aur tum upar se call option bechte ho. Premium milta hai, but upside limited ho jaata hai.

Simple definition: > Covered call = Stock rakho + Call becho = Premium income + Limited upside

How It Works

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Step 1: 100 shares of Reliance @ ₹2,500 (cost: ₹2,50,000)
• Step 2: Sell 2,600 CE @ ₹50 premium
• Step 3: Premium received: ₹50 × 25 (lot size) = ₹1,250
• If Reliance stays below ₹2,600:
• ✅ Keep shares + Keep premium = ₹1,250 income
• If Reliance goes above ₹2,600:
• ✅ Shares called away at ₹2,600
• ✅ Profit: (₹2,600 - ₹2,500) × 25 = ₹2,500
• ✅ Plus premium: ₹1,250
• ✅ Total: ₹3,750 (but missed upside beyond ₹2,600)

Covered Call Payoff

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• ₹2,300 — Stock P&L: -₹5,000, Option P&L: +₹1,250, Total P&L: -₹3,750
• ₹2,400 — Stock P&L: -₹2,500, Option P&L: +₹1,250, Total P&L: -₹1,250
• ₹2,500 — Stock P&L: ₹0, Option P&L: +₹1,250, Total P&L: +₹1,250
• ₹2,600 — Stock P&L: +₹2,500, Option P&L: +₹1,250, Total P&L: +₹3,750
• ₹2,700 — Stock P&L: +₹5,000, Option P&L: -₹1,250, Total P&L: +₹3,750
• ₹2,800 — Stock P&L: +₹7,500, Option P&L: -₹3,750, Total P&L: +₹3,750

Key insight: Profit capped at ₹3,750 regardless of how high stock goes.

When to Use Covered Call

Best When:
• Stock range-bound hai (₹2,400-2,600)
• Market sideways hai
• Income chahiye from existing holdings
• Mildly bullish (not strongly bullish)

Avoid When:
• Strongly bullish (upside miss hoga)
• Stock about to break out
• Results season (gap risk)

Indian Market Considerations

Lot Size Matters:
• Nifty lot: 25 → Sell 1 lot = 25 units covered
• Bank Nifty lot: 15 → Sell 1 lot = 15 units covered
• Stock lots vary (Reliance = 250)

Monthly Income Example:
• Portfolio: ₹10 lakh (4 stocks)
• Monthly covered call premium: ₹5,000-10,000
• Annual income: ₹60,000-1,20,000
• Return enhancement: 6-12% extra

Covered Call vs Naked Call

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Risk — Covered Call: Limited (stock hedge), Naked Call: Unlimited
• Margin — Covered Call: Lower, Naked Call: Higher
• Best for — Covered Call: Income, Naked Call: Speculation
• Complexity — Covered Call: Low, Naked Call: High
