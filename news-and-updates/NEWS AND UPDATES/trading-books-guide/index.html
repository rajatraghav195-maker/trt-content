Best Trading Books for Indian Traders (Hindi)

Best trading books for Indian traders — Hindi aur English dono mein. Technical analysis, psychology, risk management ki top books.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 18, 2026 · Features

Best trading books for Indian traders — Hindi aur English dono mein. Technical analysis, psychology, risk management ki top books.

Best Trading Books — Hindi Mein

1. Technical Analysis of Financial Markets — John Murphy

Level: Beginner to Intermediate Language: English (Hindi translation available) Kyun padho: Technical analysis ki BIBLE hai ye book. Candlesticks, indicators, chart patterns — sab cover hai.

Key Learnings:
• Chart patterns ka complete guide
• Technical indicators kaise use karein
• Market structure samjho
• Trend analysis seekho

Indian traders ke liye: 90% concepts Indian market pe directly apply hote hain. Nifty, Bank Nifty, stocks — sab pe.

2. Trading in the Zone — Mark Douglas

Level: All levels Language: English Kyun padho: Trading psychology ki BEST book. 90% traders psychology ki wajah se lose karte hain — ye book wo problem solve karti hai.

Key Learnings:
• Probability thinking seekho
• Emotions se kaise deal karein
• Trading plan kaise banao
• Consistency kaise achieve karo

Indian traders ke liye: Har trader ko ye book padhni chahiye — chahe intraday ho ya swing.

3. The Intelligent Investor — Benjamin Graham

Level: Beginner to Advanced Language: English (Hindi translation available) Kyun padho: Warren Buffett ki favorite book. Value investing ki foundation ye book hai.

Key Learnings:
• Value investing principles
• Margin of safety concept
• Long-term investing mindset
• Market Mr. Metaphor

Indian traders ke liye: Investors ke liye MUST READ. Traders ke liye bhi useful — market psychology samjhne ke liye.

4. Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques — Steve Nison

Level: Beginner to Intermediate Language: English Kyun padho: Candlestick patterns ki definitive guide. Steve Nison ne candlesticks ko West mein introduce kiya.

Key Learnings:
• 50+ candlestick patterns
• Single, double, triple candle patterns
• Candlestick + western indicators combo
• Real trading examples

Indian traders ke liye: Candlestick patterns Indian market pe bahut achha kaam karte hain. Daily chart pe especially.

5. How to Make Money in Stocks — William O'Neil

Level: Intermediate Language: English Kyun padho: CANSLIM method seekho — growth stocks identify karne ka systematic approach.

Key Learnings:
• CANSLIM method (7 factors)
• Growth stock selection
• When to buy and sell
• Chart reading for growth stocks

Indian traders ke liye: CANSLIM method Indian large-caps pe achha kaam karta hai. TCS, Infosys, Bajaj Finance jaisi growth companies ke liye.

Hindi Mein Trading Books

6. Share Market Se Kaise Kamaein — Mahesh Chandra Kaushik

Level: Beginner Language: Hindi Kyun padho: Hindi mein sabse popular trading book. Basics se lekar advanced tak — sab cover hai.

Key Learnings:
• Share market basics Hindi mein
• Technical analysis simplified
• Practical trading strategies
• Indian market specific examples

7. Stock Market Mein Safalta Ke 100 Sutra — Ravi Patel

Level: Beginner to Intermediate Language: Hindi Kyun padho: 100 practical rules for stock market success. Short, actionable, easy to follow.

Key Learnings:
• 100 trading rules
• Risk management simplified
• Market psychology Hindi mein
• Indian market specific

8. Paisa Hi Paisa — Arun Kumar Sharma

Level: Beginner Language: Hindi Kyun padho: Personal finance + investing basics Hindi mein. Agar bilkul beginner ho toh ye book se start karo.

Key Learnings:
• Savings aur investment basics
• Mutual funds, stocks, FDs
• Tax planning
• Financial planning

Advanced Books

9. Market Wizards — Jack Schwager

Level: Intermediate to Advanced Language: English Kyun padho: World ke best traders ke interviews. Soros, Dalio, Druckenmiller — sabki strategies seekho.

Key Learnings:
• Top traders ki mindset
• Risk management philosophies
• Different trading styles
• Real-world trading stories

10. Reminiscences of a Stock Operator — Edwin Lefèvre

Level: All levels Language: English Kyun padho: 1923 mein likhi gayi classic. Jesse Livermore ki story — market psychology ka sabse achha example.

Key Learnings:
• Market psychology timeless lessons
• Speculation vs investing
• Crowd behavior
• Trading history
