Stock Market Kya Hai?

Stock market ek marketplace hai jahan companies ke shares (stocks) kharidne aur bechne ka kaam hota hai. Jab tum kisi company ka stock kharidte ho, tum us company ke chhote se owner ban jaate ho.

Simple definition: > Stock market = Digital marketplace jahan companies shares bechti hain aur investors shares kharidte hain.

India Ka Stock Market

India mein 2 stock exchanges hain:
• NSE (National Stock Exchange) — Zyada popular, zyada volume
• BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) — Asia ka sabse purana exchange (1875)

SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) stock market ka regulator hai. Ye market ko regulate karta hai, investors ko protect karta hai, aur fraud prevent karta hai.

Stock Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Basic Mechanism: Demand and Supply

Stock prices demand aur supply se decide hote hain:

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Zyada log kharid rahe hain (demand > supply) → Price upar jaata hai
• Zyada log bech rahe hain (supply > demand) → Price neeche jaata hai

Trading Process:
• 1. Company IPO se shares issue karti hai
• 2. Shares NSE/BSE pe listed hote hain
• 3. Broker ke through tum buy/sell karte ho
• 4. Orders exchange pe match hote hain
• 5. Settlement T+1 hota hai (next day shares demat mein)

Types of Trading:
• Intraday — Holding Period: Same day, Chart Timeframe: 1-5 min, Risk Level: Very High
• Swing — Holding Period: 2-10 days, Chart Timeframe: Daily, Risk Level: High
• Positional — Holding Period: Weeks-months, Chart Timeframe: Weekly, Risk Level: Medium
• Long-term — Holding Period: Years, Chart Timeframe: Monthly, Risk Level: Lower

India Ke Stock Market Indices

Nifty 50:
• Kya hai: NSE ke top 50 stocks ka index
• Base value: 1,000 (Nov 1995)
• Current: ~20,000+ (2026)
• Significance: India ka sabse important benchmark

Sensex:
• Kya hai: BSE ke top 30 stocks ka index
• Base value: 100 (1979)
• Current: ~65,000+ (2026)
• Significance: India ka oldest index

Bank Nifty:
• Kya hai: Top 12 banking stocks ka index
• Significance: Banking sector ka benchmark
• Volatility: Nifty se 3x zyada

India VIX:
• Kya hai: Market volatility index
• Significance: Fear gauge — high VIX = fearful market

Stock Market Mein Paisa Kaise Banta Hai?

1. Capital Gains (Price Appreciation):
• Stock ₹100 pe kharida → ₹200 ho gaya → ₹100 profit
• Long-term: >1 year hold → 10% LTCG tax (₹1L exemption)
• Short-term: <1 year hold → 15% STCG tax

2. Dividends:
• Company profit mein se dividend deti hai shareholders ko
• ITC, Coal India, ONGC — high dividend stocks
• Tax: Slab rate (after ₹10 lakh)

3. Bonus Shares:
• Company free mein extra shares deti hai existing shareholders ko
• Example: 1:1 bonus → 100 shares ke 100 extra milenge

4. Stock Splits:
• Company share price divide karti hai
• Example: ₹10,000 share → 10 shares of ₹1,000 each
• Value same rehti hai, but liquidity badhti hai

India Ka Stock Market History

Ye das baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1992 — Event: Harshad Mehta scam, Nifty Impact: -40%
• 2000 — Event: Dot-com bubble, Nifty Impact: -50%
• 2008 — Event: Global financial crisis, Nifty Impact: -60%
• 2009 — Event: Recovery begins, Nifty Impact: +75% (2009)
• 2016 — Event: Demonetization, Nifty Impact: -6% (short-term)
• 2020 — Event: COVID crash, Nifty Impact: -40%
• 2020-21 — Event: Recovery + bull run, Nifty Impact: +100%+
• 2022 — Event: Russia-Ukraine, rate hikes, Nifty Impact: -15%
• 2023-24 — Event: New highs, Nifty Impact: +20%+
• 2025-26 — Event: Consolidation, Nifty Impact: +8-10%

Key lesson: Market crashes hota hai, but long-term mein hamesha recover hota hai. Nifty 1995 mein 1,000 tha, 2026 mein 20,000+ hai. 20x in 30 years.

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1 blue-chip share — Minimum: ₹200 (ITC), Recommended: ₹5,000+
• Nifty ETF — Minimum: ₹200 (1 unit), Recommended: ₹10,000+
• Mutual Fund SIP — Minimum: ₹500/month, Recommended: ₹5,000/month
• Swing trading — Minimum: ₹25,000, Recommended: ₹1,00,000+
• F&O trading — Minimum: ₹50,000, Recommended: ₹2,00,000+
• Recommended start — Minimum: ₹5,000, Recommended: ₹50,000

Stock Market vs Other Investments

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns (10Y) — Stock Market: 12-15%, FD: 6-7%, Real Estate: 8-12%, Gold: 8-10%
• Risk — Stock Market: High, FD: Very Low, Real Estate: Medium, Gold: Medium
• Liquidity — Stock Market: Instant, FD: Low, Real Estate: Very Low, Gold: Moderate
• Minimum — Stock Market: ₹200, FD: ₹1,000, Real Estate: ₹10L+, Gold: ₹1,000
• Effort — Stock Market: High, FD: Zero, Real Estate: High, Gold: Low
• Tax — Stock Market: LTCG 10%, FD: Slab rate, Real Estate: Indexation, Gold: Slab rate

Stock Market Beginners Ke Liye Tips
