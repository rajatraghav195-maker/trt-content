Children's Education Planning India — Hindi Guide

Bachhon ki education ke liye kaise plan karein, corpus calculation, investment options — Hindi guide.

Bachhon ki education ke liye kaise plan karein, corpus calculation, investment options — Hindi guide.

Education Planning Kya Hai?

Aaj ki date mein ek achhe college ki fees ₹20-50 lakh hai. 15-20 saal baad ₹1-2 crore ho sakti hai. Abhi se plan karo, nahi toh baad mein bahut mushkil hogi.

Education Cost Today vs Future

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Engineering (IIT) — Current Cost: ₹10-15 lakh, Cost After 15 Years (8% inflation): ₹32-48 lakh
• MBA (IIM) — Current Cost: ₹20-30 lakh, Cost After 15 Years (8% inflation): ₹64-96 lakh
• Medical (AIIMS) — Current Cost: ₹5-10 lakh, Cost After 15 Years (8% inflation): ₹16-32 lakh
• MS (USA) — Current Cost: ₹50 lakh-1 Cr, Cost After 15 Years (8% inflation): ₹1.6-3.2 Cr
• Undergraduate (Private) — Current Cost: ₹15-25 lakh, Cost After 15 Years (8% inflation): ₹48-80 lakh

Investment Options

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• SIP in Equity MF — Returns: 12-15%, Risk: Medium, Best For: Long-term (10+ years)
• PPF — Returns: 7.1%, Risk: Very Low, Best For: Safe, 15-year lock-in
• Sukanya Samriddhi — Returns: 8.2%, Risk: Very Low, Best For: Girl child (0-10 years)
• SSY + SIP combo — Returns: 8-12%, Risk: Low-Medium, Best For: Balanced approach
• Child Plan (ULIP) — Returns: 8-10%, Risk: Medium, Best For: Avoid (expensive)

Education Fund Strategy

Step 1: Calculate Corpus:
• Current cost: ₹20 lakh (engineering)
• Inflation: 8%
• Years to goal: 15
• Future cost: ₹63.5 lakh

Step 2: Start SIP:
• ₹63.5 lakh target, 15 years, 12% returns
• Monthly SIP needed: ₹14,500

Step 3: Increase SIP

Har saal 10% increase karo. ₹14,500 se start, next year ₹16,000.

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (Girl Child)

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Who — Girl child 0-10 years
• Interest — 8.2% (govt-backed)
• Lock-in — 21 years (partial at 18)
• Max deposit — ₹1.5 lakh/year
• Tax benefit — 80C + Interest tax-free

Best for: Girl child education + marriage fund.

Education Planning Mistakes

Late Start

"10 saal baad sochenge" — Compound interest ka time chahiye.

Only Insurance Child Plans

ULIP child plans expensive hain. Term insurance + SIP better hai.

No Inflation Adjustment

₹20 lakh aaj hai, 15 saal baad ₹64 lakh hoga. Inflation factor karo.

Education Loan Pe Depend

Education loan last option hai. Pehle savings karo.
