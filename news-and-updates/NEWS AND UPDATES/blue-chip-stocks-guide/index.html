Blue Chip Stocks Kya Hain?

Blue chip stocks wo companies ke shares hain jo market leaders hain — large market cap, stable earnings, consistent dividends, aur strong brand.

Simple definition: > Blue chip = Industry ka sabse bada aur reliable player. Risk kam, returns steady.
