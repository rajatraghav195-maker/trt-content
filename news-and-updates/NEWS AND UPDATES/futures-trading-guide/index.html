Futures Trading Kya Hai?

Futures ek derivative contract hai jahan buyer aur seller agree karte hain ki ek specific date pe ek specific price pe ek specific quantity ka asset exchange karenge.

Simple definition: > Futures = Agreement to buy/sell something at a fixed price on a future date. Dono parties ko fulfill karna padega — obligation hai.

Real Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Tum Nifty futures BUY karte ho at 20,050 (August expiry)
• Lot size: 25
• Margin required: ~₹1,00,000

Agar Nifty 20,300 ho gaya:
• Profit: (20,300 - 20,050) × 25 = ₹6,250

Agar Nifty 19,800 ho gaya:
• Loss: (20,050 - 19,800) × 25 = ₹6,250

Futures vs Options

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Obligation — Futures: MUST buy/sell, Options: RIGHT (not obligation)
• Premium — Futures: No premium (margin), Options: Premium pay karta hai
• Max loss — Futures: Unlimited, Options: Premium paid (buyer)
• Settlement — Futures: Daily MTM, Options: At expiry
• Margin — Futures: ~10-15% of contract, Options: Premium only (buyer)
• Best for — Futures: Directional bets, Options: Hedging, limited risk

Types of Futures

1. Index Futures:
• Underlying: Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Nifty IT
• Lot size: Nifty = 25, Bank Nifty = 15
• Expiry: Last Thursday of month
• Settlement: Cash

2. Stock Futures:
• Underlying: Individual stocks (Reliance, TCS, etc.)
• Lot size: Varies by stock
• Expiry: Last Thursday of month
• Settlement: Physical delivery (rare for retail)

3. Currency Futures:
• Underlying: USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR
• Lot size: $1,000 (USD/INR)
• Expiry: Last working day

4. Commodity Futures:
• Underlying: Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, Natural Gas
• Exchange: MCX
• Lot size: Varies

Futures Trading Mechanics

Margin System

Futures mein tumhe poora price nahi dena — sirf margin (security deposit):

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Nifty 20,000 — Contract Value: ₹5,00,000, Margin Required: ~₹1,00,000 (20%)
• Bank Nifty 45,000 — Contract Value: ₹6,75,000, Margin Required: ~₹1,35,000 (20%)
• Reliance ₹2,500 — Contract Value: ₹2,50,000, Margin Required: ~₹50,000 (20%)

Mark-to-Market (MTM)

Har din P&L settle hota hai:
• Profit hua → Paisa account mein aata hai
• Loss hua → Paisa account se jaata hai
• Margin kam hua → Margin call aata hai (aur paisa daalo)

Expiry Settlement:
• Cash settled: Index futures (Nifty, Bank Nifty)
• Physical delivery: Stock futures (rare for retail)
• Expiry: Last Thursday of month

Futures Trading Strategies

Strategy 1: Directional (Long/Short):
• Bullish → Buy futures (long)
• Bearish → Sell futures (short)
• Simple, direct, leveraged bet on direction

Strategy 2: Hedging:
• Portfolio protect karne ke liye
• Long portfolio → Sell Nifty futures
• Loss in portfolio offset by profit in futures

Strategy 3: Spread Trading:
• Buy near-month, sell far-month (calendar spread)
• Profit from time decay difference
• Lower risk than naked futures

Strategy 4: Arbitrage:
• Cash-futures arbitrage
• Price difference exploit karo between spot and futures
• Low risk, requires fast execution

Futures vs Cash Market

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Leverage — Futures: ✅ 5-10x, Cash (Delivery): ❌ 1x
• Short selling — Futures: ✅ Easy, Cash (Delivery): ⚠️ Limited
• Cost — Futures: Margin + MTM, Cash (Delivery): Full price
• Holding — Futures: Till expiry, Cash (Delivery): Unlimited
• Risk — Futures: Higher (leverage), Cash (Delivery): Lower
• Settlement — Futures: Expiry, Cash (Delivery): T+1

Risk Management for Futures

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Max risk per trade — 2% of capital
• Stop loss — Always use
• Position size — 1 lot initially
• Max open positions — 2-3
• Margin utilization — < 50%

Warning: Futures mein leverage = amplified risk. 1% move in underlying = 5-10% impact on capital.
