Bank Nifty trading strategies — intraday, expiry day, options selling — complete Hindi guide.

Bank Nifty Trading Characteristics

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Lot size — 15
• Expiry — Weekly (Wednesday)
• Daily range — 400-800 points
• Volatility — 3x Nifty
• Best for — Experienced traders

Top 5 Bank Nifty Strategies

1. Opening Range Breakout (ORB):
• First 15-30 minutes ka high/low note karo
• Break above high → BUY
• Break below low → SELL/SHORT
• Stop loss: Opposite end of range
• Best time: 9:45-11:00 AM

2. VWAP Strategy:
• Price above VWAP → BUY bias
• Price below VWAP → SELL bias
• VWAP pe bounce → Entry signal
• Best for: Trending days

3. Expiry Day Straddle Sell:
• Wednesday expiry pe ATM Call + Put sell karo
• Premium decay se profit
• Stop loss: 1.5x premium collected
• Entry: 9:45-10:30 AM

4. Bank Nifty vs Nifty Spread:
• Bank Nifty outperforming Nifty → Long Bank Nifty, Short Nifty
• Bank Nifty underperforming → Short Bank Nifty, Long Nifty
• Mean reversion play

5. Iron Condor (Expiry Week):
• Monday/Tuesday setup karo
• OTM Call spread + OTM Put spread
• Profit from range-bound movement
• Close by Wednesday 2:30 PM

Bank Nifty Risk Management

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Max risk per trade — 1%
• Position size — 1 lot initially
• Stop loss — Always use
• Daily loss limit — 3%
• No trading — First 15 min

Bank Nifty vs Nifty Options

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Lot size — Bank Nifty: 15, Nifty: 25
• Expiry — Bank Nifty: Wednesday, Nifty: Thursday
• Premium — Bank Nifty: Higher, Nifty: Lower
• Volatility — Bank Nifty: 3x, Nifty: 1x
• Best for — Bank Nifty: Experienced, Nifty: Beginners
