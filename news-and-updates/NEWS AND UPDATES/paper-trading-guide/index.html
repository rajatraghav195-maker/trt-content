Paper Trading Kya Hai?

Paper trading ka matlab hai bina real money ke trading practice karna. Virtual money se stocks kharidna aur bechna — sirf seekhne ke liye.

Simple definition: > Paper trading = Trading ka practice mode. Jaise video game mein tutorial hota hai — waise paper trading mein bina paisa lagaye seekhte ho.

Why It's Called "Paper Trading"

Purane zamane mein traders apne trades paper pe likhte the — actual trade nahi karte the, sirf record rakhte the ki "agar ye trade kiya hota toh kya hota." Isliye naam "paper trading."

• Win rate calculate karo

• Strategy refine karo

Paper Trading Ka Importance

Why Beginners MUST Paper Trade:
• No risk — Paisa nahi khooge
• Learn mechanics — Order placement, stop loss, charts
• Test strategies — Kaunsa strategy kaam karta hai
• Build confidence — Jab tak confident na ho, real money mat lagao
• Identify mistakes — Galtiyan karo bina paisa kho

Real Stat

> 90% retail traders lose money. Agar paper trading skip karoge toh real money se seekhna padega — wo bahut mehenga lesson hoga.

Paper Trading vs Real Trading

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Money — Paper Trading: Virtual, Real Trading: Real
• Risk — Paper Trading: Zero, Real Trading: Real loss possible
• Emotions — Paper Trading: None, Real Trading: High (fear, greed)
• Execution — Paper Trading: Always fills, Real Trading: Slippage possible
• Psychology — Paper Trading: Easy, Real Trading: Difficult
• Learning — Paper Trading: Safe, Real Trading: Expensive

The Gap

Paper trading mein profitable ho? Real trading mein bhi hoge? Zaroori nahi.

Kyun? Kyunki paper trading mein:
• Emotions nahi hote (paisa nahi lag raha)
• Slippage nahi hoti (order hamesha fill hota hai)
• Fear/greed nahi hota

Solution: Paper trading ke baad SMALL capital se start karo. ₹10,000-50,000 se. Emotions ko handle karna seekho.

Paper Trading Rules

Rule 1: Real Capital Treat Karo

"Virtual hai toh kuch bhi karoon" — GALAT. Real capital jaisa treat karo. Same position sizing, same risk management.

Rule 2: Same Strategy Use Karo

Har trade pe alag strategy test mat karo. Ek strategy choose karo, usko consistent use karo.

Rule 3: Minimum 3 Months Practice

3 months paper trading karo before going live. Kam se kam 50-100 trades complete karo.

Rule 4: Journal Maintain Karo

Har trade ka record rakho:
• Entry reason
• Entry price
• Stop loss
• Target
• Actual exit
• Profit/Loss
• Lesson learned

Rule 5: Don't Stay Forever

Paper trading mein 6+ months mat ruko. 3 months practice → Small capital → Scale up.

Common Paper Trading Mistakes

Realistic Capital Nahi Rakhte

₹10 crore ka paper trading karna = meaningless. Real jitna capital rakho.

Emotions Ignore Karte Hain

"Paper hai toh koi tension nahi" — Real trading mein tension hogi. Emotions ko simulate karo.

Too Long Paper Trading

6 months+ paper trading = comfort zone se bahar nahi aana. 3 months practice → live.

No Journal

Journal nahi rakho toh mistakes repeat hongi. Har trade ka record zaroori.

Strategy Change Every Week

Ek strategy pe consistent ruko. Har week naya strategy = kuch nahi seekhoge.
