BTST and STBT — Hindi Guide

BTST kya hai, STBT kya hai, BTST charges, risks — complete Hindi guide Indian traders ke liye.

BTST Kya Hai?

BTST = Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow. Aaj stock kharido, kal becho — bina demat mein aaye.

Simple definition: > BTST = Intraday aur delivery ka hybrid. Aaj buy, kal sell. Demat mein share aata nahi, but bech sakte ho.

BTST vs Intraday vs Delivery

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Buy — BTST: Today, Intraday: Today, Delivery: Today
• Sell — BTST: Next day, Intraday: Same day, Delivery: Anytime
• Demat — BTST: No credit, Intraday: No credit, Delivery: Credit hota hai
• Margin — BTST: Available, Intraday: Available, Delivery: Full payment
• Risk — BTST: Gap up/down, Intraday: Intraday only, Delivery: Long-term

BTST Charges

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Brokerage — ₹20/order
• STT — 0.1% (delivery rate)
• GST — 18% on brokerage
• Stamp duty — State-wise

BTST Risks

Gap Down Risk

Agar kal stock gap down khule toh loss ho sakta hai. Overnight news risk hai.

Short Delivery

Agar seller ne share deliver nahi kiya toh auction hoga. Short delivery mein heavy loss ho sakta hai.

No Stop Loss

BTST mein overnight stop loss nahi lag sakta. Opening mein gap up/down pe kuch nahi kar sakte.

BTST Strategy

When to Use BTST:
• ✅ Strong close (bullish candle at day end)
• ✅ Gap up expected (positive news)
• ✅ Support pe buy kiya
• ✅ Nifty bullish, stock bhi bullish

When to Avoid BTST:
• ❌ Results season (earnings risk)
• ❌ Global events (US Fed, RBI policy)
• ❌ Weak close (bearish candle)
• ❌ Low volume stocks
