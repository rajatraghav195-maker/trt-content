SUPPORT AUR RESISTANCE: LEVEL NAHI, ZONE HOTE HAIN

Ye kahan se aate hain, kyun kaam karte hain, aur kyun aksar thode se toot jaate hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Har chart par kuch keematein baar-baar aati hain aur wahan kuch hota hai. Unhe support aur resistance kehte hain. Naya trader inhe patli lines samajhta hai; purana trader inhe chaudhe zones samajhta hai. Yahi ek farq bahut saare stop loss bacha leta hai.

YE AATE KAHAN SE HAIN

Ye jaadui numbers nahi hain. Ye wo keematein hain jahan pehle bahut saara sauda hua tha. Wahan log phanse hue hain, wahan logon ne khareeda tha, wahan logon ne becha tha — aur unki yaadein wahi baithi hain.

Isliye jab price dobara wahan aata hai, to log react karte hain: koi break-even par nikalna chahta hai, koi phir se wahi trade lena chahta hai. Yahi reaction level ko zinda rakhta hai.

LINE NAHI, ZONE

Agar aap ek exact number pakad kar baithoge, to price aksar usse thoda neeche jaake wapas aayega — aur aapka stop us 'thode' mein chala jaayega.

Isliye level ko ek band ki tarah kheencho, ek line ki tarah nahi. Us band ki chaudai ussi chart ki normal daily range se aani chahiye, aapki marzi se nahi.

ROLE REVERSAL — TOOTNE KE BAAD KYA HOTA HAI

Ek purana resistance jab saaf toot jaata hai, to wo aksar naya support ban jaata hai. Wajah wahi hai: jo log wahan phanse the wo ab wahan kharidne ko tayaar hain.

Ye har baar nahi hota. Par jab hota hai, to wo aam taur par sabse saaf structure hota hai jo chart deta hai.

FALSE BREAKOUT — SABSE MEHENGA PATTERN

Level toot-ta hua dikhta hai aur wapas andar aa jaata hai. Isse bachne ke kuch aam tareeke:
• Close ka intezaar — intra-period touch se nahi, us timeframe ke close se faisla karo.
• Volume dekho — bina volume ke breakout aksar wapas aa jaata hai.
• Retest ka intezaar — toot-ne ke baad price aksar level par wapas aata hai. Wahan ka behaviour asli confirmation deta hai.
• Sabse chaudhe level ko sabse zyada izzat do — jitni baar price wahan se ghooma hai, utna wo zone bhaari hai.

AUR JAB LEVEL TOOTE

Level tootna galti nahi hai — wo information hai. Agar ek zone jise aapne mazboot maana tha wo aasani se toot gaya, to sabse zaroori sawaal ye nahi ki wapas kab aayega. Sawaal ye hai ki aapki poori padhai galat kyun thi. Wahi jawab agli trade sudharega.
