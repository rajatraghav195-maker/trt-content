SEBI Kya Hai?

SEBI ka full form hai Securities and Exchange Board of India. Ye Indian stock market ka regulator hai — jaise RBI banks ka regulator hai, waise SEBI stock market ka.

Established: 1992 (Harshad Mehta scam ke baad) Headquarters: Mumbai

SEBI Ka Kya Kaam Hai?

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Investor protection — Retail investors ko fraud se bachata hai
• Market regulation — Stock exchanges ko regulate karta hai
• Fraud prevention — Insider trading, pump-and-dump rokta hai
• Company regulation — IPO, listing, disclosure rules
• Broker regulation — Brokers ko license deta hai
• Mutual fund regulation — MF houses ko regulate karta hai

SEBI Key Regulations

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Insider Trading Prohibition — Company insiders ko insider info pe trade karna banned
• Takeover Code — Company acquisition ke rules
• Listing Obligations — Listed companies ko regular disclosure karna padta hai
• Mutual Fund Regulations — MF houses ke liye rules
• Broker Regulations — Brokers ke liye licensing aur conduct rules

SEBI Complaint (SCORES)

Agar tumhara broker ya company galat kar raha hai toh SEBI ko complaint kar sakte ho:

SCORES Portal: scores.gov.in

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Register karo
• Complaint detail daalo
• SEBI investigate karta hai
• Resolution usually 30-60 days mein

SEBI vs RBI

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Regulates — SEBI: Stock market, RBI: Banks
• Established — SEBI: 1992, RBI: 1935
• Headquarters — SEBI: Mumbai, RBI: Mumbai
• Key role — SEBI: Investor protection, RBI: Monetary policy
