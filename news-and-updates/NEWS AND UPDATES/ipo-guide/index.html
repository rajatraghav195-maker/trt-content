IPO Kya Hai? Complete Guide for Beginners (Hindi)

IPO kya hai, kaise apply karein, lot size, allotment process — complete Hindi guide Indian stock market ke liye.

IPO Kya Hai?

IPO ka full form hai Initial Public Offering. Jab ek company pehli baar apne shares public ko offer karti hai stock exchange pe — usko IPO kehte hain.

Simple definition: > IPO = Company pehli baar apne shares bech rahi hai public ko. Tum company ke part-owner ban sakte ho.

Real Example:
• Zomato IPO (2021): ₹72-76 price band, ₹9,375 crore raised
• Paytm IPO (2021): ₹2,080-2,150 price band, ₹18,300 crore raised
• Tata Technologies IPO (2023): ₹475-500 price band, huge oversubscription

IPO Process Kaise Hota Hai?

Step 1: Company Decides to Go Public

Company ko paisa chahiye expansion ke liye → IPO launch karne ka decision

Step 2: SEBI Approval

Company SEBI (market regulator) ke paas Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) file karti hai. SEBI review karta hai aur approval deta hai.

Step 3: Price Band Announced

Company ek price band announce karti hai:
• Example: ₹300-350 per share
• Tum ₹300 se ₹350 ke beech mein bid kar sakte ho

Step 4: IPO Open Date

IPO 3-5 din ke liye open hota hai. Is period mein tum apply kar sakte ho.

Step 5: Lot Size

IPO mein minimum ek "lot" apply karna padta hai:
• Example: Lot size = 40 shares
• Price ₹350 → Minimum investment: 40 × ₹350 = ₹14,000

Step 6: Subscription Status

IPO period mein subscription status aata hai:
• 1x = Exactly demand = supply
• 3x = 3x demand > supply (oversubscribed)
• 10x = Bahut zyada demand → allotment mushkil

Step 7: Allotment

Agar IPO oversubscribed hai toh lottery system se allotment hota hai:
• 1 lot guaranteed nahi hai
• Retail category mein usually 1 lot mil jaata hai (small IPOs mein)
• Bade IPOs mein partial allotment hota hai

Step 8: Listing Day

Shares stock exchange pe list hote hain:
• Listing gain: Agar IPO price se upar list ho → instant profit
• Listing loss: Agar neeche list ho → instant loss

IPO Mein Kaise Apply Karein?

Via Zerodha (Kite):
• Kite app kholo
• Console → IPO section
• Active IPOs dikhenge
• Apply karo → UPI ID daalo
• Lot size select karo
• Submit karo
• UPI mandate approve karo (bank app mein)

Via Groww:
• Groww app kholo
• IPO tab
• Active IPO select karo
• Apply karo → UPI ID
• Lot size + price select karo
• Submit + UPI mandate approve

Via Bank (ASBA):
• Net banking login karo
• IPO/ASBA section
• IPO select karo
• Application fill karo
• Amount freeze hoga (ASBA)

IPO Application Tips

Tip 1: DRHP Padho

DRHP mein company ki complete information hoti hai:
• Business model
• Financial statements
• Risk factors
• Use of proceeds
• Management team

Tip 2: Grey Market Premium (GMP) Dekho

GMP batata hai ki market IPO ko kaisa reaction de raha hai:
• GMP positive = Listing premium expected
• GMP negative = Listing discount expected
• GMP zero = Neutral

Tip 3: Subscription Status Check Karo

Oversubscription se allotment probability samjh aata hai:
• Retail 1x = Easily mil jaayega
• Retail 10x = Allotment mushkil
• QIB high = Institutional interest strong

Tip 4: Multiple Applications Mat Karo

Ek PAN se sirf ek application allowed hai. Multiple applications = rejection.

Tip 5: Listing Day Pe Decision:
• Listing gain mila? → Book profit (partial ya full)
• Listing loss hua? → Company fundamentals check karo → Hold ya exit

IPO Risks

No Guaranteed Allotment

Oversubscribed IPOs mein allotment milna mushkil hai. Apply karne ka matlab allotment nahi.

Listing Loss

IPO price se neeche list ho sakta hai. Paytm IPO ne listing day pe 27% loss diya.

Lock-Up Period

Promoters ke shares 6-12 months lock-in mein hote hain. Lock-up khatam hone pe selling pressure aa sakta hai.

Overvaluation

Kuch companies overvaluation pe IPO launch karti hain. Long-term mein stock girta hai.
