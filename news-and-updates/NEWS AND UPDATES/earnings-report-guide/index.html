Quarterly Results Kaise Padhein? Earnings Guide (Hindi)

Quarterly results kaise padhein, key metrics kya dekhein, earnings analysis — complete Hindi guide Indian stocks ke liye.

Quarterly Results Kya Hai?

Har quarter (3 months) mein listed companies apne financial results publish karti hain. Ye results batate hain ki company ne kitna profit kamaya, kitna revenue badha, aur business kaise perform kiya.

India mein results season:
• Q1 (Apr-Jun): July-August mein aate hain
• Q2 (Jul-Sep): October-November mein
• Q3 (Oct-Dec): January-February mein
• Q4 (Jan-Mar): April-May mein

Quarterly Results Mein Kya Dekhein?

1. Revenue (Top Line):
• Kya hai: Company ka total sales
• Kya dekho: Year-on-year (YoY) aur Quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) growth
• Good: Revenue growth > 15% YoY
• Bad: Revenue decline or flat

2. Net Profit (Bottom Line):
• Kya hai: Revenue - All expenses = Net Profit
• Kya dekho: Profit growth YoY and QoQ
• Good: Profit growth > 20% YoY
• Bad: Profit decline, especially with rising revenue (margin pressure)

3. EBITDA:
• Kya hai: Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization
• Kya dekho: Operating profitability
• Good: EBITDA margin improving
• Bad: EBITDA margin declining

4. Margins:
• Operating Margin: EBITDA / Revenue
• Net Margin: Net Profit / Revenue
• Good: Margins stable or improving
• Bad: Margins declining (cost pressure)

5. EPS (Earnings Per Share):
• Kya hai: Net Profit / Total Shares
• Kya dekho: EPS growth YoY
• Good: EPS growth > 15%
• Bad: EPS decline

Results Analysis Framework

Step 1: Expectations vs Reality:
• Beat expectations: Stock upar jaata hai
• Miss expectations: Stock neeche jaata hai
• In-line: Neutral reaction

Kahan dekho: Analyst estimates (Moneycontrol, Screener.in)

Step 2: Management Commentary:
• Guidance: Company aage kya expect karti hai
• Order book: Naye orders mile?
• Capex: Investment plans?
• Challenges: Kya problems hain?

Step 3: Segment Analysis:
• Kaunsa segment strong hai?
• Kaunsa segment weak hai?
• Revenue mix change hua?

Step 4: Balance Sheet Check:
• Debt badha ya gira?
• Cash flow kaisa hai?
• Working capital position?

Results Trading Strategy

Before Results:
• IV high hota hai (options expensive)
• Sell options (premium collect karo)
• Ya avoid — wait for results

After Results:
• IV crush hota hai (premium girta hai)
• Good results + IV crush → Stock up, but options may not
• Bad results + IV crush → Stock down + options crash

Results Day Trading:
• Gap up/down expected hota hai
• First 15 minutes observe karo
• Trend ke saath trade karo
• Stop loss tight rakho

Key Terms in Results

Ye nau baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• YoY — Year-on-year (same quarter last year se compare)
• QoQ — Quarter-on-quarter (previous quarter se compare)
• Revenue — Total sales
• EBITDA — Operating profit
• PAT — Profit After Tax
• EPS — Earnings Per Share
• Guidance — Company ki future expectations
• Exceptional items — One-time gains/losses
• OPM — Operating Profit Margin
