Trading Kya Hai?

Trading ka matlab hai stocks ya other financial instruments ko short-term mein kharidna aur bechna — profit ke liye. Investment lambi duration ke liye hota hai, trading chhoti duration ke liye.

Simple definition: > Trading = Stocks kharidna aur bechna jaldi — din, hafte, ya mahine mein. Profit price movement se aata hai.

Trading vs Investing:
• Duration — Trading: Minutes to months, Investing: Years
• Goal — Trading: Short-term profit, Investing: Long-term wealth
• Analysis — Trading: Technical (charts), Investing: Fundamental (financials)
• Frequency — Trading: High (daily/weekly), Investing: Low (monthly/yearly)
• Risk — Trading: Very high, Investing: Moderate
• Time required — Trading: Full-time, Investing: Part-time
• Returns — Trading: Variable (losses common), Investing: 12-15% avg (long-term)

Important: 90% retail traders lose money. Trading is NOT easy money. It requires skill, discipline, and years of practice.

Types of Trading

1. Intraday Trading (Day Trading):
• Duration: Same day (buy and sell within market hours)
• Chart: 1-min to 15-min
• Risk: Very high (leverage used)
• Best for: Full-time traders
• Capital needed: ₹1,00,000+

2. Swing Trading:
• Duration: 2-10 days
• Chart: Daily
• Risk: High
• Best for: Working professionals
• Capital needed: ₹50,000+

3. Positional Trading:
• Duration: Weeks to months
• Chart: Weekly
• Risk: Moderate
• Best for: Part-time traders
• Capital needed: ₹1,00,000+

4. Scalping:
• Duration: Seconds to minutes
• Chart: 1-min
• Risk: Very high
• Best for: Professional traders
• Capital needed: ₹5,00,000+

5. Algorithmic Trading:
• Duration: Automated
• Chart: Any
• Risk: Depends on strategy
• Best for: Tech-savvy traders
• Capital needed: ₹10,00,000+

Trading Kaise Start Karein?

Step 1: Seekho (1-3 Months):
• TRT ke articles padho (50+ Hinglish articles)
• Candlestick patterns seekho
• RSI, MACD, Moving Averages samjho
• Support/Resistance identify karna seekho
• Risk management samjho

Step 2: Paper Trading (1-3 Months):
• Virtual money se practice karo
• TradingView ya TRT pe chart analysis karo
• Apna trading plan banao
• Journal maintain karo
• Jab consistently profitable ho tab real money pe aao

Step 3: Small Capital Se Start (3-6 Months):
• ₹10,000-50,000 se start karo
• Sirf 1-2 stocks pe focus karo
• Swing trading se start karo (intraday nahi)
• 2% risk per trade rule follow karo
• Emotions control karna seekho

Step 4: Scale Up (6+ Months):
• Capital gradually badhao
• Strategies expand karo
• Intraday try karo (jab experienced ho)
• F&O tabhi try karo jab 1+ year experience ho

Trading Analysis Methods

1. Technical Analysis (Charts):
• Kya hai: Price charts aur indicators se future price predict karna
• Tools: RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, Candlestick patterns
• Best for: Short-term trading
• TRT pe: Full charting workspace available

2. Fundamental Analysis (Financials):
• Kya hai: Company ki financial health analyze karna
• Tools: P/E ratio, EPS, Revenue growth, Debt levels
• Best for: Long-term investing
• TRT pe: Index detail pages pe financial data

3. Sentiment Analysis (Mood):
• Kya hai: Market ka overall mood samajhna
• Tools: India VIX, PCR ratio, FII/DII data
• Best for: Timing entries/exits
• TRT pe: Calendar pe events, VIX data

Trading Instruments in India

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Equity (Cash) — What It Is: Shares kharidna/bechna, Risk: Medium, Complexity: Low
• Futures — What It Is: Contract to buy/sell at future date, Risk: High, Complexity: Medium
• Options — What It Is: Right (not obligation) to buy/sell, Risk: Very High, Complexity: High
• ETF — What It Is: Exchange-traded fund (index tracking), Risk: Medium, Complexity: Low
• Commodities — What It Is: Gold, silver, crude oil, Risk: High, Complexity: Medium
• Currency — What It Is: USD/INR, EUR/INR, Risk: High, Complexity: Medium

Beginner recommendation: Equity (cash) se start karo. F&O tabhi jab 1+ year experience.

Trading Costs in India

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Brokerage — ₹20/order (discount broker)
• STT (Securities Transaction Tax) — 0.1% (delivery)
• Exchange charges — 0.00345%
• GST — 18% on brokerage + charges
• Stamp duty — 0.015% (varies by state)
• SEBI turnover fee — 0.0001%

Total per trade: ~0.12-0.15% of trade value

Common Trading Mistakes

No Trading Plan

Bina plan ke trade karna = gambling. Entry, stop loss, target — sab pehle decide karo.

No Stop Loss

"Stop loss lagane se trigger ho jaata hai" — Ye sabse badi galti hai. Bina SL ke ek trade poora account kha sakta hai.

Overtrading

10-15 trades ek din mein = overtrading. 3-5 quality setups pe focus karo.

Revenge Trading

Loss hua → turant doosra trade. Ye emotional trading hai, system nahi.

Tips Follow Karna

"Ye stock badhega" — tips pe trade mat lo. Apna analysis karo.

Leverage Abuse

Intraday mein 5-10x leverage milta hai. 1% move tumhara 10% profit ya loss hai. Careful.

Ignoring Costs

Brokerage + STT + GST + Stamp duty — ye sab add hota hai. 100 trades/month pe significant amount.
