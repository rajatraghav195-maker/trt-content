Options Buying vs Selling — Which is Better? (Hindi)

Options Buying vs Selling

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Capital required — Buying: Low (premium), Selling: High (margin)
• Max loss — Buying: Premium paid, Selling: Unlimited (call seller)
• Max profit — Buying: Unlimited, Selling: Premium received
• Win rate — Buying: 30-40%, Selling: 60-70%
• Time decay — Buying: Enemy (theta hurts), Selling: Friend (theta helps)
• Best for — Buying: Directional bets, Selling: Income, range-bound

Options Buyer

Pros:
• Low capital requirement
• Unlimited profit potential
• Limited loss (premium only)
• Simple to understand

Cons:
• Low win rate (30-40%)
• Time decay against you
• Need big moves to profit
• Volatility crush after events

Best When:
• Strong directional view
• High conviction
• Breakout expected
• Before events (earnings, budget)

Options Seller

Pros:
• High win rate (60-70%)
• Time decay in your favor
• Profit from range-bound market
• Consistent income potential

Cons:
• High capital requirement (margin)
• Unlimited risk (call seller)
• Need margin management
• Black swan events dangerous

Best When:
• Range-bound market
• High IV environment
• After events (volatility crush)
• Experienced traders

Capital Requirements

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Buying 1 Nifty lot — ₹5,000-15,000 (premium)
• Selling 1 Nifty lot — ₹1,00,000-1,50,000 (margin)
• Buying 1 Stock lot — ₹3,000-10,000
• Selling 1 Stock lot — ₹50,000-1,00,000

Win Rate vs Reward

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• BUYER:
• Win rate: 35%
• Average win: ₹5,000
• Average loss: ₹2,000
• Expected value: (0.35 × ₹5,000) - (0.65 × ₹2,000) = ₹450/trade
• SELLER:
• Win rate: 65%
• Average win: ₹2,000
• Average loss: ₹5,000
• Expected value: (0.65 × ₹2,000) - (0.35 × ₹5,000) = ₹-450/trade

Key insight: Both can be profitable with proper risk management. Buyer needs bigger wins, seller needs higher win rate.

Hybrid Approach

Best of Both Worlds:
• Sell options in range-bound market (income)
• Buy options when strong view (directional)
• Spread strategies to limit risk

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 OPTIONS BUYING vs SELLING
• BUYING:
• 📊 Low capital, unlimited profit
• 📊 Win rate: 30-40%
• 📊 Time decay: Enemy
• 📊 Best for: Directional bets, breakouts
• SELLING:
• 📊 High capital, limited profit
• 📊 Win rate: 60-70%
• 📊 Time decay: Friend
• 📊 Best for: Income, range-bound, high IV
• CAPITAL:
• 📊 Buying 1 Nifty lot: ₹5-15K
• 📊 Selling 1 Nifty lot: ₹1-1.5L
• 📊 Buying 1 Stock lot: ₹3-10K
• 📊 Selling 1 Stock lot: ₹50K-1L
• BEST APPROACH:
• ✅ Sell in range-bound (income)
• ✅ Buy when strong view (directional)
• ✅ Spreads to limit risk
• ✅ Never naked sell without knowledge
