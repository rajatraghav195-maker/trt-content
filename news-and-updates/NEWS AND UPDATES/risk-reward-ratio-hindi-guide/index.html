Risk Reward Ratio Hindi: Profitable Trading Ka Foundation

Risk Reward Ratio (R/R) Kya Hai?

Risk Reward ratio batata hai ki aap kitna risk le rahe ho vs kitna profit expect kar rahe ho.

Formula: R/R = (Target - Entry) / (Entry - Stop Loss)

Example:
• Entry: ₹100
• Stop Loss: ₹95 (risk = ₹5)
• Target: ₹110 (reward = ₹10)
• R/R = 10/5 = 1:2

Matlab: ₹1 risk karke ₹2 reward expect kar rahe ho. 1:2 ratio.

Kyun R/R Sabse Important Hai?

The Math Behind Profitability:
• 30% — R/R 1:1: LOSS, R/R 1:2: BREAKEVEN, R/R 1:3: PROFIT
• 40% — R/R 1:1: LOSS, R/R 1:2: PROFIT, R/R 1:3: PROFIT
• 50% — R/R 1:1: BREAKEVEN, R/R 1:2: PROFIT, R/R 1:3: PROFIT
• 60% — R/R 1:1: PROFIT, R/R 1:2: PROFIT, R/R 1:3: PROFIT

Key insight:
• 1:1 R/R pe 60% win rate chahiye = bahut mushkil
• 1:2 R/R pe sirf 40% win rate kaafi = achievable
• 1:3 R/R pe sirf 30% win rate enough = very achievable

Professional traders 40-50% win rate rakhte hain but 1:2+ R/R se profitable hote hain.

Amateurs 70% win rate rakhte hain but 1:0.5 R/R (take profit quickly, let losses run) = net loss.

R/R Calculate Kaise Karein?

For Buy (Long) Trade:
• Entry: ₹500
• Stop Loss: ₹480
• Target: ₹540
• Risk = Entry - SL = 500 - 480 = ₹20
• Reward = Target - Entry = 540 - 500 = ₹40
• R/R = 40/20 = 1:2 ✅

For Sell (Short) Trade:
• Entry: ₹500
• Stop Loss: ₹520
• Target: ₹460
• Risk = SL - Entry = 520 - 500 = ₹20
• Reward = Entry - Target = 500 - 460 = ₹40
• R/R = 40/20 = 1:2 ✅

R-Multiple (Pro Concept):
• 1R = risk amount (e.g., ₹20)
• 2R = 2x risk = ₹40 profit
• -1R = loss = -₹20
• Trade result in R-multiples: +2R, -1R, +3R, -1R, +2R

Monthly P&L in R-multiples:
• Trade 1: +2R
• Trade 2: -1R
• Trade 3: +3R
• Trade 4: -1R
• Trade 5: +2R
• Total: +5R
• If 1R = ₹1,000 → Monthly profit = ₹5,000

Minimum R/R Rules

By Trading Style:
• Intraday — Min R/R: 1:1.5, Why: Less time, smaller moves
• Swing — Min R/R: 1:2, Why: Enough time for 2x move
• Positional — Min R/R: 1:3, Why: Days/weeks for big moves
• Options Buying — Min R/R: 1:2, Why: Theta decay = need bigger wins
• Options Selling — Min R/R: 1:1, Why: High win rate (70%+) compensates

Golden Rules:
• Never trade below 1:1.5 R/R — mathematically losing proposition
• 1:2 minimum for swing trading — standard professional
• 1:3 for positional — bigger moves justify patience
• If setup doesn't meet R/R, SKIP IT — no trade is better than bad trade

R/R Improve Kaise Karein?

Method 1: Tighter Stop Loss:
• Wider SL = worse R/R
• Tighter SL = better R/R (but more whipsaws)
• Use ATR: SL = 1-1.5 ATR (balance between tight and safe)

Method 2: Bigger Target:
• Don't exit at first profit
• Trail SL, let profit run
• "Your stop loss is your take profit's best friend"

Method 3: Better Entry:
• Buy at support (lower entry = tighter SL)
• Sell at resistance (higher entry = tighter SL)
• Patience = better entry = better R/R

Method 4: Partial Profit Booking:
• Book 50% at 1:2 R/R
• Trail SL on remaining 50% for 1:4+ R/R
• "Free trade" after first target hits

Common R/R Mistakes

Mistake 1: "Win Rate Hi Important Hai":
• 80% win rate with 1:0.5 R/R = net LOSS
• 40% win rate with 1:3 R/R = net PROFIT
• R/R > Win Rate. Always.

Mistake 2: Profit Early Book Karna:
• Target 1:2 set kiya, but at 1:0.5 fear mein exit
• "Kuch profit mil raha, book karte hain"
• Result: average win = 0.5R, average loss = 1R = net loss

Mistake 3: Loss Chalana:
• SL hit hua, but "thoda aur wait karte hain"
• SL becomes -2R instead of -1R
• One -2R trade kills two +1R wins

Mistake 4: R/R Calculate Na Karna:
• "Chart accha lag raha, trade lete hain"
• Bina R/R calculate kiye trade = gambling
• HAMESHA entry se pehle R/R calculate karo

Mistake 5: Har Setup Pe Same R/R Expect:
• Intraday: 1:1.5 is fine (fast trades)
• Swing: 1:2 minimum
• Positional: 1:3 minimum
• Adjust R/R based on timeframe

FAQ

Q1: Ideal R/R ratio kya hai?

1:2 for swing trading. 1:3 for positional. 1:1.5 for intraday (with high win rate).

Q2: R/R 1:1 kab use karein?

Options selling mein (high win rate 70%+ compensates). Ya very high probability setups pe. Beginners ke liye avoid.

Q3: R/R aur Win Rate — kaunsa zyada important?

R/R zyada important hai. 40% win rate + 1:2 R/R = profitable. 70% win rate + 1:0.5 R/R = losing.

Q4: TRT pe R/R track kaise karein?

Chart pe entry, SL, target mark karo using drawing tools. Risk-reward calculator use karo (many available online). Trading journal mein har trade ka R/R record karo.

Q5: Ek trade mein kitna risk lena chahiye?

Max 1-2% of total capital per trade. R/R based on price levels, but position size based on risk %.
