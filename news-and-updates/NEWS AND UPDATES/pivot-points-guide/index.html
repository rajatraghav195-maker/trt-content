Pivot Points Kya Hai?

Pivot Points ek mathematical formula hai jo previous din ke High, Low, aur Close se calculate hoti hai, aur aaj ke liye potential support/resistance levels predict karti hai. Ye intraday traders ke liye extremely popular tool hai kyunki ye objective, calculated levels deta hai — subjective chart reading nahi.

Bahut institutional traders aur floor traders decades se pivot points use kar rahe hain — isliye ye levels self-fulfilling prophecy ki tarah kaam karte hain (bahut log same levels dekh rahe hote hain, isliye wahan actual buying/selling activity hoti hai).

Standard Pivot Point Formula

Ye nau baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Pivot Point (PP) = (High + Low + Close) / 3
• Resistance 1 (R1) = (2 × PP) - Low
• Resistance 2 (R2) = PP + (High - Low)
• Resistance 3 (R3) = High + 2 × (PP - Low)
• Support 1 (S1) = (2 × PP) - High
• Support 2 (S2) = PP - (High - Low)
• Support 3 (S3) = Low - 2 × (High - PP)

High, Low, Close = previous trading day ke values

Simple language mein: Pivot Point "aaj ka fair value center" hai. R1, R2, R3 upar ke resistance zones hain. S1, S2, S3 neeche ke support zones hain.

Pivot Points Kaise Padhein?

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Price > Pivot Point — Bullish bias aaj ke liye
• Price < Pivot Point — Bearish bias aaj ke liye
• Price near R1/R2/R3 — Resistance zone, profit booking possible
• Price near S1/S2/S3 — Support zone, bounce possible

3 Pivot Point Trading Strategies

Strategy 1: Pivot Bounce

Setup:
• Price S1 ya S2 level ke paas aata hai
• Reversal candle pattern bante hain (hammer, doji)

Entry: Confirmation candle ke baad Stop Loss: Support level ke thoda neeche Target: Pivot Point ya R1

Reverse for resistance: Price R1/R2 ke paas reject hota hai → SELL, target S1 ya Pivot.

Strategy 2: Pivot Breakout

Setup:
• Price strong volume ke saath R1 ya S1 ko break karta hai

Entry: Breakout candle close confirm hone pe Stop Loss: Broken level ke doosri taraf Target: Next pivot level (R2 ya S2)

Strategy 3: Pivot Point Trend Bias

Din ki shuruat mein dekho price Pivot Point ke upar khula ya neeche:

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Open upar hai, price PP se upar rehta hai — Bullish — dips buy karo
• Open neeche hai, price PP se neeche rehta hai — Bearish — rallies sell karo
• Price baar-baar PP cross karta hai — Choppy/sideways — avoid strong directional trades

Types of Pivot Points

1. Standard Pivot Points (Most Common)

Upar wala formula — sabse widely used.

2. Fibonacci Pivot Points

Fibonacci ratios (38.2%, 61.8%, 100%) use karke calculate hote hain. Zyada precise levels dete hain kyunki Fibonacci retracement ke saath align hote hain.

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• R1 = PP + 0.382 × (High - Low)
• R2 = PP + 0.618 × (High - Low)
• R3 = PP + 1.000 × (High - Low)

3. Camarilla Pivot Points

Zyada levels (R1-R4, S1-S4), tighter range — scalpers ke liye popular.

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• R4 = Close + (High - Low) × 1.1/2
• R3 = Close + (High - Low) × 1.1/4

4. Woodie's Pivot Points

Current din ke open price ko zyada weight dete hain calculation mein.

Beginner ke liye: Standard Pivot Points se start karo — simplest aur sabse widely tracked hain, isliye institutional relevance zyada hai.

Pivot Points Kis Timeframe Pe Best Kaam Karte Hain?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Intraday/Scalping — Daily Pivot Points (previous day ka data)
• Swing Trading — Weekly Pivot Points
• Positional — Monthly Pivot Points

Pivot Points vs Support-Resistance (Manual)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Objectivity — Pivot Points: ✅ Mathematical, no bias, Manual Support-Resistance: Subjective, chart-reading based
• Calculation — Pivot Points: Automatic formula, Manual Support-Resistance: Manual identification
• Widely tracked — Pivot Points: ✅ Yes (institutional use), Manual Support-Resistance: Depends on trader
• Adapts to news/gaps — Pivot Points: Slower (previous day data), Manual Support-Resistance: Real-time adjustable

Best approach: Dono combine karo. Pivot points automatic reference levels dete hain, manual S/R analysis unhe confirm/refine karta hai.

Common Pivot Point Mistakes

Mistake 1: Sabhi Levels Pe Equal Weight Dena

R3/S3 rarely touch hote hain — bahut extreme moves chahiye. R1/S1 aur Pivot Point sabse zyada relevant hote hain daily trading ke liye.

Mistake 2: Gap-Up/Gap-Down Din Pe Blind Trust

Bade gap ke din pivot points less reliable hote hain kyunki calculation previous din ke data pe based hai, jo gap ke saath disconnect ho jata hai.

Mistake 3: Volume Ignore Karna

Pivot level touch hona kaafi nahi hai — volume confirmation zaroori hai reversal/breakout ke liye.

Mistake 4: Sirf Pivot Points Pe Depend Karna

Pivot points ek tool hai, complete strategy nahi. Candlestick patterns, volume, aur overall trend ke saath combine karo.

Pivot Points Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 PIVOT POINTS QUICK REFERENCE
• Levels (Top to Bottom):
• R3 → R2 → R1 → Pivot Point → S1 → S2 → S3
• BUY Signals:
• ✅ Price bounces from S1/S2 with confirmation
• ✅ Price breaks R1 with volume (breakout)
• SELL Signals:
• ❌ Price rejects at R1/R2 with confirmation
• ❌ Price breaks S1 with volume (breakdown)
• DAILY BIAS:
• 🔥 Price > Pivot at open → Bullish day
• 🔥 Price < Pivot at open → Bearish day
• AVOID:
• ⚠️ Blind trust on gap-up/gap-down days
• ⚠️ Trading R3/S3 as primary levels
• ⚠️ Ignoring volume confirmation
