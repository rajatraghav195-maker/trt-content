Full Time Trader Kaise Banein — Hindi Guide

Full time trader banna hai? Requirements, capital, plan, reality — complete Hindi guide.

Full Time Trader Banne Ki Reality

Full time trading easy nahi hai. 90% traders paisa lose karte hain. But agar proper preparation hai toh possible hai.

Requirements Before Going Full Time

1. Minimum Capital:
• Living expenses — ₹30,000-50,000
• Trading capital needed — ₹10-25 lakh
• Emergency fund — ₹3-5 lakh
• Total needed — ₹15-30 lakh

2. Proven Track Record:
• Minimum 2 years profitable part-time trading
• Consistent returns (not one lucky month)
• Journal maintained with data

3. Multiple Income Sources:
• Trading income (primary)
• Dividend income (secondary)
• Part-time consulting/teaching (backup)
• Never rely 100% on trading income initially

Full Time Trading Plan

Month 1-6: Transition Phase:
• Keep day job, trade mornings/evenings
• Build emergency fund (6 months expenses)
• Test strategy with real money (small positions)
• Track every trade in journal

Month 7-12: Part-Time Trading:
• Reduce job hours if possible
• Increase trading capital gradually
• Focus on 1-2 strategies only
• Track monthly P&L

Year 2+: Full Time (If Profitable):
• Leave job ONLY if:
• ✅ 2 years profitable track record
• ✅ 6 months expenses saved
• ✅ ₹10L+ trading capital
• ✅ Strategy works in different markets

Income Expectations

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• ₹5 lakh — Monthly Return: 3%, Monthly Income: ₹15,000
• ₹10 lakh — Monthly Return: 3%, Monthly Income: ₹30,000
• ₹25 lakh — Monthly Return: 3%, Monthly Income: ₹75,000
• ₹50 lakh — Monthly Return: 3%, Monthly Income: ₹1,50,000

3% monthly = 36% annual = Very good return for full-time trader

Full Time Trader Schedule

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 6:00 AM — Market preparation, news
• 8:00 AM — Pre-market analysis
• 9:15 AM — Market opens — trading
• 3:30 PM — Market closes
• 4:00 PM — Trade review, journal
• 5:00 PM — Study, strategy improvement
• Evening — Rest, exercise, family

Full Time Trading Risks

No Fixed Income

Salary jaisa guaranteed income nahi hai. Some months loss hoga.

Mental Pressure

Paisa lose karna = Stress, anxiety, depression risk.

Isolation

Ghar pe akele trading = Social isolation.

Health Issues

Screen pe 8+ hours = Eye strain, back pain, no exercise.

Career Gap

Agar trading nahi chali toh job wapas mushkil.

Backup Plan

Full time trader banne se PEHLE backup plan banao:
• Skills updated rakho (job market mein relevant)
• Part-time freelancing option
• Teaching/mentoring trading
• Passive income streams (dividends, rental)

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 FULL TIME TRADER
• REQUIREMENTS:
• 📊 ₹15-30 lakh total (capital + emergency)
• 📊 2 years profitable track record
• 📊 Strategy tested in different markets
• 📊 Backup income source
• INCOME EXPECTATIONS:
• 📊 ₹10L capital → ₹30K/month (3% monthly)
• 📊 ₹25L capital → ₹75K/month
• 📊 ₹50L capital → ₹1.5L/month
• BEFORE LEAVING JOB:
• ✅ 2 years profitable
• ✅ 6 months expenses saved
• ✅ ₹10L+ trading capital
• ✅ Backup plan ready
• ✅ Family support
• RISKS:
• ❌ No fixed income
• ❌ Mental pressure
• ❌ Isolation
• ❌ Health issues
• ❌ Career gap
• AVOID:
• ❌ Quitting job after 1 profitable month
• ❌ No emergency fund
• ❌ Trading with borrowed money
• ❌ No backup plan
