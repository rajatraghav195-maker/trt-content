DEMAT ACCOUNT KE CHARGES: WO CHHOTE DEBITS KAHAN SE AATE HAIN

AMC, DP charges aur baaki lines jo ledger mein dikhti hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 05, 2026 · Features

Ledger mein kabhi kabhi chhote debits dikhte hain jinka source samajh nahi aata. Wo zyadatar demat side ke charges hote hain, aur wo brokerage se alag hote hain.

AAM CHARGES

AMC yaani annual maintenance charge account ko chalu rakhne ka saalana kharcha hai. Kuch accounts mein wo nahi hota, kuch mein lifetime ek baar liya jaata hai.

DP charges har sell transaction par lagte hain — wo per scrip hote hain, quantity par nahi. Isliye chhoti quantity bechne par wo percentage mein bada lagta hai.

BAAKI LINES

Ledger mein ye bhi dikh sakta hai:
• Pledge aur unpledge charges, agar collateral use kiya ho.
• Physical statement ya courier charges.
• Call and trade charges, agar phone se order diya ho.
• Auto square off charges, agar broker ne position band ki ho.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

DP charge per scrip hota hai, isliye ek hi stock ko thode thode karke kai baar bechna mehnga padta hai. Ek hi transaction mein poori quantity bechna aksar sabse simple bachat hai jo kisi ko dikhti nahi.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
