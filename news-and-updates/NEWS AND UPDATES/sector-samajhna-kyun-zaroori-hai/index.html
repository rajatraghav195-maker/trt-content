SECTOR SAMAJHNA: EK ACCHI COMPANY GALAT INDUSTRY MEIN

Cyclical vs defensive, industry ka structure, aur company se pehle sector padhna

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Ek line jo investing mein baar-baar sach nikalti hai: ek achhi company ek buri industry mein aksar ek aam company ki tarah perform karti hai. Business ka bhaagya bahut kuch us industry ke structure se tay hota hai jisme wo khadi hai.

CYCLICAL AUR DEFENSIVE

Cyclical business economy ke saath upar-neeche jaate hain — metals, auto, real estate, capital goods. Achhe daur mein inki kamai bahut badhti hai aur bure daur mein bahut girti hai.

Defensive business rozmarra ki zaroorat par chalte hain — FMCG, pharma, utilities. Inki kamai utni tez nahi badhti, par mandi mein bhi bahut nahi girti.

CYCLICAL MEIN VALUATION ULTA PADHA JAATA HAI

Ye ek aisi baat hai jo bahut mehngi padti hai. Cyclical company ka PE sabse kam tab dikhta hai jab uski kamai peak par hoti hai — yaani cycle ke top par.

Isliye 'PE sirf 6 hai' cyclical mein aksar khatre ka nishan hai, mauke ka nahi. Wahan log kamai ke bajaye asset value ya price-to-book jaise paimane zyada dekhte hain.

INDUSTRY KA STRUCTURE

Company padhne se pehle industry se ye poocho:
• Kitne khilaadi hain — do-teen bade khilaadi wali industry mein pricing power hoti hai; sau khilaadi wali mein nahi.
• Entry aasan hai kya — agar koi bhi thoda paisa lagakar aa sakta hai, to margin lamba nahi tikega.
• Customer ke paas taakat kitni hai — agar ek-do bade buyer poori industry ko kharid rahe hain, to margin unke haath mein hai, seller ke nahi.
• Raw material kiske haath mein hai — jinka input ek hi jagah se aata hai, unka margin ussi ke rehmo-karam par hai.
• Regulation kitna bhaari hai — kahin ye moat banata hai, kahin ye kamai ki chhat ban jaata hai.

SECTOR ROTATION KA SANDARBH

Alag-alag economic phases mein alag sectors chalte hain. Interest rate, currency, commodity prices — sab kisi na kisi sector ke liye headwind hain aur kisi ke liye tailwind.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki har phase ke liye sector chunna chahiye — timing ka wo khel bahut mushkil hai. Iska matlab ye hai ki portfolio ek hi tarah ke sectors se bhara na ho, warna aapko diversification dikh raha hoga jo hai nahi.

PEHLE SECTOR, PHIR COMPANY

Ek behtar kram ye hai: pehle ye samjho ki industry paisa kaise kamati hai aur uska sabse bada dabav kya hai. Uske baad us industry ki companies ko us kasauti par tolo.

Ulta karne par aap ek acche balance sheet par fida ho jaate ho aur wo baad mein pata chalta hai ki poori industry hi dabav mein thi.
