Options Kya Hai?

Options ek derivative instrument hai jo tumhe RIGHT deta hai (obligation nahi) ki tum ek specific date pe ek specific price pe ek asset kharid ya bech sako.

Simple definition: > Options = Insurance jaisa hai. Premium do, RIGHT mil jaaye. Agar market tumhare favour mein gaya toh profit, nahi toh sirf premium ka loss.

Call Option vs Put Option

Call Option (CE):
• Right: Kharidne ka right
• Bullish view: Tumhe lagta hai price badhega
• Example: Nifty 20,000 CE kharida → Agar Nifty 20,500 gaya → Profit

Put Option (PE):
• Right: Bechne ka right
• Bearish view: Tumhe lagta hai price girega
• Example: Nifty 20,000 PE kharida → Agar Nifty 19,500 gaya → Profit

Option Terminology

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Strike Price — Price at which option can be exercised
• Premium — Price of the option (what you pay)
• Expiry — Date when option expires
• Lot Size — Minimum quantity (Nifty = 25)
• ITM — In The Money — profitable to exercise
• ATM — At The Money — strike = current price
• OTM — Out of The Money — not profitable to exercise

Example: Buying a Call Option

Nifty at 20,000. You buy 20,200 CE at ₹80.

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Strike — 20,200
• Premium — ₹80
• Lot size — 25
• Total cost — ₹80 × 25 = ₹2,000

Scenario 1: Nifty at 20,500 (above strike)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Option value: ~₹300
• Profit: (₹300 - ₹80) × 25 = ₹5,500
• Return: 275%

Scenario 2: Nifty at 20,200 (at strike)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Option value: ~₹0
• Loss: ₹2,000 (full premium)
• Return: -100%

Scenario 3: Nifty at 20,000 (below strike)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Option value: ₹0
• Loss: ₹2,000 (full premium)
• Return: -100%

Option Buyer vs Seller

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Pays/Receives — Buyer: Pays premium, Seller: Receives premium
• Max loss — Buyer: Premium paid, Seller: Unlimited (call)
• Max profit — Buyer: Unlimited, Seller: Premium received
• Win rate — Buyer: ~30-40%, Seller: ~60-70%
• Time — Buyer: Against (theta hurts), Seller: In favor (theta helps)
• Best for — Buyer: Directional bets, Seller: Income, range-bound

Options vs Futures vs Stocks

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Obligation — Options: Right (not obligation), Futures: Obligation, Stocks: Ownership
• Premium — Options: Yes, Futures: No (margin), Stocks: Full price
• Max loss — Options: Premium (buyer), Futures: Unlimited, Stocks: Full investment
• Leverage — Options: High, Futures: High, Stocks: Low
• Complexity — Options: High, Futures: Medium, Stocks: Low
• Best for — Options: Hedging, speculation, Futures: Directional bets, Stocks: Long-term

Option Chain Kya Hai?

Option chain ek table hai jo dikhata hai ki har strike price ke liye call aur put options ka premium, OI, aur volume kya hai.

How to read:
• Call side (left): Call options ka data
• Put side (right): Put options ka data
• Strike (center): Price levels
• OI: Open Interest (active positions)
• Volume: Today's trades

Greeks (Quick Overview)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Delta — Measures: Price sensitivity, Impact: Stock ₹1 badhe → option kitna badhega
• Gamma — Measures: Delta change, Impact: Delta kitna change hoga
• Theta — Measures: Time decay, Impact: Har din option kitna girega
• Vega — Measures: Volatility sensitivity, Impact: IV 1% badhe → option kitna badhega

Common Options Mistakes

OTM Options Buying

"₹5 ka option, ₹500 ho jayega!" — 95% OTM options expire worthless. ATM ya slightly OTM lo.

Options Selling Without Knowledge

Options selling mein unlimited risk hai. Bina knowledge ke sell mat karo.

No Stop Loss

Options mein bhi stop loss zaroori hai. Premium 50% gira → exit karo.

Expiry Day Buying

Last 3 din mein theta bahut fast decay karta hai. Overnight hold risky hai.

No Strategy

"Call le liya, ab dekhte hain" — Bina strategy ke options = gambling.
