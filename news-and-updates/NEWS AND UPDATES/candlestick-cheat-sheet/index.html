BULLISH PATTERNS (Price Badh Sakta Hai)

|…|

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Where: Downtrend end
• Signal: Bullish reversal
• Body: Small, near top
• Lower wick: 2x+ body
• Confirmation: Next candle green

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Where: Downtrend end
• Signal: Strong bullish reversal
• Day 1: Big red candle
• Day 2: Small body (doji)
• Day 3: Big green candle

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Where: Downtrend end
• Signal: Bullish reversal
• Pehli: Small red candle
• Doosri: Big green candle (engulfs first)

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Where: Downtrend end
• Signal: Bullish reversal
• Day 1: Big red candle
• Day 2: Gap down open, close above midpoint

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Where: Uptrend or after reversal
• Signal: Strong bullish continuation
• 3 consecutive green candles, each closing higher

INDECISION PATTERNS

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Signal: Indecision, potential reversal
• Open = Close
• Wait for next candle for confirmation

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:

• Market confused

QUICK REFERENCE CARD

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 SAVE THIS CHEAT SHEET
• BULLISH (BUY signals):
• 🔨 Hammer — Downtrend end, small body + long lower wick
• ⭐ Morning Star — 3-candle reversal (red, doji, green)
• 🟢 Bullish Engulfing — Green candle engulfs previous red
• 🔼 Piercing Line — Gap down, close above midpoint
• 🚀 Three White Soldiers — 3 consecutive green candles
• BEARISH (SELL signals):
• ⭐ Evening Star — 3-candle reversal (green, doji, red)
• 🔴 Bearish Engulfing — Red candle engulfs previous green
• 🐦 Three Black Crows — 3 consecutive red candles
• ☁️ Dark Cloud Cover — Gap up, close below midpoint
• INDECISION (WAIT):
• ✚ Doji — Open = Close, market confused
• 🔄 Spinning Top — Small body, long wicks
• COMBINATION (STRONGEST):
• 🔥 Hammer + RSI oversold = Strong BUY
• 🔥 Morning Star + Support level = Strong BUY
• 🔥 Evening Star + RSI overbought = Strong SELL
• 🔥 Bullish Engulfing + Volume spike = Very strong BUY
• RULES:
• ✅ Always wait for confirmation
• ✅ Combine with RSI/Support/Volume
• ✅ Higher timeframe = more reliable
• ✅ Daily chart > Intraday chart
