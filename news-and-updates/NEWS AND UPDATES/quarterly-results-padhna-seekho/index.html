QUARTERLY RESULTS PADHNA SEEKHO — BINA CA BANE

Revenue, PAT, EBITDA aur guidance ka matlab — aur wo teen numbers jinse ek beginner 90% kaam nikal sakta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Saal mein chaar baar har listed company ko batana padta hai ki uske saath hua kya. Us din TV par 'profit beats estimates' chal raha hota hai aur stock 4% neeche khul jaata hai. Dono cheezein ek saath sach kaise ho sakti hain? Iska jawab result padhne mein hai, headline padhne mein nahi.

PEHLE CALENDAR — Q1 JANUARY MEIN NAHI AATA

India ka financial year April se March chalta hai. Isliye Q1 April-June hai, Q2 July-September, Q3 October-December, aur Q4 January-March. Kai naye investors Q1 ko January-March samajh lete hain aur global company ke calendar se compare karke confuse ho jaate hain.

Q4 sabse important hota hai kyunki uske saath poore saal ka audited result aata hai, aur aksar dividend ka announcement bhi.

TEEN NUMBERS JO SABSE PEHLE DEKHNE HAIN

Poori report se pehle sirf ye teen:
• Revenue (ya Net Sales) — company ne bechkar kitna kamaya. Ye business ka size hai. Ye badh raha hai to demand hai.
• EBITDA aur EBITDA margin — interest, tax aur depreciation se pehle ka operating profit. Ye batata hai ki core business khud kitna efficient hai. Margin % revenue se zyada bolta hai: revenue 20% badhe par margin gir jaaye, to company mehngai ya competition se ladh rahi hai.
• PAT (Profit After Tax) — sab kuch nikaalne ke baad bacha hua profit. Yahi headline banta hai, aur yahi sabse zyada manipulate dikhta hai — kyunki ismein ek baar ka koi bhi item ghus sakta hai.

Agar aapke paas sirf teen minute hain, to yahi teen dekho — aur teenon ko sath mein dekho, alag-alag nahi.

YoY AUR QoQ — DONO ALAG SAWAAL POOCHTE HAIN

YoY (year on year) matlab is Q1 ki tulna pichle saal ke Q1 se. QoQ (quarter on quarter) matlab pichle quarter se.

Seasonal business mein QoQ jhooth bol sakta hai. Ek AC banane wali company ka Q1 (April-June) hamesha Q3 (October-December) se bada hoga — isliye QoQ growth dekhkar khush hona bekaar hai. Wahan YoY hi sahi comparison hai. Ulta, ek fast-growing company mein QoQ momentum ki asli tasveer deta hai. Business samjho, phir comparison chuno.

PROFIT BADHA PAR STOCK GIRA — AISA KYUN HOTA HAI

Aksar teen wajahon mein se ek hoti hai:
• Expectations pehle se bhari hui thi — analysts 25% growth maan chuke the, aayi 18%. Number accha hai, par jo price mein pehle se lagi hui thi usse kam hai. Market hamesha expectation ke against react karta hai, absolute number ke against nahi.
• Profit quality kharab hai — PAT badha par operating profit nahi. Fark kisi one-time item se aaya: koi property bech di, koi tax refund mila, koi provision wapas liya. Ye agle quarter dobara nahi hoga.
• Guidance kamzor hai — result accha hai par management ne aage ke liye jo bola wo darawana hai. Market past ka paisa nahi, future ka paisa price karta hai.

ONE-TIME ITEMS: JAHAN SABSE ZYADA DHOKA HOTA HAI

Report mein 'exceptional items' ya 'other income' naam ke line items hote hain. Other income mein aksar company ke cash par mila interest ya investments ka gain hota hai — ye business ka profit nahi hai, ye bank balance ka profit hai.

Test simple hai: PAT mein se other income aur exceptional items ko mann hi mann hata do. Jo bachta hai, wo asli business hai. Agar us hisaab se profit gir raha hai jabki headline PAT badh raha hai, to aapko wo baat pata chal gayi jo headline ne nahi batayi.

CONFERENCE CALL — SABSE KAM PADHI JAANE WALI SABSE ACCHI CHEEZ

Result ke ek-do din baad management analysts ke saath call karta hai, aur uska transcript company ki website aur exchange filings par free milta hai. Ye numbers se zyada batata hai, kyunki yahan management ko sawaalon ka jawab dena padta hai.

Call mein ye sunno:
• Demand commentary — 'demand environment challenging hai' jaise line agle do quarters ka mood set kar deti hai.
• Capex plan — company kitna naya invest kar rahi hai. Bada capex matlab management ko growth dikh rahi hai, par short term mein cash aur margin par dabav.
• Debt aur working capital — udhaar badh raha hai ya ghat raha hai. Ye ek quarter ke profit se zyada company ki sehat batata hai.
• Jo sawaal management taal deta hai — analysts ek hi cheez teen baar poochein aur jawab har baar ghooma diya jaaye, to wahi asli issue hai.

KAHAN SE PADHO — AUR KAHAN SE NAHI

Result company ki investor relations page par aur exchange ki website par filing ke roop mein aata hai. Yahi original source hai. Uske baad hi kisi summary, kisi screenshot, kisi Telegram forward par jao — aur us summary ko original se match karo, kam se kam shuru ke kuch mahine.

Beginner ka sabse bada upgrade yahi hota hai: ek baar khud original filing kholna. Pehli baar 20 minute lagenge. Dasvi baar teen minute lagenge, aur tab tak aap wo dekh rahe honge jo headline padhne wale kabhi nahi dekhte.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
