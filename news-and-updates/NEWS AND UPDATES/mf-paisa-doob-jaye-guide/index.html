MF Mein Paisa Doob Raha Hai — Kya Karein?

Panic mat karo. Market gir raha hai toh MF bhi girega. Ye normal hai. Recovery hoti hai.

Step-by-Step Action Plan

Step 1: Check Fund Type:
• Large Cap — Current Situation: -10 to -15%, Action: Hold, SIP continue
• Mid Cap — Current Situation: -15 to -25%, Action: Hold, SIP continue
• Small Cap — Current Situation: -25 to -40%, Action: Hold, SIP continue
• Sectoral — Current Situation: -30%+, Action: Review sector

Step 2: Check Fundamentals:
• Fund ka track record achha hai? → Hold
• Fund manager change hua? → Review
• Category overall down hai? → Normal, recovery hogi
• Fund specific problem hai? → Switch to better fund

Step 3: Continue SIP

SIP band mat karo. Low prices = Zyada units = Faster recovery.

Step 4: Average Down

Agar extra paisa hai toh lump sum add karo. Cheap prices pe zyada units milenge.

Recovery Timeline

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• -10% — 2-3 months
• -20% — 6-12 months
• -30% — 1-2 years
• -40% — 2-3 years
• -50% — 3-5 years

When to Worry

Normal (Don't Worry):
• Market correction (-10 to -20%)
• Category down (all mid caps falling)
• Short-term volatility

Worry (Take Action):
• Fund consistently underperforming benchmark (3+ years)
• Fund manager changed
• Fund house shutting down
• Sector permanently declining

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 MF MEIN PAISA DOOB JAYE
• ACTION PLAN:
• 1️⃣ Check fund type (large/mid/small)
• 2️⃣ Check fundamentals (track record, manager)
• 3️⃣ Continue SIP (low prices = more units)
• 4️⃣ Average down if extra money
• RECOVERY TIMELINE:
• 📊 -10% → 2-3 months
• 📊 -20% → 6-12 months
• 📊 -30% → 1-2 years
• 📊 -40% → 2-3 years
• NORMAL:
• ✅ Market correction (-10 to -20%)
• ✅ Category down (all similar funds falling)
• ✅ Short-term volatility
• WORRY:
• ❌ Consistent underperformance (3+ years)
• ❌ Fund manager change
• ❌ Sector permanently declining
• AVOID:
• ❌ Panic selling
• ❌ Stopping SIP
• ❌ Checking daily (monthly check enough)
