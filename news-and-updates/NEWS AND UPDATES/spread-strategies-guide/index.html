Spread Strategies Ki Buniyad — Options Trading Guide (Hindi)

Spread strategies kya hain aur options trading mein kaise use karein — bull spread, bear spread, calendar spread sab samjhein Hindi mein.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 16, 2026 · Features

Spread strategies kya hain aur options trading mein kaise use karein — bull spread, bear spread, calendar spread sab samjhein Hindi mein.

Spread Strategy Kya Hai?

Spread strategy mein tum ek option buy karte ho aur doosra sell karte ho — same underlying, different strike ya expiry. Ye risk limit karta hai aur profit potential bhi limit karta hai.

Simple definition: > Spread = Buy one option + Sell another option = Limited risk, limited reward

Why Spreads?:
• Unlimited risk (selling) — Limited risk
• High premium (buying) — Reduced cost
• Directional bet — Direction + range play
• Simple — Slightly complex

4 Main Spread Strategies

1. Bull Call Spread (Bullish)

Setup:
• ATM Call buy (long leg)
• OTM Call sell (short leg)

Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Buy 20,000 CE @ ₹100
• Sell 20,200 CE @ ₹50
• Net cost: ₹50 (₹100 - ₹50)

Max Profit: ₹200 (width) - ₹50 (cost) = ₹150 Max Loss: ₹50 (premium paid) Breakeven: 20,050

When to use: Moderately bullish

2. Bear Put Spread (Bearish)

Setup:
• ATM Put buy (long leg)
• OTM Put sell (short leg)

Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Buy 20,000 PE @ ₹100
• Sell 19,800 PE @ ₹50
• Net cost: ₹50

Max Profit: ₹150 Max Loss: ₹50 Breakeven: 19,950

When to use: Moderately bearish

3. Bull Put Spread (Credit Spread — Bullish)

Setup:
• OTM Put sell (collect premium)
• Further OTM Put buy (protection)

Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Sell 19,800 PE @ ₹70
• Buy 19,600 PE @ ₹30
• Net credit: ₹40

Max Profit: ₹40 (credit received) Max Loss: ₹160 (width) - ₹40 = ₹120 Breakeven: 19,760

When to use: Bullish, want income

4. Calendar Spread (Time Spread)

Setup:
• Near-month option sell (fast theta decay)
• Far-month option buy (slow theta decay)

Example:
• Nifty at 20,000
• Sell August 20,000 CE @ ₹80
• Buy September 20,000 CE @ ₹150
• Net cost: ₹70

Max Profit: If Nifty stays near 20,000 at August expiry Max Loss: ₹70 (net premium)

When to use: Neutral, expect low volatility near-term

Spread Strategy Selection

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Moderately bullish — Strategy: Bull Call Spread, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited
• Moderately bearish — Strategy: Bear Put Spread, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited
• Bullish + income — Strategy: Bull Put Spread, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited
• Bearish + income — Strategy: Bear Call Spread, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited
• Neutral — Strategy: Calendar Spread, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited
• High volatility expected — Strategy: Long Straddle, Risk: Limited, Reward: Unlimited
• Low volatility expected — Strategy: Iron Condor, Risk: Limited, Reward: Limited

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 SPREAD STRATEGIES QUICK REFERENCE
• BULL CALL SPREAD (Bullish):
• ✅ Buy ATM Call + Sell OTM Call
• ✅ Net debit (pay premium)
• ✅ Max profit = Width - Cost
• ✅ Max loss = Premium paid
• BEAR PUT SPREAD (Bearish):
• ✅ Buy ATM Put + Sell OTM Put
• ✅ Net debit (pay premium)
• ✅ Max profit = Width - Cost
• ✅ Max loss = Premium paid
• BULL PUT SPREAD (Income + Bullish):
• ✅ Sell OTM Put + Buy further OTM Put
• ✅ Net credit (receive premium)
• ✅ Max profit = Credit received
• ✅ Max loss = Width - Credit
• CALENDAR SPREAD (Neutral):
• ✅ Sell near-month + Buy far-month
• ✅ Profit from time decay difference
• ✅ Best when IV low, expect range-bound
• SELECTION GUIDE:
• 📊 Moderately bullish → Bull Call Spread
• 📊 Moderately bearish → Bear Put Spread
• 📊 Want income → Credit Spreads
• 📊 Neutral → Calendar Spread / Iron Condor
• 📊 Expect breakout → Long Straddle/Strangle
