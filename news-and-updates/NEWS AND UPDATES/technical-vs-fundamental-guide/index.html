Technical vs Fundamental Analysis — Kya Difference Hai? (Hindi)

Technical analysis aur fundamental analysis mein kya fark hai, kaunsa better hai — complete Hindi guide Indian traders ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 18, 2026 · Features

Technical analysis aur fundamental analysis mein kya fark hai, kaunsa better hai — complete Hindi guide Indian traders ke liye.

Technical Analysis Kya Hai?

Technical analysis mein tum charts aur price data se future price predict karte ho. Past price movement se patterns nikalte ho aur usse trading decisions lete ho.

Core belief: > "Price sab kuch batata hai." — Market mein sab information price mein reflect hoti hai.

Technical Analysis Ke Tools:
• Candlestick Patterns — Price action patterns
• RSI — Momentum (overbought/oversold)
• MACD — Trend direction + crossover
• Moving Averages — Trend identification
• Bollinger Bands — Volatility + mean reversion
• Volume — Confirmation tool
• Support/Resistance — Key price levels
• Fibonacci — Retracement levels

Technical Analysis Best For:
• Short-term trading (intraday, swing)
• Entry/exit timing
• Risk management (stop loss placement)
• Trend identification

Fundamental Analysis Kya Hai?

Fundamental analysis mein tum company ki financial health analyze karte ho — revenue, profit, debt, management quality. Company ka intrinsic value nikalte ho.

Core belief: > "Market price aur real value mein fark ho sakta hai." — Undervalued stocks kharido, overvalued becho.

Fundamental Analysis Ke Tools:
• P/E Ratio — Price vs earnings comparison
• EPS — Earnings per share
• Revenue Growth — Company ka sales growth
• Profit Margin — Kitna profit bachta hai
• Debt-to-Equity — Company ka leverage
• ROE — Return on equity
• Book Value — Company ka net asset value
• Dividend Yield — Income potential

Fundamental Analysis Best For:
• Long-term investing (5+ years)
• Stock selection (kaunsi company achhi hai)
• Value investing (saste stocks dhundhna)
• Portfolio building

Technical vs Fundamental — Comparison

Ye nau baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Focus — Technical Analysis: Price charts, Fundamental Analysis: Company financials
• Timeframe — Technical Analysis: Short-term (days-weeks), Fundamental Analysis: Long-term (years)
• Data — Technical Analysis: Price, volume, indicators, Fundamental Analysis: Revenue, profit, debt
• Tools — Technical Analysis: RSI, MACD, patterns, Fundamental Analysis: P/E, EPS, ROE
• Best for — Technical Analysis: Trading, Fundamental Analysis: Investing
• Entry/exit — Technical Analysis: Specific signals, Fundamental Analysis: Valuation-based
• Effort — Technical Analysis: Daily chart watching, Fundamental Analysis: Quarterly reports
• Speed — Technical Analysis: Fast decisions, Fundamental Analysis: Slow, research-heavy
• Emotion — Technical Analysis: Can be mechanical, Fundamental Analysis: Requires patience

Kaunsa Better Hai?

Answer: DONO ka combination.

Combination Strategy:
• Fundamental analysis se stock select karo — Quality company dhundho
• Technical analysis se entry/exit decide karo — Kab kharidna hai, kab bechna hai

Example:
• Fundamental: TCS achhi company hai (strong financials, market leader, growing)
• Technical: TCS 50 DMA pe support pe hai, RSI 40, bullish candle → BUY signal
• Result: Quality stock + right timing = best trade

Indian Market Mein Application

For Nifty 50 Trading:
• Technical — Nifty ka trend identify karo, support/resistance levels
• Fundamental — Nifty PE ratio dekho, FII/DII flows, macro data

For Stock Selection:
• Fundamental — Company financials analyze karo, sector comparison
• Technical — Entry/exit timing, stop loss placement

For F&O Trading:
• Technical — Option chain analysis, PCR ratio, Max Pain
• Fundamental — Earnings expectations, event analysis

Common Myths

Myth 1: Sirf Technical Se Sab Ho Jaata Hai

Technical analysis SIRF timing tool hai. Company ka business nahi batata. Fundamental analysis zaroori hai stock selection ke liye.

Myth 2: Fundamental Analysis Boring Hai

Nahi! Fundamental analysis se tum companies ke baare mein deep knowledge gain karte ho — jo long-term wealth banata hai.

Myth 3: Dono Same Hain

Nahi! Technical = charts + indicators. Fundamental = financials + business. Alag approach, alag tools, alag timeframe.

Myth 4: Beginners Ko Sirf Fundamental Seekhna Chahiye

Nahi! Beginners ko DONO seekhna chahiye. Technical se timing seekho, fundamental se stock selection.

Combined Approach — Best Practice

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• STEP 1: FUNDAMENTAL FILTER
• • Revenue growth > 15% (3-year CAGR)
• • Profit margin > 10%
• • Debt-to-equity < 1
• • ROE > 15%
• • P/E < Industry average
• → 20-30 quality stocks shortlist
• STEP 2: TECHNICAL ENTRY
• • Stock above 200 DMA (uptrend)
• • RSI 35-50 (pullback, not overbought)
• • Support level (50 DMA or horizontal)
• • Bullish candlestick pattern
• • Volume confirmation
• → Best 3-5 stocks mein entry
• STEP 3: RISK MANAGEMENT
• • Stop loss: Support se 2-3% neeche
• • Target: Previous high / Fibonacci extension
• • Position size: 2% risk per trade
• • Trail stop loss as price moves up
• → Systematic, disciplined approach
