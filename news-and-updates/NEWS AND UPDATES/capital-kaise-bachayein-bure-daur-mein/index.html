BURE DAUR MEIN CAPITAL BACHANA: EK PRACTICAL PLAN

Kill switch, size ghatana, cash ka kaam, aur wapas kab aana hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Har trader ka ek bura daur aata hai — system wahi, mehnat wahi, par nateeje ulte. Us daur mein jo kiya jaata hai, wo poore saal se zyada maayne rakhta hai. Aur wo faisle us daur ke andar nahi liye ja sakte; wo pehle likhne padte hain.

PEHLE YE MAANO KI YE AAYEGA

Har system ka apna bura patch hota hai. Backtest se uska mota andaza mil jaata hai — sabse lamba haar ka silsila aur sabse bada drawdown.

Jab aap us number ko pehle se jaante ho, to wo aane par ek aapda nahi lagti, ek expected cheez lagti hai. Yahi ek baat aadhi ghabrahat khatam kar deti hai.

KILL SWITCH — PEHLE SE LIKHA HUA

Teen level ka ek simple dhaancha:
• Daily — ek din mein tay seema se zyada nuksaan hua to screen band. Agla din naya.
• Weekly — hafte mein tay seema paar hui to size aadhi, aur sirf apne sabse acche setup par trade.
• Overall — kul drawdown tay level tak pahuncha to trading rok kar review. Rok-na haar nahi hai, wo ek step hai.

Ye numbers khud chuno, par shaant dimaag se chuno — aur likh kar rakho. Jo rule likha nahi hai, wo bure din mein maujood hi nahi hota.

SIZE PEHLE, SYSTEM BAAD MEIN

Bura daur aane par pehla reflex system badalna hota hai. Ye lagbhag hamesha galat hai — aap ek system ko theek us waqt chhod rahe ho jab uske nateeje sabse bure hain, yaani jab uske wapas aane ki sambhavna sabse zyada hai.

Sahi kram ulta hai: pehle size ghatao, phir frequency ghatao, aur system ko tabhi chhedo jab data kahe ki wo actually toot gaya hai — apni feeling par nahi.

CASH EK POSITION HAI

Bure daur mein sabse kam istemal hone wala tool cash hai. Har waqt market mein hona zaroori nahi hai, aur na hi wo koi tamga hai.

Cash do cheezein deta hai — capital ki suraksha aur dimaag ki jagah. Doosri cheez pehli se zyada keemti hai, kyunki bure faisle aksar us waqt aate hain jab har rupaya kahin na kahin phansa ho.

WAPAS KAB AANA HAI

Wapas aane ka faisla P&L se nahi hona chahiye. Wo process se hona chahiye: kya aap apne rules par wapas lauta chuke ho, kya aapko pata hai ki kya galat hua tha, aur kya aap chhoti size par lagataar apne setup follow kar paa rahe ho.

Jab teenon ka jawab haan ho, tab size dheere-dheere wapas badhao. 'Sab wapas jeet lene' ke iraade se lauta hua trader phir se wahi cycle shuru karta hai — bas is baar chhoti capital ke saath.
