Margin Trading Facility (MTF) — Hindi Guide

MTF kya hai, margin trading kaise kaam karta hai, risks aur benefits — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 10, 2026 · Features

MTF Kya Hai?

MTF = Margin Trading Facility. Broker tumhe loan deta hai stock kharidne ke liye. Apna paisa ₹1 lakh hai, broker ₹3-4 lakh ka exposure deta hai.

Simple definition: > MTF = Broker se loan leke stock kharidna. Zyada quantity, zyada profit — but zyada risk bhi.

How MTF Works

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Tumhara paisa: ₹1,00,000
• Broker margin: 4x
• Total buying power: ₹4,00,000
• Agar stock 10% badhe:
• Without MTF: ₹1,00,000 → ₹1,10,000 (₹10,000 profit)
• With MTF: ₹4,00,000 → ₹4,40,000 (₹40,000 profit)
• Your investment: ₹1,00,000 → 40% return!
• Agar stock 10% gire:
• Without MTF: ₹1,00,000 → ₹90,000 (₹10,000 loss)
• With MTF: ₹4,00,000 → ₹3,60,000 (₹40,000 loss)
• Your investment: ₹1,00,000 → 40% loss!

MTF Charges

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Interest rate — 12-18% per annum
• Brokerage — ₹20/order
• Margin required — 25-33% of trade value
• Pledge charge — ₹20-30 per stock

MTF vs Normal Trading

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Leverage — MTF: 3-4x, Normal Trading: 1x
• Interest — MTF: 12-18%, Normal Trading: None
• Risk — MTF: Very high, Normal Trading: Normal
• Best for — MTF: Short-term (days), Normal Trading: Medium-long term
• Margin call — MTF: Yes, Normal Trading: No

MTF Risks

Margin Call

Agar stock price gira toh broker aur paisa maangega. Nahi diya toh forcefully position close ho jaayega.

Interest Cost

12-18% interest lagta hai. Short-term trade ke liye theek hai, long-term ke liye mehenga.

Unlimited Loss

Leverage se loss bhi multiply hota hai. 10% stock gira = 40% portfolio loss.

Forced Liquidation

Broker bina bataye position close kar sakta hai agar margin nahi maintain hota.

When to Use MTF

Use When:
• Short-term directional trade (2-5 days)
• High conviction setup
• Strict stop loss hai
• Trend clear hai

Avoid When:
• Uncertain market
• No stop loss
• Long-term investment
• Low conviction
