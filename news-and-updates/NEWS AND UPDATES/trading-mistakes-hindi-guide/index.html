Trading Mistakes Hindi: 15 Sabse Common Galtiyan Jo Traders Karte Hain

Trading mistakes hindi — 15 sabse common galtiyan jo 90% traders karte hain. Har mistake ka solution aur prevention strategy. Complete guide.

90% Traders Kyun Fail Hote Hain?

Koi strategy ki kami nahi. Koi indicator ki kami nahi. Koi platform ki kami nahi. 90% traders isliye fail hote hain kyunki wo same galtiyan baar-baar karte hain.

Ye 15 mistakes yaad rakho, unhe avoid karo, aur aap 90% se 10% mein aa jaoge.

Mistake 1: Bina Plan Ke Trade Lena

Galti: "Chart accha lag raha hai, trade lete hain" — koi entry rule, koi exit rule, koi risk rule nahi. Solution: Har trade se pehle: entry price, stop loss, target, position size — sab likho. Bina plan ke trade = gambling.

Mistake 2: Stop Loss Na Lagana

Galti: "SL lagate hain toh hit hota hai, isliye nahi lagate" — ek hi bad trade account khatam kar deti hai. Solution: Hamesha real SL lagao (broker pe order). Mental SL kaam nahi karta. SL = insurance, premium pay karo par risk cover karo.

Mistake 3: Risk-Reward Ignore Karna

Galti: ₹5 ka profit book karte hain, ₹20 ka loss tolerate karte hain. Net = loss. Solution: Minimum 1:2 R/R. Entry pe calculate karo. Agar 1:2 nahi milta, trade skip karo.

Mistake 4: Overtrading

Galti: Din mein 10-15 trades. Brokerage + loss = capital gone. Solution: Max 2-3 trades/day (intraday), 3-5/week (swing). No setup = no trade.

Mistake 5: Averaging Down (Loss Mein Aur Kharidna)

Galti: Stock neeche ja raha → "aur kharid leta hu, average ho jaayega" → deeper loss. Solution: SL hit = exit. NEVER add to a losing position. Averaging down = adding risk.

Mistake 6: FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)

Galti: Stock 20% upar gaya → "miss ho raha hai" → chase entry → top pe buy → crash. Solution: Pre-defined watchlist. Missed move = missed move. Next setup aayega. Patience.

Mistake 7: Revenge Trading

Galti: Loss hua → "wapas chahiye" → bigger position → bigger loss → cycle. Solution: Daily loss limit (3%). Limit hit = terminal band, bahar jao. 2 consecutive loss = stop.

Mistake 8: Tips Pe Trade Lena

Galti: "Friend ne bola Reliance upar jaayega" → buy → loss. Solution: Apna analysis karo. Tips pe trade nahi. Chart pe setup hona chahiye.

Mistake 9: Profit Early Book Karna

Galti: ₹10 profit → "book karte hain" → stock doubled → regret. Solution: Target pre-set karo. 1:2 R/R pe hi exit. Trail SL for bigger moves. "Cut losses, let profits run."

Mistake 10: Loss Hold Karna

Galti: SL hit → "wait karte hain, recover hoga" → small loss = big loss. Solution: Real SL = auto-exit. No emotion. SL hit = trade wrong tha. Re-enter on new setup.

Mistake 11: Har Indicator Use Karna

Galti: Chart pe 10 indicators → conflicting signals → confusion → bad trades. Solution: Max 3 indicators. RSI + MACD + Volume = enough. Simple = effective.

Mistake 12: Higher Timeframe Ignore Karna

Galti: 5 min chart pe trade le rahe, daily trend against hai → counter-trend trade = loss. Solution: Hamesha higher timeframe trend check karo. Daily trend = direction filter. 5 min = entry timing.

Mistake 13: Position Size Na Calculate Karna

Galti: "₹50,000 daalte hain" — bina risk calculation ke. Ek trade mein 50% capital. Solution: 1-2% risk per trade. Position size = max risk / (entry - SL). Calculate before entry.

Mistake 14: Trading Journal Na Rakhna

Galti: Trades liye, record nahi rakha → same mistakes repeat → no improvement. Solution: Har trade journal mein: entry, exit, reason, emotion, lesson. Weekly review. Monthly analysis.

Mistake 15: Market Ko Personal Lena

Galti: "Market ne mujhe loot liya" — anger, frustration, revenge. Solution: Market impersonal hai. Aapke feelings se matter nahi. System follow karo, result accept karo.

Bonus: 5 Rules of Profitable Traders

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Capital protection > Profit generation — survive today, trade tomorrow
• Process > Outcome — "did I follow my system?" > "did I make money?"
• Patience = Profit — kam trades, zyada profit
• Discipline > Intelligence — IQ se nahi, discipline se profit hota hai
• Journal = Mirror — apni galtiyan dekho, fix karo, improve karo
