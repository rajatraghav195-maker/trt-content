Share Market Kya Hai?

Share market (ya stock market) ek jagah hai jahan companies ke shares (hissese) kharidne aur bechne ka kaam hota hai. Jab tum kisi company ka share kharidte ho, tum us company ke chhote se hissese ke malik ban jaate ho.

Simple definition: > Share market = Jahan companies apne shares bechti hain aur log shares kharidte hain. Tum ₹100 ka share kharido = company ke ₹100 ke part-owner.

Real Example:
• Reliance Industries ka ek share ~₹2,500 ka hai
• Tum 1 share kharido = Reliance ke ~0.00000003% owner
• Agar Reliance ka profit badhega → share price badhega → tumhara ₹2,500 → ₹3,000+

Share Market Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Basic Flow:
• Company → IPO → Stock Exchange → Broker → You
• └── Shares listed ←── Trading ←── You buy/sell

Step by Step:
• Company decide karti hai shares bechne ka (IPO)
• Stock exchange pe shares list hote hain (NSE, BSE)
• Broker ke through tum shares kharidte ho (Zerodha, Groww)
• Demand/Supply se price decide hota hai
• Price upar jaaye → profit. Neeche jaaye → loss.

India Ke 2 Stock Exchanges:
• NSE — Full Name: National Stock Exchange, Location: Mumbai, Listed Companies: 2,000+
• BSE — Full Name: Bombay Stock Exchange, Location: Mumbai, Listed Companies: 5,000+

NSE zyada popular hai — zyada volume, zyada liquidity. Most trading NSE pe hoti hai.

India Mein Share Market Kaise Start Karein?

Step 1: Demat Account Kholein

Demat account = Tumhare shares ka digital locker. Bina demat ke shares nahi kharid sakte.

Documents chahiye:
• PAN card
• Aadhaar card
• Bank account
• Passport photo
• Signature

Kahan kholein:
• Zerodha — Account Opening Fee: ₹200, Brokerage: ₹20/order
• Groww — Account Opening Fee: Free, Brokerage: ₹20/order
• Angel One — Account Opening Fee: Free, Brokerage: ₹20/order
• Upstox — Account Opening Fee: Free, Brokerage: ₹20/order

Step 2: Trading App Download Karo

Zerodha Kite, Groww, Angel One — koi bhi use karo. Sab similar hain.

Step 3: Pehla Investment Karo

Beginner ke liye recommended:

• Blue-chip stocks — Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank
• Small amount se start — ₹1,000-5,000

Step 4: Seekhte Raho:

Share Market Ke Types

1. Primary Market:
• Kya hai: Naye shares kharidne ka market (IPO)
• Example: Zomato IPO, Paytm IPO
• Kaise: IPO mein apply karo via broker

2. Secondary Market:
• Kya hai: Existing shares kharidne-bechne ka market
• Example: NSE/BSE pe daily trading
• Kaise: Broker app se buy/sell karo

3. Derivatives Market (F&O):
• Kya hai: Futures aur Options trading
• Example: Nifty options, Bank Nifty futures
• Risk: Bahut high — experienced traders only

Share Market Mein Paisa Kaise Banta Hai?

Tarika 1: Capital Appreciation (Price Badhne Se):
• Share ₹100 pe kharida → ₹150 ho gaya → ₹50 profit
• Timeframe: Weeks to years
• Risk: Price neeche bhi ja sakta hai

Tarika 2: Dividend (Company Ka Profit Share):
• Company profit mein se dividend deti hai
• Example: ITC ₹6/share dividend deta hai
• Tumhare paas 100 shares → ₹600 dividend income

Tarika 3: Trading (Short-Term Buy/Sell):
• Intraday: Same day buy/sell
• Swing: 2-10 din hold
• Risk: Bahut high — 90% traders lose money

Share Market Mein Kitna Paisa Chahiye?

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 1 share kharidna — ₹1 (penny stock) se ₹50,000+ (MRF)
• Nifty 50 ETF — ~₹200 (1 unit)
• Mutual Fund SIP — ₹500/month
• F&O Trading — ₹50,000+ (margin requirement)
• Recommended start — ₹5,000-10,000

₹5,000 se start kar sakte ho. Nifty 50 ETF ya index fund se.

Share Market Ke Risks

Market Risk

Stock market upar-neeche hota hai. 2008 mein Nifty 60% gira. 2020 mein 40% gira. Long-term mein recover hota hai, but short-term mein bahut painful ho sakta hai.

Company Risk

Company ka business kharab ho sakta hai. Yes Bank, DHFL, IL&FS — sab ne investors ko bahut nuksan pahunchaya.

Liquidity Risk

Kuch stocks mein buyers kam hote hain. Bechne waqt price bahut ghat sakta hai.

Emotional Risk

Fear aur greed se galat decisions le sakte ho. 90% retail traders loss mein hote hain kyunki emotions control karte hain.

Beginner Ke Liye Golden Rules

Ye aath baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Pehle seekho, phir invest karo — TRT ke 50+ articles padho
• Long-term socho — 5-7 years ka horizon rakho
• Diversify karo — Ek stock pe sab mat lagao
• SIP se start karo — Mutual fund SIP safest hai
• Tips pe trust mat karo — Apna research karo
• Emergency fund pehle rakho — 6 months expenses
• Borrow karke invest mat karo — Sirf extra paisa lagao
• Emotions control karo — Plan banao, uspe stick karo

Share Market vs Other Investments

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — Share Market: 12-15% (long-term), FD: 6-7%, Gold: 8-10%, Real Estate: 8-12%
• Risk — Share Market: High, FD: Very Low, Gold: Medium, Real Estate: Medium
• Liquidity — Share Market: Instant, FD: Low, Gold: Moderate, Real Estate: Very Low
• Minimum — Share Market: ₹200, FD: ₹1,000, Gold: ₹1,000, Real Estate: ₹10 lakh+
• Tax benefit — Share Market: ELSS only, FD: ❌, Gold: ❌, Real Estate: ❌
• Effort — Share Market: High (research), FD: Zero, Gold: Low, Real Estate: High
