Expense Ratio Kya Hai? Mutual Fund Cost Guide (Hindi)

Expense ratio kya hai, direct vs regular plan, TER comparison, long-term impact — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 05, 2026 · Features

Schema: FAQPage, Article

Expense Ratio Kya Hai?

Expense ratio (jise TER — Total Expense Ratio bhi kehte hain) fund ki annual fee hai jo fund house tumhare investment se automatically kaat leta hai — fund manage karne, research karne, aur operate karne ke charges cover karne ke liye.

Ye fee tumhare bank account se seperately nahi kati jaati — NAV mein hi adjust ho jaati hai, isliye zyadatar investors ko ye "invisible" lagta hai. Lekin long-term mein ye returns pe bahut bada impact daalta hai.

Example: ₹1,00,000 invested hai ek fund mein, expense ratio 1.5% hai:

Annual charge = ₹1,00,000 × 1.5% = ₹1,500/year

Ye ₹1,500 automatically fund ki NAV se adjust ho jaata hai — tumhe alag se bill nahi milta.

Expense Ratio Mein Kya Include Hota Hai?

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Fund Management Fee — Fund manager aur research team ki salary
• Administrative Costs — Operations, compliance, reporting
• Distribution Commission — Agent/distributor ka commission (Regular plan mein)
• Marketing Expenses — Fund promotion, advertising
• Registrar & Transfer Agent Fees — Record-keeping, statements

SEBI ne expense ratio ki maximum limit decide ki hai fund size ke hisaab se — equity funds ke liye typically 2.25% tak (AUM slabs ke hisaab se ghatta jaata hai).

Direct vs Regular Plan — Expense Ratio Ka Farak

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Direct Plan — Expense Ratio: 0.5-1%, Returns: Higher, Kyun: Koi distributor commission nahi
• Regular Plan — Expense Ratio: 1.5-2%, Returns: Lower, Kyun: Agent/distributor commission included

Same fund, same portfolio, same fund manager — sirf commission structure alag hai. Direct plan mein tum directly AMC (fund house) se invest karte ho, Regular plan mein agent/distributor ke through, jise commission milta hai jo tumhare returns se kaata jaata hai.

Hamesha Direct plan lo. Agar tumhe advice chahiye, ek fee-based financial advisor hire karo (flat fee), commission-based agent nahi.

Long-Term Impact — 1% Ka Farak Kitna Bada Hai?

Ye numbers dekh kar samjho ki expense ratio kyun itna important hai:

Assumption: ₹10,000/month SIP, 30 years, 12% gross annual return (before expenses)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 0.5% (Direct) — Net Return: 11.5%, Final Value (30 years): ₹2.85 Crore, Regular Plan Se Difference: —
• 1.5% (Regular) — Net Return: 10.5%, Final Value (30 years): ₹2.28 Crore, Regular Plan Se Difference: ₹57 lakh kam
• 2.0% (Regular, high cost) — Net Return: 10%, Final Value (30 years): ₹2.03 Crore, Regular Plan Se Difference: ₹82 lakh kam

Sirf 1-1.5% ka expense ratio difference 30 years mein ₹50-80 lakh ka farak bana deta hai — compounding ke wajah se ye chhota-sa % gap exponentially bada ho jaata hai.

Expense Ratio Fund Type Ke Hisaab Se Kaisa Hota Hai

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Index Funds — Typical Expense Ratio (Direct): 0.1-0.3%, Kyun: Passive — koi active research/manager decisions nahi
• Large-cap Active Funds — Typical Expense Ratio (Direct): 0.5-1%, Kyun: Kam research-intensive (established companies)
• Mid/Small-cap Funds — Typical Expense Ratio (Direct): 0.7-1.2%, Kyun: Zyada research-intensive
• Debt Funds — Typical Expense Ratio (Direct): 0.2-0.6%, Kyun: Simpler asset class, kam active management
• International Funds — Typical Expense Ratio (Direct): 0.8-1.5%, Kyun: Extra costs (currency, foreign research)

Index funds sabse kam expense ratio offer karte hain kyunki wo simply ek index (Nifty 50, etc.) ko track karte hain — koi active stock-picking nahi hoti.

Expense Ratio Compare Kaise Karein — Practical Steps

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Same category ke funds compare karo — large-cap ko large-cap se, mid-cap ko mid-cap se
• Direct plan ka ratio dekho, Regular ka nahi
• Returns ke saath compare karo — sirf lowest expense ratio wala fund best nahi hota agar returns bhi kam hain
• AMFI/fund factsheet se verify karo — official numbers hamesha check karo
• Consistency dekho — expense ratio time ke saath change ho sakta hai as AUM grows (usually ghatta hai)

Common Mistakes — Ye Mat Karna

Mistake 1: Sirf Lowest Expense Ratio Dekh Kar Fund Select Karna

Lowest cost fund hamesha best nahi hota agar uske returns bhi consistently kam hain. Net returns (returns minus expenses) dekho.

Mistake 2: Regular Plan Mein Rehna "Advisor Support" Ke Naam Pe

Agar advisor sirf commission ke liye recommend kar raha hai, ye conflict of interest hai. Fee-only advisor better option hai.

Mistake 3: Expense Ratio Ko One-Time Cost Samajhna

Ye recurring annual charge hai — har saal lagta hai jab tak tum invested ho, ek baar ka fee nahi.

Cheat Sheet

Ye nau baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 EXPENSE RATIO QUICK REFERENCE
• 📊 Annual fee jo fund manage karne ke liye charge hota hai
• 📊 NAV mein automatically adjust hota hai — alag se bill nahi aata
• 📊 Direct plan: 0.5-1% | Regular plan: 1.5-2%
• 📊 Hamesha Direct plan lo — same fund, kam cost
• 📊 Index funds sabse kam expense ratio (0.1-0.3%)
• 📊 1% ka farak = ₹50-80 lakh difference in 30 years (compounding effect)
• 📊 Same category ke funds hi compare karo

FAQ — Expense Ratio Ke Baare Mein Common Sawaal

Expense ratio kaise pay karte hain — alag se bill aata hai kya?

Nahi, expense ratio automatically fund ki daily NAV calculation mein adjust ho jaata hai. Tumhe alag se koi bill ya payment nahi karna padta.

Kya zero expense ratio wale funds hote hain?

Kuch AMCs occasionally promotional zero-expense-ratio funds launch karte hain limited period ke liye, lekin ye rare hai aur long-term mein normal rates apply hote hain.

Expense ratio change ho sakta hai?

Haan, SEBI regulations ke hisaab se fund ka AUM grow karne pe expense ratio typically ghatta hai (slab-based structure). Fund houses ise adjust kar sakte hain within SEBI limits.

Direct plan mein switch karne se koi tax lagta hai?

Haan, Regular se Direct plan mein switch karna ek "redemption + fresh purchase" mana jaata hai — capital gains tax apply ho sakta hai. Isliye best hai shuruaat se hi Direct plan choose karo.

Index funds ka expense ratio kyun itna kam hota hai?

Kyunki index funds passively ek index (jaise Nifty 50) ko replicate karte hain — koi active research ya stock-picking decisions nahi hoti, isliye management cost bahut kam hota hai.
