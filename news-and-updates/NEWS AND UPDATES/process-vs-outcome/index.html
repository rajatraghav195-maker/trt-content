PROCESS AUR NATEEJA: EK ACCHI TRADE JO PAISA GANWA DE

Faisla aur nateeja alag cheezein hain — aur inhe milane se seekhna ruk jaata hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Ek trade jo aapne apne saare rules ke hisaab se li thi, wo stop hit kar gayi. Ek trade jo aapne bina soche, badi size par li thi, wo double ho gayi. Kaunsi acchi thi? Zyadatar log P&L dekh kar jawab dete hain, aur wahin se seekhna ruk jaata hai.

CHAR KHANE

Har faisle ko do axes par rakho — process sahi tha ya nahi, aur nateeja accha aaya ya nahi:
• Achha process, achha nateeja — yahi target hai. Repeat karo.
• Achha process, bura nateeja — ye normal hai. Isse system nahi badalna chahiye.
• Bura process, bura nateeja — sabse saaf sabak. Isse seekhna aasan hai.
• Bura process, achha nateeja — sabse khatarnak khana. Yahin aadatein banti hain jo baad mein mehngi padti hain.

SABSE KHATARNAK KHANA

Chautha khana isliye khatarnak hai kyunki usme inaam mil jaata hai. Aapne stop hataya aur stock wapas aa gaya — ab agli baar stop hatana aur aasan ho gaya.

Market galat aadaton ko random inaam deta hai. Isliye aadatein sirf nateeje se nahi sudhar sakti — unhe rules aur review se sudharna padta hai.

EK TRADE SE KOI NATEEJA NAHI NIKALTA

Ek trade ka outcome bahut kuch randomness se aata hai. 20 trades ka nateeja thoda kam random hai. 200 ka aur kam.

Isliye system par faisla ek trade se nahi hota. Jo trader har haar ke baad tarika badalta hai, wo kabhi kisi tarike ka asli data hi nahi bana paata.

REVIEW MEIN KYA POOCHNA HAI

P&L pehla sawaal nahi hona chahiye. Pehla sawaal ye ho: kya maine wahi kiya jo maine karne ka plan banaya tha?

Agar haan, to trade acchi thi — chahe paisa gaya ho. Agar nahi, to trade buri thi — chahe paisa aaya ho. Ye ek chhota sa reframe hai jo lambe samay mein sabse bada farq banata hai.

AAKHIR MEIN

Trading mein aap nateeje control nahi kar sakte. Aap sirf process control kar sakte ho — kaunsi trade leni hai, kitni size par, aur kahan nikalna hai. Lambe samay mein wahi process nateeja ban jaata hai, aur uske pehle jitne bhi individual results aayein, wo sirf shor hain.
