ASSET ALLOCATION: PORTFOLIO KA SABSE BADA FAISLA

Equity, debt, gold aur cash ka mix — aur wo kyun stock selection se zyada maayne rakhta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Zyadatar log apna poora waqt is sawaal par lagate hain ki 'kaunsa stock lein'. Research lagataar ye batati rahi hai ki portfolio ke nateeje ka bada hissa is sawaal se aata hai ki 'kitna kis asset class mein hai'.

ASSET ALLOCATION KA MATLAB

Apni capital ko alag-alag tarah ke assets mein baantna — equity, debt, gold, cash, aur zaroorat ho to real estate.

Iska maqsad sirf return nahi hai. Maqsad ye hai ki jab ek cheez bahut kharab kar rahi ho, tab poora portfolio uske saath na doobe.

HAR ASSET KA KAAM ALAG HAI

Portfolio mein har cheez ka apna role hai:
• Equity — lambe samay ka growth engine. Sabse zyada volatile, aur sabse zyada return dene ki sambhavna.
• Debt — sthirta aur predictability. Iska kaam chamakna nahi, girawat mein cushion dena hai.
• Gold — aksar equity ke ulta ya alag chalta hai, khaaskar currency aur crisis ke daur mein.
• Cash — sabse kam samajha jaane wala asset. Uska return kam hai par uska option value bahut zyada hai: wo aapko mauke par kharidne ki aazadi deta hai.

MIX KAISE TAY HOTA HAI

Iska koi ek sahi jawab nahi hai, aur jo koi ek jawab de wo aapki zindagi nahi jaanta. Mix teen cheezon se banta hai: paisa kab chahiye, kitna nuksaan jhel sakte ho, aur aapki aamdani kitni sthir hai.

Sabse zaroori inme se doosra hai — aur usme log apne aap ko lagbhag hamesha zyada bahadur maante hain. Ek allocation jo kaagaz par sahi hai par jise aap girawat mein tod dete ho, wo sahi allocation nahi tha.

UMAR SE ZYADA GOAL MAAYNE RAKHTA HAI

'100 minus age' jaise formula aam hain, par wo bahut mote hain. Ek 30 saal ka aadmi jise do saal baad ghar ke liye paisa chahiye, uska risk 55 saal ke aadmi se kam hona chahiye jiska paisa 20 saal ke liye pada hai.

Isliye allocation goal ke hisaab se socho, umar ke hisaab se nahi. Har paise ka ek kaam aur ek timeline honi chahiye.

SABSE PRACTICAL SAWAAL

Ek chhota sa test: agar aapka equity hissa 40% gir jaaye, to aapke poore portfolio par kitna asar aayega, aur kya aap us haalat mein apna plan chala paoge?

Agar us sawaal ka jawab asehaj karta hai, to equity ka hissa aapke liye zyada hai — chahe wo kisi bhi formula ke hisaab se sahi ho.
