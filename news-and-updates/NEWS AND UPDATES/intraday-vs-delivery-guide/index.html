Swing Trading vs Intraday — Kya Difference Hai? (Hindi)

Swing trading aur intraday trading mein kya fark hai, kaunsa better hai — complete Hindi guide Indian traders ke liye.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 07, 2026 · Features

Swing trading aur intraday trading mein kya fark hai, kaunsa better hai — complete Hindi guide Indian traders ke liye.

Swing Trading Kya Hai?

Swing trading mein tum 2-10 din ke liye trade lete ho. Daily chart pe analysis karte ho, order lagao, do din baad check karo.

Core concept: > Swing trading = Short-term trend ka profit uthana. 2-10 din ka game.

Intraday Trading Kya Hai?

Intraday trading mein tum same day buy aur sell karte ho. Position overnight carry nahi hoti.

Core concept: > Intraday = Subah kharido, shaam tak becho. Same day, same trade.

Swing vs Intraday — Full Comparison

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Duration — Swing Trading: 2-10 days, Intraday Trading: Same day
• Chart — Swing Trading: Daily, Intraday Trading: 1-15 min
• Time required — Swing Trading: 30 min/day, Intraday Trading: Full-time screen
• Leverage — Swing Trading: 1x (full capital), Intraday Trading: 5-20x
• Risk — Swing Trading: High, Intraday Trading: Very High
• Stress — Swing Trading: Moderate, Intraday Trading: Very High
• Win rate — Swing Trading: 45-55%, Intraday Trading: 35-45%
• Capital needed — Swing Trading: ₹50,000+, Intraday Trading: ₹1,00,000+
• Best for — Swing Trading: Working professionals, Intraday Trading: Full-time traders
• Brokerage — Swing Trading: Lower (fewer trades), Intraday Trading: Higher (many trades)
• Overnight risk — Swing Trading: Yes (gap up/down), Intraday Trading: No
• Analysis — Swing Trading: Technical + Fundamental, Intraday Trading: Purely technical
• Indicators — Swing Trading: RSI, MA, MACD (daily), Intraday Trading: VWAP, EMA (intraday)

Risk Comparison

Swing Trading Risks:
• Gap risk: Overnight gap up/down
• Weekend risk: Global events during weekend
• Earnings risk: Results announcement during hold
• Trend reversal: Trend change during hold

Intraday Trading Risks:
• Leverage risk: 5-20x leverage = amplified losses
• Whipsaw risk: False breakouts intraday
• Slippage: Fast market mein order fill price different
• Emotional risk: Full-time screen = emotional decisions
• Overtrading: Multiple trades = multiple losses

Profitability Comparison

Swing Trading:
• Average return: 2-5% per trade
• Trades per month: 8-15
• Monthly target: 10-20%
• Win rate needed: 45%+ (with 1:2 R:R)
• Time to profitability: 6-12 months learning

Intraday Trading:
• Average return: 0.5-2% per trade
• Trades per day: 3-5
• Monthly target: 5-15%
• Win rate needed: 50%+ (with 1:1.5 R:R)
• Time to profitability: 12-24 months learning

Swing trading zyada profitable hai most traders ke liye — kam stress, kam time, better risk-adjusted returns.

Kaunsa Choose Karein?

Swing Trading Choose Karo Jab:
• ✅ Working professional ho (full-time job hai)
• ✅ Screen pe zyada time nahi de sakte
• ✅ Kam stress chahiye
• ✅ Overnight risk handle kar sakte ho
• ✅ Daily chart pe analysis prefer karte ho
• ✅ Patience hai (2-10 din wait kar sakte ho)

Intraday Choose Karo Jab:
• ✅ Full-time trader ho
• ✅ Screen pe 6+ hours de sakte ho
• ✅ Fast decisions le sakte ho
• ✅ Emotional control strong hai
• ✅ Leverage risk samajhte ho
• ✅ Quick results chahiye

Dono Mat Karo Jab:
• ❌ Part-time screen time ho
• ❌ Emotional control weak ho
• ❌ Capital < ₹50,000 ho
• ❌ Experience < 6 months ho
• ❌ Risk management nahi seekha

Transition Plan: Intraday Se Swing

Bahut traders intraday se start karte hain (galat) aur phir swing pe shift karte hain (sahi). Better approach:

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• PHASE 1: LEARNING (Month 1-3)
• • Swing trading seekho (daily chart)
• • Paper trading karo
• • TRT articles padho
• • Demo account pe practice
• PHASE 2: SMALL SWING TRADES (Month 4-6)
• • ₹25,000-50,000 capital
• • 2-3 swing trades per week
• • Daily chart + RSI + MA
• • 2% risk per trade
• PHASE 3: SCALE UP (Month 7-12)
• • ₹1,00,000+ capital
• • 5-8 swing trades per month
• • Multiple strategies
• • Consistent profitability
• PHASE 4: OPTIONAL INTRADAY (Month 12+)
• • Swing trading profitable hai tab
• • Intraday add karo (small capital)
• • Parallel dono chala sakte ho
• • Separate intraday capital

Swing + Intraday Combo

Advanced traders dono karte hain:

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 70% — Style: Swing trading, Capital: ₹3,50,000, Time: 30 min/day
• 30% — Style: Intraday trading, Capital: ₹1,50,000, Time: 6 hours/day

Fayda: Swing se consistent income, intraday se extra alpha. Dono ka risk alag hai — diversification.
