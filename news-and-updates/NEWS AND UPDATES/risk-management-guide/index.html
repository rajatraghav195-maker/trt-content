Risk Management Kya Hai? Trading Capital Protection Guide (Hindi)

Risk management kya hai aur trading mein capital kaise protect karein — complete Hindi guide. Position sizing, stop loss, risk-reward sab samjhein.

Risk Management Kya Hai?

Risk management ka matlab hai apne paise ko protect karna pehle, profit baad mein aayega.

Golden rule of trading: > Pehle risk manage karo, profit apne aap aayega. Agar capital khatam ho gayi toh trade hi nahi kar paoge.

Simple Analogy

Risk management = Car ka seatbelt

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Seatbelt pehente ho kyunki accident ho sakta hai
• Accident hone ki expectation nahi hai, but protection zaroori hai
• Same way — stop loss lagao kyunki trade galat ja sakta hai

5 Rules of Risk Management (Non-Negotiable)

Rule 1: Maximum 2% Risk Per Trade

Formula:
• Risk Amount = Account Size × 2%
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Risk per trade: ₹5,00,000 × 2% = ₹10,000
• Matlab: Ek trade mein maximum ₹10,000 ka loss hoga.

Kyun 2%?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 10 consecutive losses → Account 82% of original (still alive)
• 5% risk → 10 losses → Account 60% (nearly dead)
• 10% risk → 10 losses → Account 35% (game over)

2% se kam bhi ho sakta hai — conservative traders 1% use karte hain.

Rule 2: Stop Loss Hamesha Lagao

Stop loss = Insurance. Bina stop loss ke trade karna = bina insurance ke car chalana.

Types of stop loss:
• Fixed — Kaise Lagao: Entry price se X% neeche, Best For: Beginners
• Support-based — Kaise Lagao: Support level se thoda neeche, Best For: Swing traders
• ATR-based — Kaise Lagao: 2× ATR from entry, Best For: Advanced
• Trailing — Kaise Lagao: Move with price, Best For: Trend following

Stop loss SE PEHLE decide karo — trade lene ke baad nahi.

Rule 3: Minimum 1:2 Risk-Reward Ratio

Formula:
• Risk:Reward = (Entry - Stop Loss) : (Target - Entry)
• Example:
• Entry: ₹2,500
• Stop Loss: ₹2,450 (Risk: ₹50)
• Target: ₹2,600 (Reward: ₹100)
• Risk:Reward = ₹50:₹100 = 1:2 ✅

Kyun 1:2?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 50% win rate + 1:2 R:R = Profitable (₹100 gain per 2 trades)
• 50% win rate + 1:1 R:R = Breakeven (before costs)
• 50% win rate + 1:3 R:R = Very profitable

Minimum 1:2 chahiye. 1:1.5 se kam pe trade mat lo.

Rule 4: Maximum 5 Open Positions

Why?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 5 positions × 2% risk = 10% total portfolio risk
• Ek din mein sab galat jaaye toh maximum 10% loss
• 10% se zyada ek din mein = emotional disaster

Better: 3-4 positions = 6-8% total risk = Comfortable.

Rule 5: Daily Loss Limit 5%

Rule:
• Daily Loss Limit = Account × 5%
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Daily limit: ₹25,000
• 3 consecutive losses × ₹10,000 = ₹30,000 → EXCEEDED → Done for the day

Kyun?

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Ek din ka loss recoverable hai
• Ek hafta ka loss (overtrading se) = devastating
• Daily limit ensures tum kabhi overtrade nahi karoge

Position Sizing — Kitna Trade Karo?

Method 1: Fixed Percentage (Simplest):
• Position Size = (Account × Risk%) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Risk: 2% = ₹10,000
• Entry: ₹2,500
• Stop Loss: ₹2,450
• Risk per share: ₹50
• Position Size = ₹10,000 / ₹50 = 200 shares
• Total investment: 200 × ₹2,500 = ₹5,00,000

Method 2: ATR-Based (Advanced):
• Position Size = (Account × Risk%) / (2 × ATR)
• ATR (Average True Range) = Stock ka average daily movement
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Risk: 2% = ₹10,000
• Stock ATR: ₹30
• Stop loss: 2 × ATR = ₹60
• Position Size = ₹10,000 / ₹60 = 166 shares

ATR-based sizing volatile stocks mein kam shares leta hai — automatically adjust hota hai.

Method 3: Volatility-Adjusted:
• Volatility Factor = Stock ATR / Nifty ATR
• If Volatility Factor = 1.5 (stock 1.5x more volatile than Nifty)
• → Position size 1.5x kam lo
• Example:
• Normal position: 200 shares
• Volatility factor: 1.5
• Adjusted position: 200 / 1.5 = 133 shares

Risk Management for Different Trading Styles

Intraday Trading:
• Risk per trade — 1-2%
• Daily loss limit — 3-5%
• Max positions — 1-2
• Stop loss — Tight (0.5-1%)
• Risk:Reward — 1:1.5 minimum

Swing Trading:
• Risk per trade — 2%
• Daily loss limit — 5%
• Max positions — 3-5
• Stop loss — Medium (2-3%)
• Risk:Reward — 1:2 minimum

Positional Trading:
• Risk per trade — 2-3%
• Max positions — 5-8
• Stop loss — Wide (5-8%)
• Risk:Reward — 1:3 minimum

Capital Preservation Mindset

Wrong Mindset: "Kitna kama sakta hoon?":
• Focus on profit
• High risk, high reward
• Overtrading
• No stop loss
• Result: Account wiped out

Right Mindset: "Kitna kho sakta hoon?":
• Focus on risk
• Controlled risk, consistent reward
• Quality trades
• Always stop loss
• Result: Long-term survival → profit

The Math of Recovery:
• -10% — +11%
• -20% — +25%
• -30% — +43%
• -50% — +100%
• -70% — +233%
• -90% — +900%

Matlab: 50% loss karne ke baad 100% gain chahiye sirf breakeven ke liye. Capital protect karna profit se zyada important hai.
