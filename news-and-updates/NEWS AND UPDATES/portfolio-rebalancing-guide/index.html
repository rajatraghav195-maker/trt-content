Portfolio Rebalancing Kya Hai?

Portfolio rebalancing ka matlab hai apne portfolio ko original target allocation ke wapas lana. Time ke saath allocation bigad jaata hai — rebalancing usse fix karta hai.

Simple definition: > Rebalancing = Portfolio ka "reset button." Agar target 60% equity tha aur equity badh ke 75% ho gayi → equity bech ke debt mein shift karo taaki wapas 60% ho jaaye.

Why Rebalancing?

Example

Starting allocation: 60% Equity, 30% Debt, 10% Gold

After 1 year (equity rally):
• Equity: 75% (bahut badh gayi)
• Debt: 20% (proportionally kam)
• Gold: 5%

Problem: Equity 75% = bahut risky ho gaya portfolio. Agar equity gira toh bahut bada loss.

Solution: Rebalance karo → Equity bech ke debt/gold mein shift → Wapas 60/30/10.

Rebalancing Methods

Method 1: Calendar Rebalancing:
• Frequency: Quarterly ya yearly
• Kya karo: Har quarter/year mein allocation check karo, adjust karo
• Best for: Simple, easy to follow

Method 2: Threshold Rebalancing:
• Trigger: Jab allocation 5%+ deviate ho
• Kya karo: Jab threshold cross ho tab rebalance karo
• Best for: More responsive, fewer transactions

Method 3: Cash Flow Rebalancing:
• Trigger: Naya paisa invest karte waqt
• Kya karo: New money ko under-allocated asset mein daalo
• Best for: SIP investors, no selling required

Rebalancing Process

Step 1: Current Allocation Check Karo:
• Equity kitna % hai?
• Debt kitna % hai?
• Gold kitna % hai?
• Cash kitna % hai?

Step 2: Target Allocation Se Compare Karo:
• Equity — Target: 60%, Current: 75%, Difference: +15%
• Debt — Target: 30%, Current: 20%, Difference: -10%
• Gold — Target: 10%, Current: 5%, Difference: -5%

Step 3: Rebalance Karo:
• Equity sell karo: 15% shift to debt/gold
• Debt buy karo: 10% add
• Gold buy karo: 5% add

Step 4: Verify:
• Check new allocation matches target
• Transaction costs consider karo
• Tax implications dekho

Rebalancing Frequency

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Monthly — Pros: Very responsive, Cons: Too many transactions, high costs
• Quarterly — Pros: Good balance, Cons: Moderate transactions
• Half-yearly — Pros: Less transactions, Cons: May miss big moves
• Yearly — Pros: Simple, low cost, Cons: May be too late
• Threshold (5%) — Pros: Responsive, Cons: Requires monitoring

Recommendation: Yearly rebalancing + threshold trigger (5% deviation).

Rebalancing Costs

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Brokerage — ₹20 per order
• STT — 0.1% on equity sell
• Capital gains tax — STCG 20% ya LTCG 12.5%
• Exit load — Mutual funds (0-1%)
• Stamp duty — Minimal

Tip: Cash flow rebalancing (new money) avoids selling costs.

Rebalancing Mistakes

Never Rebalancing

"Equity badh rahi hai, kyun bechu?" — Over-concentration risk. Ek crash mein bahut bada loss.

Too Frequent Rebalancing

Har month rebalance = unnecessary costs. Yearly ya threshold-based kaafi hai.

Ignoring Tax Implications

Equity sell karne pe STCG 20% lagta hai. Tax-efficient rebalancing karo (new money se).

Emotional Rebalancing

"Bull market hai, equity mat bechu" — Discipline maintain karo. Plan ke according rebalance karo.

Tax-Efficient Rebalancing

Method 1: New Money Se Rebalance:
• Naya paisa under-allocated asset mein daalo
• Kuch bechne ki zaroorat nahi
• Zero tax impact

Method 2: Loss Harvesting:
• Loss wale stocks sell karo (tax loss harvesting)
• Profit wale stocks hold karo
• Loss se capital gains offset karo

Method 3: LTCG Exemption Utilize:
• ₹1.25L LTCG tax-free hai
• Har saal ₹1.25L tak profit book karo (tax-free)
• Reinvest in under-allocated asset
