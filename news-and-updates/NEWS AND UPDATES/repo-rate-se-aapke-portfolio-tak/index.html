REPO RATE: EK NUMBER SE AAPKI EMI AUR STOCKS TAK

RBI rate kaise tay karta hai, transmission kya hai, aur kaunse sectors sabse pehle hilte hain

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Har kuch mahine mein ek meeting hoti hai jiske baad ek number aata hai, aur agle din har business channel uspar baat karta hai. Wo number repo rate hai, aur uska raasta aapke home loan se lekar aapke portfolio tak jaata hai.

REPO RATE HAI KYA

Repo rate wo dar hai jispar banks RBI se short-term paisa udhaar lete hain. Ye poore system mein paise ki laagat ka reference ban jaata hai.

Ye faisla Monetary Policy Committee karti hai, jiska mandate inflation ko ek tay daayre mein rakhna hai — growth ko dhyaan mein rakhte hue. Isliye rate ka faisla akela inflation ya akela growth nahi dekhta, dono ka santulan dekhta hai.

TRANSMISSION — RATE SE ZAMEEN TAK

Rate badalne ka asar turant nahi hota. Banks apni deposit aur lending rates dheere badalte hain, aur purane loans ka reset apne cycle par hota hai.

Isliye 'rate cut hua, ab EMI kam' turant nahi hota. External benchmark se linked loans jaldi badalte hain, baaki dheere. Ye lag hi wajah hai ki policy ka poora asar dikhne mein mahine lag jaate hain.

KAUNSE SECTORS SABSE PEHLE REACT KARTE HAIN

Rate ke saath sabse seedha rishta:
• Banks aur NBFCs — inka poora business hi paise ki laagat par hai. Rate ka asar margin par seedha aata hai, par direction hamesha simple nahi hota.
• Real estate aur auto — EMI par chalne wali demand. Rate girne par demand ka mood behtar hota hai.
• Capital-heavy companies — jinka udhaar bada hai, unka interest cost seedha badhta-ghatta hai.
• IT aur exporters — inhe rate se zyada currency aur global demand chalati hai, isliye domestic rate ka asar kam seedha hota hai.

STOCKS AUR VALUATION KA RISHTA

Rate kam hone par future ki kamai ki aaj ki keemat badh jaati hai — isi wajah se lambe growth wale stocks sasti rates mein zyada mehnge dikhte hain aur unhe wo mehngai maaf bhi ho jaati hai.

Rate badhne par ulta hota hai: door ki kamai ki aaj ki value ghat jaati hai, aur wahi stocks sabse pehle correct hote hain. Ye kisi ek company ki kahani nahi hoti — wo poore market ka discount rate badalna hota hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Policy day par turant reaction aksar ulta ya adhoora hota hai, kyunki market ne faisle ka bada hissa pehle hi price kar rakha hota hai. Zyada kaam ki cheez rate ka number nahi hota — governor ka statement aur aage ka tone hota hai.

Isliye policy ke din headline se zyada, uske saath aane wale commentary ko padhna zyada informative rehta hai.
