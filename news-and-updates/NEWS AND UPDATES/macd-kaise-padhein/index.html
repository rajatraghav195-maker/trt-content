MACD: DO AVERAGES KA FAASLA, AUR USKA MATLAB

MACD line, signal line, histogram — teenon ka kaam alag hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

MACD complicated dikhta hai kyunki uske teen hisse hain, par uske peeche ka vichaar ek line ka hai: do moving averages ke beech ka faasla badh raha hai ya ghat raha hai.

TEEN HISSE

Chart par jo dikhta hai:
• MACD line — ek tez average aur ek dheeme average ka antar. Ye khud momentum hai.
• Signal line — MACD line ka hi ek average. Ye MACD ko smooth karke uska reference banata hai.
• Histogram — MACD aur signal ke beech ka faasla, bars mein. Ye sabse jaldi badalta hai.

ZERO LINE KA MATLAB

MACD zero ke upar hai matlab tez average dheeme ke upar hai — yaani madhyam avadhi ka jhukav upar hai. Zero ke neeche ulta.

Isliye zero line ke aar-paar ka aana-jaana trend ke character badalne ka mota nishan hai, aur uske upar/neeche rehna trend ki disha ka.

HISTOGRAM SABSE PEHLE BOLTA HAI

Histogram ki bars chhoti hone lagein — bhale hi wo abhi bhi zero ke upar hon — to iska matlab hai ki dhakka kam ho raha hai. Ye crossover se pehle aata hai.

Isliye tajurbekaar log crossover ka intezaar karne ke bajaye histogram ke shrink hone ko dekhte hain. Ye jaldi aata hai, aur isliye zyada galat bhi hota hai — dono baatein saath chalti hain.

MACD KAHAN FAIL HOTA HAI

Sideways market mein MACD lagataar zero ke aar-paar naachta hai aur ek ke baad ek jhootha signal deta hai. Ye moving average par bana hua indicator hai, isliye moving average ki saari kamzoriyan isme bhi hain.

Doosra: MACD ke numbers absolute hain, isliye do alag stocks ke MACD ko aapas mein compare karna bekaar hai. Ek mehnge stock ka MACD hamesha bade numbers dikhayega.

EK IMANDAAR ISTEMAL

MACD ko entry trigger ki tarah mat use karo. Use is sawaal ke liye use karo: jis trend mein main trade lena chahta hoon, wo abhi tez ho raha hai ya thak raha hai? Us ek sawaal ka jawab wo theek-thaak deta hai, aur usse zyada maangna hi zyadatar nuksaan ki wajah hai.
