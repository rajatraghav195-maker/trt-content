Share Market Kya Hai?

Share market (stock market) ek jagah hai jahan companies ke shares (stocks) buy aur sell hote hain. Ye ek marketplace hai jahan investors aur traders aate hain apna paisa lagane.

Simple words mein:
• Company ka share = company ka chhota hissa (ownership)
• Share market = wo jagah jahan ye shares trade hote hain
• Price = demand aur supply pe depend karta hai

India ke 2 main exchanges:
• NSE (National Stock Exchange) — Nifty 50 index
• BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) — Sensex index

Share Market Ke Types

Primary Market (Naya IPO):
• Jab company pehli baar public ko shares bechti hai → IPO (Initial Public Offering)
• Aap IPO mein apply karte ho → allotment milta hai → listing pe profit/loss
• Example: Zepto IPO ₹8,000 Cr, Shiprocket IPO ₹2,500 Cr

Secondary Market (Normal Trading):
• IPO ke baad shares exchange pe list ho jate hain
• Phir aap koi bhi time pe buy/sell kar sakte ho
• Ye wo market hai jismein daily trading hoti hai

Share Market Mein Entry Kaise Karein?

Step 1: Demat Account Kholo:
• Demat account = shares ka digital locker
• Popular brokers: Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One, ICICI Direct
• Documents: PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, photo
• Process: Online, 2-3 din mein account ready

Step 2: Trading Account Kholo:
• Trading account = buy/sell orders place karne ke liye
• Usually demat + trading account saath mein khulta hai
• Same broker se dono khul jate hain

Step 3: TRT Pe Chart Dekho:
• theretailtrader.org/charting pe free charting terminal hai
• Live NSE/BSE data, 3 indicators free, 26 years history
• Stock search karo (Ctrl+K), chart dekho, analysis karo

Step 4: Pehla Trade Karo:
• Broker app mein login karo
• Stock search karo (e.g., RELIANCE)
• Quantity daalo (start with 1-2 shares)
• Market order ya limit order place karo
• Done!

Trading vs Investing — Kya Difference Hai?

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Time period — Trading: Minutes to weeks, Investing: Months to years
• Goal — Trading: Short-term profit, Investing: Long-term wealth
• Analysis — Trading: Technical (charts), Investing: Fundamental (company)
• Risk — Trading: Higher, Investing: Lower
• Effort — Trading: Daily monitoring, Investing: Occasional review
• Best for — Trading: Active people, Investing: Busy professionals

Beginner ke liye: Investing se start karo. Trading baad mein seekho.

Important Terms (Hinglish)

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Share/Stock — Company ka hissa
• Nifty 50 — Top 50 companies ka index (NSE)
• Sensex — Top 30 companies ka index (BSE)
• Bull market — Market upar ja raha hai
• Bear market — Market gir raha hai
• IPO — Initial Public Offering (company pehli baar shares bech rahi hai)
• Dividend — Company se mila profit share
• Market cap — Company ka total value
• Volume — Ek din mein kitne shares trade hue
• Volatility — Price kitna upar/neeche move karta hai
• Circuit limit — Ek din mein max price move limit
• FII/FPI — Foreign investors
• DII — Domestic institutional investors

Common Mistakes Beginners Karte Hain

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Bina seekhe trade karna — Pehla trade bina practice mat karo. TRT pe free chart hai, paper trade karo.
• Tips pe trade karna — Telegram/WhatsApp pe milne wale tips pe mat chalo. Apna analysis karo.
• Stop loss na lagana — Hamesha stop loss. Non-negotiable.
• Saara paisa ek stock mein daalna — Diversify karo. 5-10 stocks mein spread karo.
• Emotional trading — FOMO (miss hone ka darr) aur revenge trading se bachein.
