Bank Nifty expiry day strategy, Wednesday expiry trading, weekly options — complete Hindi guide.

Bank Nifty Expiry Kya Hai?

Bank Nifty ka weekly expiry har Wednesday hota hai. Ye India ka sabse traded weekly options contract hai.

Key Facts:
• Expiry day: Every Wednesday
• Lot size: 15
• Settlement: Cash
• Premium decay: Extreme (theta maximum)

Bank Nifty Expiry Day Characteristics

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Theta decay — Maximum (premium girta hai fast)
• Gamma — Highest (ATM options volatile)
• Volume — 5-10x normal
• Range — 400-800 points typical
• Max Pain — Market gravitates toward it

Bank Nifty Expiry Strategies

Strategy 1: Sell ATM Straddle (Most Popular):
• Sell ATM Call + ATM Put
• Profit from rapid time decay
• Stop loss: 1.5x premium collected
• Best entry: 9:45-10:30 AM

Strategy 2: Iron Condor (Limited Risk):
• OTM Call spread + OTM Put spread
• Defined risk, defined reward
• Width: 500-800 points

Strategy 3: Max Pain Trading:
• Identify Max Pain level
• Trade toward Max Pain
• Entry: When market is away from Max Pain

Strategy 4: Expiry Scalping:
• ATM options
• Quick 10-20 point moves
• Multiple trades
• Very risky, experienced only

Bank Nifty Expiry Risk Management

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Max risk per trade — 1%
• Position size — Half of normal
• No new entries — After 2:30 PM
• Close all — By 3:15 PM
• Stop loss — Mandatory

Bank Nifty vs Nifty Expiry

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Expiry day — Bank Nifty: Wednesday, Nifty 50: Thursday
• Volatility — Bank Nifty: 3x Nifty, Nifty 50: Moderate
• Range — Bank Nifty: 400-800 pts, Nifty 50: 100-200 pts
• Premium — Bank Nifty: Higher, Nifty 50: Lower
• Best for — Bank Nifty: Experienced, Nifty 50: Beginners
