Loan Against Shares (LAS) Kya Hai?

LAS mein tum apne shares girvi rakh ke bank se loan lete ho. Shares bechne ki zaroorat nahi — shares tumhare paas hain, bank ke paas pledge hote hain.

Simple definition: > LAS = Shares bechne ki jagah pledge karo, bank se loan lo. Shares wapas mil jaayenge jab loan repay karo.

How It Works

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Tumhare paas: ₹10 lakh ke shares
• Bank deta hai: 50% loan = ₹5 lakh
• Interest: 9-12% per annum
• Shares: Pledge (girvi) hote hain
• Loan repay karo → Shares wapas milte hain

LAS vs Selling Shares

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Ownership — LAS: Retained (pledged), Selling Shares: Lost
• Tax — LAS: No capital gains tax, Selling Shares: Capital gains tax applicable
• Future upside — LAS: Milta hai, Selling Shares: Nahi milta
• Interest cost — LAS: 9-12%, Selling Shares: None
• Best for — LAS: Short-term need, Selling Shares: Permanent exit

LAS Details

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Loan amount — 50-70% of share value
• Interest rate — 9-12% per annum
• Tenure — 1-3 years (renewable)
• Min loan — ₹50,000-1 lakh
• Processing time — 1-3 days
• Eligible securities — Large cap, liquid stocks

When to Use LAS

Good Use Cases:
• Emergency fund nahi hai but shares hain
• Short-term cash chahiye (3-12 months)
• Shares bechna nahi chahta (future upside expected)
• Tax event avoid karna hai

Bad Use Cases:
• Long-term debt repay
• Speculative trading
• Shares already down (margin call risk)
• High-interest alternative available

LAS Risks

Margin Call

Agar share price gira toh bank aur collateral maangega. Nahi diya toh shares sell ho jaayenge.

Interest Cost

9-12% interest lagta hai. Agar share returns kam hain toh nuksan hai.

Forced Selling

Bank shares bech sakta hai agar loan nahi chukaya.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:

• 📊 Shares girvi rakh ke loan lo
