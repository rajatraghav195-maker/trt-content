How to Analyze a Stock — Complete Hindi Guide

Stock Analysis Kya Hai?

Stock analysis ka matlab hai ek company ke financial health, valuation, aur price movement ko study karna — taaki decide kar sako ki stock kharidna chahiye ya nahi.

Simple definition: > Stock analysis = Company ki jaanch. Jaise doctor checkup karta hai — waise stock ki financials, valuation, aur chart check karo.

2 Types of Analysis

1. Fundamental Analysis (FA):
• Kya karta hai: Company ki financial health analyze karta hai
• Data: Revenue, profit, debt, margins, ratios
• Timeframe: Long-term (years)
• Best for: Stock selection (kaunsi company achhi hai)

2. Technical Analysis (TA):
• Kya karta hai: Price charts aur indicators analyze karta hai
• Data: Price, volume, patterns, indicators
• Timeframe: Short-term (days-weeks)
• Best for: Entry/exit timing (kab kharidna hai)

Best approach: Dono use karo. Fundamental se stock select karo, technical se entry/exit decide karo.

Step-by-Step Stock Analysis

Step 1: Business Samjho:
• Company kya karti hai? (Business model)
• Revenue kahan se aata hai? (Revenue streams)
• Competitors kaun hain? (Competition)
• Industry kaisi hai? (Sector health)
• Management kaun hai? (Promoter quality)

Step 2: Financial Analysis

Revenue (Top Line):
• Revenue growth: 5-year CAGR > 15%?
• Consistency: Har saal grow ho raha hai?
• Sources: Domestic vs export, product vs service

Profit (Bottom Line):
• Net profit growth: 5-year CAGR > 15%?
• Margins: Operating margin > 15%?
• Trend: Margins improving ya declining?

Balance Sheet:
• Debt-to-Equity: < 1 (low debt)?
• Cash flow: Operating cash flow positive?
• Interest coverage: > 3x (can easily pay interest)?

Key Ratios:
• P/E — Good Value: < 20, What It Tells: Valuation
• P/B — Good Value: < 3, What It Tells: Asset value
• ROE — Good Value: > 15%, What It Tells: Profitability
• ROCE — Good Value: > 15%, What It Tells: Capital efficiency
• Debt/Equity — Good Value: < 1, What It Tells: Leverage
• Dividend Yield — Good Value: > 1%, What It Tells: Income
• EPS Growth — Good Value: > 15%, What It Tells: Earnings growth

Step 3: Valuation Check:
• P/E ratio vs industry average
• PEG ratio = P/E / Growth rate (< 1 = undervalued)
• P/B ratio vs book value
• DCF (Discounted Cash Flow) — advanced

Step 4: Technical Analysis:
• Trend: Above 200 DMA? Uptrend?
• Momentum: RSI 30-50 (pullback) ya 50-70 (strong)?
• Entry: Support level pe? Bullish pattern?
• Volume: Breakout with volume?

Step 5: Risk Assessment:
• Promoter holding: > 50%?
• Pledged shares: < 10%?
• Corporate governance: Clean record?
• Litigation: Any major lawsuits?

Red Flags (Avoid These Stocks)

High Debt

Debt/Equity > 2 → Company zyada borrowed money use kar rahi hai. Interest payments profit kha jaayenge.

Declining Revenue

3 consecutive years revenue gira → Business shrinking. Turnaround story rare hai.

Promoter Selling

Promoter continuously selling shares → Management ko company pe confidence nahi hai.

Pledged Shares > 20%

Promoter ne shares pledge kiye hain → Margin call risk. Agar stock gira, forced selling hogi.

Corporate Governance Issues

Auditor change, related party transactions, SEBI notices → Stay away.

Penny Stocks

₹1-10 ke stocks → Mostly manipulated. Operators pump and dump karte hain.

Real Example: Analyzing Reliance Industries

Fundamental:
• Revenue: ₹8,00,000+ crore (growing)
• Net Profit: ₹70,000+ crore
• P/E: ~25 (slightly expensive)
• ROE: ~10% (below average)
• Debt/Equity: ~0.4 (manageable)
• Promoter: Mukesh Ambani (50%+)

Technical:
• Price: ~₹2,500
• 200 DMA: Above ✅
• RSI: ~50 (neutral)
• Support: ₹2,300
• Resistance: ₹2,700

Verdict:
• Fundamental: Good company but expensive (high P/E)
• Technical: Neutral — wait for pullback to ₹2,300-2,400
• Action: Accumulate on dips. Hold if already invested.
