Stocks vs FD — Complete Hindi Guide

Stocks aur fixed deposit mein kya difference hai, kahan invest karein — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 17, 2026 · Features

Stocks aur fixed deposit mein kya difference hai, kahan invest karein — complete Hindi guide.

Stocks vs FD

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — Stocks: 12-15%, Fixed Deposit: 6-7%
• Risk — Stocks: Medium-High, Fixed Deposit: Zero
• Liquidity — Stocks: High (T+1), Fixed Deposit: Medium (premature penalty)
• Tax — Stocks: LTCG 12.5%, Fixed Deposit: Slab rate
• Min amount — Stocks: ₹100, Fixed Deposit: ₹1,000
• Tenure — Stocks: No lock-in, Fixed Deposit: 1-10 years
• Guarantee — Stocks: No, Fixed Deposit: Yes

Returns Comparison (20 years, ₹10 lakh)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• FD @ 7% — Returns: 7%, Final Value: ₹38.7 lakh
• Stocks @ 12% — Returns: 12%, Final Value: ₹96.5 lakh
• Stocks @ 15% — Returns: 15%, Final Value: ₹1.64 crore

Stocks beat FD by 2.5-4x over long-term.

FD Advantages

Guaranteed Returns

Market se koi fark nahi padta. Fixed interest milta hai.

Zero Risk

Deposit insurance ₹5 lakh tak. Bankruptcy mein bhi paisa safe.

Easy to Understand

No complexity. Paisa daalo, interest milta hai.

Stocks Advantages

Higher Returns

12-15% vs 6-7%. Long-term mein bahut fark padta hai.

Beats Inflation

FD returns = Inflation ke barabar. Real returns zero.

Liquidity

Click karo, paisa next day (T+1). FD mein premature penalty.

Tax Efficiency

LTCG 12.5% vs FD pe slab rate (30% for high earners).

When to Choose FD

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Emergency fund — ✅ FD/Liquid fund
• 1-3 year goal — ✅ FD
• Risk nahi lena — ✅ FD
• Guaranteed return chahiye — ✅ FD

When to Choose Stocks

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 5+ year horizon — ✅ Stocks
• High returns chahiye — ✅ Stocks
• Inflation beat karna hai — ✅ Stocks
• Tax efficient chahiye — ✅ Stocks

Best Strategy: Dono Karo

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Conservative — FD: 70%, Stocks/MF: 30%
• Balanced — FD: 40%, Stocks/MF: 60%
• Aggressive — FD: 20%, Stocks/MF: 80%

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 STOCKS vs FD
• FD:
• 📊 Returns: 6-7%, Risk: Zero
• 📊 Guaranteed, easy to understand
• 📊 Tax: Slab rate (up to 30%)
• 📊 Best for: Emergency, short-term, risk-averse
• STOCKS:
• 📊 Returns: 12-15%, Risk: Medium-High
• 📊 Beats inflation, tax efficient
• 📊 LTCG: 12.5%
• 📊 Best for: Long-term, wealth creation
• RETURNS (₹10L, 20 years):
• 📊 FD @ 7%: ₹38.7L
• 📊 Stocks @ 12%: ₹96.5L
• 📊 Stocks @ 15%: ₹1.64 Cr
• BEST STRATEGY:
• 📊 FD for emergency + short-term goals
• 📊 Stocks/MF for long-term wealth
• 📊 Dono karo for diversification
