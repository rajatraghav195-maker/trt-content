Tax Harvesting Mutual Funds — Hindi Guide

Tax harvesting kya hai, mutual funds mein tax kaise save karein — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 18, 2026 · Features

Tax harvesting kya hai, mutual funds mein tax kaise save karein — complete Hindi guide.

Tax Harvesting Kya Hai?

Tax harvesting ka matlab hai capital losses book karna taaki capital gains offset ho jaayein aur tax kam lage.

Simple definition: > Tax harvesting = Loss wale stocks bechke profit wale stocks ke gains offset karna. Tax bachta hai.

How It Works

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock A: ₹50,000 profit (book karne pe)
• Stock B: ₹30,000 loss (book karne pe)
• Net gain: ₹20,000
• Tax: ₹20,000 pe lagega (₹50,000 pe nahi)
• Loss carry forward: Next 8 years tak use kar sakte ho

Equity Tax Rules (2024-25)

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• < 1 year — Type: STCG, Tax Rate: 20%
• > 1 year — Type: LTCG, Tax Rate: 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh

Tax Harvesting Strategy

Step 1: Loss Identify Karo

Portfolio mein jo stocks loss mein hain unko dekho.

Step 2: Loss Book Karo

Loss wale stocks becho. Realized loss hoga.

Step 3: Gains Offset Karo

Profit wale stocks ke gains se loss minus karo.

Step 4: Net Tax

Sirf net gain pe tax lagega.

Example

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Reliance — +₹1,00,000
• TCS — +₹50,000
• Infosys — -₹30,000
• Wipro — -₹20,000

Without harvesting: Tax on ₹1,50,000 With harvesting: Tax on ₹1,00,000 (₹1.5L - ₹50K loss)

Rules

Same Day Buy/Sell

Loss book karne ke baad same stock wapas kharid sakte ho. No wash sale rule in India.

Loss Carry Forward

Unused loss next 8 years tak carry forward kar sakte ho.

Set Off Rules:
• Equity loss → Equity gains se set off
• Equity loss → Salary income se set off NAHI kar sakte

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
