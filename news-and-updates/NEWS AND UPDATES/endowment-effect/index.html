ENDOWMENT EFFECT: JO MERE PAAS HAI WO ZYADA KEEMTI LAGTA HAI

Ownership ka valuation par asar, aur ek simple test

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 02, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Ek hi stock ke baare mein aapki raay badal jaati hai jab wo aapke portfolio mein aa jaata hai. Ye documented human behaviour hai, aur portfolio review mein iska seedha nuksaan hota hai.

YE HOTA KYA HAI

Jo cheez hamare paas hai, hum uski value zyada aankte hain. Isi wajah se apni holding ke liye hum jitna maangte hain, utna dene ko taiyaar nahi hote.

Portfolio mein iska roop ye hai ki apne stocks ke liye tark aasani se mil jaate hain aur unke against ke tark kamzor lagte hain.

EK SIMPLE TEST

Ye sawaal is bias ko turant saamne le aata hai:
• Agar ye position aaj mere paas na hoti, to kya main ise aaj ke bhaav par kharidta.
• Agar jawaab nahi hai, to hold karna ek naya faisla hai — aur wo faisla justify hona chahiye.
• Poochho ki iske badle mein aaj main kya kharidta.
• Ye review har position par saal mein ek baar honi chahiye.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Portfolio ka sabse imaandaar review wo hota hai jisme aap use ek khaali page se dobara banate ho. Jo naam us nayi list mein nahi aate, unke baare mein kam se kam ek sawaal to banta hi hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
