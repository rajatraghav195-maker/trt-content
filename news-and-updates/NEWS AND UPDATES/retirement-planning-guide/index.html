Retirement Planning Kya Hai?

Retirement planning ka matlab hai jab tum kaam nahi karoge tab ke liye abhi se paisa jama karna. Jaldi start karo, compound interest ka magic kaam karega.

Retirement Corpus Calculator

• Current Age: 30

Investment Options for Retirement

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• EPF/VPF — Returns: 8.15%, Risk: Very Low, Lock-in: Till retirement, Tax Benefit: 80C
• PPF — Returns: 7.1%, Risk: Very Low, Lock-in: 15 years, Tax Benefit: 80C
• NPS — Returns: 10-12%, Risk: Medium, Lock-in: Till 60, Tax Benefit: 80C + 80CCD(1B)
• ELSS — Returns: 12-15%, Risk: Medium, Lock-in: 3 years, Tax Benefit: 80C
• SIP in MF — Returns: 12-14%, Risk: Medium, Lock-in: None, Tax Benefit: No
• SGB — Returns: Gold + 2.5%, Risk: Low, Lock-in: 8 years, Tax Benefit: Tax-free at maturity

Retirement Mistakes

Late Start

"40 ke baad start karenge" — Compound interest ka fayda nahi milega.

No Inflation Adjustment

₹50,000 expenses aaj hai, 30 saal baad ₹2.87 lakh honge. Inflation factor karo.

Relying Only on EPF

EPF alone retirement ke liye kaafi nahi hai. SIP + NPS bhi chahiye.

Withdrawing EPF on Job Change

Job change pe EPF mat nikalo. Transfer karo. Compound interest ka fayda milega.

Health Insurance Skip

Retirement mein sabse bada kharcha healthcare hai. Health insurance zaroori hai.

Retirement Planning Steps

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Emergency fund banao — 6 months expenses
• EPF maximize karo — VPF bhi add karo
• NPS start karo — ₹50,000 extra tax benefit
• SIP start karo — ₹10,000-25,000/month
• Health insurance lo — ₹10-20 lakh cover
• Review annually — Har saal portfolio check karo
