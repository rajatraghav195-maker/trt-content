Volume Analysis: Price Ke Saath Volume Kaise Padhein? (Hindi Guide)

Volume analysis kya hai aur price ke saath volume kaise padhein — complete Hindi guide. High volume, low volume, divergence sab samjhein.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 19, 2026 · Features

Volume analysis kya hai aur price ke saath volume kaise padhein — complete Hindi guide. High volume, low volume, divergence sab samjhein.

Volume Kya Hai?

Volume batata hai ki ek specific time period mein kitne shares trade hue. Simple hai:

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• High Volume = Bahut log trade kar rahe hain → Strong interest
• Low Volume = Kam log trade kar rahe hain → Weak interest

Volume Ka Importance

Price batata hai KYA hua. Volume batata hai kitna STRONG tha.

Example:
• Nifty 200 points upar gaya HIGH volume pe → Rally REAL hai, buyers serious hain
• Nifty 200 points upar gaya LOW volume pe → Rally WEAK hai, jaldi toot sakta hai

Volume = Confirmation tool. Price movement tab reliable hota hai jab volume support kare.

Volume Padhne Ke 4 Rules

Rule 1: Uptrend + High Volume = STRONG Uptrend

Jab price upar ja raha hai aur volume bhi badh raha hai:
• Buyers strong hain
• Trend healthy hai
• Continue hoga

Action: Uptrend mein buy positions hold karo. Volume badh raha hai toh confidence rakhao.

Rule 2: Uptrend + Low Volume = WEAK Uptrend (Caution)

Jab price upar ja raha hai lekin volume ghat raha hai:
• Buyers kamzor ho rahe hain
• Trend khatam ho sakta hai
• Reversal possible

Action: Trailing stop loss tight karo. New buying avoid karo. Profit booking consider karo.

Rule 3: Downtrend + High Volume = STRONG Downtrend

Jab price neeche ja raha hai aur volume badh raha hai:
• Sellers strong hain
• Panic selling ho sakti hai
• Bottom abhi nahi aaya

Action: Buying mat karo. Wait for volume to decrease + price to stabilize.

Rule 4: Downtrend + Low Volume = SELLING EXHAUSTION

Jab price neeche ja raha hai lekin volume ghat raha hai:
• Sellers kam ho gaye hain
• Bottom aa sakta hai
• Reversal possible

Action: Watchlist mein daalo. Volume spike + green candle = potential buy signal.

Volume + Candlestick Patterns

Volume candlestick patterns ko CONFIRM karta hai:

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Hammer (bullish) — Without Volume: Weak signal, With High Volume: 🔥 Strong signal
• Bullish Engulfing — Without Volume: Moderate signal, With High Volume: 🔥🔥 Very strong signal
• Morning Star — Without Volume: Good signal, With High Volume: 🔥🔥🔥 Excellent signal
• Doji — Without Volume: Indecision, With High Volume: Volume pe depend karta hai
• Breakout candle — Without Volume: Suspicious (false?), With High Volume: 🔥🔥 Confirmed breakout

Rule: Breakout candle pe volume 2x+ hona chahiye previous candle se. Tab breakout real hai.

Volume Divergence (Sabse Powerful Signal)

Bearish Volume Divergence:
• Price NAYA HIGH banaye (higher high)
• Volume GHAT raha ho (lower volume)
• Matlab: Uptrend weak hai, buyers kam ho rahe hain
• Action: Caution — reversal possible

Bullish Volume Divergence:
• Price NAYA LOW banaye (lower low)
• Volume GHAT raha ho (lower volume)
• Matlab: Downtrend weak hai, sellers kam ho rahe hain
• Action: Watch — reversal possible

Ye signal sabse underutilized hai Indian traders ke beech. Volume divergence dekhna seekh lo — edge mil jayega.

Volume Indicators

1. Volume Bars (Simple)

Chart ke neeche colored bars — har candle ka volume dikhata hai:
• Green bar = Upar jaate waqt ka volume
• Red bar = Neeche jaate waqt ka volume

2. Volume Moving Average (VMA)

Volume ka 20-period average. Jab current volume VMA se zyada hai → significant day.

3. OBV (On Balance Volume)

Cumulative indicator:
• Agar close > open → volume ADD karo
• Agar close < open → volume SUBTRACT karo
• OBV ka trend price trend se match karna chahiye

OBV divergence = Strongest signal:
• Price up, OBV down → Distribution (smart money selling)
• Price down, OBV up → Accumulation (smart money buying)

4. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)

Intraday traders ka favorite. Volume-weighted average price dikhata hai.

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Price VWAP ke upar → Bullish (buyers in control)
• Price VWAP ke neeche → Bearish (sellers in control)
• VWAP pe bounce → Support/resistance level

Volume on Indian Markets — Special Notes

Expiry Day Volume

Nifty aur Bank Nifty options expiry (Thursday) pe volume bahut high hota hai. Is din ka volume analysis normal days se alag karo.

Results Season Volume

Quarterly results ke din volume spike hota hai. Post-results volume analysis important hai:
• Results achhe + high volume gap up → Strong buy signal
• Results achhe + low volume gap up → Weak, jaldi wapas aa sakta hai

F&O Expiry Week

Last week of every month mein F&O expiry ki wajah se volume distorted hota hai. Is week ka volume comparison previous weeks se carefully karo.

Volume Trading Strategies

Strategy 1: Volume Breakout

Setup:
• Stock range-bound ho (consolidation)
• Price range ke upar close kare (breakout)
• Breakout candle ka volume 2x+ ho previous average se
• Follow-through candle (agla din bhi upar)

Entry: Breakout ke baad pullback pe Stop loss: Range ke low se neeche Target: Range ki height = minimum target

Ye sabse reliable breakout strategy hai. Volume without breakout = false breakout.

Strategy 2: Volume Climax (Selling Exhaustion)

Setup:
• Strong downtrend ho
• Ek din EXTREMELY high volume pe badi red candle aaye
• Agle din volume decrease ho + small green candle

Kya hua: Panic selling ho gayi. Sellers exhaust ho gaye. Bottom ban sakta hai.

Entry: Climax ke baad 2-3 din wait karo. Agar price stabilize kare + volume normalize ho → BUY.

Caution: Ye strategy risky hai — catching a falling knife. Small position size + tight stop loss.

Strategy 3: Accumulation/Distribution (OBV Analysis)

Setup:
• Price sideways ho (range-bound)
• OBV slowly upar ja raha ho (even though price flat hai)
• Matlab: Smart money accumulate kar raha hai

Entry: Jab OBV new high banaye + price range break kare → BUY

Opposite: OBV slowly neeche ja raha ho while price flat → Distribution → SELL signal

Common Volume Mistakes

Intraday Volume Compare Karna Across Days

Intraday volume pattern daily alag hota hai. Monday ka volume Friday se zyada hota hai naturally. Same day ka comparison karo.

Low Liquidity Stocks Pe Volume Analysis

Small-cap ya penny stocks mein volume bahut erratic hota hai. Volume analysis large-caps aur indices pe sabse reliable hai.

Volume Alone Use Karna

Volume SIRF confirmation tool hai. Direction nahi batata. Hamesha price + volume saath mein dekho.

Volume Spike = Buy Signal

Volume spike = INTEREST badha. Direction price batata hai. High volume + price up = bullish. High volume + price down = bearish. Spike alone kuch nahi batata.

Volume Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 VOLUME QUICK REFERENCE
• VOLUME + PRICE COMBINATIONS:
• 📊 Price ↑ + Volume ↑ = STRONG UPTREND (hold/buy)
• 📊 Price ↑ + Volume ↓ = WEAK UPTREND (caution)
• 📊 Price ↓ + Volume ↑ = STRONG DOWNTREND (avoid buying)
• 📊 Price ↓ + Volume ↓ = SELLING EXHAUSTION (watch for reversal)
• BREAKOUT RULES:
• ✅ Breakout candle pe volume 2x+ previous average
• ✅ Follow-through candle next day
• ✅ Without volume = FALSE BREAKOUT
• DIVERGENCE:
• 🔥 Price new high + Volume declining = BEARISH divergence
• 🔥 Price new low + Volume declining = BULLISH divergence
• 🔥 OBV divergence with price = STRONGEST signal
• INDIAN MARKETS:
• 📅 Expiry day volume = distorted, compare carefully
• 📅 Results day volume spike = look at post-results behavior
• 📅 F&O expiry week = volume distorted
• TOOLS:
• 📊 Volume bars (simple)
• 📊 20-period Volume MA
• 📊 OBV (On Balance Volume)
• 📊 VWAP (intraday)
