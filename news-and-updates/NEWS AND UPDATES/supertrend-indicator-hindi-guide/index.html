Supertrend Indicator Hindi: Trend Follow Karne Ka Sabse Aasan Tarika

Supertrend Kya Hai?

Supertrend ek trend-following indicator hai jo ATR (Average True Range) ka use karta hai. Ye indicator price ke upar ya neeche ek line draw karta hai jo trend ka direction batati hai.

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Green line = Bullish trend (buy zone)
• Red line = Bearish trend (sell zone)

Supertrend sabse simple indicators mein se ek hai — sirf ek line dekho aur trend pata chal jaata hai. Isliye beginners ke liye perfect hai.

Formula:
• Bullish: (High + Low)/2 + Multiplier × ATR
• Bearish: (High + Low)/2 – Multiplier × ATR

TRT pe use karein: theretailtrader.org/charting → indicators → Supertrend select karo. Free plan mein available hai.

Supertrend Settings

Default Settings: 10, 3:
• Period (10): 10 candles ka ATR calculate hota hai
• Multiplier (3): ATR ko 3x se multiply karta hai (bandwidth ke liye)

Custom Settings:
• 10, 3 (default) — Best For: Swing trading, Characteristics: Balanced signals
• 10, 2 — Best For: Intraday, Characteristics: Faster signals, more whipsaws
• 10, 4 — Best For: Long-term, Characteristics: Slower, fewer signals, more reliable
• 7, 3 — Best For: Intraday (fast), Characteristics: Quick trend changes
• 14, 3 — Best For: Positional, Characteristics: Very slow, strong trends only

Kaunsi Setting Best Hai?:
• Beginners: Default 10, 3 rakho. Change mat karo jab tak samajh na aaye.
• Intraday traders: 10, 2 try karo (faster signals)
• Swing traders: 10, 3 (default) best hai
• Positional traders: 14, 3 (slower, more reliable)

Supertrend Strategy — Basic

Buy Signal (Bullish):
• Supertrend line red se green mein change hoti hai
• Price Supertrend line ke upar hai
• Green line = support ka kaam karti hai
• Entry: Green line banne ke baad first candle close pe
• Stop loss: Green line ke thoda neeche

Sell Signal (Bearish):
• Supertrend line green se red mein change hoti hai
• Price Supertrend line ke neeche hai
• Red line = resistance ka kaam karti hai
• Entry: Red line banne ke baad first candle close pe
• Stop loss: Red line ke thoda upar

Example: NIFTY 50 pe Supertrend

Nifty daily chart pe Supertrend (10, 3) lagao. Dekho:
• Jab Supertrend green hua → Nifty upar gaya
• Jab Supertrend red hua → Nifty neeche gaya
• Kuch false signals bhi honge — isliye confirmation zaroori hai

Supertrend + Other Indicators Combo

Combo 1: Supertrend + RSI (Best for Beginners):
• Supertrend = trend direction
• RSI = momentum confirmation
• Buy: Supertrend green + RSI > 50
• Sell: Supertrend red + RSI < 50
• Avoid: Supertrend green + RSI < 50 (weak trend)

Combo 2: Supertrend + MACD:
• Supertrend = trend
• MACD = momentum + crossover
• Buy: Supertrend green + MACD bullish crossover
• Sell: Supertrend red + MACD bearish crossover

Combo 3: Supertrend + 200 EMA:
• 200 EMA = big trend filter
• Buy only when: Price above 200 EMA AND Supertrend green
• Sell only when: Price below 200 EMA AND Supertrend red
• Ye combo false signals bahut kam karta hai

Supertrend for Intraday

Intraday ke liye Supertrend ek go-to indicator hai.

Intraday Setup:
• Timeframe: 5 min or 15 min
• Supertrend: 10, 2 (faster)
• RSI: 14 (confirmation)
• Volume: Must check

Intraday Rules:
• Buy: Supertrend green on 5 min + RSI > 50 + Volume above average
• Sell: Supertrend red on 5 min + RSI < 50 + Volume above average
• No trade: Supertrend frequently switching (choppy market)
• Stop loss: Supertrend line ke thoda opposite side
• Target: 1:2 minimum risk-reward

Intraday Example: BANK NIFTY

Bank Nifty 5 min chart pe Supertrend (10, 2) lagao. Morning 9:15 se 11 AM ke beech jab Supertrend green ho + RSI 50+ ho + volume badhe = buy. Opposite = sell.

Supertrend Limitations

Supertrend Ki Kamzoriyan:
• Choppy market mein useless — Jab market sideways hai, Supertrend bar-bar flip karta hai (green→red→green→red). Bahut false signals.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Late entry — Supertrend trend confirmation ke baad signal deta hai, isliye entry thodi late hoti hai. Initial move miss ho sakta hai.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• No target given — Supertrend sirf trend direction dikhata hai, target price nahi. Aapko alag se support/resistance dekhna padta hai.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Gap up/down problem — Agar overnight gap ho, Supertrend ka signal wrong ho sakta hai. Morning gap ke baad 15 min wait karo.

Supertrend Kab Use NA Karein:
• Sideways/choppy market mein
• Major news events ke around (RBI, Fed, budget)
• Very low volume stocks mein
• First 15 min of market (volatility too high)

Common Mistakes

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Sirf Supertrend pe rely karna — Ek indicator kabhi enough nahi hai. RSI ya MACD ke saath use karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Choppy market mein trade lena — Supertrend sideways mein bar-bar flip hota hai. Pehle trend check karo (ADK > 25 = trending market).

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stop loss na lagana — Supertrend trailing stop deta hai but usse thoda aur tight stop loss rakho for protection.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Har flip pe trade lena — Har green→red ya red→green pe trade mat lo. Confirmation candle ka wait karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Settings constantly change karna — Default settings se start karo. Jab tak backtest na karo, settings change mat karo.

FAQ

Q1: Supertrend best kis timeframe pe kaam karta hai?

15 min, 1 hour, aur daily pe sabse best. 1 min pe bahut noise hai. Intraday ke liye 5 min acceptable hai.

Q2: Kya Supertrend alone enough hai?

Nahi. Supertrend + RSI ya MACD ke saath use karo. Single indicator se 60% accuracy, combo se 70%+.

Q3: Supertrend ka multiplier kya karta hai?

Multiplier (default 3) Supertrend line ki distance ko control karta hai. Zyada multiplier = line door = fewer signals but more reliable. Kam multiplier = line paas = more signals but more whipsaws.

Q4: TRT pe Supertrend free hai?

Haan! TRT free plan mein 3 indicators milte hain. Supertrend unme se ek hai. theretailtrader.org/charting pe try karo.

Q5: Supertrend aur moving average mein difference?

Moving average sirf average price dikhata hai. Supertrend ATR use karke volatility-adjusted line dikhata hai — volatile market mein line door chalti hai, stable market mein paas.
