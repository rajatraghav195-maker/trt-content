Market Cap Kya Hai?

Market cap ka full form hai Market Capitalization. Ye batata hai ki ek company ka total market value kitna hai — yaani agar tum poori company kharidne jaao toh kitna lagega.

Market Cap = Current Share Price × Total Shares Outstanding

Example:
• Reliance Industries:
• Share price: ₹2,500
• Total shares: 676 crore
• Market cap: ₹2,500 × 676 crore = ₹16,90,000 crore (~₹17 lakh crore)

Matlab: Reliance ki poori company ka market value ₹17 lakh crore hai.

Market Cap Categories

India Mein Classification:
• Mega Cap — Market Cap Range: > ₹1,00,000 crore, Examples: Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank, Count: ~15
• Large Cap — Market Cap Range: ₹20,000-1,00,000 crore, Examples: Asian Paints, Titan, Bajaj Finance, Count: ~80
• Mid Cap — Market Cap Range: ₹5,000-20,000 crore, Examples: Coforge, Persistent, Dixon, Count: ~150
• Small Cap — Market Cap Range: ₹1,000-5,000 crore, Examples: Small industrials, regional companies, Count: ~500
• Micro Cap — Market Cap Range: < ₹1,000 crore, Examples: Penny stocks, new listings, Count: ~1,000+

SEBI Classification (Mutual Funds):
• Large Cap — Top 100 companies by market cap
• Mid Cap — 101st to 250th by market cap
• Small Cap — 251st onwards by market cap

Large Cap vs Mid Cap vs Small Cap

Ye aath baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Size — Large Cap: Big (₹20K+ crore), Mid Cap: Medium (₹5-20K crore), Small Cap: Small (₹1-5K crore)
• Stability — Large Cap: High, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: Low
• Growth potential — Large Cap: Low-Moderate, Mid Cap: Moderate-High, Small Cap: High
• Risk — Large Cap: Low, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: High
• Liquidity — Large Cap: Very high, Mid Cap: High, Small Cap: Low
• Volatility — Large Cap: Low, Mid Cap: Moderate, Small Cap: High
• Returns (10Y) — Large Cap: 10-14%, Mid Cap: 14-18%, Small Cap: 15-22%
• Best for — Large Cap: Conservative, Mid Cap: Balanced, Small Cap: Aggressive

Market Cap Investing Strategy

Strategy 1: Market Cap Weighted (Index Investing):
• Nifty 50 market cap weighted hai
• Top companies ka zyada weight
• Index fund lo = automatic market cap weighting

Strategy 2: Multi-Cap Portfolio:
• 50% — Category: Large Cap, Why: Stability, dividends
• 30% — Category: Mid Cap, Why: Growth + stability balance
• 20% — Category: Small Cap, Why: High growth potential

Strategy 3: Market Cap Rotation:
• Bull market → Small caps outperform
• Bear market → Large caps outperform
• Rotation karo cycle ke hisaab se

Market Cap Ke Limitations

Market Cap ≠ Company Value

Market cap sirf EQUITY value hai. Company ka total value (Enterprise Value) = Market Cap + Debt - Cash.

Price Se Manipulate Ho Sakta Hai

Low float companies mein price manipulate ho sakta hai → market cap artificially high ya low ho sakta hai.

Doesn't Reflect Fundamentals

High market cap = good company? Nahi! Kuch companies ka market cap high hai despite poor fundamentals (hype).
