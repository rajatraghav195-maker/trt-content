SUNK COST: PURANE NUKSAAN KI WAJAH SE NAYA NUKSAAN

Jo paisa ja chuka hai wo aaj ke faisle mein kyun nahi aana chahiye

Published by The Retail Trader · JUL 24, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Ek position mein pehle se bada nuksaan hai, aur usi wajah se use hold kiya jaata hai. Tark ye hota hai ki itna to nikal hi chuka hai. Yahi wo jagah hai jahan ek galti do galtiyan ban jaati hai.

SUNK COST KA MATLAB

Jo kharcha ya nuksaan ho chuka hai wo wapas nahi aa sakta, chahe aap aage jo bhi karo. Isliye aaj ke faisle mein uska koi role nahi hona chahiye.

Sahi sawaal ye hai — agar aaj mere paas ye position nahi hoti, to kya main aaj ke bhaav par ise kharidta. Agar jawaab nahi hai, to hold karna ek naya faisla hai, purane ka continuation nahi.

YE KAHAN KAHAN DIKHTA HAI

Sirf stocks mein nahi:
• Ek course ya subscription jiska paisa de diya, isliye uske hisaab se trade karna.
• Ek strategy jispar mahine lagaye, isliye uske results ignore karna.
• Ek stock jispar bahut research ki, isliye thesis toot jaane par bhi hold karna.
• Ek averaging ki hui position, jise ab nikaalna "waste" lagta hai.

EK PRACTICAL BAAT

Har position ka review is ek line se shuru karo — aaj ke bhaav par, aaj ki jaankari ke saath, kya main ise lunga. Entry price aur pehle ka nuksaan is sawaal mein aate hi nahi.
