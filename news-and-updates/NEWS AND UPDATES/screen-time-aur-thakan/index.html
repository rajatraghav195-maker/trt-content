SCREEN TIME: THAKA HUA TRADER SABSE MEHENGI GALTI KARTA HAI

Decision fatigue, market ke waqt ka structure, aur ek routine jo galtiyan kam karta hai

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Trading ek din mein kai chhote faisle maangti hai. Har faisla thoda sa dhyaan kharch karta hai. Din ke ant tak dhyaan bacha hi nahi hota — aur aksar sabse badi galti wahin hoti hai.

DECISION FATIGUE

Lagataar faisle lene se faislon ki quality girti hai. Ye trading ki khaas baat nahi hai — ye har us kaam mein hota hai jismein bar-bar chunna padta hai.

Trading mein iska nateeja saaf dikhta hai: din ke aakhri hisse mein liye gaye trades aksar sabse kam soche hue hote hain aur sabse zyada size par hote hain.

MARKET KA DIN BARABAR NAHI HOTA

Din ka pehla hissa volatile hota hai, beech ka hissa aksar dheema, aur ant mein phir se activity badhti hai. In teenon ka character alag hai.

Bahut trader isiliye apne aap ko ek window tak seemit rakhte hain. Poora din dekhne se zyada trade nahi milte — sirf zyada thakan milti hai.

EK SIMPLE ROUTINE

Jo cheezein sabse zyada farq karti hain:
• Market khulne se pehle ka plan — kaunse naam, kaunse level. Baad mein sochna aadha kaam khud hi kharab kar deta hai.
• Ek tay window mein hi trade — aur uske baad screen se hat jaana.
• Har trade ke baad ek chhota break — position lene ke turant baad wali soch sabse gadbad hoti hai.
• Din ke ant mein 5 minute ka review — kya kiya, kyun kiya, aur kya alag karna tha.

SCREEN PAR HONA KAAM NAHI HAI

Chart dekhte rehna productive lagta hai par aksar wo sirf intezaar ko busy dikhana hai. Us waqt mein aap wo cheezein dekhne lagte ho jo hain hi nahi.

Alerts lagana yahan sabse practical badlav hai. Level pehle se tay karo, alert laga do, aur screen chhod do. Machine ko dekhne do; aapka kaam faisla lena hai, nigrani karna nahi.

NEEND, KHANA, AUR BAAKI BORING CHEEZEIN

Ye salah ghisi hui lagti hai par isse bachna mushkil hai: thakan, bhookh aur neend ki kami seedha faislon par asar daalti hai. Trading ek performance activity hai, aur uska ek hissa screen ke bahar hota hai.

Sabse sasta risk management aksar yahi hai — ek din trade na karna jab aapko pata ho ki aap us haalat mein nahi ho.
