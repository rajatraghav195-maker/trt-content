Options trading strategies — bull spread, bear spread, iron condor, straddle, strangle — complete Hindi guide.

Options Strategies Overview

Options strategies multiple legs use karti hain — buy + sell combinations jo risk aur reward ko manage karti hain.

Bullish Strategies

1. Bull Call Spread:
• Buy ATM Call + Sell OTM Call
• Limited risk, limited reward
• When: Moderately bullish

2. Bull Put Spread (Credit):
• Sell OTM Put + Buy further OTM Put
• Net credit, limited risk
• When: Bullish, want income

3. Long Call:
• Buy ATM/OTM Call
• Unlimited reward, limited risk
• When: Very bullish

Bearish Strategies

1. Bear Put Spread:
• Buy ATM Put + Sell OTM Put
• Limited risk, limited reward
• When: Moderately bearish

2. Bear Call Spread (Credit):
• Sell OTM Call + Buy further OTM Call
• Net credit, limited risk
• When: Bearish, want income

3. Long Put:
• Buy ATM/OTM Put
• Large reward, limited risk
• When: Very bearish

Neutral Strategies

1. Iron Condor:
• OTM Call spread + OTM Put spread
• Profit from range-bound movement
• When: Low volatility expected

2. Short Straddle:
• Sell ATM Call + ATM Put
• Max profit at ATM, unlimited risk
• When: Very low volatility

3. Long Straddle:
• Buy ATM Call + ATM Put
• Profit from big move either direction
• When: Event-based, big move expected

4. Short Strangle:
• Sell OTM Call + OTM Put
• Wider profit zone than straddle
• When: Range-bound

5. Long Strangle:
• Buy OTM Call + OTM Put
• Cheaper than straddle
• When: Big move expected

Strategy Selection Matrix

Ye das baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Very bullish — Volatility: Any, Strategy: Long Call
• Moderately bullish — Volatility: Low, Strategy: Bull Call Spread
• Moderately bullish — Volatility: High, Strategy: Bull Put Spread
• Very bearish — Volatility: Any, Strategy: Long Put
• Moderately bearish — Volatility: Low, Strategy: Bear Put Spread
• Moderately bearish — Volatility: High, Strategy: Bear Call Spread
• Neutral — Volatility: High, Strategy: Iron Condor
• Neutral — Volatility: Very low, Strategy: Short Straddle
• Big move expected — Volatility: Low, Strategy: Long Straddle
• Big move expected — Volatility: High, Strategy: Long Strangle

Greeks Impact on Strategies

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Bull Call Spread — Delta: +, Vega: +
• Bear Put Spread — Vega: +
• Iron Condor — Delta: Neutral, Theta: +
• Short Straddle — Delta: Neutral, Theta: +
• Long Straddle — Delta: Neutral, Vega: +

Key: + means benefits, - means hurts
