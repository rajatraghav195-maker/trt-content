PROMOTER HOLDING: MALIK KITNA ANDAR HAI

Holding badhna-ghatna, pledged shares, aur shareholding pattern kaise padhein

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Company ke maalik us company mein kitna paisa laga kar baithe hain — ye ek aisi jaankari hai jo har quarter public hoti hai aur bahut kam log dekhte hain. Uska naam shareholding pattern hai.

YE MILTA KAHAN HAI

Har listed company ko har quarter apna shareholding pattern exchange ko dena hota hai. Usme promoters, foreign investors, domestic institutions aur public — sabka hissa alag-alag likha hota hai.

Ye free hai, exchange ki site par hai, aur uske alag-alag quarters ko saath rakhkar ek trend dikh jaata hai.

HOLDING BADHNA AUR GHATNA

Promoter ka apni hi company mein hissa badhana aam taur par ek positive signal maana jaata hai — jo log andar hain wo aur paisa laga rahe hain.

Ghatna hamesha bura nahi hota. Kabhi wo kisi personal zaroorat se hota hai, kabhi regulatory requirement se, kabhi ek stake sale se jismein naya partner aata hai. Isliye number dekh kar rukna nahi hai — wajah dhundhna hai, jo aksar company ki apni announcement mein hoti hai.

PLEDGED SHARES — SABSE ZAROORI COLUMN

Promoter apne shares girvi rakh kar udhaar le sakta hai. Isi ko pledge kehte hain aur ye bhi disclose hota hai.

Iska risk seedha hai: agar stock gir jaaye to lender ko aur collateral chahiye. Na mile to lender wo shares bech sakta hai, jisse stock aur girta hai, jisse aur bikwali aati hai. Ye ek self-feeding girawat hai aur India mein iske kai udaharan hain.

KYA-KYA DEKHNA HAI

Quarterly pattern mein teen cheezein:
• Promoter holding ka trend — chaar-paanch quarters saath mein dekho, ek quarter se nateeja mat nikalo.
• Pledge ka percentage — aur wo badh raha hai ya ghat raha hai.
• Institutional participation — mutual funds aur foreign investors ka hissa badh raha hai ya nikal rahe hain.
• Public shareholders ki sankhya — bahut tezi se badhta retail count aksar us daur mein hota hai jab stock kisi kahani ki wajah se chal raha ho.

EK ZAROORI SEEMA

Bahut zyada promoter holding apne aap mein safety nahi hai, aur kam holding apne aap mein khatra nahi. Kai badi professional companies mein promoter hai hi nahi.

Ye data ek sandarbh hai, faisla nahi. Uska sabse acha istemal ye hai ki wo aapko sawaal dega — aur us sawaal ka jawab company ki filings mein hi milta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
