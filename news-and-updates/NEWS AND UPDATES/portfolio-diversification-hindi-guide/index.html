Portfolio Diversification Hindi: Apne Risk Kaise Spread Karein

Portfolio diversification hindi mein samjho — kya, kyun, kaise. Stocks, sectors, asset classes pe risk spread karne ka complete guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 13, 2026 · Features

Portfolio Diversification Kya Hai?

"Saare ande ek tokri mein mat rakhna" — ye exactly diversification hai.

Portfolio diversification = apna paisa alag-alag stocks, sectors, aur asset classes mein spread karna taaki ek investment ka loss dusre ko affect na kare.

Example:
• Agar poora paisa IT sector mein → IT crash = 100% loss
• Agar 5 sectors mein spread (IT 20%, Banking 20%, Auto 20%, Pharma 20%, FMCG 20%) → IT crash = sirf 20% impact

Kyun Diversify Karein?

1. Risk Reduction:
• Ek stock crash = portfolio damage limited
• Sector crash = impact partial
• Market crash = impact moderate (not total)

2. Consistent Returns:
• Kabhi IT rally, kabhi Banking rally, kabhi Pharma
• Diversified portfolio = hamesha kuch sector positive
• Non-diversified = kabhi +40%, kabhi -40% (rollercoaster)

3. Peace of Mind:
• Ek stock neeche → portfolio stable (dusre compensate)
• Sleep peacefully — no single stock can destroy your portfolio

Kaise Diversify Karein?

Level 1: Stock Diversification (Min 8-12 Stocks):
• Ek sector mein minimum 2-3 stocks
• Single stock mein max 10-15% of portfolio
• Example: Reliance (15%) + TCS (10%) + HDFC Bank (10%) + 9 more stocks

Level 2: Sector Diversification (5-7 Sectors):
• IT, Banking, Auto, Pharma, FMCG, Metal, Energy
• Ek sector mein max 25-30% of portfolio
• Example: Banking 25%, IT 20%, FMCG 15%, Auto 15%, Pharma 10%, Metal 10%, Energy 5%

Level 3: Asset Class Diversification:
• Stocks (60-70%) + Gold (10-15%) + FD/Bonds (15-20%) + Cash (5-10%)
• Stocks: growth
• Gold: hedge against equity crash
• FD/Bonds: stability
• Cash: opportunity fund

Level 4: Market Cap Diversification:
• Large cap (60%): Reliance, TCS, HDFC — stable, low risk
• Mid cap (25%): Tata Motors, Godrej, Bajaj Finance — growth + moderate risk
• Small cap (15%): High risk, high reward

Diversification Rules

Rule 1: Correlation Check:
• Correlated stocks = fake diversification
• Example: TCS + Infosys + Wipro = all IT, same movement
• Better: TCS (IT) + HDFC (Banking) + HUL (FMCG) = low correlation

Rule 2: Max Position Size:
• Single stock: max 10-15% of portfolio
• Single sector: max 25-30%
• Single theme: max 40%

Rule 3: Rebalance Quarterly:
• Har 3 months pe portfolio check karo
• Agar ek sector 40% ho gaya (target was 25%) → profit book karo, reduce to 25%
• Reallocate to underweight sectors

Rule 4: Cash Reserve:
• Hamesha 10-20% cash rakho
• Crash mein buying opportunity ke liye
• Cash = option to buy at discount

Common Diversification Mistakes

Mistake 1: Over-Diversification:
• 30-40 stocks = over-diversified = no benefit, hard to track
• Sweet spot: 8-15 stocks across 5-7 sectors
• "Diworsification" = diluting returns without reducing risk

Mistake 2: Fake Diversification:
• 10 IT stocks = NOT diversified (all same sector)
• 10 Nifty stocks = partially (same large-cap market)
• Check correlation, not just count

Mistake 3: Never Rebalancing:
• Portfolio started 25% IT, 25% Banking...
• IT rallied 50% → now IT is 40%
• Don't let winners become oversized → rebalance

Mistake 4: Ignoring Gold/Cash:
• 100% equity portfolio = no hedge
• 2020 crash: 100% equity down 38%
• With 20% gold: portfolio down only 25% (gold rallied)
• Gold = insurance, not investment

Mistake 5: Chasing Returns, Ignoring Risk:
• "Small cap ne 100% return diya, daalo poora paisa"
• Next year: small cap -50%
• Risk-adjusted returns matter, not just returns

FAQ

Q1: Kitne stocks hone se portfolio diversified hota hai?

8-15 stocks across 5-7 sectors. Isse kam = under-diversified. Isse zyada = over-diversified.

Q2: Mutual fund mein diversification automatic hai?

Haan, mutual funds hold 30-50 stocks. But fund ka category check karo (large cap fund = sirf large caps, not truly diversified).

Q3: Gold kyun rakhein?

Gold equity ke saath negative correlation rakhta hai. Equity crash = gold up. Hedge = insurance.

Q5: Rebalancing mein tax implications?

Short-term capital gains (STCG) = 20% on equity. Long-term (LTCG) = 12.5% above ₹1.25L. Rebalance mein tax cost consider karo — don't rebalance too frequently.
