IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP) — Hindi Guide

IPO GMP kya hai, grey market premium kaise check karein, GMP se listing price prediction — Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 08, 2026 · Features

GMP Kya Hai?

GMP = Grey Market Premium. Ye wo premium hai jo IPO shares ka unofficial market mein trade hota hai — listing se pehle.

Simple definition: > GMP = IPO listing se pehle kitna premium/discount hai. Agar GMP ₹100 hai toh listing price ₹100 zyada hogi expected.

How GMP Works

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• IPO Price: ₹500
• GMP: ₹150
• Expected Listing Price: ₹500 + ₹150 = ₹650
• Agar GMP negative (-₹50):
• Expected Listing: ₹500 - ₹50 = ₹450 (listing below IPO price)

GMP Factors

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Company quality — Strong company → High GMP
• Subscription — Oversubscription → High GMP
• Market sentiment — Bull market → High GMP
• Grey market demand — High demand → High GMP
• Sector — Hot sector (IT, EV) → High GMP

GMP Interpretation

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• GMP > 50% — Listing Expectation: Strong listing, Apply?: ✅ Apply
• GMP 20-50% — Listing Expectation: Decent listing, Apply?: ✅ Apply
• GMP 0-20% — Listing Expectation: Flat listing, Apply?: ⚠️ Think
• GMP negative — Listing Expectation: Below IPO price, Apply?: ❌ Avoid

Where to Check GMP

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• IPO Watch — ipowatch.in
• IPO Central — ipocentral.in
• Chittorgarh — ipo.chittorgarh.com
• InvestorZone — investorzone.com

GMP Reliability

GMP is Reliable When:
• Grey market is active (high volume)
• Multiple sources show similar GMP
• Market sentiment is clear

GMP is Unreliable When:
• Low grey market volume
• Extreme volatility
• Operator manipulation

GMP Limitations

Not Official

GMP is unofficial. SEBI regulated nahi hai.

Can Change Fast

GMP ek din mein 50% badh sakta hai, next day 50% gir sakta hai.

Manipulation Risk

Operators GMP inflate kar sakte hain.

No Guarantee

GMP high hai toh listing acchi hogi — MOSTLY true, but not always.
