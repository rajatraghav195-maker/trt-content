FOMO: WO TRADE JO AAP ISLIYE LETE HO KYUNKI AAP PEECHE REH GAYE

Chalti hui cheez mein der se ghusna — kyun hota hai aur usse rokne ka ek practical niyam

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Stock teen din se bhaag raha hai. Aapne pehle din dekha tha aur chhod diya tha. Chauthe din aap bardasht nahi kar paate aur ghus jaate ho. Wahi din top nikalta hai. Ye ek kahani nahi hai — ye lagbhag ek pattern hai.

YE HOTA KYUN HAI

Do cheezein saath chalti hain. Pehli, chalti hui cheez sabse zyada dikhti hai — news mein, screener mein, chat groups mein. Doosri, dusron ka faayda apne nuksaan se zyada chubhta hai, kyunki wo lagataar saamne rehta hai.

Nateeja ye hota hai ki aap us waqt ghuste ho jab risk sabse zyada hai aur baaki sab pehle se andar hain. Yaani aapke baad kharidne wale sabse kam bache hain.

FOMO TRADE KI PEHCHAAN

Ye nishan lagbhag hamesha milte hain:
• Aapne stop pehle nahi socha — sirf entry socha.
• Aapka reason 'ye bhaag raha hai' hai, koi setup nahi.
• Size normal se badi hai, kyunki 'ye pakka hai'.
• Aap ye trade chart dekh kar nahi, kisi ke message ya headline se le rahe ho.
• Agar aaj mauka nikal gaya to aapko lagta hai ki phir kabhi aisa nahi milega.

EK NIYAM JO ACTUALLY KAAM KARTA HAI

Koi bhi trade lene se pehle use likh kar 10 minute ruko — entry, stop aur wajah. Agar 10 minute baad bhi wo trade utni hi sahi lag rahi hai, to lo.

FOMO ki poori taakat uski jaldi mein hai. 10 minute uska sabse bada hissa khatam kar dete hain. Zyadatar log paate hain ki us waqt tak wo trade lene layak lagti hi nahi.

'AAKHRI MAUKA' EK BHRAM HAI

Market roz khulta hai. Ek saal mein sau se zyada trading days hote hain aur hazaron setups. Jo mauka aaj chhoot gaya, uski jagah agla aa jaayega.

Jo cheez wapas nahi aati wo capital hai. Isliye ek mauka chhodna sasta hai; ek galat trade lena mehnga.

AUR EK IMANDAAR BAAT

FOMO ka ilaaj discipline se zyada exposure kam karne mein hai. Agar aapki screen din bhar chalne wali cheezon se bhari hai, to aap roz apne aap ko test mein daal rahe ho. Kam dekhna kam ladna hai.
