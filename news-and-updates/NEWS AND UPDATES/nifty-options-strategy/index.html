Nifty options trading strategies — straddle, strangle, iron condor, credit spreads — complete Hindi guide.

Nifty Options Kya Hain?

Nifty options Nifty 50 index ke upar based options hain. Tum Nifty ka direction ya range trade kar sakte ho options ke through.

Key Facts:
• Lot size: 25
• Expiry: Weekly (Thursday) + Monthly (Last Thursday)
• Settlement: Cash
• Most liquid options in India

Top 5 Nifty Options Strategies

1. Bull Call Spread (Moderately Bullish)

Setup:
• Buy ATM Call (e.g., 20,000 CE)
• Sell OTM Call (e.g., 20,200 CE)
• Net debit (pay premium)

Max Profit: Width - Premium paid Max Loss: Premium paid When: Moderately bullish

2. Bear Put Spread (Moderately Bearish)

Setup:
• Buy ATM Put (e.g., 20,000 PE)
• Sell OTM Put (e.g., 19,800 PE)
• Net debit (pay premium)

Max Profit: Width - Premium paid Max Loss: Premium paid When: Moderately bearish

3. Iron Condor (Range-Bound)

Setup:
• Sell OTM Call + Buy further OTM Call
• Sell OTM Put + Buy further OTM Put
• Net credit (receive premium)

Max Profit: Credit received Max Loss: Width - Credit When: Range-bound market

4. Short Straddle (High Premium Collection)

Setup:
• Sell ATM Call
• Sell ATM Put
• Net credit

Max Profit: Total premium (if Nifty at ATM at expiry) Max Loss: Unlimited When: Low volatility, range-bound

5. Long Straddle (Big Move Expected)

Setup:
• Buy ATM Call
• Buy ATM Put
• Net debit

Max Profit: Unlimited Max Loss: Total premium paid When: Event-based, big move expected

Strategy Selection Guide

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Moderately bullish — Strategy: Bull Call Spread, Risk: Limited
• Moderately bearish — Strategy: Bear Put Spread, Risk: Limited
• Range-bound — Strategy: Iron Condor, Risk: Limited
• Low volatility — Strategy: Short Straddle, Risk: Unlimited
• Big move expected — Strategy: Long Straddle, Risk: Limited
• No view — Strategy: Calendar Spread, Risk: Limited

Nifty Options Trading Tips

Weekly vs Monthly Expiry:
• Premium — Weekly: Lower, Monthly: Higher
• Theta decay — Weekly: Faster, Monthly: Slower
• Gamma risk — Weekly: Higher, Monthly: Lower
• Best for — Weekly: Experienced, Monthly: Beginners

Best Entry Time:
• 9:30-10:30 AM: Premium stabilized
• Avoid first 15 min: Volatile, wide spreads
• Avoid last 2 hours: Extreme gamma risk

Position Sizing:
• Max 2 lots per strategy initially
• Max 3% of capital per trade
• Scale up with experience
