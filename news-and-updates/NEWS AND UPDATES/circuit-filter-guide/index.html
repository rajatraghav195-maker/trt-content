Circuit Filter Kya Hai?

Circuit filter ek price limit hai jo stock exchange set karta hai. Agar kisi stock ka price ek din mein zyada upar ya neeche jaaye toh trading temporarily rok di jaati hai.

Upper Circuit vs Lower Circuit

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• What happens — Upper Circuit: Price maximum upar gaya, Lower Circuit: Price maximum neeche gaya
• Trading — Upper Circuit: Sirf buyers, sellers nahi, Lower Circuit: Sirf sellers, buyers nahi
• Implication — Upper Circuit: Stock aur badh sakta hai, Lower Circuit: Stock aur gir sakta hai
• Next day — Upper Circuit: Gap up possible, Lower Circuit: Gap down possible

Circuit Filter Levels (NSE/BSE)

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Large cap (Nifty 50) — 20%
• Mid cap — 10-20%
• Small cap — 5-10%
• Newly listed — No circuit (first day)
• SME stocks — 5-10%

Circuit Hone Pe Kya Hota Hai?

Upper Circuit:
• Stock price upper limit touch karta hai
• Trading halt lag jaata hai (ya auction session)
• Sirf buyers hain, sellers nahi
• Agar tumhare paas stock hai — hold karo (aur badh sakta hai)
• Agar kharidna hai — order daalo, match hone ka wait karo

Lower Circuit:
• Stock price lower limit touch karta hai
• Trading halt lag jaata hai
• Sirf sellers hain, buyers nahi
• Agar tumhare paas stock hai — bechna mushkil (buyer nahi milenge)
• Panic mat karo — next day recovery ho sakti hai

Circuit Filter Se Bachne Ke Tips

Stop Loss Lagao

Circuit lagne se pehle exit ho jaao. Stop loss tumhe bachata hai.

Liquidity Check Karo

Low liquidity stocks mein circuit zyada lagta hai. High volume stocks prefer karo.

News Follow Karo

Circuit usually news ke baad lagta hai — earnings, SEBI action, promoter change.

Avoid Circuit Stocks

Agar stock regularly circuit lagaa raha hai toh avoid karo — operator driven ho sakta hai.

Circuit Filter vs Trading Halt

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Trigger — Circuit Filter: Price limit hit, Trading Halt: Corporate announcement
• Duration — Circuit Filter: Intraday ya next day, Trading Halt: 15 min - few hours
• Purpose — Circuit Filter: Price stability, Trading Halt: Information dissemination

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 CIRCUIT FILTER
• 📊 Upper circuit — Price max upar, sirf buyers
• 📊 Lower circuit — Price max neeche, sirf sellers
• 📊 Large cap: 20%, Small cap: 5-10%
• UPPER CIRCUIT:
• 📊 Trading halt, sirf buyers
• 📊 Stock aur badh sakta hai
• 📊 Agar hold karo → wait for next day
• LOWER CIRCUIT:
• 📊 Trading halt, sirf sellers
• 📊 Bechna mushkil (buyer nahi)
• 📊 Panic mat karo, wait for recovery
• TIPS:
