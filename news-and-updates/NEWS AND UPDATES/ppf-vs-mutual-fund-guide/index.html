PPF vs Mutual Fund — Complete Hindi Guide

PPF aur mutual fund mein kya difference hai, kahan invest karein — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 14, 2026 · Features

PPF aur mutual fund mein kya difference hai, kahan invest karein — complete Hindi guide.

PPF Kya Hai?

PPF = Public Provident Fund. Government ka savings scheme hai — safe, guaranteed returns, tax-free.

PPF vs Mutual Fund

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Returns — PPF: 7.1% (guaranteed), Mutual Fund: 12-14% (market-linked)
• Risk — PPF: Zero, Mutual Fund: Medium
• Lock-in — PPF: 15 years, Mutual Fund: None (open-ended)
• Tax benefit — PPF: 80C (₹1.5L), Mutual Fund: ELSS only (80C)
• Tax on returns — PPF: Tax-free, Mutual Fund: LTCG 12.5%
• Liquidity — PPF: Low (partial after 7 years), Mutual Fund: High (anytime)
• Min deposit — PPF: ₹500/year, Mutual Fund: ₹500 SIP

PPF Details

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Interest rate — 7.1% (govt quarterly)
• Tenure — 15 years (extendable in 5-year blocks)
• Max deposit — ₹1.5 lakh/year
• Tax benefit — 80C + Interest tax-free + Maturity tax-free
• Partial withdrawal — After 7 years
• Loan facility — After 3 years

Returns Comparison (30 years)

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• PPF — Monthly: ₹12,500 (₹1.5L/year), Returns: 7.1%, Final Value: ₹1.62 crore
• SIP (Index Fund) — Monthly: ₹12,500, Returns: 12%, Final Value: ₹4.50 crore
• SIP (Active MF) — Monthly: ₹12,500, Returns: 15%, Final Value: ₹7.00 crore

Difference: ₹5.38 crore! MF wins massively over long-term.

When to Choose PPF

PPF Choose Karo:
• Risk nahi lena (guaranteed returns chahiye)
• Tax-free returns chahiye
• Retirement ke liye safe corpus chahiye
• Debt allocation badhani hai

PPF Mat Karo:
• 15 saal se pehle paisa chahiye
• Zyada returns chahiye (12%+)
• Liquidity chahiye

When to Choose Mutual Fund

MF Choose Karo:
• High returns chahiye (12-15%)
• Liquidity chahiye (kabhi bhi withdraw)
• 5+ years ka horizon hai
• Risk le sakte ho

MF Mat Karo:
• Risk nahi lena
• Guaranteed returns chahiye
• 1-2 saal ka horizon hai

Best Strategy: Dono Karo

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Conservative — PPF: 60%, Mutual Fund: 40%
• Balanced — PPF: 40%, Mutual Fund: 60%
• Aggressive — PPF: 20%, Mutual Fund: 80%

Example: ₹1.5L PPF (tax benefit) + ₹10K/month SIP in MF

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 PPF vs MUTUAL FUND
• PPF:
• 📊 7.1% guaranteed, tax-free
• 📊 15-year lock-in, zero risk
• 📊 Max ₹1.5L/year, 80C benefit
• MUTUAL FUND:
• 📊 12-15% market-linked
• 📊 No lock-in, medium risk
• 📊 LTCG 12.5% tax
• RETURNS (30 years):
• 📊 PPF: ₹1.62 Cr (₹12,500/month)
• 📊 MF @ 12%: ₹4.50 Cr
• 📊 MF @ 15%: ₹7.00 Cr
• BEST STRATEGY:
• 📊 Dono karo — PPF (safety) + MF (growth)
• 📊 PPF: ₹1.5L/year (tax benefit)
• 📊 SIP: ₹10-25K/month (wealth creation)
• CHOOSE PPF IF:
• ✅ Risk nahi lena
• ✅ Guaranteed returns
• ✅ Tax-free returns
• CHOOSE MF IF:
• ✅ High returns (12%+)
• ✅ Liquidity chahiye
• ✅ 5+ years horizon
