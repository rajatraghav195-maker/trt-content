Penny Stocks Kya Hain?

Penny stocks wo stocks hain jo bahut saste hote hain — usually ₹1-₹50 ke beech. Ye choti companies ke shares hain.

Simple definition: > Penny stocks = Low price, low market cap, high risk, high reward potential.

Penny Stocks vs Blue Chips

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Price — Penny Stock: ₹1-50, Blue Chip: ₹500+
• Market cap — Penny Stock: < ₹5,000 Cr, Blue Chip: > ₹50,000 Cr
• Risk — Penny Stock: Very high, Blue Chip: Low
• Returns potential — Penny Stock: 100-1000%, Blue Chip: 10-20%
• Liquidity — Penny Stock: Very low, Blue Chip: Very high
• Research — Penny Stock: Very limited, Blue Chip: Abundant

Penny Stock Risks

Operator Manipulation

Penny stocks mein operators price manipulate karte hain. Pump and dump schemes common hain.

No Liquidity

Kharidne ke baad bechne ka mauka nahi milega. Volume bahut kam hota hai.

No Fundamentals

Choti companies ka financial data limited hota hai. Analysis mushkil.

Can Go to Zero

Penny stocks ka price ₹0 tak ja sakta hai. Full loss possible.

Pump and Dump

WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels pe tips aate hain — "Ye stock 10x hoga!" — Most scams hain.

How to Find Penny Stocks (If You Must)

Screeners:
• Screener.in → Price filter < ₹50
• Chartink → Market cap < ₹5,000 Cr
• Tijori Finance → Small cap screeners

Quality Check:
• ✅ Profitable company (PAT > 0)
• ✅ Revenue growing (3 year CAGR)
• ✅ Low debt (Debt/Equity < 1)
• ✅ Promoter holding > 50%
• ✅ Regular dividends
• ❌ Avoid loss-making companies
• ❌ Avoid high debt companies
• ❌ Avoid tips from WhatsApp/Telegram

Penny Stock Allocation Rule

Maximum 5-10% of portfolio in penny stocks. Baaki 90% blue chips aur mutual funds mein.

Example: ₹10 lakh portfolio → Max ₹50,000-1,00,000 in penny stocks.
