TradingView — Overview

Strengths:
• Global platform — 400+ indicators, Pine Script, social features
• Best charts — Most advanced charting in the world
• Mobile app — Full-featured iOS/Android
• Community — 50M+ users, ideas stream, scripts marketplace

Weaknesses for Indian Traders:
• Expensive — ₹3,995-17,333/mo for Indian market features
• English only — No Hindi/Hinglish education
• Not India-first — Indian market is secondary focus
• No Indian market calendar — No IPO/earnings/dividend tracking for NSE/BSE

Best For:
• Traders who need advanced features (Pine Script, custom indicators)
• Those who trade both Indian and global markets
• Budget is not a constraint

Chartink — Overview

Strengths:
• Best screener — Most advanced stock scanner for Indian markets
• Custom scanners — Build your own scanning conditions
• Real-time alerts — Email, SMS, webhook alerts
• Indian focus — NSE/BSE data primary

Weaknesses:
• Basic charting — Charts are not advanced (not a charting platform)
• No education — Minimal educational content
• Expensive premium — ₹999-1,499/mo for advanced features
• No market calendar — No IPO/earnings/dividend tracking

Best For:
• Stock screeners (finding trade setups)
• Traders who use external charting + Chartink for scanning
• Those who need custom scanner conditions

The Retail Trader (TRT) — Overview

Strengths:
• Hinglish education — 50+ articles in Hindi-English mix (unique)
• Full charting terminal — 100+ indicators, drawing tools, multiple timeframes
• Indian market calendar — IPO, earnings, dividends, economic events
• Affordable — ₹199-499/mo (95-97% cheaper than TradingView)
• Index detail pages — 10 indices with fundamental data, components, seasonal patterns
• Screener — NSE/BSE stock screener with technical + fundamental filters

Weaknesses:
• No mobile app — Browser-based (PWA planned Q1 2027)
• No custom scripts — Pine Script equivalent coming
• Smaller community — Growing but smaller than TradingView/Chartink
• Fewer indicators — 100+ vs TradingView's 400+

Best For:
• Indian retail traders who want education + charting combo
• Beginners who prefer learning in Hindi/Hinglish
• Budget-conscious traders who want 95% of features at 5% of cost
• Those who want Indian market calendar (IPO, earnings, dividends)
