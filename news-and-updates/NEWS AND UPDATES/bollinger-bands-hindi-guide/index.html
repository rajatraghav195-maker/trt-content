Bollinger Bands Kya Hai?

Bollinger Bands ek volatility indicator hai jo John Bollinger ne 1980s mein banaya. Ye 3 lines se banta hai:

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Middle Band = 20-period SMA (Simple Moving Average)
• Upper Band = Middle Band + (2 × Standard Deviation)
• Lower Band = Middle Band – (2 × Standard Deviation)

Simple words mein: Bollinger Bands price ke around ek "channel" banata hai. Jab volatility badhti hai → bands expand hote hain. Jab volatility kam hoti hai → bands squeeze ho jaate hain.

Key insight: Price zyadatar middle band (20 SMA) ke around rehta hai. Jab price upper ya lower band tak jaata hai, toh reversal ki probability badh jaati hai.

Bollinger Bands Read Kaise Karein

Band Expansion (Volatility Badh Rahi Hai):
• Upper aur lower bands door ho rahe hain
• Matlab market volatile ho raha hai
• Strong trend chal raha hai
• Trend continuation likely

Band Squeeze (Volatility Kam Ho Rahi Hai):
• Upper aur lower bands paas aa rahe hain
• Matlab market calm hai, consolidation chal raha hai
• BREAKOUT AANE WALA HAI — squeeze ke baad expansion hota hai
• Ye sabse important signal hai!

Band Contraction vs Expansion Cycle:
• Squeeze → Expansion → Contraction → Squeeze → Expansion...
• (calm) (volatile) (calming) (calm) (volatile)

Market hamesha is cycle mein rehti hai. Squeeze = storm aane se pehle ki shanti.

Bollinger Bands Strategies

Strategy 1: Squeeze Breakout (Sabse Popular)

Setup:
• Bollinger Bands squeeze ho rahe hain (bahut paas)
• Price middle band ke around consolidating
• Suddenly price upper band ke upar break karta hai + volume spike
• Buy entry: Breakout candle close ke baad
• Stop loss: Middle band (20 SMA) ke thoda neeche
• Target: Band width jitni hai, utna target (measured move)

Sell setup: Reverse — price lower band break kare + volume.

Strategy 2: Mean Reversion (Band Bounce)

Logic: Price hamesha middle band ki taraf wapas aata hai (mean reversion).

Buy setup:
• Price lower band ko touch karta hai
• RSI oversold (< 30) hai
• Bullish reversal candle (hammer, bullish engulfing) lower band pe banti hai
• Entry: Reversal candle close ke baad
• Target: Middle band (20 SMA)
• Stop loss: Lower band ke thoda neeche

Sell setup: Reverse — price upper band touch + RSI overbought + bearish candle.

Strategy 3: Trend Riding (Walking the Bands)

Logic: Strong trend mein price band ke outside reh sakta hai — "walking the band".

Buy setup:
• Price upper band ke upar hai (strong uptrend)
• Price upper band ko touch karta hai but close upper band ke upar hota hai
• Bands expanding
• Entry: Pullback to middle band → bounce
• Stop loss: Middle band break
• Target: Next upper band level

Important: Trend riding mein band ko touch karna reversal nahi hai — trend continuation hai. Don't short just because price touched upper band in uptrend!

Bollinger Bands + RSI Combo (Powerful)

Bollinger Bands + RSI = best combo for mean reversion trades.

Perfect Buy Setup:
• Price Bollinger Lower Band ko touch karta hai
• RSI < 30 (oversold)
• Bullish candle pattern (hammer, morning star)
• Volume spike on reversal candle
• Entry: Candle close ke baad
• Target: Middle band
• Stop loss: Lower band – 1 ATR

Perfect Sell Setup:
• Price Bollinger Upper Band ko touch karta hai
• RSI > 70 (overbought)
• Bearish candle pattern (shooting star, evening star)
• Volume spike on reversal candle
• Entry: Candle close ke baad
• Target: Middle band
• Stop loss: Upper band + 1 ATR

Example: RELIANCE pe Bollinger + RSI

RELIANCE daily chart pe Bollinger Bands + RSI lagao. Dekho:
• Kabhi price lower band + RSI < 30 = bounce back to middle band
• Kabhi price upper band + RSI > 70 = pullback to middle band
• Squeeze ke baad breakout with volume = strong move

Bollinger Band Settings

Default: 20, 2:
• Period (20): 20 candles ka SMA
• Std Dev (2): 2 standard deviations
• Best for: Daily charts, swing trading

Custom Settings:
• 20, 2 (default) — Best For: Swing trading, Notes: Standard, reliable
• 20, 1.5 — Best For: Intraday, Notes: Tighter bands, more touches
• 20, 2.5 — Best For: Long-term, Notes: Wider bands, fewer signals

Common Mistakes

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Har band touch pe trade lena — Upper band touch = sell, lower band touch = buy. GALAT. Trend mein bands walk karte hain. Context dekho.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Squeeze mein trade lena — Squeeze ke dauran sideways chalta hai. Breakout ka wait karo, squeeze ke andar mat trade karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Target bina set kiye trade lena — Bollinger Bands target deta hai (middle band for mean reversion, band width for breakout). Use it.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Sirf Bollinger use karna — RSI ya volume ke bina Bollinger signals 50% accurate. RSI + volume = 70%+.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Standard deviation samajhe bina settings change karna — Default 2 std dev statistical significance rakhta hai. 1.5 ya 2.5 change karne se meaning badal jaata hai.

FAQ

Q1: Bollinger Bands aur Keltner Channels mein difference?

Bollinger Bands Standard Deviation use karta hai (volatility based). Keltner Channels ATR use karta hai. Bollinger zyada responsive hai, Keltner zyada smooth.

Q2: Squeeze kitne din tak reh sakta hai?

Kabhi 2-3 din, kabhi 2-3 weeks. Squeeze jitni lambi, breakout utna bada. Lekin exact time predict nahi kar sakte.

Q3: Kya Bollinger Bands intraday mein kaam karta hai?

Haan, but 5 min ya 15 min chart pe use karo. 1 min pe bahut noise hai. Intraday ke liye 20, 1.5 settings try karo.

Q5: Bollinger Bands se 100% accurate signals?
