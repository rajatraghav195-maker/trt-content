Retail Investor Checklist — Apply Karne Se Pehle Kya Check Karein

1. Official Sources Se Confirm Karo:
• NSE/BSE circulars check karo
• Broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One) mein dates verify karo
• Company ka RHP (Red Herring Prospectus) padho
• Mat use karo: Aggregator sites, WhatsApp forwards, "IPO gyan" Telegram channels

2. Financials Padho — Sirf Buzz Nahi:
• Revenue growth: Last 3 years consistent growth?
• Profitability: Profit ban raha hai ya loss? Loss acceptable hai agar path to profit clear ho
• Debt levels: High debt = risk
• IPO proceeds ka use: Fresh issue (company ko paisa) vs OFS (existing investors exit) — fresh issue better

3. GMP (Grey Market Premium) — Trap Hai:
• GMP unofficial, unregulated indicator hai
• Double-digit GMP listing day pe negative ho sakta hai
• GMP = short-term sentiment, NOT fundamentals
• Rule: GMP dekho as one data point, decision ka basis mat banao

4. Minimum Investment Aur Lot Size:
• Mainboard IPOs: ₹9,000-15,000 per application (approximately)
• SME IPOs: ₹1,00,000 minimum (SEBI mandated)
• Apne broker mein application amount check karo

5. Investor Category Aur Quota:
• Retail quota: usually ~35% of net offer
• QIB (Qualified Institutional Buyers): ~50%
• NII (Non-Institutional Investors): ~15%
• Retail mein allotment lottery-based hai (oversubscription ho toh)

6. Sector Diversification

August mein sectors diverse hain:

Strategy: Ek hi sector mein sab IPOs pe mat lagao. Diversify karo — 2-3 different sectors mein applications spread karo.

Mainboard vs SME IPO — Key Differences

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Platform — Mainboard: NSE/BSE main, SME: BSE SME / NSE Emerge
• Compliance — Mainboard: Higher, SME: Lighter
• Min investment — Mainboard: ~₹14,000-15,000, SME: ₹1,00,000
• Allotment — Mainboard: Lottery-based, SME: Proportionate
• Liquidity — Mainboard: High, SME: Low
• Volatility — Mainboard: Moderate, SME: High
• Risk — Mainboard: Moderate, SME: High

TRT Recommendation: Beginners ke liye mainboard IPOs better hain — lower minimum investment, better liquidity, higher compliance standards.

Common IPO Mistakes

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• GMP pe decision lena — Consequence: GMP negative ho toh loss, Fix: Fundamentals padho
• Har IPO pe apply karna — Consequence: Capital分散, allotment kam, Fix: Selective apply karo
• SME IPO mein "sasta" sochna — Consequence: ₹1L minimum, low liquidity, Fix: SME avoid beginners
• Listing day pe hold karna — Consequence: Profit evaporate, Fix: Listing pe 50% sell, 50% hold
• Loan lekar IPO apply karna — Consequence: Interest > listing gain, Fix: Sirf spare capital use karo
• OFS-heavy IPO pe apply — Consequence: Company ko paisa nahi jaa raha, Fix: Fresh issue prefer karo

FAQ

Q: Kitne IPOs mein ek saath apply kar sakte hain? A: Jitne chahein, lekin capital limited hai. 3-4 good IPOs select karo, spread karo. Over-commit mat karo.

Q: Zepto kab list hoga? A: Official dates announce nahi huye hain. RHP file hua hai but price band aur dates pending. Track SEBI/exchange filings.

Q: IPO mein loss ho gaya toh? A: IPO listing below issue price ho sakta hai. Listing pe 50% sell karne ka rule rakhho. Baaki hold karo agar fundamentals strong hain.

Q: UPI se IPO apply karna safe hai? A: Haan, UPI-based IPO application (ASBA) completely safe hai. Paisa block hota hai, allotment na ho toh unblock ho jata hai. No risk.

Q: IPO review kahan se padhein? A: RHP padho (company ki website ya SEBI). Broker research reports dekho. TRT pe educational content padho. Tip channels se avoid karo.
