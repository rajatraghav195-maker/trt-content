Margin of Safety Kya Hai?

Margin of safety = Intrinsic value aur market price ka difference. Agar tumhare calculation ke according stock ki value ₹100 hai aur market price ₹70 hai, toh ₹30 ka margin of safety hai.

Warren Buffett: "Price is what you pay, value is what you get."

How to Calculate

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Intrinsic Value: ₹100 (tumhare calculation ke according)
• Market Price: ₹70
• Margin of Safety: (₹100 - ₹70) / ₹100 = 30%

Why It Matters

Protection Against Errors

Agar tum galat ho toh bhi margin of safety tumhe bachata hai.

Downside Protection

30% margin = Stock 30% gira toh bhi tumhara paisa safe hai (break-even).

Upside Potential

30% discount pe kharida → Agar stock ₹100 tak jaaye → 43% return.

How Much Margin of Safety?

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stable large cap — 20-30%
• Mid cap — 30-40%
• Small cap — 40-50%
• Cyclical stock — 50%+
• Turnaround story — 50%+

Example

Ye saat baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Reliance intrinsic value: ₹3,000
• Market price: ₹2,100
• Margin of safety: 30%
• If correct: Stock goes to ₹3,000 → 43% return
• If wrong (value is ₹2,500): Still ₹400 upside → 19% return
• If very wrong (value is ₹2,100): Break even
