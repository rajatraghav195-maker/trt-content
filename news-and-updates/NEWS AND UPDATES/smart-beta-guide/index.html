Smart Beta Kya Hai?

Smart beta = Index funds jaisa passive, but rules-based stock selection — market cap ke bajay factors jaise quality, momentum, value, low volatility use karte hain.

Simple definition: > Smart beta = Index fund + smart rules. Passive hai, but market-cap weighted nahi.

Smart Beta vs Index Fund vs Active Fund

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Management — Smart Beta: Rules-based, Index Fund: Passive, Active Fund: Active
• Expense — Smart Beta: 0.3-1%, Index Fund: 0.1-0.3%, Active Fund: 1-2%
• Stock selection — Smart Beta: Factor-based, Index Fund: Market cap, Active Fund: Fund manager
• Returns — Smart Beta: Alpha potential, Index Fund: Market returns, Active Fund: Variable
• Risk — Smart Beta: Medium, Index Fund: Medium, Active Fund: High

Common Factors

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Quality — What It Does: High ROE, low debt stocks, Best When: Stable markets
• Momentum — What It Does: Recent winners, Best When: Bull markets
• Value — What It Does: Undervalued stocks, Best When: Bear markets/recovery
• Low Volatility — What It Does: Defensive stocks, Best When: Bear markets
• Size — What It Does: Small cap tilt, Best When: Early bull market
• Dividend — What It Does: High dividend yield, Best When: Income seeking
