Position Sizing Kya Hai?

Position sizing ka matlab hai ki ek trade mein kitna paisa lagana hai. Ye risk management ka sabse important part hai.

Simple definition: > Position sizing = "Is trade mein kitne shares kharidne hain?" Ye tumhara risk decide karta hai.

Why Position Sizing Matters

Without Position Sizing:
• Ek trade mein ₹5,00,000 laga diya
• 10% loss = ₹50,000 (10% of ₹5,00,000 capital)
• 3 consecutive losses = ₹1,50,000 gone

With Position Sizing (2% Rule):
• Max risk per trade: ₹10,000 (2% of ₹5,00,000)
• 10% loss on position = ₹10,000 (controlled)
• 10 consecutive losses = ₹1,00,000 (survivable)

Position Sizing Formula

Method 1: Fixed Percentage (Simplest):
• Position Size = (Account × Risk%) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Risk: 2% = ₹10,000
• Entry: ₹2,500
• Stop Loss: ₹2,450
• Risk per share: ₹50
• Position Size = ₹10,000 / ₹50 = 200 shares
• Total investment: 200 × ₹2,500 = ₹5,00,000

Method 2: ATR-Based:
• Position Size = (Account × Risk%) / (2 × ATR)
• ATR = Average True Range (stock's volatility)
• Example:
• Account: ₹5,00,000
• Risk: 2% = ₹10,000
• Stock ATR: ₹30
• Stop loss: 2 × ATR = ₹60
• Position Size = ₹10,000 / ₹60 = 166 shares

Method 3: Volatility-Adjusted:
• Volatility Factor = Stock ATR / Nifty ATR
• If factor = 1.5 (stock 1.5x more volatile than Nifty)
• → Position size 1.5x smaller
• Normal: 200 shares
• Adjusted: 200 / 1.5 = 133 shares

Position Sizing Rules

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Max risk per trade — Value: 2%, Why: Survive 10 consecutive losses
• Max positions — Value: 5, Why: 10% total portfolio risk
• Daily loss limit — Value: 5%, Why: Stop trading if losing day
• Weekly loss limit — Value: 10%, Why: Take a break
• Risk:Reward minimum — Value: 1:2, Why: Profitable even at 50% win rate

Position Sizing by Trading Style

Ye teen baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Intraday — Risk/Trade: 1-2%, Max Positions: 1-2, Total Risk: 2-4%
• Swing — Risk/Trade: 2%, Max Positions: 3-5, Total Risk: 6-10%
• Positional — Risk/Trade: 2-3%, Max Positions: 5-8, Total Risk: 10-15%

Common Position Sizing Mistakes

All-In (Sab Ek Trade Mein)

"Mujhe confidence hai, sab laga deta hoon" — Ek galat trade = account khatam.

Averaging Down with Bigger Size

"Gir raha hai, aur kharid leta hoon (badi quantity)" — Loss double ho jaata hai.

Increasing Size After Wins

"3 jeet liye, ab bada lot lagata hoon" — Overconfidence → big loss.

Same Size for All Stocks

Reliance aur small-cap stock mein same quantity = galat. Volatility adjust karo.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
