SABAR: MARKET MEIN SABSE KAM MEHSOOS HONE WALA SKILL

Kam trade lena, intezaar karna, aur 'kuch na karna' ko ek faisle ki tarah dekhna

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Trading Psychology

Trading ka sabse kam glamorous hissa yahi hai: zyadatar waqt kuch nahi karna. Aur yahi sabse mushkil hissa hai, kyunki screen ke saamne baith kar kuch na karna galat lagta hai.

HAR DIN TRADE NAHI HOTA

Aapka setup roz nahi banta. Agar aap roz trade le rahe ho, to iska matlab hai ki aap apne setup ke bahar bhi trade le rahe ho — aur wahi trades aksar poore mahine ka nateeja tay karti hain.

Ek imandaar hisaab: apne pichle 50 trades mein ginno ki kitni aapke likhe hue setup ki thi. Zyadatar logon ke liye ye number chaunkane wala hota hai.

BOREDOM SABSE MEHENGA HAI

Boredom se li gayi trade ka koi thesis nahi hota, isliye uska koi exit plan bhi nahi hota. Wo aksar hold ho jaati hai, average ho jaati hai, aur ek chhoti si galti bade nuksaan mein badal jaati hai.

Yahi wajah hai ki full-time screen ke saamne baithna kai logon ke liye ulta padta hai. Zyada time zyada opportunity nahi deta — wo zyada temptation deta hai.

INTEZAAR KO KAAM MEIN BADLO

Jab koi setup nahi hai, tab ye karne layak hai:
• Watchlist banao — kaunse naam kis level par interesting honge. Level pehle likho, baad mein nahi.
• Purane trades review karo — ek hafte purani entries ab kaisi dikhti hain.
• Ek setup ka data ikattha karo — 20 udaharan dhundo aur dekho wo kaisa chalta hai.
• Screen band karo — ye bhi kaam hai, aur sabse kam kiya jaata hai.

'CASH MEIN BAITHNA' BHI EK POSITION HAI

Har waqt market mein hone ki koi zaroorat nahi hai, aur na hi usme koi bahaduri hai. Jab conditions aapke system ke khilaaf hon, to cash sabse acchi position hoti hai.

Iska sabse bada faayda paisa bachana nahi hai — wo dhyaan bachana hai. Ek khaali account us setup ko turant le sakta hai jo asli mein aata hai; ek bhara hua account use dekh kar reh jaata hai.

EK LINE

Trading mein kamai activity se nahi, selectivity se aati hai. Jo trader kam trade karta hai par apne rules par karta hai, wo lambe samay mein us trader se aage nikal jaata hai jo roz kuch na kuch karta hai.

Ye article sirf educational hai — koi buy ya sell recommendation nahi, koi guaranteed return ka daawa nahi, aur koi advisory nahi. Rules, rates aur mechanics exchange, SEBI aur RBI time-time par badalte hain, isliye har number apne broker ya official source par khud verify karo.
