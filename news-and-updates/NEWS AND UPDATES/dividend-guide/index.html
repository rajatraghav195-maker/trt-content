Dividend Kya Hai?

Dividend ek cash payment hai jo company apne shareholders ko deti hai — apne profit mein se. Jab company profit kamati hai, wo profit ka kuch hissa shareholders ko dividend ke form mein deti hai.

Simple definition: > Dividend = Company ka profit share jo tumhe cash mein milta hai. Tumhare paas shares hain = tumhe dividend milega.

Real Example:
• ITC dividend deta hai: ₹6.25 per share (2025)
• Tumhare paas 1,000 shares: 1,000 × ₹6.25 = ₹6,250 cash
• Ye paisa directly tumhare bank account mein aata hai

Dividend Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Timeline:
• Declaration Date → Ex-Date → Record Date → Payment Date
• Company Shares Shareholders Cash

Key Dates:
• Declaration Date — Company announce karti hai dividend
• Ex-Date — Is date ke baad share kharidne pe dividend NAHI milega
• Record Date — Is din shareholders ki list banti hai
• Payment Date — Dividend cash mein milta hai

Important: Ex-Date

Ex-date se PEHLE share kharidna padega dividend ke liye. Ex-date ke baad kharidoge toh dividend nahi milega.

Ex-date pe stock price bhi dividend amount se girta hai — kyunki company ne wo paisa de diya.

Dividend Yield Kya Hai?

Dividend yield batata hai ki stock price ke comparison mein kitna dividend mil raha hai.

Dividend Yield = (Annual Dividend per Share / Stock Price) × 100

Example:
• ITC price: ₹450
• Annual dividend: ₹6.25
• Dividend yield: (₹6.25 / ₹450) × 100 = 1.39%

Dividend Yield Levels:
• < 1% — Low dividend (growth company)
• 1-3% — Moderate dividend
• 3-5% — Good dividend
• 5-8% — High dividend
• > 8% — Very high (check if sustainable)

Dividend Taxation (India)

After April 2020

Dividend income tumhari taxable income mein add hota hai. Company TDS bhi katti hai.

Ye do baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Dividend > ₹5,000 (per company) — 10% TDS
• PAN not provided — 20% TDS

Total tax: Tumhari income tax slab ke according. Agar 30% bracket mein ho toh dividend pe 30% tax.

Tax Planning:
• Dividend stocks tax-inefficient hain — income pe full slab rate lagta hai
• Growth stocks tax-efficient hain — LTCG sirf 10% (₹1L exemption)
• Better strategy: Growth stocks lo, capital gains pe 10% tax do

Dividend vs Growth Investing

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Income — Dividend Investing: Regular cash flow, Growth Investing: No income (reinvested)
• Tax — Dividend Investing: Slab rate (high), Growth Investing: LTCG 10% (low)
• Volatility — Dividend Investing: Lower (stable companies), Growth Investing: Higher (growth companies)
• Returns — Dividend Investing: 6-10% (yield + appreciation), Growth Investing: 12-20% (appreciation)
• Best for — Dividend Investing: Retirees, income seekers, Growth Investing: Long-term wealth builders
• Risk — Dividend Investing: Lower, Growth Investing: Moderate-High

Recommendation: Under 40? Growth investing karo. Over 50? Dividend investing consider karo.

Common Dividend Mistakes

High Yield = Good Stock

8%+ yield check karo — sustainable hai ya company stock price gir gayi isliye yield high hai? (Yield trap)

Ex-Date Se Pehle Sirf Dividend Ke Liye Kharidna

Ex-date pe stock price dividend amount se girta hai. Net effect zero hai. Dividend ke liye stock mat kharido.

Dividend Tax Ignore Karna

Dividend pe full slab rate lagta hai. ₹10 lakh income pe 30% tax = ₹30,000 on ₹1 lakh dividend. Tax planning zaroori.

Sirf Dividend Stocks Rakhna

Diversification zaroori hai. Sirf dividend stocks = sector concentration risk.
