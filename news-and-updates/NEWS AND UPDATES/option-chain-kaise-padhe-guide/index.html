Option Chain Kya Hai?

Option chain ek table hai jisme ek stock/index ke saare strike prices ke Call aur Put options ka data ek saath dikhta hai.

Option chain mein kya data milta hai:
• Strike price
• Call: Open Interest (OI), OI change, Volume, Premium, Implied Volatility (IV)
• Put: OI, OI change, Volume, Premium, IV
• PCR (Put-Call Ratio)

TRT pe: theretailtrader.org/index-details/NSEI → Options tab. Nifty 50 ka full option chain available.

Option Chain Components

Open Interest (OI):
• Kitne contracts abhi "open" hain (not yet squared off)
• High OI = zyada interest at that strike
• OI badhna = naye positions aa rahe hain
• OI ghatna = positions close ho rahe hain

OI Change:
• Aaj ka OI minus kal ka OI
• Positive change = naye positions (bullish/bearish depends on Call/Put)
• Negative change = positions closing

Volume:
• Kitne contracts aaj trade hue
• High volume = active strike (good for entry/exit)
• Low volume = illiquid (avoid — slippage)

Implied Volatility (IV):
• Market ki volatility expectation
• High IV = expensive options
• Low IV = cheap options
• IV increases before events (RBI, budget, results)

Premium:
• Option ki current price
• ITM options ka premium zyada
• OTM options ka premium kam

Option Chain Analysis

Step 1: Max OI Strike Dhoondo:
• Call side mein sabse high OI wala strike = resistance (writers bullish nahi hain)
• Put side mein sabse high OI wala strike = support (writers bearish nahi hain)

Example:
• Nifty at 24,600
• Call max OI: 25,000 strike (resistance)
• Put max OI: 24,400 strike (support)
• Expected range: 24,400 - 25,000

Step 2: PCR (Put-Call Ratio) Check:
• PCR = Total Put OI / Total Call OI
• PCR > 1.5 = bullish (zyada puts being bought = hedging/protection)
• PCR < 0.7 = bearish (zyada calls being bought = speculation)
• PCR 0.8-1.2 = neutral

Step 3: OI Change Analysis:
• Call OI increasing + Put OI decreasing = bearish (writers selling calls, puts being closed)
• Put OI increasing + Call OI decreasing = bullish (writers selling puts, calls being closed)
• Both increasing = range expected
• Both decreasing = trend expected (positions unwinding)

Step 4: Max Pain:
• Max pain = wo price jahan sabse zyade option writers ka loss minimum hoga
• Matlab: expiry pe price max pain level ki taraf jaane ki tendency hai
• Calculation: sum of all option writers' losses at each strike → minimum loss strike = max pain

Example:
• Nifty at 24,600
• Max pain: 24,500
• On expiry day, price may gravitate towards 24,500

Option Chain se Market Direction

Bullish Signals:
• Put OI increasing at support → writers selling puts = support strong
• PCR increasing (> 1.2) → puts being bought = hedging = bullish sentiment
• Call OI decreasing → call writers closing = bullish
• IV decreasing → panic reducing = bullish

Bearish Signals:
• Call OI increasing at resistance → writers selling calls = resistance strong
• PCR decreasing (< 0.8) → calls being bought = speculative bearish
• Put OI decreasing → put writers closing = bearish
• IV increasing → panic increasing = bearish

Option Chain Trading Strategy

Strategy 1: Range Trading (Non-Expiry):
• Find max Call OI (resistance) and max Put OI (support)
• Buy near support (Put side), sell near resistance (Call side)
• Range trade between these levels

Strategy 2: Expiry Day Max Pain:
• On expiry day, identify max pain level
• Price tends to move toward max pain
• If price above max pain → sell Call (price may come down)
• If price below max pain → sell Put (price may go up)
• Advanced only — option selling with hedging

Strategy 3: OI Breakout:
• Find strike with rapidly increasing OI
• If Call OI surging → resistance being built → sell near that strike
• If Put OI surging → support being built → buy near that strike

Common Option Chain Mistakes

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Static data pe trade — OI changes every minute. Use live data (TRT Pro).

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Sirf OI dekhna — OI + Price + Volume + IV = full picture. Don't use OI alone.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Max pain pe blindly trade — Max pain is tendency, not guarantee. 30% time it doesn't work.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• PCR ko ultimate signal manna — PCR is one indicator. Combine with price action and trend.

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Option chain on expiry day pe over-analyze — Expiry day OI data unreliable (positions being squared off). Use pre-expiry data.
