Market Order vs Limit Order — Hindi Guide

Market order aur limit order mein kya difference hai, kaunsa use karein — complete Hindi guide.

Published by The Retail Trader · AUG 09, 2026 · Features

Market order aur limit order mein kya difference hai, kaunsa use karein — complete Hindi guide.

Market Order vs Limit Order

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Price — Market Order: Current market price, Limit Order: Tumhara specified price
• Execution — Market Order: Instant (guaranteed), Limit Order: Only if price match
• Best for — Market Order: Liquid stocks, Limit Order: Specific price target
• Risk — Market Order: Slippage, Limit Order: Non-execution
• Use case — Market Order: Quick entry/exit, Limit Order: Patience se buy/sell

Market Order

Buy market: Current price pe turant kharidna.

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock: Reliance
• Current price: ₹2,500
• Market order: Buy 100 shares
• Result: 100 shares @ ~₹2,500 (instant execution)

Pros: Instant execution, guaranteed Cons: Slippage (price difference), especially in volatile market

Limit Order

Buy limit: Specific price pe kharidna.

Ye chaar baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Stock: Reliance
• Current price: ₹2,500
• Limit order: Buy 100 @ ₹2,450
• Result: Only executes if Reliance falls to ₹2,450

Pros: Exact price control Cons: May not execute if price doesn't reach

When to Use

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Liquid stock, quick entry — Market order
• Specific price target — Limit order
• Volatile market — Limit order
• Stop loss — SL-M (market)
• Buying dip — Limit order below current

Order Types Summary

Ye paanch baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Market — Current price, instant
• Limit — Your price, may not execute
• SL (Stop Loss) — Trigger → Market order
• SL-L (SL Limit) — Trigger → Limit order
• AMO — After market hours

Cheat Sheet

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📌 MARKET vs LIMIT ORDER
• MARKET ORDER:
• 📊 Current price pe instant execution
• 📊 Guaranteed execution
• 📊 Slippage risk (volatile market)
• 📊 Best for: Liquid stocks, quick entry
• LIMIT ORDER:
• 📊 Tumhara specified price
• 📊 May not execute
• 📊 Price control
• 📊 Best for: Specific target, dip buying
• WHEN TO USE:
• 📊 Quick entry/exit → Market
• 📊 Specific price → Limit
• 📊 Volatile market → Limit
• 📊 Stop loss → SL-M (market)
