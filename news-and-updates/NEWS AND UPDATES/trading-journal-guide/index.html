Trading Journal Kya Hai?

Trading journal = Har trade ka record. Entry price, exit price, reason, emotion, result — sab likhna. Improvement ka sabse powerful tool.

Why Keep a Journal

Pattern Recognition

Pata chalega ki kaunsi strategy kaam karti hai, kaunsi nahi.

Mistake Tracking

Same mistake baar-baar kar rahe ho? Journal dikhayega.

Emotional Control

"Revenge trade kiya" ya "FOMO mein buy kiya" — Journal se pata chalega.

Confidence Building

Profitable months dekhke confidence badhta hai.

What to Record

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Date — 14 Aug 2026
• Stock — Reliance
• Direction — Long (Buy)
• Entry — ₹2,500
• Stop Loss — ₹2,450
• Target — ₹2,600
• Quantity — 100
• R:R Ratio — 1:2
• Reason — Support bounce + RSI oversold
• Emotion — Confident
• Result — Target hit → ₹10,000 profit
• Lesson — Patience pays. SL nahi hita.

Monthly Review

Har month end pe journal review karo:

Ye chhe baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• Win rate — Kitne trades profitable?
• Average R:R — Average risk-reward?
• Best strategy — Kaunsi strategy best?
• Worst strategy — Kaunsi strategy worst?
• Emotional trades — Kitne emotion-based?
• Max drawdown — Sabse bada loss streak?

Journal Template

Ye baatein dhyaan mein rakho:
• 📊 TRADE #[Number]
• Date: DD/MM/YYYY
• Stock: Name
• Direction: Long/Short
• Entry: ₹…
• SL: ₹…
• Target: ₹…
• R:R: 1:…
• Reason: [Why this trade?]
• Emotion: [How did you feel?]
• Result: Profit/Loss ₹…
• Lesson: [What did you learn?]
